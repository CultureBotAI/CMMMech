## Scientific boundaries for a future record

The Mibladen, Morocco natural material contains ankerite and barite. Approximately1mm³ surface fragments and 0.2%(w/v) ground powder are separate assay preparations. Synthetic Pb5(VO4)3OH contains litharge/Pb8V2O13 and cannot be treated as the same chloride mineral.

Methods specify A.niger ATCC 201373, at least triplicate Czapek-Dox agar assays, 25°C in darkness, initial pH adjusted 5.5, 25 cm³ agar per 90mm plate, and 5 mm mycelial-core inocula. Mineral sterilization at 105°C for 48h,80°C-water crystal extraction and subsequent drying are relevant preparation boundaries.

The text reports surface attack after 3weeks and natural-powder lead oxalate/whewellite by XRPD after 2months. In contrast, larger natural crystals reportedly lacked detectable lead-oxalate XRPD even after 3months. These are different analytical scales and endpoints. Original Fig. 5 is still needed to independently assess the phase assignments. The Fig. 4 caption points to Fig. 2 for an A.niger product although Fig. 3 is the A.niger microscopy set; do not silently repair that source cross-reference.

Table 3 lists fungal natural-feed pH 3.96±0.55 after 12days, with dispersion described by the source as relative standard deviations. The missing uninoculated mineral entries prevent a paired measured acidification estimate. Tables 1/2 describe fungal extension/biomass tolerance, not a V/Pb yield.

Abiotic oxalic-acid reactions provide mechanistic context, with separately reported slide 0.05mM, agar-well 0.1–0.75M oxalic/0.5–1M citric, and liquid 50/100mM acid conditions. These are not matched live-culture quantitative controls. Organic-acid data are preliminary and not shown. Geochemical plots are modeled, not measured dissolved-species data; Fig. 8 assumes 100mM oxalate while Fig. 9/methods use 25mM. A chemical yellow-to-blue change is not direct microbial V-reduction evidence. No extraction percentage, isolated recovered-metal yield, mass balance, or industrial deployment is supported.
