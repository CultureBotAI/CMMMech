## Nejad2020: complete original HTML and graphical review

Publisher PDF request returned article HTML and PMC PDF returned a challenge page: neither is falsely counted as an acquired PDF. Full HTML includes all methods, Tables1–3, references and Figures1–7. All seven original publisher PNG figures were acquired and visually inspected. The attached11-page ESM was acquired and inspected. No source-derived raw files enter Git.

- **Feed:** methods “Oily sludge waste” and Table1 identify a black semisolid crude-oil reservoir tank-bottom sludge from Karoon Oil and Gas Company, Khuzestan, Iran. Tank identifier, collection date, weathering history and replicate feed batches are unreported. Table1 givespH7.1,19%solids,20.65wt%water,1.36wt%ash and44,500mg/kgTPH. The different bases do not constitute a closed composition balance; this work does not reconstruct one. Metal concentrations includeNi23,Cu14,Zn168mg/kg, and source also containsFe/Al/Ca/As/Cr/Cd. No mineral or ion speciation is demonstrated.
- **Organism:** source calls live isolate *Pseudomonas balearica*Z8. Its ESM1 is the complete earlier Ahmadi2017 paper, *A novel salt-tolerant bacterial consortium for biodegradation of saline and recalcitrant petrochemical wastewater*, [DOI10.1016/j.jenvman.2017.01.010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2017.01.010). Sections2.1–2.2/3.2.3 and Figure2/PDFpp2,4–5 give prior wastewater isolation/16S identification, not oily-feed metal evidence. The order of author names or taxon lists cannot assign these automatically. Current NCBITaxon74829 is *Stutzerimonas balearica*, species rank, with *Pseudomonas balearica* synonym. The Z8 nucleotide record references the2017 article and Iranian petrochemical wastewater. A partial16S label corroborates reported provenance; it does not authenticate the later stock or imply genome-level identity.
- **Acclimation:**10gsludge/100mLmineralmedium in250mLflask,5mLnutrient-brothculture atOD6002, medium initialpH7.0. Medium suppliesFe/Mn and traceZn/Co/Mo/Cu, plus phosphate salts. No dose equivalence with every later treatment vessel is assumed. ClaimedmethodUSEPA3050B is not proof of total silicate dissolution. No reagent/matrix blanks, certified-material recovery, detection limits, metal-phase sampling or independent before/after dry-mass inventory are supplied. Instrument calledICP-MS(ModelSPECTROARCOS) conflicts with the [manufacturer's ICP-OES designation](https://www.spectro.com/products/icp-oes-aes-spectrometers/arcos-inductively-coupled-plasma); actual laboratory platform remains unresolved.

Enrichment medium inputs further complicate metal accounting. Living culture association does not demonstrate that one molecular product caused a metal change.

Figure2 labels growth up to14.1logMPN without a usable dimensional denominator; this is not a verified biomass dose. Figure7c includes a green near-initialTPH curve without a visible legend assignment in the acquired image. These hydrocarbon/surfactant results are not used to compute metal removal. No congener identity, calibrated cell count, metal sorption capacity or biodegradation-derived metal recovery is adopted.
