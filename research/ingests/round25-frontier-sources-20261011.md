Methods 2.2 and Results 3.1 directly identify Danish biogas-desulfurization powder and a repeated-cycle culture producing acidic liquor. The record preserves the actual operating conditions, withdrawals/replenishment, source-labelled medium and measured liquor endpoints. Retained material and missing per-cycle accounting prohibit a sulfur-conversion yield; no matched uninoculated residue or commercial-sulfur comparison is reported here.

The paper's A. thiooxidans DSM 1927 conflicts with the live [DSMZ 1927 catalogue](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-1927), which identifies A. ferrooxidans F221. The experimental stock cannot be repaired from that contradiction. [DSM 9466](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-9466) resolves A. caldus C-SH12, but no individual culture cause is established. The mixed mechanism has no invented organism CURIE.

A nominal 1 L vessel is still laboratory work. Commercial-source advantages attributed to prior papers are not controlled in this experiment.
