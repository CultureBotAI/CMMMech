Franciscanite (IMA 1985-038) is an IMA-accepted manganese-vanadium nesosilicate mineral of the welinite group, the V5+ analogue of welinite, with IMA September 2026 Master List formula Mn2+6(V5+☐)(SiO4)2O3(OH)3 and empirical formula Mn5.96[V0.98[]1.02]Si2.00(O,OH)~14. Described by Dunn et al. (1986) from the Pennsylvania manganese mine in the Franciscan Complex, San Antonio Valley, Santa Clara County, California, USA (holotype NMNH 163041), where it occurs as dark brownish-red grains with gageite, sonolite, hausmannite, and barite. Electron microprobe analysis in Table 2 (p. 1523) gives SiO2 18.4, MnO 64.9, V2O5 13.5, Fe2O3 0.0, MgO 0.0, As2O5 0.0, and H2O 3.4 wt% (calculated from the Si:H ratio of type welinite because paucity of franciscanite precluded direct water/TGA measurement; thermal analyses in the paper were performed on welinite and impure örebroite), total 100.2 wt%. Hexagonal/trigonal (space group P3, inferred from possible P3 or P1), with a = 8.148(1) Å, c = 4.804(1) Å, V = 276.2 Å3, Z = 1, Dmeas = 4.1 g/cm3, Dcalc = 3.93 g/cm3, Mohs hardness 4, and uniaxial (+) optics with omega = 1.856(3) and epsilon = 1.882(3).
