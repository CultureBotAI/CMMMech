Preobrazhenskite is the grandfathered magnesium borate species Mg3B11O15(OH)9 in the September 2026 IMA-CNMNC list. Its 1956 entry is an original-description year, not an approval identifier. Burns and Hawthorne refined a natural crystal from the Inder borate deposit, Kazakhstan, in orthorhombic Pbcn symmetry, with a = 16.291(4), b = 9.181(2) and c = 10.571(2) angstrom. Complex borate sheets parallel to (010) are linked through two octahedrally coordinated magnesium sites. Hydrogen positions were located from difference-Fourier maps; a crystallographically symmetric H site is an average structural model whose true local symmetry the authors explicitly left uncertain. Possible off-centre disorder and a high-temperature transition were suggestions, not experimentally demonstrated transformations. The study reported no new bulk chemical analysis or microbial experiment. This magnesium borate is distinct from potassium-bearing kaliborite, elemental boron and the borates commodity. No criticality designation, extraction mechanism or industrial recovery is inferred.
