### blazevic2019

Fresh full-text XML and publisher PDF methods, results and discussion; Table 1 and rendered Figure 3 (PDF page 6), feed chemistry page 5 and EDS page 4.

Scheelite-bearing mixed concentrate microbial W mobilization; no pure mineral yield, energy-source identification, W-redox proof or WC recovery.

- Methods report 19 wt% Fe oxide while Table 1 gives 22.6 wt% Fe; these are unreconciled.
- Figure 3 uses days while its caption says hours, and its log10/mg/L axis is ambiguous; no digitization.
- Microscopy involved 30 days of oxic dehydration after the 21-day culture; carbide-like deposits are not confirmed pure WC.
- This is the same experiment as the tungsten record, not new replication.

Rights: CC BY stated in publisher and PMC article. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### topolska2014

Fresh XML Experimental methods, Results, Discussion and Tables 1-3; retained publisher PDF and Figures 3-5 pixels after the fresh university PDF transfer was incomplete.

Controlled bacterial Pb remobilization from characterized synthetic pyromorphite; pH and pathways unresolved.

- Synthetic wet-chemistry formula Pb5.09(PO4)2.96Cl is retained; the feed is not a field soil.
- The unusual reported 30 mM FeEDTA concentration is retained.
- pH was not matched; 305-hour filtered Pb is separate from the 100-hour cell-associated 94% fraction.
- No isolated product or durable field-remediation claim is made.

Rights: PMC license URL specifies CC BY-NC 2.0 despite generic attribution prose; no raw redistribution. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### kato2022

Fresh XML Results 2.3 and 2.6 and Discussion: phosphate-condition phase assignments reverse across sections; only qualified context is reused.

Different deposition conditions do not directly refute dissolution, and disputed phosphate causality is not transferred.

Rights: CC BY 4.0. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### kang2022

Fresh XML Methods and Results, and the university-hosted published PDF; rendered Table 1 and Figures 1-2 (PDF pages 5-6, printed pages 824-825).

Fungal phosphate mobilization from processed struvite/dittmarite mixture, not pure struvite conversion.

- Na2HPO4 in the medium and mixed feed phases prevent a species-specific mass balance.
- The feed was pretreated at 105 degrees C for 48 hours; XRD showed struvite plus dittmarite.
- pH 5.5 was before autoclaving, not a post-feed measurement.
- No acid-matched abiotic contrast; Figure 2 compares inoculated cultures with versus without feed.
- The Pi procedure is absent from the inspected methods; no complete dissolution fraction is inferred.

Rights: CC BY 4.0 in article. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### sklute2023

Fresh XML and publisher PDF methods, ferrihydrite results and Figures 1-4; retained supplement S2-S5, including rendered S2 and S5, after the fresh archive timed out.

Ferrihydrite reduction and magnetite-rich mixed formation are paired species perspectives on one assay, not independent experiments.

- The synthetic two-line substrate formula is not independently resolved to the ideal IMA formula.
- All arms included 1.3 mM FeCl2 and 0.5 mM cysteine.
- 24 mM Fe(II) is from oxalate extraction, not dissolved iron or a yield.
- 84% pre-oxalate filter retention is not a magnetite phase percentage.
- Mossbauer excludes pure magnetite; XRD cannot separate magnetite and maghemite. Minor magnetite is possible in the heated control.

Rights: CC BY in primary article. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### schaefer2017

Fresh XML Experimental and Results/Discussion; publisher PDF Figure 1 on page 3 and Figures 2-3 on page 5 rendered. The supplementary acid-extraction table was not acquired, and no cells from it were imported.

Accept the microbial:false redox mechanism for characterized reagent beta-MnO2 as an abiotic alternative, not microbial causation.

- Co versus Cu radiation labels conflict.
- Methods describe approximately 3 mM Fe(II) and 90-minute runs; Figure 2 uses measured 2.4 mM Fe(II) and 75-minute exposures.
- Reaction temperature and replicate count remain unresolved.
- The Mn assay does not speciate dissolved manganese; Mn(II) is inferred.
- Later magnetite and maghemite are grouped; the Mn balance is nonstoichiometric.

Rights: CC BY 4.0; CC0 for data unless otherwise indicated. Raw full text remains outside Git.

### retnaningrum2019

All eight pages of the publisher PDF; Methods on pages 713-714, Figures 2-4 and Table 1 on page 717. Figure 2 and Table 1 were visually checked.

Do not accept a phase-specific microbial mechanism: the feed is an uncharacterized mixed ore, the uninoculated ore control is missing, and protocol/composition reports conflict.

- The feed is reported as 25% Mn, 30.4% Fe and 34% S, with no XRD phase confirmation.
- The C arm has 0 g/cm3 added mineral and is not a sterile ore control.
- The methods repeat loading 0.03 while the caption lists 0.01; methods pH 6.0 conflicts with caption pH 2.0.
- Table 1 uses atomic wording versus a mass-percent header. Day-16 values sum to 193.03%, not 100%.
- Text claiming sulfur oxidation to H2S conflicts with the sulfate interpretation; sulfur causality is not accepted.
- No strain accession is supplied, and no taxon ID was invented.

Rights: Publisher full text openly readable; reuse license not independently resolved; no raw redistribution. Raw full text remains outside Git.

## Deferred scientific work

The 1990 mixed-ore Enterobacter study (10.1016/0009-2541(90)90108-J) reports pyrolusite unaffected in its abstract, but full methods and normative phase measurements remain needed. It is not an accepted universal null. Borkar et al. 2025 (10.1016/j.hydromet.2024.106430) is a strong follow-up lead because its university-hosted abstract describes an actual pilot manganese process; verify feed phases, community, controls, flow, recovery and denominator in full text before any deployment claim. Lan 2020 (10.1016/j.jclepro.2019.119354) and the 2021 fungal ore study (10.1016/j.jenvman.2021.112153) need accessible full methods. No literature-exhaustion claim is made.
