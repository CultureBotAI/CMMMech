## MLCC claim boundaries

The source reports 0.2 g pulverized feed with 10 g DES at 90°C and 400 rpm for up to 24 h, with formulations in Table 4. Figures 1–4 support qualitative dissolved-metal comparisons. Table 1 supplies XRF percentages; Table 2 offers qualitative phase assignments without a displayed diffractogram. Replicate numbers, error-bar definitions, particle-size distribution and a complete sampling-loss calculation are not provided.

Ni ternary/glycerol endpoints are 25%/6% in one paragraph and Figure 2/Conclusions, but 68%/16% in the next paragraph. Fe maximum is 60% in the abstract versus about 68% in Figure 3/Conclusions. Methods omits the 16 h sample shown in the figures. These conflicts remain explicit rather than selecting a preferred percentage. Table 3/Section 3.3 identify ternary viscosity as estimated; molecular causation, energy savings and scalability are not directly measured. No supplement is declared in the complete article/XML.

## SmCo claim boundaries

Sections 2–3.2, Tables 1–2 and Figure 1 define the feed and factorial leach. The additional 15% HCl, 70°C, 3 h arm reports 98.5% Sm and 99% Co solution extraction, conditional on the unresolved 5% versus 1% peroxide recipe. Its out-of-domain predictions above 100% are explicitly capped by the source and are not measured extraction values. Center-point replication does not establish replication of each treatment.

Sections 2.3.3/3.3 and Figures 2–3 support sulfate precipitation around 94% of liquor Sm at pH 1, with negligible removal at pH 0.5 and increasing Fe coprecipitation above pH 1. Time/aging variation had little reported effect. Oxalate conversion and 800°C calcination produced Figure S1 diffraction assignments. Phase identification does not establish numerical chemical purity.

Table S3 gives 20 g feed, 12.47 g Sm2O3 and 84.83% overall recovery. The main article's 32.2% Sm implies 6.44 g input Sm, insufficient for the printed pure-oxide product mass. This is an explicit curator consistency check, not a replacement measurement. The table also misprints the oxalate formula. Fe CaO/NaOH and Cu strip-acid recipe conflicts remain unreconciled. Figure 12 is a proposed flowsheet, not proof of continuous operation, closed water reuse or discharge suitability.

After the owner freeze the author detected a minor record locator: the precipitation context cites Section 2.3.2 instead of 2.3.3. The independent reviewer was notified and the original owner remains frozen pending a saved finding. The actual conditions were read in Section 3.3. This record correction is distinct from unresolved source contradictions.
