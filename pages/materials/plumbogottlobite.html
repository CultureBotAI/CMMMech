Plumbogottlobite mineral IMA: Pgtb

Plumbogottlobite (IMA 2025-075) is an IMA-accepted hydrous lead–magnesium vanadate mineral of the adelite–descloizite group with September 2026 IMA-CNMNC Master List ideal formula PbMg(VO4)(OH) (PDF p. 174, status A; official IMA symbol Pgtb, whereas Section 1 on p. 209 lists "Warr symbol: Pgot"; ideal composition PbO 61.41 wt%, V2O5 25.02 wt%, MgO 11.09 wt%, H2O 2.48 wt%, total 100 wt%), representing the Pb2+ analogue of gottlobite (CaMg(VO4)(OH)), the Mg2+ analogue of descloizite (PbZn(VO4)(OH)), the V5+ analogue of duftite-alpha (Pb(Cu,Mg)(AsO4)(OH)), and the Mg-dominant analogue of mottramite (PbCu(VO4)(OH)) and cechite (Pb(Fe2+,Mn2+)(VO4)(OH)). Described by Kampf, Désor, and Ma (2026) from a single hydrothermal baryte-vein specimen collected on the dumps of the Glücksstern mine, Gottlob Hill, Friedrichroda, Gotha District, Thuringia, Germany (50°51'6" N, 10°34'10" E; holotype deposited in the Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County under catalogue no. 77403, pp. 209–210). Occurs as subparallel intergrowths of orange-brown tablets flattened on {001} (bounded by observed crystal forms {100}, {010}, {001}, {110}, {011}, {101}, and {111}) up to ~0.2 mm across on fracture surfaces in baryte with hausmannite (Sections 2–3, p. 210, Figs. 1–2). Exhibits a light-orange-brown streak, transparent character with adamantine lustre, non-fluorescence under long- or short-wave UV, Mohs hardness 4–4.5, brittle tenacity with irregular to conchoidal fracture, no cleavage or twinning, rapid decomposition in dilute HCl at room temperature forming a white residue that slowly dissolves, and calculated density Dcalc = 5.359 g/cm3 for the empirical formula and 5.516 g/cm3 for the structural formula on the single-crystal cell (Section 3, p. 210, and Table 3, p. 212). Optically biaxial (–) with 2Vmeas = 84(1)°, optical orientation Y = c (whereas Section 3 on p. 210 erroneously writes "uniaxial (–)" in the sentence immediately preceding 2V = 84(1)° and Y = c), non-pleochroic in demonstrable orientations, and Gladstone–Dale predicted mean refractive index n_calc = 2.077 (exceeding available index liquids, Section 3, p. 210). Micro-Raman spectroscopy (532 nm laser, 2 mW, Section 5, pp. 210–212, Fig. 3) shows a broad weak O–H stretching band at ~3217 cm-1, a weak overtone at ~1550 cm-1, VO4 stretching bands at 847 (very strong), 781 (shoulder), and 732 (medium) cm-1, VO4 bending modes at 441, 381, and 325 cm-1, and external/lattice modes at 275, 198, and 138 cm-1. Mean of 4 WDS electron-microprobe point analyses (JEOL JXA-iHP200F field-emission electron probe microanalyser, 15 kV, 10 nA, 10 um beam; Section 4, p. 210, Table 1, p. 211) yielded raw means PbO 52.47 wt% (range 51.80–53.41, SD 0.69), V2O5 23.96 (23.85–24.06, SD 0.09), MgO 8.20 (8.00–8.38, SD 0.15), CuO 3.94 (3.60–4.12, SD 0.24), MnO 1.69 (1.58–1.88, SD 0.14), which when normalised with stoichiometric structural H2O (V = 1, O = 5) give PbO 56.48, V2O5 25.79, MgO 8.82, CuO 4.24, MnO 1.81, H2O* 2.85, Total = 99.99 wt% (raw analytical totals were low by ~7.5% because the crystals did not take a good polish, Section 4, p. 210); on 5 O apfu, Section 4 (p. 210) partitions Mn2+ across both cation sites as (Pb0.89Mn2+0.05[]0.06)Sigma1.00(Mg0.77Cu2+0.19Mn2+0.04)Sigma1.00(VO4)(OH) (+0.12 H for charge balance), whereas the Abstract (p. 209) places all Mn2+ at the octahedral site as (Pb0.89[]0.11)Sigma1.00(Mg0.77Cu2+0.19Mn2+0.09)Sigma1.05(VO4)(OH) (+0.12 H). Orthorhombic, space group Pnma (no. 62), Z = 4, descloizite structure type (Sections 6–7, pp. 211–215, Tables 2–7, Fig. 4): single-crystal MoKalpha XRD refinement on a 120 x 80 x 60 um crystal (Rigaku R-Axis Rapid II, 429 reflections with I > 2sigma(I) out of 430 unique, 7391 collected, Rint = 0.142, R1 = 0.0302, wR2 = 0.0754, R1(all) = 0.0304, wR2(all) = 0.0755, GoF = 1.309, refined structural formula (Pb0.941[]0.059)(Mg0.700Cu0.300)(VO4)(OH); Table 3 index ranges transpose l and k as -7 <= l <= 7, -11 <= k <= 11 for b = 6.11 Å and c = 9.40 Å) gives a = 7.6104(7) Å, b = 6.1091(6) Å, c = 9.3968(8) Å (Abstract, p. 209, and Table 7, p. 215, consistent with V = 436.88 Å3; printed with digit transposition as c = 9.3986(8) Å in Table 3 on p. 212), V = 436.88(7) Å3 (436.88(6) Å3 in Table 7), with [010] chains of edge-sharing (Mg,Cu)O4(OH)2 octahedra (Mg site at 4a: Mg–OH = 2.025(6) Å x 2, Mg–O1 = 2.156(7) Å x 2, Mg–O3 = 2.187(7) Å x 2; true six-bond mean = 2.123 Å, printed as "<Mg–O> = 2.157 Å" in Table 5 on p. 214, BVS = 1.86 v.u. in Table 6 on p. 214) corner-linked by VO4 tetrahedra (V site at 4c: V–O2 = 1.651(10) Å, V–O3 = 1.756(9) Å, V–O1 = 1.758(8) Å x 2; printed "<V–O> = 1.722 Å" in Table 5 vs true four-bond mean 1.731 Å, BVS = 4.81 v.u. in Table 6 on p. 214, which misplaces the "x2 down-arrow" multiplier onto V–O3 instead of V–O1) and 9-coordinated lone-pair-asymmetric PbO8(OH) polyhedra (Pb site at 4c: three short bonds Pb–OH = 2.370(9) Å and Pb–O1 = 2.471(7) Å x 2 with <Pb–Oshort> = 2.437 Å on one hemisphere, and six longer bonds Pb–O3 = 2.725(9) Å, Pb–O1 = 2.749(7) Å x 2, Pb–O2 = 2.801(10) Å, and Pb–O2 = 3.233(3) Å x 2 with <Pb–Olong> = 2.915 Å on the other, BVS = 2.01 v.u. in Table 6 on p. 214 and Section 7 on p. 215); Gandolfi MoKalpha powder XRD (Table 2, p. 211, and Section 6.1, p. 212) refines to a = 7.599(12) Å, b = 6.118(10) Å, c = 9.394(15) Å, V = 436.7(10) Å3, with eight strongest lines [dobs in Å (Iobs; hkl)] at 3.230 (100; 210), 2.102 (69; 123), 1.660 (55; 133, 323), 5.115 (53; 011), 2.887 (46; 103), 2.612 (46; 113), 2.651 (43; 212), and 2.303 (42; 221). Distinct from gottlobite, descloizite, mottramite, cechite, and elemental vanadium, magnesium, or lead; no criticality designation for plumbogottlobite itself, no microbial or biological mechanism, and no industrial recovery yield are asserted.
