# Remaining CMMMech frontier after round 10

The [exact-root reconciliation](round10-frontier-20261011.json) compares the
current corpus with the historical
[authority inventory](../sources/expansion-authorities/20261011T040103Z.md).
A candidate is represented only when both its exact record ID and proposed
material kind match. This is a local inventory reconciliation, not a fresh
census of all criticality lists or a decision that every proposed commodity
requires its own record.

The corpus contains **61 records: 54 elements, four mineral species, two
secondary resources and one commodity**. Boron, fluorine, niobium, tantalum,
titanium and zirconium add six element roots; natural graphite adds the first
commodity root in this corpus. Their scientific claims and limits are documented
in the [B/F](round10-b-f-20261011.md), [Nb/Ta](round10-nb-ta-20261011.md),
[Ti/Zr](round10-ti-zr-20261011.md) and [graphite](round10-graphite-20261011.md)
dossiers. Natural graphite has no accepted microbial mechanism in this batch.

Of the historical inventory's 94 proposed roots, **55 are represented**;
**10 element and 29 overlapping commodity proposals remain**. Root coverage
does not measure coverage of every designation, organism, substrate or mechanism.
Minerals and secondary resources outside the inventory remain eligible, and
there is no exhaustion claim.

The remaining element proposals are **beryllium, hafnium, helium, iridium,
lutetium, osmium, promethium, rhenium, rubidium and thulium**. Each requires
independent identity, designation-scope and primary-evidence assessment. Group
membership does not establish an unqualified individual criticality entry.

## Next priorities

1. **Adjudicate overlapping commodity scopes.** Natural graphite represents its
   exact origin-defined commodity only. Broad graphite and battery-grade graphite
   remain deferred, overlapping proposals; neither is counted as represented.
   Follow the [graphite boundary assessment](round10-graphite-20261011.md) before
   adding them. Likewise, elemental boron and fluorine do not represent borates
   and fluorspar. An identity-only record remains acceptable when a mechanism
   lacks adequate primary support.
2. **Research the remaining elements in small evidence-led batches.** Separate
   element, ion, salt, oxide and organometallic identity before evaluating uptake,
   removal or particle-synthesis claims. The retained Horiike 2015 T9 experiment
   covers Y, Pr, Nd, Eu, Tb, Dy and Yb; it does not supply Tm or Lu observations
   to copy into those roots. That limit belongs to the inspected experiment,
   not to the broader literature.
3. **Acquire the strongest deferred primary studies.** The
   [Nb/Ta dossier](round10-nb-ta-20261011.md) identifies a 2023 Ta hybrid-process
   follow-on whose full manuscript and controls remain unavailable, plus a
   cell-free Nb-binding study needing figure/EXAFS assessment. The
   [B/F dossier](round10-b-f-20261011.md) retains boron accumulation and
   fluorapatite-leaching leads requiring full-text review. The graphite dossier
   identifies spent-battery studies requiring full methods and independent
   confirmation of graphite origin before commodity mapping. Abstract claims
   are research leads, not accepted measurements or product-purity evidence.
4. **Resolve interpretation gaps when new evidence becomes available.** The
   admitted Nb ash experiment leaves control composition, sample fraction and
   replication unresolved; the Ta null lacks a numerical detection limit.
   Boron solids lack a resolved mineral phase and matched abiotic preparation,
   while fluorine conversion is limited to selected monofluorinated substrates.
   Ti passive-film interpretation has reported analytical/control limitations;
   Zr preparation includes calcination without a demonstrated pre-calcination
   phase or necessary microbial contribution. Preserve these limits rather
   than filling them with assumptions. Supported corrections need new history
   and successor review artifacts.
5. **Refresh authority versions explicitly.** The graphite dossier freshly
   inspected UK replacement report **OR/24/059** and preserved the older landing
   page's inconsistent replacement number. The historical inventory remains
   unchanged. Any future UK-wide refresh needs explicit version reconciliation;
   this root-count update does not silently recertify all UK designations or
   claim the assessment report is government policy.
