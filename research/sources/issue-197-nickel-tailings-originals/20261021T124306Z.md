The current primary indexed abstract reports one Sudbury sample with 0.59% Ni and 0.017% Co and approximately 51 wt% pyrrhotite and 1 wt% pentlandite. This is a mixed feed, not either pure mineral. Magnetic material is described as having about 50% higher nickel grade **than the nonmagnetic fraction**. That comparison is neither 50% nickel recovery nor a 50% upgrade relative to the original bulk. Fraction masses, complete stream assays and nickel distributions are missing, so no separation balance or constituent-mineral yield is reconstructed. The statement that residual nonmagnetic pentlandite is split between the two fractions does not assign all released nickel to pyrrhotite.

The abstract describes comparisons labelled abiotic, biotic and anoxic and a pH range of 1.6–3 at 30–50 °C. It reports 80–90% Ni and 70–80% Co leaching in 10 days at temperatures at least 40 °C. The latter condition must not be broadened to every temperature in the tested range. These are reported envelopes, not digitized original curve endpoints, replicate distributions or statistically established benefits.
