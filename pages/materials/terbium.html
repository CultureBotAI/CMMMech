Terbium element Critical Material

The element Tb, distinct from its dissolved ions, salts, host minerals, phosphors and waste streams. The dated US designation concerns the named terbium mineral commodity; it does not assign criticality to every Tb-bearing material. The curated mechanism is a laboratory screening observation using lyophilized cyanobacterial biomass and a synthetic terbium solution, without demonstrated isolated-product recovery.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Terbium biosorption with lyophilized Synechococcus elongatus UTEX 2973

biosorption Microbial

Associated Organisms: Synechococcus elongatus UTEX 2973 (NCBITaxon:1350461) [TaxonMech]

A decrease in solution terbium after contact with prepared cyanobacterial biomass supports a biosorption interpretation. The assay used lyophilized biomass and does not demonstrate growth-dependent uptake, terbium redox transformation or a particular molecular binding mechanism. Apparent uptake was calculated from the aqueous concentration difference per dry biomass mass, rather than measured as a purified terbium product.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Paper et al. identified UTEX 2973 in their 12-strain screen. This strain was cultivated in BG11 medium in a 3.7 L photobioreactor at 37 degrees C with constant illumination at 300 micromol photons per square metre per second and pH 8 maintained by CO2 addition, then harvested by centrifugation in stationary phase. Before sorption, biomass was washed three times in demineralized water, frozen at -80 degrees C and lyophilized. The separate laboratory sorption assay used 10-20 mg dry biomass in 2 mL single-metal solution, initially 10 mM and pH 5 +/- 0.2, shaken for 3 h at room temperature. The exact terbium salt counter-ion, numerical sorption temperature and strain-specific mass within that range are not reported. Experiments were performed in triplicate, without a stated distinction between independent biomass preparations and technical replicates. After centrifugation at 10,000 rcf for 5 min, supernatant concentrations were analyzed by ICP-OES with multielement calibration standards. Biomass viability was not assessed. No matched biomass-free terbium solution-control result is reported in the main article or its two supplementary tables, so alternative solution-loss processes are not fully excluded.
Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: paper2023
Table 1, Sections 2.1-2.5 and 3.2, and Figure 2 support the strain, preparation, assay and positive terbium uptake plotted for UTEX 2973. Figure 2 expresses this screening endpoint as mg REE per g dry biomass and labels it Qmax, but it derives from a single 10 mM screening condition; it is not a terbium Langmuir-fit capacity or a recovery percentage. No numerical value has been reconstructed from the graph or from the abstract's range across four elements.
Partial Source: paper2023
Sections 2.6-2.10 and 3.3-3.7, Figures 3-7 and Supplementary Tables S1-S2 concern cerium: their pH response, rapid kinetics, fitted isotherms, competing-metal response, ion release and FTIR changes do not establish those properties for terbium. The water-only comparison for released Na, K, Mg and Ca in Section 3.6 belongs to the Ce/Al/Pb/Ni/Zn experiment and is not a biomass-free terbium blank. Section 4.2 recommends experiments on other REEs and discusses desorption and recovery as follow-up processes. This study does not demonstrate terbium-selective separation, regeneration, purified-product yield or industrial deployment.
