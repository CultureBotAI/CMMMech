[Scandium](../../data/records/scandium.yaml) and [praseodymium](../../data/records/praseodymium.yaml) were unrepresented element candidates in the round 7 frontier. Sc was selected for direct malonate elution experiments with immobilized lanmodulin. Pr was upgraded from a grouped Pr–Eu lead to a newer primary experiment specifically separating Pr from La. ## Denominators, controls and uncertainty

**Sc:** Figure 5E–F reports the high-purity aqueous fraction. The Methods define yield relative to loaded target REE. The citrate comparator yields approximately 96 mol% Sc at 78% yield, supporting chemistry-dependent limits. The paper has no matched Sc-only resin-without-LanM column in the inspected text/SI. Protein binding is supported separately by fluorescence and circular dichroism; an apparent fitted affinity is not a recovery measurement. A direct Sc resin control, process replication and independently closed mass balance remain priorities. Figure 5 says 0.4 mM total REEs and points to Table S9, while the appended table is actually labeled Table S10 (PDF pages 42–43) and its 16 means sum to243.0µM. Sc is 23.8 ± 1.1 µM. The record preserves that unresolved discrepancy. Main-text SI references are shifted; use actual captions. Subsidiary fluorescence methods say 20 µM protein versus 10 µM in Figure 4; no fixed protein concentration is curated for that assay. The model-derived Kd changes with the chosen chelator constants; it is not used to infer a universal affinity.

**Pr:** Main Figure 6d uses 0.8 mL beds and a binary 0.4 mM La/0.4 mM Pr feed for 2 min (nominal 10% capacity), 0.32 mL/min, a 20 min wash, pH 2.4 for 140 min then 1.5 for 25 min. Mex-LanM is the protein comparator; it largely co-elutes the ions. The single o-36 run reports 99.97% Pr purity and 83.16% yield in the printed figure (>99.9 mol% and 83.2% in prose). The shaded selected range is BV 48–80. In Source Data Figure 6, sheet `o36 Agarose La-Pr`, summing column C within those fractions and dividing by column C across all 96 fractions reproduces 83.1605795%. This supports an eluted-fraction denominator, not a separately validated balance on the original feed or isolated product. Figure units are ppm, not native schema mg/kg observations; no sample date or censoring meaning was invented.

Refabricated 0.4 mL columns, a new protein batch and three independent repeats yield 96.5 ± 0.2 mol% Pr purity and 72.0 ± 5.8% yield (Extended Data Figure 8d), with collection fractions 53–93 and an initial pH 2.5 shown in the graph. These lower results remain in the record. The repeat workbook sheet `o-36 La-Pr` has misleading `o621` in A1, while the sheet label and traces agree with Figure 8d; the ambiguity is preserved, not silently used to pool experiments. Source capacity descriptions (2 versus approximately 2.8 µmol/mL) are not used as an exact Pr capacity. Column temperature is not supplied for either accepted separation. Both mechanisms are laboratory and cell-free; industrial feedstock resemblance does not demonstrate deployment.
