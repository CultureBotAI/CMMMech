## Adversarial evidence boundaries

- Section 3.5 assigns falottaite after 20 days; Discussion says first detection after 15 days and miscites a biomass figure. Figure 6 has an unlabelled lowest trace. No precise onset is reconstructed.
- Figure 6b displays several phase references; it is not a quantitative falottaite yield. No independent water analysis, phase mass balance or purified product is reported. Methods' stated scan window differs from the displayed lower-angle range.
- Arsenic-free and oxide-free cultures do not supply the absent matched uninoculated or metabolite-only formation comparison. Oxalate excretion and hyphal nucleation remain proposed explanations.
- Figures 2–5 measure element pools in solution, mixed solids or mineral-bearing mycelium. They neither isolate falottaite's arsenic capacity nor demonstrate intracellular uptake or a falottaite recovery percentage. Figures 7–8 concern the authors' later dihydrate assignment, not phase-specific falottaite localization.
- The dihydrate labelled lindbergite is a separate identity question assessed in the lindbergite dossier. Shared citation across mineral owners is the same experiment, not independent replication. No environmental stability or industrial deployment claim is admitted.
