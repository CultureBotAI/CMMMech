## Anorthite evidence and boundaries

The IMA September2026 list p11 names anorthite Ca(Al2Si2O8), G, original-description year1823. The one-page Handbook gives the calcium-rich plagioclase compositional interval and ideal chemistry. A natural An90 component is a feldspar composition, not a measured 90% mineral phase fraction. Neither elemental calcium/aluminium/silicon nor a generic feldspar resource is the same owner.

**Zhou2011.** The original Chinese article is Scientia Sinica Terrae41(10):1454–1462. Its front page explicitly identifies the corresponding English Science China Earth Sciences54:204–211 DOI10.1007/s11430-010-4068-y. These are editions of one experiment, not replication. Crossref Chinese metadata lists author order differently from the displayed PDF; the record follows the displayed Zhou/Wang/Lu order. The experiment used natural An~90 from Daiqianshan layered amphibole gabbro, ethanol/acid-cleaned75–180µm powder. Triplicate water-only, inoculated direct-contact, and inoculated14kDa-bag arms ran0.8g/100mL at30°C/100rpm/240h. The source's growth-density timing is taken from an earlier study and was not assigned as this experiment's measured inoculum density. The Chinese original names a CGMCC stock without accession; an English search snippet's stock number was not imported.

Figure2 shows Ca higher with the barrier, Al/Si higher with contact, and both bacterial arms higher than water. Acidification differs later between arms. Acidification to pH1–2 before centrifugation intentionally desorbs cell-bound ions, so measurements are not exclusively free dissolved species. The water control omits medium; no matched sterile-medium control isolates the microbial contribution. Barrier transport and exclusion of macromolecules confound contact. Raman shifts and fitted trajectories do not directly establish unique proton-exchange or ligand reactions. Table1's fitted control-Al form differs from the text's general linear description; no fitted equation or rate was imported. SEM particles are not compositionally identified secondary phases. No yield, recovered product, elemental mass balance or industrial effect is asserted.

**Ma2024.** Bibliographic identity is Chinese Journal of Process Engineering24(8):982–992. The paper and landing metadata disagree on some received/online dates; record uses year/volume/pages without silently resolving these differences. Public publisher PDF SHA256 `d992ae88313aa299c4a06165564f0bb1ca4ab769cf867566122494d7fb6a6234` is complete. Purchased38–74µm powder is called anorthite, with Figure1 PDF41-1486 and Figure11 anorthite/quartz assignments. However Table1 reports30.93%SiO2,24.21%CaO,19.17%MgO,16.50%Fe2O3 and4.06%Al2O3. Pure anorthite has no Mg and much more Al; selected EDS atom percentages are not a replacement bulk composition. The defensible claim is culture-associated whole-feed release, not anorthite-specific magnesium extraction. Relative quartz peak increase does not establish new quartz formation.

CMS-1 is source-identified R.apiculatum by ITS comparison. NR_145091.1 resolves to CBS156.59 type-derived ITS, not a deposit of CMS-1; its NCBI species is470080. The phylogenetic tree has unusual reference presentation and was not reconstructed; authentication remains source-reported. P.polymyxa1406 was separately checked. Ma's2g/100mL,30°C/150rpm/21d triplicate flasks include water and uninoculated-medium comparators, and a separate10kDa-bag comparison. Direct contact gives more Ca/Mg than the bag. The record does not equate this result with Zhou's compositionally and biologically different experiment. The final Czapek substitutions are incompletely restated, especially the growth versus leaching Mg/Fe omission. EDTA titration-derived Mg is not an independently measured phase host. Similar minimum pH is not a matched trajectory. Acid identity, mechanical force, CO2 mineralization and industrial deployment were not established.
