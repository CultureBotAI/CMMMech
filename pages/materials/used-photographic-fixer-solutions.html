Used photographic fixer solutions secondary resource

Silver-bearing liquid discarded after photographic fixing, represented here by used black-and-white film fixer collected from a local processing unit. This secondary feed is distinct from discarded solid films, unused fixer, developer solutions and prepared silver salts. Its composition is source-specific; constituent criticality is not inherited.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Bacterial conditioning and fungal biosorption before chemical silver recovery

recovery Microbial

Associated Organisms: Thiobacillus thioparus (NCBITaxon:931) [TaxonMech], Cladosporium cladosporioides (NCBITaxon:29917) [TaxonMech]

Thiobacillus thioparus MCM B-39 treated diluted fixer before silver sorption onto harvested Cladosporium cladosporioides number 1 biomass beads. Chemical elution and electrowinning followed; the final metallic product was not produced by microbial reduction. Bead viability and matrix composition are unresolved in this paper.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Sections 2.1-2.4 describe 25 mL bacterial inoculum at 10^7 cells/mL added to 250 mL nominally 1:10 diluted fixer, pH 6.5, approximately 25 degrees C and 120 rpm for four days. Centrifugation and filtration preceded sorption. Section 2.7 then diluted treated liquid to nominal 100 mg Ag/L and passed it upwards through 3 g beads in a 10 cm by 1 cm internal-diameter column with 20 min retention. The fungal biomass was grown separately, washed and granulated by a proprietary process. Alcoholic cyanide elution and electrolysis at 0.8 V and 224 mA completed the laboratory sequence; scale-up and economics were not established.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: pethkar2003
Sections 2.2-2.4, Table 1 and Results pp.857-859 support real-waste origin, thiosulfate depletion with sulfur/sulfate production and subsequent silver recovery. The authors report 127 mg metallic silver and 70% overall recovery. These are source-reported process outcomes, not independently reconciled yields or product-purity measurements. The bacterial step alone did not substantially remove silver.
Partial Source: pethkar2003
Section 2.2 assigns 2.8 g Ag/L and 18.5 g thiosulfate/L before dilution, while Table 1 labels those concentrations as 1:10 diluted; Section 2.7 instead gives about 280 mg Ag/L after bacterial treatment. Sulfate is 32.25 g/L in Table 1 versus 31.16 in Results and 31.2 in the abstract. No repaired inventory or detection-limit-derived zero is inferred. Section 2.5 gives 1% ethanol in the eluent but Results gives 10%; the recipe remains unresolved. Cumulative column uptake is expressed per gram biomass in Results versus per gram beads in Methods; bead biomass fraction is unspecified.
Partial Source: pethkar2003
An uninoculated fixer control is specified, but its separate endpoint results are not presented. No matched untreated-fixer column, matrix-only control or independent replicate count is reported. Figures 1-6 concern prepared silver solutions, not real-feed recovery; plotted dose/time ranges and plateaus differ from some textual optimization claims. Fitted monolayer capacity is not cumulative column uptake, and model agreement does not establish a molecular binding mechanism. A complete process mass balance and phase/purity characterization of the recovered metal are unavailable.

Causal Graphs & Molecular Pathways (1)

Thiobacillus thioparus Sox-mediated thiosulfate biodegradation, Cladosporium cladosporioides bead biosorption, and chemical elution/electrowinning of silver from used fixer

REPRESENTATIVE EXAMPLE Mechanism: thiosulfate_oxidation_biosorption_then_electrowinning

Causal pathway linking representative Thiobacillus thioparus (NCBITaxon:931) periplasmic thiosulfohydrolase SoxB (soxB, UniProtKB:U5TVQ7) and reverse-type dissimilatory siroheme sulfite reductase subunit A (dsrA, UniProtKB:U5TVR3) in Stage 1 bacterial conditioning (25 mL T. thioparus MCM B-39 inoculum at 10^7 cells/mL in 250 mL nominally 1:10 diluted used photographic fixer at pH 6.5, ~25 C, and 120 rpm for 4 days, depleting complexing thiosulfate to elemental sulfur and 32.25 g/L sulfate in Table 1 [vs 31.16 g/L in Results and 31.2 g/L in abstract] without precipitating silver) to Stage 2 packed-column silver biosorption onto 3 g proprietarily granulated Cladosporium cladosporioides strain 1 (NCBITaxon:29917) biomass beads (10 cm x 1 cm ID column, 20 min retention, feed diluted to nominal 100 mg Ag/L) and Stage 3 abiotic alkaline alcoholic cyanide elution (1% ethanol in Section 2.5 vs 10% in Results) and electrowinning (0.8 V, 224 mA, yielding 127 mg metallic Ag and 70% reported overall recovery) in Pethkar and Paknikar (2003), while preserving the Table 1 vs Section 2.2/2.7 dilution-factor conflict (2.8 g Ag/L and 18.5 g thiosulfate/L vs ~280 mg Ag/L) and absence of matched uninoculated fixer endpoint controls.
