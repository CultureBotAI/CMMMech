- Table2 labels fresh bulkCr as96g/kg; adjacent prose says96mg/kg. Table1's18.1wt%Cr2O3 is not used to invent a reconciled total. Section4.3 also uses158mg/kg for old-surface CrVI whereasTable2 distinguishes149CrVI from158totalCr. Figure2 caption/body switch elementalCr versusCr2O3 for grain assays. These inconsistent details are not promoted to corrected measurements.
- Methods end sampling at30days; plots/results andTablesS2/S3 use34days, and some prose says40days. Each20mL aliquot was replaced with fresh medium; working liquid volume and cumulative withdrawal correction are unresolved. Filtered leachate losses are not a closed mass balance. No analysis dates are invented for native quantitative observations.
- FigureS2'sFe axis ismg/L, whileS3 ismg/kg; their magnitudes are not reconciled. Section4.2's low-loadingFe comparison does not consistently matchFigure4 ordering. The accepted421→1234 example is anchored specifically toSection3.3/Figure4b. The concentrate-versus-tailings approximatelythreefold statement also mixes unlike trajectories; no matched-time ratio is curated.
- Direct batch CrIII/CrVI time courses, identified precipitates, reductase assays and molecular reductants are absent from the inspected evidence. CrVI DPC is described for separate extraction tests. MINTEQTablesS1-S3 are predictions from specified input species and omit adsorption parameters. FeII-mediated chemistry, sulfur intermediates, biomass binding, adsorption, precipitation and sampling contribute unresolved alternatives to declining filteredCr.
- Stockpile depth comparisons and annual release extrapolations do not demonstrate field microbial remediation, industrial operation or isolated-product recovery. The source's language about bacterial reduction is narrowed to the actual measured associations.
