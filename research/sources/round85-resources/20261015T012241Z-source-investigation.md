## Accepted observations and exact boundaries

- Methods: 5 or 10 g ground/sieved/dried waste in 150 mL YPD, 250 mL flasks, 2 mL activated inoculum, 36°C, 180 rpm, dark, five days, three replicates. Controls include uninoculated waste/medium, medium and yeast without waste. No matched-pH sterile, killed-cell or polymer-free causal series was reported.
- Figure 2D: TG with yeast has **lower filtered aqueous fluoride** than uninoculated TG. Figure 7D: fluoride occurs in the extracted ethanol-precipitated/dialyzed polymer-associated fraction. Figure 5D supports cell association with TG. This is qualified culture-associated fluoride redistribution; no enhanced dissolved-fluoride extraction or isolated product yield is asserted.
- Figure 2B: phosphorus shows no significant TG versus TG-Rho change. Figure 2C:pH differs. Source inhibition by fluoride is an interpretation, not a demonstrated causal toxicity pathway. TG lacks the conspicuous extracellular material described for PG.
- Table 1: unusual reported calcium/sulfur composition is retained. Figure 3B is the TG compositional XRF panel although the prose points to3A; its normalized solid percentages after 600°C preparation do not establish whole-process recovery.
- Figure 4B: mixed calcium-salt phase assignments include phosphate-related marks in uninoculated TG too. No exclusive microbial fluorophosphate formation or phase purity is inferred.
- Abstract g/mL loadings conflict with explicit 5/10 g in 150 mL. A TG10-Rho comparison repeats the same sample label. These inconsistencies are preserved; unsupported source percentage claims are omitted.
- Supplement Figure S1 labels a separate deionized-water phosphorus assay with insufficient protocol to resolve mass balance. Figure S2 concerns PG only. No plant, soil-toxicity or field-reuse experiment demonstrates safe use.
