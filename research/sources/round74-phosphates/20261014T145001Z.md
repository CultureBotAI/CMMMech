## Accepted evidence checked

Read the complete original, including all Figures 1–10 and Tables 1–5, and visually inspected all nine pages. Methods p.5198 specify equal 5 mL stocks: 0.1 M metal nitrate plus 0.1 M KH2PO4/0.09 M KOH. The 100 °C arm used 98 °C preheating and sealed Teflon-lined reactors shaken three times per day. The reported pH of about 1.6 was measured at 25 °C. Quenched, washed and room-temperature-dried solids were characterized by XRD, FTIR, SEM/EDS and TEM/SAED.

- **Variscite:** Table 1, Figure 1, Section 3.1 and Figures 3–6 support mixed variscite/metavariscite after 21 days at 100 °C. Both 50/100 °C twelve-hour specimens were amorphous; the 100 °C twelve-day TEM specimen also remained amorphous. At 150 °C after twelve hours, berlinite plus hydrated Al phosphate was observed; the 200 °C twelve-hour solid was reported as berlinite. These are separate arms, not yields from conversion of a pure variscite feed.
- **Strengite:** Table 1, Figure 2, Section 3.2 and Figures 7–10 identify mixed strengite/phosphosiderite after nine days at 100 °C. The paper inconsistently says amorphous iron phosphate persisted at least nine days (p.5199) and transformed within five days (p.5200). Both statements remain visible in the record; only the characterized nine-day endpoint is adopted. The 150/200 °C twelve-hour arms produced ferric giniite.
- **Constraints:** No phase fractions, isolated pure product, biological comparison, industrial demonstration or phase-specific yield is established. PHREEQC computations lack thermodynamic data for several coexisting phases; measured aqueous composition and calculated saturation are separated. Rapid initial depletion is not imported as a crystalline yield. Ambiguous diffraction spacing and table-to-figure cross-references are not adopted.

No SI callout was found in the complete original. The ACS direct DOI route returned 403, and a bounded DOI/SI search did not locate a separate supplement.
