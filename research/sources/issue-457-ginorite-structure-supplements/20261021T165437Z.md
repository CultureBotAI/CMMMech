## Specimen, method and model separation

Pankova p.279 uses natural ginorite from the Chelkar salt dome, western Kazakhstan, from the fourth author's collection. Qualitative electron microprobe screening reported no detectable elements heavier than B except O and Ca; no quantitative detection limit or full compositional assay is supplied. A0.36×0.22×0.10mm crystal was examined on a Bruker APEX II DUO using MoKα radiation0.71073Å,50kV/40mA,0.5°frames and10s/frame, with multiscan absorption correction.
