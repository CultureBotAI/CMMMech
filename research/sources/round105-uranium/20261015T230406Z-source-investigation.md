The 2003 publication is shared with existing owners; those records are not additional experiments. Its figures 1-4 portray phosphate analogues. Its hydrothermal 220°C preparation and twinning refinements are metazeunerite-specific. The zeunerite gel preparation yielded a twelve-water synthetic crystal, coated in epoxy for diffraction; agreement with natural cell dimensions was the authors' basis for presumed natural equivalence. Their P4/nnc assignment disagrees with the earlier Hennig I4/mmm model. Hennig remains an unacquired primary comparator, not directly checked measurement evidence.

The institutional thesis is `MacIver-JonesF_2023.pdf`, DOI 10.7488/era/3163, issued 2023-03-13; its title page says 2021 and its references include2022. Both dates are preserved. Chapter 3 compares I4/mmm/P4/nnc and prefers the latter for the synthetic neutron dataset. The split H/D model and proposed additional hydrogen bond do not establish a causal stability advantage or long-term retention. The thesis calls for a torbernite neutron experiment and future zeunerite AIRSS work.

## Contradictions and exclusions

- Chapter 2 describes stainless-steel sample cells; Chapter 3 says vanadium can. The precise run setup requires clarification.
- Zeunerite Cu occupancy uncertainty is 1.00(2) in prose versus 1.00(3) in Table 3.1. Water-angle uncertainties differ between prose and Table 3.3. Refined values are not direct compositional assays.
- Appendix 3.2 repeats a zeunerite caption while its spectra label phosphorus; it is excluded as zeunerite assay evidence. The abbreviations page reverses CuUP/CuUAs labels relative to explicit formulas in the chapters.
- The neutron sample was only partly deuterated; FTIR and the model include residualH. Soft bond restraints and constrained displacement factors limit claims of unconstrained atomic determination.
- Hennig full text, CISTI structure factors and actual ISIS run files remain unacquired. Dataset metadata is not a sampled reusable diffraction table or independent replication.
- The thesis thermal, bicarbonate dissolution and industrial-waste chapters were not fully reviewed in this scope and provide no accepted recovery/field claim. No concentration observation was added from diffraction numbers.
