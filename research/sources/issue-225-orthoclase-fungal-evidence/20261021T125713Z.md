## Scientific assessment of the accessible primary text

The Sun CN abstract's method/result paragraphs, English abstract and all26references were inspected. The study labels powders orthoclase and muscovite and also describes bauxite and mineral-free Czapek media. Crucially, the same abstract describes **orthoclase containing some muscovite and muscovite containing some orthoclase**. It reports preferential loss of muscovite reflections while orthoclase reflections become slightly stronger. Those statements do not establish a measured orthoclase mass loss: peak intensity can change relatively, phase amounts are unavailable, and solution K/Si from mixed powders cannot be apportioned without phase and mass accounting. Neither phase purity nor an all-feldspar causal mechanism is accepted.

The abstract names one *Aspergillus niger* strain but supplies no verified strain accession, historical stock provenance or molecular identity. Shake-flask/solid and liquid Czapek context is reported; complete medium formulation, mineral loading, temperature, agitation, sampling/replacement, analytical fraction, quantitative phase analysis, replication, error definition and matched sterile or pH controls remain unverified. A mineral-free fungal medium is a different comparator from an uninoculated mineral control. The abstract's proposed crystal-structure dependence is not isolated from mineral chemistry, impurity, biomass or medium differences by the evidence available here.

The outside-Git numeric audit preserves **18 selected abstract tokens**: nine metabolite concentrations, two between-feed concentration differences, four Zhan percentages and three times. Sun reports10-day acids/polysaccharides/proteins for mineral-free, orthoclase-labelled and muscovite-labelled media, respectively:1.45/1.97/2.45,5.11/9.96/12.25 and6.25/13.78/16.97g/L. The CN abstract assigns the differences79.10mg/L K2O and57.78mg/L SiO2 to30days; the corresponding English sentence omits that explicit time. These are **differences between feed results**, not absolute concentrations, percentage yields, elemental K/Si or a phase balance. The13 conditional arithmetic checks retain that scope; no significance, net metabolite production, rate or organism ranking is inferred. There are no original table/figure cells to verify, no digitized endpoints and no missing measurements replaced by zeros.

The Chinese conclusion describes a deficiency of available potassium; the English wording says “free available potassium.” This translation/wording discrepancy is retained without reconstructing the unavailable recipe. Neither wording verifies a potassium-free measured blank. The reported g/L metabolite labels are retained without unit repair.
