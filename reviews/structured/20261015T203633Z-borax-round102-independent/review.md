## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Accepted borax versus experimental reagent

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:borax.

Current species formula and unresolved description metadata are correctly separated from the reagent experiments.

IMA lists Na2B4O5(OH)4(H2O)8, equivalent in bulk to Na2B4O7·10H2O, with G status and unresolved original year/locality. No invented approval number is supplied. The original experiments use guaranteed-reagent Fujifilm Wako sodium tetraborate decahydrate and crystals recrystallized at 293 K, not characterized natural ore. Single-crystal C2/c is supported; tincalconite, kernite and anhydrous phases are separate identities.

### Separate experimental arms and measured phase changes

scope: supported. Targets: cmmmech:borax.

Powder transformation and protected single-crystal failure are not conflated.

Powder: 0.7-mm Lindemann capillary, one end open, ambient atmosphere, 303-473 K in 10-K steps, 5-min hold and 3-min exposure. Figure 2 supports borax reflections nearly unchanged through 353 K, weaker above 363 K, absent by 403 K; tincalconite appears above 353 K and remains weak to 473 K. Separate nitrocellulose-protected single crystal uses nitrogen temperature control at 113-323 K in 30-K steps and loses crystallinity at 333 K. Different containment/preparation prevent a universal transition threshold or a continuous single-crystal refinement across the powder transition.

### Observed structure trends versus mechanistic inference

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:borax.

Modelled structural-unit inheritance and uncertainty in water-site occupancy remain bounded.

Table 1, Figures 4-5 and S1-S2 support stronger b-axis expansion and comparatively small B-O bond changes. The manuscript reports no resolved water/hydroxyl oxygen occupancy loss at 323 K within refinement error. Proposed borate-unit persistence combines separate refinements, comparison with tincalconite and quantum-chemical calculations; it is not time-resolved molecular tracking, quantified water loss or an established palaeoenvironmental indicator.

### DSC inconsistency and transfer limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:borax.
