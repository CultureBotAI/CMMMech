## Accepted evidence and precise limits

**Current identity.** The freshly downloaded [IMA-CNMNC September 2026 list](https://cnmnc.units.it/files/editor/IMA_Master_List_(2026-09).pdf) has 244 pages. Definitions on pages 1–2 and rows on pages 6, 14, 31 and 241 were visually inspected. Argyrodite is Ag8GeS6, grandfathered, original-description year 1886. Briartite is Cu2FeGeS4, approved 1965-018. Alburnite Ag8GeTe2S4 and zincobriartite Cu2ZnGeS4 are distinct accepted species. The complete list was acquired, but not every row was individually read. The source explicitly states CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported; no PDF is vendored.

**Briartite.** [Francotte, Moreau, Ottenburgs and Lévy (1965)](https://www.persee.fr/doc/bulmi_0037-9328_1965_num_88_3_5876), DOI 10.3406/bulmi.1965.5876, pages 432–437: all six original page images and complete OCR were read, including Figures 1–5 and Tables I–IV. Polished sections, powder diffraction and electron microprobe measurements characterize natural Kipushi material, with comparative Tsumeb chemistry. The historical Cu2(Fe,Zn)GeS4 scope is broader than the current Fe species. Table III's prose and footnote column labels conflict; no ambiguous row is imported. Original structural alternatives are not silently resolved. Intergrown grains do not establish a pure bulk extraction feed.

**Argyrodite.** [Ono and Sato (1995)](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/ganko/90/8/90_8_268/_article), DOI 10.2465/ganko.90.268, pages 268–279: all 12 scanned pages, Figures 1–9 and Tables 1–4 were visually read. PDF extraction yielded only whitespace; it is not claimed as readable full text. Figure 3F (page 271), methods (page 272), section IV.5 and Table 2 (page 274) establish natural argyrodite in specimen 81092210, with minor Cu, Fe and As. The EPMA conditions for the other silver minerals, including argyrodite, are 15 kV and 0.01 microampere, with ZAF correction; electrum used different conditions and correction. Table 4 separates relative mineral occurrence from bulk silver assays. These are not bulk germanium measurements. Sphalerite fluid inclusions and ore-formation interpretations are not argyrodite processing conditions. The landing explicitly lists Supplementary material (0). Publication in 1995 is distinct from J-STAGE posting in 2006.

**Handbook context.** The complete one-page [argyrodite](https://handbookofmineralogy.org/pdfs/argyrodite.pdf), [briartite](https://handbookofmineralogy.org/pdfs/briartite.pdf) and [zincobriartite](https://www.handbookofmineralogy.org/pdfs/Zincobriartite.pdf) entries were visually inspected.
