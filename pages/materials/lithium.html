Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Transient microbial enhancement of Li release from jadarite-bearing material

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans (NCBITaxon:920) [TaxonMech]

Inoculation accelerated Li solubilization from a jadarite-bearing sample relative to uninoculated acidic medium during part of a laboratory time course. The uninoculated control reached similar dissolved Li by day 30, and sulfuric-acid-only leaching released more Li. Biofilm involvement was suggested, without establishing a unique molecular cause.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Live in-lab ore-derived culture was reported as At. ferrooxidans, without a strain accession. The species identifier normalizes the reported name to Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans; the title and abstract spell the genus Acidiothiobacillus. This mapping does not independently identify the culture. Ground Jadar Valley material contained jadarite, dolomite and probertite, with possible unidentified Li-bearing phases. Triplicate biotic flasks ran statically at 23 degrees C for 30 days, targeting pH 1.8; addition of jadarite raised pH immediately and required repeated sulfuric acid doses. Acid additions differed between treatments, limiting attribution of the observed difference solely to biology. Methods state 2 g powder, 190 mL basal medium and 10 mL inoculum but label this 2% w/v; those amounts imply 1% w/v. The discrepancy is unresolved and no normalized yield is curated. No purified Li product or industrial heap operation was demonstrated.
Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: kirk2024
Sections 2.1-2.3 and 3.1-3.2, Fig. 1 and Table 1 support the scoped ICP-OES dissolved-Li comparison and mixed substrate identity. Acid leaching contributed substantially in all treatments.
Partial Source: kirk2024
Section 3.2 limits the advantage to a transient comparison: by day 30 the uninoculated control was similar, and acid-only leaching remained higher. Sections 3.3-3.4 and Figs. 4-5 report no material microbial enhancement for the tested spodumene- and lepidolite-bearing samples. The lepidolite sample name is retained without treating it as a unique mineral species. The result cannot be generalized to all Li ores.
Partial Source: kirk2024_data_v1
The eight-file version 1 deposit was acquired and its issuing hashes verified. Jadarite day-30 values are consistent with the reported control convergence and higher acid-only result, but quantitative verification remains limited. Bioleaching workbook headers use mg/kg rather than the paper's mg/L; mineral-digestion units also require reconciliation. In Jadarite bioleaching results from ICP-OES.xlsx, Jadarite!F5 and F7 do not equal the arithmetic means of the labelled replicate cells C:E. Standalone spodumene and lepidolite workbooks contain identical Li replicate blocks at Sheet1!C3:E12, requiring provenance clarification before independent comparison. These are unresolved mappings and inconsistencies, not identified corrections or specific refutations of the paper's qualitative comparisons. The files provide no loading or per-flask acid-dose ledger to resolve the published contradictions. No deposited number was corrected or imported as a normalized yield or quantitative benchmark.
