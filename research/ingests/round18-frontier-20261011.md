Six additions bring the corpus to 138 records. The exact-state

## Accepted scope

- Dunite is a primary geological resource; its variably altered experimental
  powders are not silently equated with the ideal rock-classification definition.
- Hematite is a mineral species. Potassium feldspar denotes an informal mineral
  family; a commercial assay label does not identify a member species.
- Raw spent diesel-hydrodesulfurization catalyst is a process-defined secondary
  resource, distinct from the existing FCC and hydrocracking catalyst feeds.
- Electrolytic manganese metal and dioxide are manufactured commodity families,
  distinct from elemental identity, ore phases, sulfate products and waste feeds.
