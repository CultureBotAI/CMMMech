Arsenic element Critical Material

Arsenic (As), grounded by the neutral-atom ChEBI identifier. Arsenate, arsenite, gallium arsenide and metal hydroxide sludge are different chemical or resource entities. The US designation maps the listed arsenic commodity to this element without designating all arsenic-bearing wastes. Arsenic was retained in the final 2025 list despite proposed removal in the draft. The mechanism concerns co-mobilization from sludge in the same experiment curated for gallium, not an independent replication or an arsenic detoxification process.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Arsenic co-mobilization during hydroxide-sludge bioleaching

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Acidithiobacillus thiooxidans (NCBITaxon:930) [TaxonMech]

Arsenic entered solution during acidic laboratory leaching of sludge with living sulfur-oxidizing Acidithiobacillus thiooxidans cultures. Similar final arsenic leaching in the acid-adjusted abiotic comparator does not establish a microbial enhancement. The observed process is co-mobilization, without a demonstrated arsenic redox reaction, detoxification or recovered arsenic product.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
DSM 14887 type-strain cultures were adapted to 5% w/v sludge with aluminum; the taxon identifier resolves the species, not the strain. The GaAs-wafer-production hydroxide sludge was dried at 60 degrees C for 48 hours and mortar-homogenized. Table 1 reports 1.34% As by handheld XRF. Section 2.1 assumes arsenate associated with iron phases; it does not measure arsenic oxidation state in this assay. The curated 3% w/v experiment used 50 mL shake-flask setups, HBS medium, 1% elemental sulfur, initial pH 1.8, 30 degrees C and eight days. Table 2 gives 12.9 mM Al (molar Al:F 1.5:1) and 1 mL sulfuric acid initially. Section 2.3 labels the 10% inoculum w/v, an unresolved unusual basis. Biotic setups were triplicate; controls were uninoculated, either initially pH 1.8 or acid-adjusted to follow the biotic pH. Dissolved elements were measured by ICP-MS, without arsenic speciation. The exact shaking speed and sample separation protocol for this assay are not resolved in Section 2.3.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: fritze2026
Section 3.2 and Figure 3 report 68% of feed arsenic in solution after eight days in the 3% sludge biotic assay. The figure presents biotic means and SD, without numeric SD transcribed here. The authors associate arsenic release with dissolution of ferrihydrite; this is a host-dissolution interpretation, not evidence for microbial reduction of arsenate. Industrial feed provenance does not establish industrial operation of this laboratory process.
Partial Source: fritze2026
The same Section 3.2 reports 66% arsenic leaching in the acid-adjusted abiotic comparator and describes simplified controls requiring replicate confirmation. The small reported difference therefore does not establish arsenic-specific microbial benefit. Its statement placing initial 24% As at day 1 conflicts with Figure 3, which places that point at day 0; no initial value or derived arsenic rate is curated. Section 3.4 discusses subsequent chemical arsenic removal prospectively, not a tested microbial recovery or detoxification result. Methods and Table 2 specify Al:F, whereas the abstract says Al:Fe; this record follows the flask-specific Table 2 ratio.

Causal Edges (4):
  • sqr_reductase → oxidation of reduced sulfur intermediates to polythionates (supplies_substrate_for) → tth_hydrolase : Sulfide/sulfur oxidation generates reduced sulfur and polythionate intermediates metabolized by periplasmic tetrathionate hydrolase.[fritze2026]
  • tth_hydrolase → polythionate disproportionation and sulfuric-acid proton generation (produces) → biogenic_sulfuric_acid_lixiviant : Periplasmic tetrathionate hydrolysis generates sulfate and protons, acidifying the sulfur-oxidizing culture medium.[fritze2026]
  • biogenic_sulfuric_acid_lixiviant → acidic dissolution of ferrihydrite-hosted GaAs-production hydroxide sludge at 3% w/v (solubilizes) → gaas_hydroxide_sludge_feed : Sludge-adapted A. thiooxidans DSM 14887 in HBS medium with 1% elemental sulfur, initial pH 1.8, 12.9 mM Al (molar Al:F 1.5:1), and 1 mL H2SO4 maintains acidic conditions that dissolve the poorly crystalline ferrihydrite host phase of 3% w/v dried sludge over 8 days at 30 C.[fritze2026]
  • gaas_hydroxide_sludge_feed → non-selective arsenic co-mobilization during ferrihydrite host dissolution (produces) → comobilized_dissolved_arsenic : 8-day leaching at 3% w/v dried sludge releases 68% of feed As into solution in the biotic assay versus 66% As in the acid-adjusted abiotic comparator, reflecting acid-driven ferrihydrite host dissolution without arsenic-specific microbial enhancement over acid.[fritze2026]
