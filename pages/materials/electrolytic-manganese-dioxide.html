Electrolytic manganese dioxide commodity commodity

Manufactured manganese dioxide products obtained by electrolysis. Tosoh identifies uses as positive-electrode material in alkaline primary batteries and as feed for lithium-manganate cathode materials, with application-specific representative product properties. USGS separately reports chemically and electrolytically produced synthetic manganese dioxide, supporting the process distinction. This commodity is distinct from manganese metal, the manganese element, natural manganese-dioxide ore or a particular mineral species, spent battery material, and a finished electrode. The manufacturer reports active oxygen expressed as manganese dioxide in one product table; this does not establish a universal crystalline-phase purity or grade threshold. The product family does not itself resolve the broader battery-grade manganese designation. No inherited criticality designation, unique crystal phase, or microbial production of qualified electrolytic manganese dioxide is asserted.
