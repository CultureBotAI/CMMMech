## Phosphophyllite evidence

[Hill (1977), complete original](https://msaweb.org/AmMin/AM62/AM62_812.pdf), pages 812-817, Tables 2-4 and Figure 1 support a natural Potosi monoclinic P21/c structure. The single-crystal experiment resolves earlier model alternatives; it is not a bulk-resource assay or a microbial experiment. The [AM-77-046 deposit](https://msaweb.org/MSA/AmMin/Supplemental_Data/Vol%2062%20p0812%20AM-77-046.pdf) includes observed/calculated structure factors on PDF pages 10-11; it is part of the same study. No re-refinement was performed.

[Dunn and Norberg (1977)](https://msaweb.org/AmMin/AM62/AM62_818.pdf), complete page 818, partially analyzed eleven Hagendorf and three Potosi specimens. Their borderline 51:49 Mn:Fe result is a weight ratio and did not justify a new species given uncertainty. Potosi water was measured on one sample, and individual analyses were available only on request. Curation preserves this limit and does not infer a universal substitution boundary.

## Laueite evidence and competing interpretations

[Moore (1965)](https://msaweb.org/AmMin/AM50/AM50_1884.pdf), pages 1884-1892, Tables 1-3 and Figures 1-4 establish the Palermo crystal's P-1 framework. Hydrogen atoms were not located. Other-mineral heating and hypothetical strunzite/stewartite structures are not laueite transformation experiments.

[Baur (1969)](https://www.rruff.net/doclib/am/vol54/AM54_1312.pdf), pages 1312-1323, reuses earlier laueite coordinates and proposes Mn(III) occupancy from short/distorted bonds on pages 1321-1322 while acknowledging other substituents. This is preserved as an interpretation rather than a direct oxidation-state assay or replacement for the current IMA formula.

[Grey et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1180/mgm.2025.21), pages 234-241, Tables 1-5 and Figures 1-6, demonstrates a natural epitaxial orientation of laueite on stewartite. The rhomb is mixed phase. The EDS composition scaled to three metals differs from the simplified refined Mn/Mg occupancy; inferred ferric/hydroxyl/water allocation is separate from direct measurement. The proposed solution change, interface protonation and heterosite-cluster nucleation remain hypotheses. Stewartite B-1 is a deliberately transformed comparison setting.

All four publisher supplements were inspected. The laueite checkCIF report contains three A-level displacement alerts, missing-H and formula/Z reporting alerts, and says structure factors were not supplied for its full checks. Those alerts remain unresolved qualifications; some reporting alerts are not necessarily incorrect chemistry. The stewartite report separately flags the nonstandard B-1 setting explained in the article. CIFs were read without re-refinement. The article's bibliography water-count typo and figure-number slip were checked against the actual original and figure.
