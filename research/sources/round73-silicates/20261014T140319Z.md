## Accepted evidence

### Microcline

Rogers1998 DOI10.2138/am-1998-11-1241: complete nine-page original, Tables1–2 and Figures1–7 inspected. South Dakota microcline is heterogeneous; apatite is within albite intergrowths. Field colonization/weathering contrasts with a homogeneous P-poor Ontario specimen. All enrichment cultures were positive even when attached cells were sparse. This is not a pure-phase K/P yield, a strain-specific mechanism or a matched sterile field experiment. The abstract and tabulated P values disagree, so exact grade is excluded.

Rogers2004 DOI10.1016/j.chemgeo.2003.09.001: complete eighteen-page original, Tables1–5 and Figures1–10 inspected. Its feldspar field summary reuses the earlier lineage and describes microcline as lightly etched rather than extensive. New field tests concern glasses. In distinct laboratory microcosms, microcline biomass increased without a corresponding Si increment; Table5 marks weathering absent. Model ligand addition, Tween-carried P and differing plot blank corrections are retained. Abiotic powder dissolution cannot substitute for live performance.

### Muscovite

Pinzari2024 DOI10.1016/j.funbio.2024.08.002: complete fourteen-page published original, Figures1–5 and Tables1–5 plus official DOCX supplement inspected. The DOCX renders to five pages with MethodsS1–S3 and FiguresS1–S2 intact. SI identifies museum BM1937,454, Afghanistan, SEM-EDX/microprobe composition and no XRD-detected accessory phases. Flakes were cleaned and autoclaved; reported P.involutus ATCC200175 was assessed by ITS sequencing. Species taxon71150 and live GEO GSE158973 metadata were verified; the attempted ATCC catalogue URL returned404 and is not claimed as strain authentication.

SEM supports localized surface alteration after21days at25°C. Table3 reports mycelial K6.80±0.96 versus5.52±1.69 micrograms/g dry mycelium in the depleted control, both groupc, n5. This does not establish significantly improved K acquisition. Residual K, possible inoculum carryover, replacement of KH2PO4 with ammonium phosphate, no aged abiotic mineral control and harvest carryover/mass-balance limits are explicit. Gene expression is context, not a demonstrated causal pathway. The published earlier2022 work is not independently accepted here.

Official supplemental XLSX files mmc2/mmc3 were acquired; each has four sheets and representative headers/rows were inspected. They contain expression/enrichment results, not mineral extraction yields. No bulk gene/concentration import or raw RNA-seq reanalysis occurred.
