identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:gowerite.

### Historical precipitation and failed replication

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:gowerite.

The 1959 original directly supports the 20 g calcium acetate monohydrate plus 40 g H3BO3 in 100 mL water stirred for 24 h at 35°C, initial/final pH about 5. Its separate unstirred room-temperature 18-day arm produced larger crystals. The 2025 failure is explicitly attached to that single-crystal route, not generalized to the stirred experiment or all gowerite formation.

### Hydrothermal formation conditions

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:gowerite.

Methods support 1.00 g H3BO3, 0.476 g CaCl2·2H2O and 0.129 g NaOH in 10 mL water in a 28 mL vessel, 120°C for 12 h under autogenous pressure, 0.2°C/min cooling to 30°C, 0.1 µm filtration, rinsing and overnight 20°C drying. The selected 0.10×0.05×0.05 mm crystal and reported refinement metrics support identity; numerical pressure, pH, recovery and bulk quantitative purity remain unreported.
