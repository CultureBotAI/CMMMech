Polylithionite mineral

Polylithionite is the lithium-bearing mica mineral species listed by IMA-CNMNC in September 2026 with formula KLi2AlSi4O10F2 and approved status (PDF page 175). Its 1998 s.p. entry denotes a special-procedure action, not a modern approval-number CURIE. The Handbook of Mineralogy describes monoclinic material and uses the generalized formula KLi2AlSi4O10(F,OH)2; its natural specimen analyses and hydroxyl substitution are not substituted for the current IMA formula. Documented geological occurrence includes late-stage segregations, veinlets and metasomatic replacement in syenite pegmatites of the Lovozero alkalic massif. This establishes a lithium-bearing geological host, not a universal ore grade, present mining status or recovery process. The IMA-approved mica nomenclature report defines lepidolite as a series name for trioctahedral micas on or near the trilithionite-polylithionite join. Lepidolite is therefore not used as an exact synonym for this endmember; a lepidolite-labelled mixed feed needs specimen-specific characterization before its behavior can be assigned to polylithionite. This species is distinct from elemental lithium and a bulk lithium concentrate. No microbial mechanism or criticality designation is assigned here.
