Montroseite mineral

Montroseite is the vanadium oxyhydroxide mineral with ideal formula V3+O(OH), listed as grandfathered (G), original-description year 1953, in the September 2026 IMA list. That year is not a numbered IMA approval. The original Colorado Plateau specimens were iron-bearing, commonly altered and incompletely separated from associated minerals. Single-crystal diffraction assigned the sharp orthorhombic Pbnm lattice to montroseite; the diffuse lattices and much of the original bulk powder pattern represent altered material, subsequently distinguished as paramontroseite or unresolved diffuse A. The historical Fe2+/V4+ compensation model is a chemical interpretation, not a modern site-specific valence measurement. Montroseite is distinct from paramontroseite, elemental vanadium and mixed uranium-vanadium ore. The cited natural evidence does not establish microbial formation, extraction, isolated-product recovery or industrial deployment; criticality is not inherited from vanadium.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Inferred oxidation and hydrogen loss during natural weathering

redox transformation

Evans and Mrose interpreted natural montroseite-paramontroseite intergrowths as oxidation of VO(OH) to orthorhombic VO2 with hydrogen loss while retaining a related oxygen framework. Their proposed reaction is 2VO(OH) + 0.5O2 -> 2VO2 + H2O. This is a structural and geological inference from altered natural crystals, not a controlled conversion experiment or measured hydrogen-transport mechanism.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
The Bitter Creek mine, Paradox Valley, Colorado, supplied two altered crystals. One contained sharp montroseite plus diffuse paramontroseite and a weaker unresolved diffuse-A phase; the other gave nearly pure paramontroseite diffraction with slight residual montroseite reflections. The 1955 study reused the 1953 montroseite intensity data, so the papers and the two mineral records do not represent independent replications. The proposed below-50-degrees-C atmospheric or groundwater weathering setting has no measured reaction duration, pH, oxygen activity, conversion fraction or matched laboratory control. Diffuse A could be an intermediate or a separated Fe-rich phase; its identity was unresolved.
Curated Evidence Points:
Partial Source: evans_mrose1955
Pages 863 and 867 distinguish the two natural crystals, visually estimated 99 montroseite and 34 paramontroseite reflections, no absorption correction, and poorer diffuse-phase data. Figures 1-4 and Table 4 show related, parallel lattices and an interoxygen separation interpreted as a hydrogen bond in montroseite but not paramontroseite. Pages 872-874 infer oxidation and hydrogen migration; they do not directly track atoms or establish a universal low-temperature rate or equilibrium boundary.
Context_only Source: weeks1953
Pages 1239-1241 and Tables 2-3 show that bulk powder patterns and chemical analyses include altered and impure material. The Matchless composition was calculated as about 35 percent substituted oxyhydroxide and 65 percent VO2, not measured conversion in a timed assay. Bitter Creek, Matchless and Whitney separates have different impurities and must not be pooled as pure montroseite or paramontroseite.
