## Source assessments

### Aragonite: culture precipitation and separate abiotic comparison

Han2023 Sections 2.1–2.5 and 3.1–3.2, Figure 2 p.7 and Supplementary Figure S5 are the direct phase evidence. Nominal Mg/Ca6/9 cultures change from mixed aragonite/Mg-calcite at day14 to aragonite-only fitted crystalline phases at day25; ratio12 is assigned aragonite at both times. Ratio3 retains mixed phases and ratio0 is calcite. The recipe, inoculation, triplication, 37°C/130rpm incubation and harvest washing are scoped to this experiment. Drying temperature is unspecified. Figure1 and growth-assay S3 support alkalinization; they do not constitute a matched-pH phase-control experiment.

The actual Figures2/3 and S5 were inspected at readable resolution; all other main and supplementary images were also examined. Rietveld labels of100% describe the modeled crystalline assemblage, not recovery yield or exclusion of amorphous/organic matter. Sections2.10/3.7 and FigureS13 include separate aragonite precipitation without biomolecules after adding carbonate. This prevents biological-necessity overstatement. Table2 acid-digest Mg and selected HRTEM/SAED spacings do not uniquely prove coordination; Section4.2 itself retains uncertainty. Subsequent dolomite-assigned surface domains/alternative hypotheses in4.3 are not a bulk-conversion or atomistic-cause assertion in the record.

NCBI MF808044.1 names B.licheniformis strainY1, partial16S; taxon1402 is the species. Neither is an authenticated culture-collection accession. No independent replication is counted merely because the article has PMC, publisher and Figshare representations.

### Monohydrocalcite: narrow phase assignments despite taxonomy and methods conflicts

Han2022 Section2.2, Figure2, Table1 and SI FigureS6d2/e1/e2 support sole fitted crystalline MHC at nominal Mg/Ca7/day27 and9/day14/27; d3/e3 contain MHC plus hydromagnesite at140days. Figures5/8 provide morphology/thermal context, not independent mass yields. The whole text and all ten original main figures were inspected, and all nineteen SI pages were read/rendered. TableS1/FigureS5 supply FTIR; S6 percentages remain fitted proportions, not isolated recovery.

The paper calls WMS2 H.utahensis, whereas current GenBank ON000249.1 is an **uncultured Halomonas sp. clone WMS2**, environmental sample, taxon173971. This is not species confirmation. The record uses verified Halomonas genus2745 and explicitly attributes the phenotype to the source-designated culture. Species/strain confirmation is a concrete unresolved question, requiring sequence-to-culture reconciliation or a verified deposit, not an assumed renaming.

Section5.3 lists lowest Mg0.01M with Ca0.01M while Figure2/Table1 call the lowest arm Mg/Ca0. That discrepancy is retained. Accepted0.07/0.09M arms are explicit. The section does not independently state mineralization temperature/agitation;37°C/120rpm occurs in growth/salinity methods and is not transferred silently. The1% inoculum basis and mineral washing/drying are unspecified. Control FigureS4 photographs extend through14days only; broader text reports no control minerals without a matched quantitative late-time control series.

At ratio5/day140, Section2.3 mentions MHC morphologies but Table1/Figure2/S6c3 assign aragonite and hydromagnesite. That arm is excluded from proof of MHC persistence. Temporal phase differences do not follow single crystals or distinguish direct transformation from dissolution/reprecipitation. Selected Figure9 intracellular diffraction does not prove organism authentication or a transport mechanism.
