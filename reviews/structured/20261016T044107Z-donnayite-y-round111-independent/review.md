### Species identity and scope

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:donnayite-y.

Current IMA p. 59 verifies renamed Donnayite-(Y), NaSr3CaY(CO3)6(H2O)3. The 1987 s.p. field is a special-procedure year, not a unique approval identifier. Species, rare-earth element, mixed ore and alicewilsonite-(YCe) remain distinct.

### Natural characterization and measurement provenance

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:donnayite-y.

The full 2023 original pp. 133–136 and Tables 1–2 support seven catalogued cotypes, six reassigned to alicewilsonite-(YCe), and CMNMC 39396 retained as holotype under approved proposal 22-F. Methods p. 135 gives 20 kV, 10 nA, 30 micrometre beam and time-dependent intensity correction. The 17-analysis holotype mean is 10.18 wt% Y2O3; volatile contents are calculated, not measured. The original chemistry is not a uniform single-species dataset.

### Experiment, observation and causal limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:donnayite-y.

No extraction or controlled transformation is asserted. The unsatisfactory R1=0.139 holotype experiment is separated from CMNMC 90405, whose 293 K P 1 refinement gave rounded R1=0.055. Figures 2–4 and Tables 5–7 show split carbonate/water positions and proxy Dy cations; the official CIF has no hydrogen-coordinate model. Main Table 5 Rint=0.092 versus CIF _diffrn_reflns_av_R_equivalents=0.0342 is retained without choosing a preferred value. Bond-valence Table S1 and powder-pattern files were examined without independent structural refinement.
