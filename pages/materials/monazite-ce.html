Monazite-(Ce) mineral

Monazite-(Ce) is the cerium-dominant phosphate mineral species with ideal formula Ce(PO4), distinct from elemental cerium, other monazite species and mixed monazite-bearing ores. Ni, Hughes and Mariano (1995) characterized a natural crystal from the Kangankunde carbonatite, Malawi, using chemical analysis and single-crystal X-ray diffraction. Its monoclinic P2_1/n structure has nine-coordinate rare-earth sites. The natural crystal contains other rare-earth elements; the separately studied synthetic CePO4 crystal is not that specimen. This structural study establishes neither microbial transformation nor metal recovery. No dated criticality designation or industrial deployment is assigned.
