Silicon carbide commodity Critical Material

Manufactured silicon carbide (SiC) considered as an industrial and wide-bandgap semiconductor/abrasive commodity, distinct from elemental silicon (cmmmech:silicon), natural silica feedstocks, and the rare natural meteoritic or terrestrial mineral species moissanite. The 2023 US Department of Energy Final Critical Materials List (88 FR 51792-51798) designates silicon carbide as a critical material for energy on the basis of supply risk for wide-bandgap power-electronics wafers in electric vehicles and clean energy systems. USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 covers manufactured silicon carbide in its Abrasives (Manufactured) chapter (printed pp. 26-27, chapter PDF pp. 1-2; HTS 2849.20.1000 crude and 2849.20.2000 grain) and notes in its Silicon chapter (printed p. 171) that silicon carbide can substitute for ferrosilicon in some metallurgical applications. Trade and production statistics in the USGS abrasives chapter do not disaggregate semiconductor-wafer-grade boules from abrasive or metallurgical grades; this grade boundary is retained rather than conflated. No microbial synthesis, bioleaching, or biological recovery mechanism is asserted.
