## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Material and identifier boundaries

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioburbankite.

The current IMA September 2026 p.35 gives Na3(Ca,Ce,Sr,La)3(CO3)5, approved IMA1993-001. This species is distinct from burbankite and the group; the holotype CMN50804 is a specimen accession, not the approval ID.

### Specimen and analytical evidence

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioburbankite.

The natural Mont Saint-Hilaire holotype has uncertain precise quarry and geological setting; its partly altered surface is described separately from a later La-rich specimen. Three analyses on three grains establish Ca-dominant chemistry. Carbon was detected using an elemental analyzer; CO2 was calculated stoichiometrically using a related structure, not measured independently. IR did not detect OH or water. The Table 2 Pr2O3 mean/range and duplicated Ca atomic-proportion range are inconsistent; disputed ranges are not adopted.

### Model and process limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioburbankite.

Precession/powder diffraction leaves three possible space groups in the 1995 study. Its discussion of burbankite coordination is not a solved calcioburbankite atomic model. Rapid dissolution in 10%HCl is qualitative, with no time, temperature, loading, recovery yield or product. The later 2001 refinement and 2024 formation lead are explicitly outside accepted evidence.

### Criticality, organism and deployment claims
