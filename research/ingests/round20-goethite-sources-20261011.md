## Claim-level assessment and contradictions

**Series 1 natural goethite:** S343 from Wolf mine, Germany, is goethite by XRD/Rietveld, but XRF Table 1 contains 85% Fe2O3 and 2% SiO2. The text's mineralogical “pure” designation does not prove elemental purity, and oxide-equivalent percentages are not phase fractions. BGR living cultures were tested separately with 0.5% w/v finely ground mineral, 1 g/L sulfur and 10% v/v ten-day inoculum. Methods specify 30 degrees C/120 rpm for thiooxidans and 45 degrees C shake cultivation for caldus over five weeks. Figure 1 starts near pH3; endpoint pH1.2/1.0 accompanies 1.9/3.8 mM total filtered iron versus 0.08/0.3 mM temperature-matched uninoculated controls (Results 3.1). The filter was 0.2 micrometre and Fe(II)/Fe(III) were measured by ferrozine. This supports culture-associated mobilization, not isolated elemental iron. Distinct temperatures prevent a species-only ranking.

Uninoculated controls do not match culture acidification or reduced-sulfur chemistry; no Series 1 acid-matched/killed-cell comparator is described. The paper itself explains that Fe(II)/Fe(III) changes cannot separate direct microbial reduction from chemical sulfur reduction. Methods say duplicate abiotic controls, whereas Figure1 caption says three assays each; the record preserves the discrepancy. Figure2 plots R² 0.967/0.9874, whereas Discussion gives 0.93/0.97. No fitted value or causal proof is adopted. Figure1 panelD reports percent extraction with a denominator not re-established here; the record retains concentration endpoints only. No field or industrial deployment, pure product or complete mass balance is demonstrated.

**Series 3 conditional null:** living *A. cryptum* DSM2389 and SJH, centrifuged and washed twice after five-day growth, were inoculated at 10% v/v into anaerobic pH2 glycerol/yeast-extract medium with separately tested S018/S337, 0.1g (0.5% w/v), at30 degrees C without shaking, in triplicate for42days. S018 contains gibbsite and S337 1% lepidocrocite; BET areas are35/19m2/g. Separate25mM soluble Fe(III) controls demonstrated reduction; endpoint transfers into fresh aerobic medium supported viable cells. Results3.3 reports no significant enhancement over abiotic mineral controls, as Figures6-7 show. No statistical test details or detection limit are supplied in those passages. Finite small natural-feed values appear in Figure9. The null is not converted to an exact zero, analytical non-detection, or a universal limitation of the species/mineral.

**Synthetic/natural boundary:** Series4 is33days, with a stated13day repeat, not the42day Series3. The synthetic preparations have BET areas62/97m2/g; the authors infer ferrihydrite precursor contamination but do not quantify its phase fraction. The larger sG2 response cannot be assigned wholly to goethite. Figure8 has finite natural-feed values despite “not detectable” prose. No absolute zero or pure-mineral yield is curated. Series2 ferrooxidans results remain background rather than an extra mechanism added for coverage count. No changes to the existing ferrihydrite or iron-tailings records are needed for this accepted source scope.
