## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Species identity and scope

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kukharenkoite-ce.

Current official IMA September 2026 p. 118 verifies Ba2Ce(CO3)3F, approved 1995-040. This is the Ce mineral species, distinct from a mixed carbonatite resource, its elements and the La species. No historical tentative synonym is silently adopted as a verified separate identifier.

### Natural characterization and measurement provenance

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kukharenkoite-ce.

Zaitsev et al. 1996 original pp. 1328–1333, Figure 1 and Table 2 support natural specimens from Russia and Quebec. KH1 has twelve analyses and means of Ce2O3 15.30 and La2O3 6.61 wt%; carbonate was calculated because insufficient material prevented direct CO2 analysis. Ba interference uncertainty prevents the small KH5 apparent La excess from establishing La-species identity. The qualitative HCl effervescence statement p. 1330 supplies no concentration, time, temperature, loading or yield.

### Experiment, observation and causal limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kukharenkoite-ce.

Krivovichev et al. 1998 p. 810 identifies the natural manganoan-ankerite-carbonatite crystal as KH1 from the earlier work. Table 1 gives 293(2) K, P21/m, 2296 observed reflections and R=0.044. Tables 2–3 and Figures 1–4 support the idealized Ce structural model and fluorine-centered coordination interpretation. Neither this model nor the paper's secondary quotation of a different synthetic preparation is an extraction, formation or microbial experiment. Published coordinates are distinct from unacquired deposited structure factors. Zaitsev 1996 p. 1334 explicitly states original zhonghuacerite material was unavailable; the proposed equivalence remains tentative.
