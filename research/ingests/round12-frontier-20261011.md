## Next priorities

1. Resolve four battery-grade products and metallurgy-grade boron using authoritative specifications and actual product forms; a criticality label alone is insufficient.
2. Resolve metallurgical coal boundaries across authorities before treating it as a coking-coal alias or broader material category.
3. Review primary mineral and secondary-feed cohorts, including the existing colemanite, phosphate-rock, catalyst, battery and waste leads. Distinguish a new material root from another assay on an existing material.
4. Pursue deferred full-methods evidence and independent controls for identity-only records. New lanmodulin devices and studies need their own experiment-level verification; they do not imply new material identities.

The broad graphite commodity deliberately contains natural and synthetic products; the earlier natural-graphite record is a narrower subset. EU light/heavy REE and permanent-magnet groupings differ from the Australian sixteen-member rare-earth designation and from chemical definitions including scandium. These overlaps are explicit, not hidden duplicate counts.

Searches included hidden and ignored files in the named corpus/research/review/history trees and cited raw-source roots. Source access failures and unresolved controls remain in the dossiers. This dated, bounded reconciliation makes **no claim that all new CMMs or microbial mechanisms have been found**.
