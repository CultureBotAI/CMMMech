Ginelfite mineral IMA: Glf

IMA-approved complex silver-thallium-lead-antimony-arsenic sulfosalt mineral (IMA 2022-110; symbol Glf; status A, Approved 2023; full description published May 2025) with ideal end-member formula Ag2(Ag0.5Fe2+0.5)TlPb24.5(Sb,As)32.5S75.5 reported in the primary type description (Biagioni et al., 2025, European Journal of Mineralogy 37:319-335; listed in the September 2026 IMA-CNMNC Master List, PDF p. 82, under the pre-refinement formula Ag2(Ag0.5Fe2+0.5)TlPb23.5(Sb,As)33.5S76 prior to recognizing the half-occupancy of the S(71) site and final Pb/(Sb,As) site assignments). Discovered in historical samples collected in the 1960s from the Jas Roux hydrothermal deposit, La Chapelle-en-Valgaudemar, Gap, Hautes-Alpes, Provence-Alpes-Cote d'Azur, France (formerly corresponded to the unnamed phase "mineral Ao" of Mantienne, 1974 and UM2000-44-S:AgAsPbSb of Smith and Nickel, 2007; named after the abbreviation G.I.N.E.L.F. for the Groupe d'Etude sur la Mineralogie de Jas Roux created by French geologists Francois Permingeat, Roland Pierrot,Paul Picot, and Johan Zdenek), where it occurs in cavities of baryte + quartz gangue within Triassic hydrothermally altered calcareous-dolomitic sedimentary rocks along the east-west pre-Triassic Rif du Sap fault, intimately associated with Fe-bearing sphalerite and lopatkaite (which forms 100 um thick replacement rims around ginelfite); holotype material is deposited in the Museo di Storia Naturale of the Universita di Pisa, Calci (PI), Italy (catalogue number 20023) and in the Department of Mineralogy and Petrology, National Museum, Prague, Czech Republic (catalogue number P1P 56/2022). Forms dark-grey to black acicular to striated prismatic crystals up to 0.5 mm long with a black streak, metallic lustre, opaque diaphaneity, brittle tenacity, conchoidal fracture, indistinct cleavage, calculated density Dcalc = 5.656 g/cm3 from the empirical formula and single-crystal unit cell, and historical Vickers microhardness VHN25 = 136-163 kg/mm2 (Mantienne, 1974; corresponding to Mohs hardness ~3.5). In reflected light it is light grey with weak bireflectance, non-pleochroic character, and weak anisotropy with greenish and brownish rotation tints and no internal reflections; Commission on Ore Mineralogy (COM) reflectance values in air (Rmin, Rmax in %; Table 1) are 36.2, 38.0% (470 nm), 34.9, 36.7% (546 nm), 34.0, 35.9% (589 nm), and 32.1, 33.9% (650 nm). Wavelength-dispersive electron microprobe analysis (35 spot analyses, Cameca SX100, 25 kV, 20 nA, 2 um beam; Table 2) yielded Pb 45.44 wt% (range 44.81-45.83, e.s.d. 0.21), Sb 23.46 wt% (23.10-23.94, e.s.d. 0.19), S 21.07 wt% (20.62-21.32, e.s.d. 0.14), As 6.17 wt% (5.84-6.48, e.s.d. 0.17), Ag 2.34 wt% (2.20-2.42, e.s.d. 0.05), Tl 1.14 wt% (0.98-1.34, e.s.d. 0.09), Fe 0.22 wt% (0.20-0.25, e.s.d. 0.01), and Cu 0.07 wt% (0.00-0.09, e.s.d. 0.02), total 99.91 wt% (range 99.21-100.59, e.s.d. 0.32), corresponding on the basis of SigmaMe = 61 atoms per formula unit to the empirical formula Cu0.12(4)Ag2.51(5)Fe0.46(3)Tl0.65(5)Pb25.40(14)Sb22.32(18)As9.54(24)S76.12(74) (Sb + As = 31.86(12) apfu, Sb/(Sb + As) = 0.701(7), and valence equilibrium error Ev(%) = -1.1(1.0)%, with Table 2 printing the Ev(%) range as -2.3 to +1.7% whereas the prose on p. 323 misprints the upper bound sign as "-1.7%"). Crystallizes in the triclinic system, space group P-1 (#2), with single-crystal X-ray diffraction unit-cell parameters a = 8.3635(6) A, b = 27.5481(19) A, c = 29.199(2) A, alpha = 95.335(3) deg, beta = 94.123(3) deg, gamma = 94.367(3) deg, V = 6657.7(8) A3, and Z = 2 (refined with twin ratio 62.2 : 37.8(2) around [100] to R1 = 0.0876, R(all) = 0.1131, wR2 = 0.1764, and GoF = 1.221 for 23,779 reflections with Fo > 4sigma(Fo) out of 30,492 unique reflections from 291,055 measured at 293(2) K using MoKalpha radiation, Table 4). The chabourneite-related modular sulfosalt structure contains 61 independent cation sites (two Ag sites Ag(1) and Ag(2), one mixed tetrahedral (Ag0.54Fe0.46) site M(23) with <M(23)-S> = 2.48 A, 18 Pb sites, one mixed ninefold tricapped trigonal prismatic Tl(20) site Tl0.65Pb0.35 with <Tl(20)-S> = 3.29 A and BVS = 1.30 v.u., 12 mixed Pb/Sb sites, 24 mixed Sb/As sites, and 3 pure As sites) and 76 independent S sites (including half-occupied S(71), occupancy 0.50; Tables 5-6), built from five-octahedra-thick SnS-like rods (A and B) and PbS-like rods (C) alternating along c. The eight strongest observed Gandolfi powder X-ray diffraction lines (dobs in A (Iobs; hkl), CuKalpha, Table 3) are 3.383 (vs; 0 -8 1, 2 4 3, 2 -4 4), 2.104 (vs; 0 8 10, 0 -8 11, 4 0 0), 3.854 (ms; 2 -2 2), 3.635 (ms; -2 2 3, 0 5 6), 3.470 (ms; 0 -6 6, -2 4 1, -2 -1 5), 2.982 (ms; -2 -4 5, -2 3 6, 0 -9 2), 2.863 (ms; 0 9 3, 2 -1 7, 2 -7 1), and 2.752 (ms; 0 -5 9, -2 -6 1, 0 -10 0). No biological or microbial interaction data are reported in the type mineralogical description.
