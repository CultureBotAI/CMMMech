### Culture provenance versus current nomenclature

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:petroleum-coke.

No accession, sequencing result, purity proof or identification procedure establishes the exact culture. NCBI930 provides a species-level synonym mapping only; no type strain is transferred.

### Sequential arms and missing comparator endpoint

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:petroleum-coke.

Methods pp82–83 specify5mL culture, one week then fresh culture for another week, WhatmanNo4 filtration and approximately1g analytical portions. Table2 gives reagent masses without solution volume; medium pH2.5/35C growth versus culture pH3.54 are distinct. Treatment loading, volume and exposure settings are not silently inferred. The described uninoculated comparison has no numerical result and is not assumed sterile; bacterial effect size remains unresolved.
