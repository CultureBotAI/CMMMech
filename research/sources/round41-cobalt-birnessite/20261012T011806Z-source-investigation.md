## Candidate and claim assessment

This source is a primary laboratory experiment, not a criticality list or reusable tabular dataset. A. niger ATCC1015 is maintained on MEA; sterile powder below90µm is introduced at0.2%w/v to approximately25cm3 in90mm dishes, with a5mm colony plug,14days at25°C dark. Species5061 and catalogue designation A1144 3528.7/CBS113.46 were resolved; neither authenticates experimental stock. Separate sodium-feed Wei2012 strain201373 is retained untouched.

Synthetic preparation used cobalt chloride, potassium permanganate and hydrochloric acid with heating, washing and desiccation, then a nitric-acid exchange step and105°C pretreatment. The nitric-acid concentration unit is omitted in the original printed recipe and remains unresolved. Table1 gives73.41%MnO,7.74%Co,4.48%K2O on its mixed oxide/element basis, excluding detected elements absent from synthesis solutions; it is not a complete elemental mass balance or refined formula.

Original Figure6 shows changed morphology relative to uninoculated controls; Figure7 shows qualitative Co association with examined transformed material. Figure8 aligns control peaks with birnessite and transformed peaks with lindbergite. Its heading incorrectly says Nkamouna but its detailed caption, Results and analytical methods identify this synthetic arm. Replicates were pooled and a subsample analysed; no phase fraction or residual-feed detection threshold. Synthetic samples used Cu-radiation powder XRD on PVC slides; do not copy the laterite arm's Co-radiation/acetone protocol.

Table2 contains matched inoculated/abiotic, mineral/no-mineral comparisons. Its synthetic-arm means are2.17±0.03(n6),2.26±0.02(n3),6.43±0.04(n2),5.32±0.02(n3), respectively, with SD. The narrative's increased-pH explanation does not match the synthetic fungal means; no silent correction. Biomass tolerance94.1% and extension-rate ratio1.47 are not extraction fractions. No structured mg/kg observations are appropriate for these pH/ratio/percentage values.

Reported complete transformation is supported qualitatively by microscopy/recovered phase comparison, not an isolated-product or dissolved-Co yield. Hot70°Cwater recovery and at least48hdesiccation limit in-culture hydration assignment. Oxalate-mediated transformation, Mn reduction and especially Co reduction/lattice incorporation require narrower wording because there are no direct metabolite/redox/site assays, matched acid/killed-biomass/cell-free controls, or complete elemental balances.
