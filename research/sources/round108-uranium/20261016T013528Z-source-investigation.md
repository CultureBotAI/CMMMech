## Evidence assessments

**IMA September 2026:** the fresh 244-page authority PDF was acquired, with page 1 definitions and exact sklodowskite/soddyite rows on pages 201/202 visually checked. Both are grandfathered species. Years 1924 and 1922 are original descriptions, not approval accessions. The current sklodowskite formula distinguishes four coordinated waters and two additional waters, with six waters in bulk.

**Giammar and Hering 2002**, DOI `10.1016/S0016-7037(02)00927-4`: all 11 original pages, nine figures and two tables were read and visually checked. Synthetic powders were tested in batch and flow systems at 22 ± 2°C, nominal pH 6, with NaNO3 and MES. A clarkeite-like secondary phase accompanied declining dissolved uranium and continuing silicon release. XRD does not uniquely separate that phase from related sodium uranates. Flow release was lower with 100 µmol/L added silicon. Rates were calculated from measured concentrations, residence time, loading and BET area; the batch rates were linear fits with different time windows. Section 3.3 reports 0.70/0.45 while the abstract and section 4.4 give 0.71/0.44 µmol U m−2 h−1. Those differences, lost later silicon samples, wall-adhered solids, inferred batch pH stability and modeled equilibrium results are preserved. There is no isolated product yield or industrial test.

**Plášil 2018**, DOI `10.3390/min8120551`: all 15 pages, eight figures and three tables were read and visually checked, together with publisher XML and the original supplement. Although labelled a Review, section 2.1 and Table 2 contain new natural-crystal measurements. Both relevant CIFs were checked for identity, symmetry, cell dimensions, refinement and hydrogen treatment; their reflection data were acquired but not independently re-refined. Natural museum provenance is kept separate from photographed locality specimens. The article/CIF uncertainty differs for sklodowskite beta, 105.882(14)/(3), and soddyite c, 18.6576(11)/(7). Incomplete CIF publication templates and a Cu0 formula term do not establish a different publication or copper-bearing species. Broad paragenetic and synthesis review passages remain secondary context. The paper declares CC BY 4.0; raw artifacts remain external.

**Vochten et al. 1997**, Canadian Mineralogist 35:735–741: all seven original pages, three figures and four tables were inspected. Page 736 pairs the 2 M MgSO4 treatment with sklodowskite formation from synthetic boltwoodite, at 185°C and reported 1.3 MPa. The 24-hour treatment was repeated until a qualitative supernatant potassium test was negative. The cycle count, detection threshold, loading, volume, pH, yield and phase fractions are not supplied. Powder diffraction and Table 4 identify the synthetic product. Precursor synthesis conditions, boltwoodite solubility data and the sodium arm's fluorinated byproduct are not transferred to the Mg arm. No DOI was verified in the bounded search; the original bibliographic identity is retained. Redistribution rights were not established.
