## Summary

Chlorapatite is the Cl-dominant apatite species Ca5(PO4)3Cl, distinct from hydroxylapatite, pyromorphite and elemental phosphorus. Renamed/2010 special-procedure status is correct. Table 1 XRD and Figure 1b SEM-EDS support the source-assigned synthetic analogue; the record does not transfer the pyromorphite wet-chemical formula or assert an independently quantified purity. Figure 2 supports limited growth with chlorapatite in phosphate-deficient medium and no detectable mineral benefit in phosphate-rich medium. Table 3 endpoint concentrations of 119 +/-10.7 and 2796 +/-9 micromolar P are correctly assigned to abiotic MP- and MP+ arms. The latter includes initially dissolved P. This is nutrient access and abiotic release, not bacterial enhancement of chlorapatite dissolution or product recovery.
