The chemical aliquot's estimated tincalconite impurity is not a measured thermal-aliquot purity. The 635°C phase assignment has optical discrepancies, the proposed 700°C phase was not found, and products after a separate 850°C/12h hold were too scarce for full identification. No recovery yield is supplied.

The 1975 ameghinite structure supports triborate units and modeled H positions. The official 18-page AM-75-007 deposit was acquired after CREATE. Its Table 3 header and representative factor rows were checked, without independent re-refinement.

The 1974 aristarainite chemical analysis and 1977 model differ in ideal hydration. The model-based revision is not a new bulk-water assay. The 1974 optical-sign conflict and 1977 Table 1 bracket-formula discrepancy remain qualified. An apparent beta-angle discrepancy was rejected after enlarging the original print; it was OCR error. The official 39-page AM-77-052 deposit was acquired; representative factor rows and the final summary were checked, without exhaustive transcription.

The complete 27-page 2017 OSTI manuscript identifies a different sodium hexaborate tetrahydrate polymorph. Its proposed conversion to ameghinite is future work. The solution experiment and derived constants are therefore not an ameghinite mechanism. A 2012 spectroscopy manuscript was acquired but only partly assessed; its referenced XRD supplement was not acquired and no new measurements were imported.
