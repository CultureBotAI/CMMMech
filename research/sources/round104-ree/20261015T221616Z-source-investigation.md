## Florencite-(La)

[Lefebvre and Gasparrini1980](https://www.rruff.net/doclib/cm/vol18/CM18_301.pdf), all11 pages acquired, supplies actual Shituru drill-core chemistry and diffraction. Methods pp303-304 use wavelength-dispersive P/Al and energy-dispersive rare-earth estimates. Table1 column1 reports La2O3 19.0 and Ce2O3 10.5wt%; water was not determined and Pr detected qualitatively was not quantified. Table2's measured-REE normalization is not a complete formula. The mixed hematite/florencite concentrate produced only one attributable line; a separately extracted60µm crystal gave the Gandolfi pattern (p306; Tables5-6). Isotropic darker cores were proposed to be metamict, not proved by a separate controlled test. The hydrothermal versus later supergene interpretation at pp307-308 is not a laboratory conversion mechanism.

[Ghignone etal2024](https://doi.org/10.1002/jrs.6640) is a separate Raman/computation study, online15Dec2023, issue55:394-405 in2024. The complete institutional PDF (cover calls it author's manuscript, internal pages are journal-formatted) was checked against publisher HTML. F1 is the La-dominant IgarapeBahia collection specimen; F2 and both garnet inclusions are Ce-dominant. F1/F2 are unpolished/uncoated semiquantitative SEM-EDS, unlike the coated thin-section arms. Table1 F1's35.6wt% total is not a complete bulk assay. Raman methods:532nm laser,20(1)°C,30s/four repetitions,1–2µm spot; four acquisitions are not four independent specimens. Similar Raman bands do not resolve La/Ce dominance. Ideal La geometry is computed using the Ce geometry and modeled thermal/zero-point corrections, not experimentally refined F1 crystallography.

Table3 identifies experimental VS17/F2, while adjacent p401 prose says F1. Section4.3 says985cm-1; Figure3 labels983cm-1. Neither exact universal band nor a complete pure-La formula is imported.
