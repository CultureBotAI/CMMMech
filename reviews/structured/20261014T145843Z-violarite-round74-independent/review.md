## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Ideal species versus broad experimental label

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:violarite.

IMA/Handbook support cubic FeNi2S4 species. The paper expressly uses violarite for intermediate Fe–Ni thiospinels; the owner preserves that product-composition limit rather than claiming a pure endmember.

### Abiotic replacement in separate mixed-feed arms

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:violarite.

Natural concentrate at80°C and synthetic pentlandite/pyrrhotite at120°C have different preparation, fluids and sampling. Figures1–3 support a source-assigned replacement phase without any organism or inoculum.

### Textural interpretation versus complete conversion

scope: supported. Targets: cmmmech:violarite.

XRD without independent standard is semiquantitative and Figure2 retains pentlandite despite abstract complete-conversion wording. BSE textures support dissolution/reprecipitation but failed EBSD and exsitu drying prevent stronger crystallographic or direct-interface assertions.

All four Pring2005 pages were independently compared with the2006 letter. Shared authors, feeds and graphics show related experiment lineage;35day/50percent/pH3.5 differ from the later33day/40percent/pH4.0 descriptions. The accepted owner follows the complete2006 source without pooling versions.
