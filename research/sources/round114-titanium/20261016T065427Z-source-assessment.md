# Round114 titanium-bearing mineral source assessment

## Selection and identity

Ilmenite and perovskite add two distinct IMA species. The official September 2026 rows (PDF100 and169, visually inspected with definitions) give Fe2+Ti4+O3/G/1827 and CaTiO3/G/1839. Years are original-description dates, not approval accessions. Neither mineral inherits titanium criticality. Existing rutile, titanium products, mixed concentrates and the generic perovskite structure family remain distinct.

## Accepted evidence

- **Ilmenite:** complete Sun et al. primary, Minerals15:20, DOI10.3390/min15010020; ten pages read, Figures3–6 and Table1 inspected. Publisher PDF dated27December2024 belongs to the2025 issue. Three ground placer concentrates show HCl dissolution; the owner retains preparation, acid strength, temperature, analytical endpoint and unavailable replication/control details. It makes no isolated-product or deployment claim. Feed inclusions and supplier/literature analyses confound a causal iron-valence inference. TIS spectral-fit constraints and standard-error/standard-deviation wording conflict remain explicit. Figure3 supports solubilization, not a measured absence of every possible secondary solid. No fitted numerical rate precision is imported.
- **Perovskite:** complete Keller and Buseck1994 original, American Mineralogist79:73–79, read including natural-grain TEM/diffraction methods and separate synthetic cooling arms. The owner uses natural identity evidence only. Detection-limited cation analysis is not absolute purity or measured oxygen. Historical transition models and inferred meteoritic cooling rates are not generalized. Current IMA identity corroborates the named species. No guessed DOI is assigned.
