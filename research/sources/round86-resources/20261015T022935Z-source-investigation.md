## Claim boundaries and adverse evidence

Pradhan's municipal sludge is initially 5.59% total solids; 10 mL is combined with 90 mL medium/culture in 250 mL bottles at 30°C/150 rpm. Ten percent of the incubated mixture is replaced by fresh sludge at days 21 and 42; nominal 10 g/L elemental sulfur described as 80% pure is added at 42, with endpoint 63 days. Added cultures are DSM 29444 and DSM 14887, separately and in equal-volume mixtures. Water and uninoculated-medium comparators contain live indigenous sludge organisms. Selective culture is not molecular identification, and the added strains' necessity is not demonstrated.

Table 5's T+S phosphorus value 92% at pH 0.9 is retained only as the authors' assay report. Medium contains phosphate/iron/trace elements (§2.4), while explicit nutrient-input subtraction and a closed sampling/replacement balance are not given. Thus it is not a verified sludge-derived product yield. Earlier T phases report 10% and 5%; late treatment is not represented as a short independent assay. Table 6 contains values exceeding 100%, and some Figure 1 final-pH traces disagree with Table 5. The claim of 0.125 M sulfuric acid calculated directly from pH is not adopted as measured acid production. Figure 2 is a filtered soluble phosphate endpoint; no isolated phosphate product, universal hygienization or industrial operation is accepted.

Qiu's separate Tianjin sludge was diluted to 4.0% solids. The stated assay uses 300 mL sludge, 5% v/v indigenous iron-oxidizer inoculum, 4 g/L Fe2+ as FeSO4, 28°C/150 rpm and ten days, with 20 mL sampling every 48 h. No community taxon is inferred from generic references. Reported Zn/Ni mobilization exceeds Cr/As/Pb response, preserving weak extraction. No matched sterile Fe-fed or acidity-matched arm is presented. In a separate chemical extension, H2O2 is added when prior bioleaching reaches selected pH; increases for several metals coexist with worse As/Pb removal. Proposed radicals/FeAsO4/PbSO4 are interpretations, and BCR fractions are operational extractions rather than phase identification. Internal Ni 65.81/65.19 and Cu13/15 g/L endpoint assignments prevent accepting a precise hybrid optimum. A reference to earlier methods is not proof that their inoculum, chemistry or assay details have been independently checked.
