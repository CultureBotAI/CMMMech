## Claim-level assessment

Payne, Spietz and Boyd2021, ISME Journal15:3498–3507, used M. voltae A3 (ATCCBAA-1334) and a separately labelled M. barkeri culture. NCBI's live API resolves456320 to the strain `Methanococcus voltae A3`; the issuing ATCC page independently labels BAA-1334 A3. No genotype or protein mechanism is inferred from identity.

The natural specimen from Zacatecas was crushed, sieved63–125micrometres, acid/water/acetone-washed, and supplied as1.5g/75mL. FigureS3a demonstrates day5 cell and methane production relative to no-added-Fe/S controls. It does not report natural-specimen sulfide production or a dissolved-iron/mineral mass balance. Figure4b and S7–S8 show cell association. S1 explicitly characterizes laboratory-synthesized FeS and FeS2; its caption is not evidence of a separate natural-specimen XRD pattern. Consequently natural specimen purity and an exact mineral-specific dissolution rate remain unestablished.

Separate synthesized-FeS2 arms used2mMFe, pH7 basal medium, washed10%inoculum, formate, static38°C incubation,75mLmedium in165mLbottles,80:20N2/CO2 at3.14atm. M.voltae medium recipe is TableS1, despite mistaken table references in main text. Main Figure1a,c,e shows cells/methane and sulfide with triplicate biological reactors and single abiotic controls. TableS3/Figure3 report day5 accessible-mineral sulfide10.55±0.13micromol (mean±SD,n3), while sequestered/unamended/abiotic sulfide is B.D.; the detection bound is not numerically supplied there. This is a sulfide amount in one assay, not percent mineral extraction. It is separate from the seven-day Figure1 series and day5 natural-specimen assay.

The3.5kDa dialysis experiment supports mineral-access dependence but does not by itself distinguish electron transfer, product accessibility, or large extracellular assemblies. Main text reports acid-labileFeII below ~11micromolar; that is censoring, not zero. Secondary FeS/pyrrhotite, transport machinery and direct electron-transfer chemistry remain proposals. The0.23fmol/cell reduction estimate combines an assumed sulfur requirement from a related species and unpublished protein/cell information; it is not adopted as a measured yield. S6 shows growth when only sulfide was added, indicating residual traceFe. Surface area matching was geometric estimation, not a measurement of equivalent reactivity. Laboratory culture growth is not isolated-metal recovery or industrial deployment.
