The source characterized two sepiolite samples with other crystalline phases below its approximately 2 volume-percent XRD detection limit. XRF gives Mg:Si 3.34:6 rather than ideal 4:6. Chemical flow and batch assays at 25 °C show preferential magnesium release under acidic conditions and preferential silicon release under alkaline conditions. All citrate was supplied chemically; no microbial experiment is accepted.

## Evidence limits retained in the owner

- Approximately 98 percent Mg removal in the lowest-pH batch arm is a source-derived element mass-balance estimate relative to the starting mineral. It is not isolated-product recovery, product purity or total feed conversion.
- The prose, Tables 2/4 and Figure 7 caption use 688 h, while Appendix 2 and Figures 6/7 label the final sample 699 h. The endpoint remains unresolved. Initial pH 2.56/2.57 also differs between source locations, and measured pH drifts despite the source's description of stable buffering.
- Needle morphology remains while XRD crystallinity decreases. Shape does not prove surviving crystalline sepiolite. EDS and XRF compositions are not interchangeable bulk measurements.
- Channel-mediated proton/Mg exchange and interface-coupled dissolution/reprecipitation remain competing interpretations. Modeled saturation or a hypothetical brucite upper bound is not direct evidence of a new crystalline product.
- Rates use initial BET area and source stoichiometry. The source's approximately 15 percent estimated rate uncertainty excludes changing reactive area. Batch/flow comparisons do not isolate dialysis effects.
- Seven sacrificial bottles per batch series are time points, not seven independent endpoint replicates. Separate no-mineral blanks and independent replication of each 2020 condition are not specified.
- Further source inconsistencies were screened without importing them as facts: inverted residence-time wording, Table 3 saturation-index sign/units, software-version citation, unequal rate-fitting windows, and buffer-formula/flow-pH labels. The record does not use these formulas or indices quantitatively.
- Carbon sequestration is discussed as potential relevance. No carbon-capture demonstration, isolated Mg product or industrial operation was tested.

No separate supplement was identified in the complete UCL entry, manuscript or bounded exact-title search; the manuscript includes both appendices. The correct publisher route, PII S0016703719307343 from Crossref, returned 403, so publisher-side supplement availability was not exhaustively verified. ## Earlier same-group study
