Orpiment mineral

Orpiment is the monoclinic As2S3 mineral species, grandfathered in the September 2026 IMA list, whose original-description year is unspecified. It is distinct from elemental arsenic, realgar and amorphous As2S3. The observation below concerns an XRD-assigned secondary component of arsenopyrite leaching residues, not an isolated orpiment feed or recovered product. No criticality designation is inherited from arsenic.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Orpiment-assigned secondary phase during arsenopyrite culture leaching

redox transformation Microbial

Associated Organisms: Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans (NCBITaxon:920) [TaxonMech]

During laboratory arsenopyrite leaching in Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans culture, the authors assigned orpiment among the crystalline components of washed residual solids using XRD and Rietveld phase analysis. They proposed formation through ferric-mediated arsenopyrite oxidation in a system with biological iron oxidation. This is a culture-associated secondary-phase observation with an inferred chemical pathway, not a demonstration that cells are necessary for orpiment formation. It is the same Liu 2019 experiment curated for the distinct arsenopyrite owner; treating orpiment as its own material does not add an independent experiment.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
The source used high-purity natural Yaogangxian, Hunan arsenopyrite, XRF composition Fe1.00As0.99S0.97 with trace Si, Co and Ni. Wet-milled particles were over 90% below 74 micrometres and loaded at 10 g/L. Cultures in 250 mL flasks contained 100 mL 9K medium plus 15 mL inoculum, at 30 degrees C and 160 rpm, initially pH 1.8 adjusted with approximately 3 M sulfuric acid. Medium per litre contained 44.8 g FeSO4.7H2O, 3.0 g (NH4)2SO4, 0.50 g K2HPO4.3H2O, 0.1 g KCl, 0.5 g MgSO4.7H2O and 0.01 g Ca(NO3)2.4H2O. Bacteria had been acclimated with arsenopyrite for three months, but strain accession and initial cell density are unspecified. Solids were filtered, washed at least ten times with deionized water and vacuum-dried overnight at 35 degrees C. Separate approximately 15 by 15 by 5 mm cubes were polished and cleaned with acid, methanol and water for microscopy, then fixed, rinsed and ethanol-dehydrated. The paper does not specify replicate count or error-bar definition, matched sterile or matched-pH kinetic controls, or a selective film-removal test. No orpiment-specific isolated yield, arsenic mass balance or industrial deployment is established.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: liu2019
Sections 3.2-3.3 and 4.1.2, Figure 2a-b, identify orpiment alongside realgar, sulfur, jarosite and residual arsenopyrite in a time series including days 1, 3, 5, 9 and 19. The source describes early orpiment formation and declining relative content as leaching progresses. The normalized solid-phase proportions do not quantify absolute orpiment production or destruction. Source phase assignment is based on the diffraction analysis, not inferred here from As2S3 formula or EDS alone.
Partial Source: liu2019
Figures 4-5 and Table 1 show a dense As/S-rich early surface film on separate cubes, with cracks attributed to drying, and a later porous jarosite-associated overlayer. The proposed orpiment-containing passivating film combines particle XRD with cube morphology/EDS; it is not an orpiment-specific causal intervention. Figure 6 and Table 2 are a proposed pathway and thermodynamic reactions, not directly measured reaction fluxes. The 25 degrees C equilibrium calculation differs from the 30 degrees C culture and the authors acknowledge disagreement with early-stage phase observations. Neither a unique passivating role nor a microbial increment over abiotic orpiment formation is isolated.
Context_only Source: newman1997
The distinct Desulfotomaculum auripigmentum precipitation experiment is not accepted as crystalline orpiment evidence. Page 2023 explicitly reports electron diffraction showing an amorphous precipitate; elemental proportions and Figure 2 support As2S3 composition without establishing the orpiment crystal structure. Its microbial precipitation and aqueous arsenic-removal measurements are not transferred to this mineral-specific mechanism.

Causal Edges (4):
  • sqr_reductase → oxidation of sulfide/sulfur intermediates to polythionates (supplies_substrate_for) → tth_hydrolase : Sulfide/sulfur oxidation generates reduced sulfur and polythionate intermediates metabolized by periplasmic tetrathionate hydrolase.[liu2019]
  • tth_hydrolase → polythionate hydrolysis and sulfuric acid proton generation (produces) → acidic_9k_lixiviant : Periplasmic tetrathionate hydrolysis regenerates sulfate and protons in the acidic culture lixiviant.[liu2019]
  • acidic_9k_lixiviant → culture-associated Fe(II) oxidation and ferric-mediated corrosion of arsenopyrite (FeAsS) (oxidizes) → yaogangxian_arsenopyrite_feed : Inoculated A. ferrooxidans culture oxidizes Fe(II) to Fe(III) and corrodes arsenopyrite, generating intermediate arsenic-sulfur solid phases (without a matched sterile control).[liu2019]
  • yaogangxian_arsenopyrite_feed → formation of XRD/Rietveld-assigned secondary orpiment in washed residual solids (transforms_into) → secondary_orpiment_bearing_residue : Partial oxidation of arsenopyrite yields secondary orpiment alongside realgar, elemental sulfur, and jarosite in washed particle residues.[liu2019]
