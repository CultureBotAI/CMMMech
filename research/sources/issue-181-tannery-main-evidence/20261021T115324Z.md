### Strains, media and inoculation

Table S1, local page 13, reports Acidithiobacillus sp. ZJ1 (At), Acidiphilium sp. ZJ2 (Ap) and Rhodotorula sp. ZJ3 (Rd), with 100% closest-species sequence similarities; the yeast row expressly labels ITS. These similarities do not authenticate a collection stock or prove a member-specific function. The merged resource cell attributes the three isolates to a tannery-sludge bioleaching reactor with citation “[10].” The SI contains no corresponding reference list, and the available main landing bibliography does not establish that numbered join.

The other two rows name Sulfobacillus acidophilus ATCC 700253 (Sb) and Alicyclobacillus cycloheptanicus CGMCC 1.7014 (Ab). The former catalog identity was independently checked; the latter stock remains source-reported, without a current collection authentication. The table's five growth recipes include 31 concentration entries, all checked against native XML and the rendered original table. They mix g/L and mg/L; the literal `HBO3` token is preserved. Growth media are not established final leaching-media recipes. The At row specifies 5 g/L sulfur and pH 4; Ap specifies pH 3–4, Sb pH 2 and Ab pH 4. The Rd row provides no pH. These entries do not establish incubation temperature, inoculum washout, residual growth-medium carryover or final reactor composition.

Table S2, local page 14, specifies an inoculated At control at `5×10^6 cell/mL` and fifteen mixed arms across six groups. There is no listed abiotic arm in this table; missing main methods prevent declaring that the study had no additional controls. Group 1 has AtAp 1:10 and the three other two-member combinations 1:1. Groups 2–4 add two or three partners with Ap represented at ten ratio units. Group 5 is AtApAbSb 1:10:1:1; Group 6 adds Rd at 1:10:1:1:1. All 47 ratio components and their arm mapping were checked.

If At were held at the control density in every arm, the reported ratios would imply different total cell densities, from two to fourteen times the At-only control. The fifteen corresponding calculations are conditional diagnostics, not observed total inoculum densities: S2 itself does not explicitly establish the constant-At assumption across mixed arms. Consequently, higher combined performance cannot yet be attributed solely to species synergy, independent of inoculum amount, pH or carryover chemistry. The sequencing plots contain repeated labelled samples, but the complete biological/technical replicate scheme and statistical independence are not established from these graphics alone.

## New patent lead: useful context without an experimental join

The original CN110724649A cover identifies Sichuan University, a filing date of 2019-10-09 and publication on 2020-01-24. Its named inventors include Tang Yueqin, Zeng Jing and Gou Min, corresponding to names in the later article. This supports a same-group lead by names/institution; it is not proof that two reports use the same assay runs.

Patent paragraphs [0028] and [0031], original page 5, describe 250 mL flasks, 100 mL working volume, 2% w/v dry sludge, 0.5% w/v sulfur, 30 °C and 200 rpm, with two-day sampling and at least two repeats. Paragraph [0028] expressly describes sludge sterilization and a water blank for its single-strain experiment. That detail cannot be generalized silently to every composite arm or transferred to Yao2022. The patent names At as A. thiooxidans ATCC 53990, A as A. cryptum ATCC 33463 and Rm as R. mucilaginosa ATCC 9449, whereas the article SI lists ZJ isolates. Only the reported Sulfobacillus and Alicyclobacillus collection numbers coincide. Its Sa/Ac aliases therefore contextualize the article's inconsistent graphic labels but do not resolve them.

Original patent Table 1 on page 6 contains sixteen arms, a repeated 16,371 mg/kg feed-Cr entry and six-day outcomes from 71 to 100%, with ratios matching a similar combination design. These sixteen feed/outcome/time rows and literal inoculation ratios are transcribed in the raw audit. Table 2 on page 7 compares other cited experiments; those are literature attributions, not additional patent runs or newly checked source evidence. The patent's text rounds some table outcomes (for example 78.8% to 79%); no merged corrected series is created.

There is an unresolved dimensional gap in paragraphs [0038]–[0039], page 6: the printed efficiency is `(V×Cw/T)×100`, while V is declared in mL, Cw in mg/L and T in mg. A 1/1000 volume conversion is required for those stated units but is not printed in the equation. This does not establish how the actual laboratory calculations were performed and does not authorize changing any published percentages. Conditional checks only: 2 g dry sludge at 16,371 mg/kg gives 32.742 mg Cr; in 0.100 L that corresponds to 327.42 mg/L at complete transfer. The 0.5% sulfur recipe implies 0.5 g sulfur per stated flask, and 5% of a 10^8-cell/mL At stock implies 5×10^6 cells/mL. These are patent-only calculations, not recovered Yao inventories.

No explicit original-run identifier, raw-data key, stock-equivalence statement or correction notice joins the patent to Yao2022. Similar ratios and six-day endpoints are insufficient. This patent also does not replace Wang2019's separate Xiamen feed, cultures and assay. Its feed number is not a universal tannery-sludge composition.
