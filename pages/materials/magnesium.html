Magnesium element Critical Material

Magnesium (Mg), the chemical element. The named magnesium commodity in the US 2025 list is mapped to this element; its designation is not inherited by every magnesium mineral. The experiment concerns magnesium-containing mineral transformation, not production of elemental magnesium metal.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Magnesium oxalate formation during fungal mineral transformation

biomineralization Microbial

Associated Organisms: Aspergillus niger ATCC 1015 (NCBITaxon:380704) [TaxonMech]

Aspergillus niger ATCC 1015 formed magnesium oxalate dihydrate (glushinskite) during growth with magnesium ammonium phosphate feeds.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Laboratory malt extract agar contained 1% w/v natural struvite from a Veolia wastewater site in Exeter, UK, or synthetic feed identified by XRD as dittmarite (MgNH4PO4.H2O), despite the paper's synthetic struvite terminology. Colonies grew from 5-mm mycelial plugs over cellophane for seven days at 25 degrees C in darkness. Crystals were extracted from agar with hot water, washed and dried before analysis.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: suyamud_2020
Figures 3-5 show crystals beneath colonies, Mg/C/O by EDXA, and XRD patterns assigned to magnesium oxalate dihydrate in both feed treatments, compared with struvite and dittmarite controls. Figure 5 reports representative patterns from at least three determinations. Table 2 documents accompanying acidification.
Partial Source: suyamud_2020
Mineral-free fungal and feed controls do not isolate oxalate's causal contribution from acidification with a chemistry-matched comparison. Phase identification establishes formation, not an isolated magnesium recovery yield or industrial deployment.
