1. The record appropriately separates experimental uptake from Table 1's fitted capacity and does not convert aqueous removal into purified-product recovery.
2. The source's inconsistent precipitation discussion is retained as a limitation in the second evidence item. The molecular binding explanation remains tentative.
3. The described Methods do not specify a matched biomass-free blank. Without that control, stronger attribution of all solution loss to binding would require further evidence.
4. Source claims about selective feed preparation cannot establish selectivity for untreated waste. The current record preserves this distinction.
5. Pilot-scale cultivation of the starting biomass does not establish deployment of the Nd separation process; the current laboratory scope is appropriate.
