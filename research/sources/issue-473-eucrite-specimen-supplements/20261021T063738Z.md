## EU dataset audit

The workbook has `Sum bioleaching data` (861 populated records) and
`Raw bioleaching data (ppm)` (48 populated records), covering seven substrate
arms. No stored cell formulas were found in the complete OOXML worksheet scan.
Unique keys are `(Organism, Rock, Element)` and `(Organism, Rock, Replicate)`
respectively. The summary contains 41 elements; the raw table has 48. Ca, K, Mg,
Na, P, S and Si are omitted from the summary. The SI data dictionary on PDF p.12
labels both types as ppm and defines `S.desiccabilis`, `Non-bio` and `Rock`.

For EU, raw rows 6–8 are the three cultured replicates, row 9 is the sole
uninoculated control, and rows 44–46 are three bulk-rock characterization
replicates. All 336 EU elemental raw cells were inspected, together with all
123 EU summary rows. Culture summary rows are 7, 14, …, 287; the matching Rock
and Non-bio rows are offset by 287 and 574. Summary n values are consistently
3/3/1 for culture/rock/control. The similarly numbered rock and culture
replicates are **not documented paired tube inventories**; no such join is made.
The other six arms were inventoried for table structure only and contribute no
EU claims.

### Missingness, zero and negative cells

The 336 EU raw cells contain no blanks, 84 literal zeros and eight negatives.
Negative values occur only in bulk rows: B (`D44:D46`), Ag (`AA44:AA46`) and
Ni (`R45:R46`). All 123 EU group mean/SE pairs match arithmetic after replacing
negative inputs with zero; only these three bulk groups fail to match the
untransformed inputs. For Ni, raw `R44:R46` is
4.384378759, -1.883866780, -2.031698017; summary `E448:F448` is
1.461459586333333 ± 1.461459586333333, exactly matching the zero-substituted
triplicate. This does not establish the authors' intended processing rule.
Neither the inspected methods nor SI dictionary specifies analytical detection
limits or negative-value censoring semantics. Do not recode the negatives as
below-detection qualifiers or interpret zeros as demonstrated chemical absence.

EU culture-derived cells G:J contain 100 finite numbers, nine `Inf` strings,
and 55 blanks. All 100 finite numbers reproduce the stated numerical
ratio/propagation calculations. Eighteen blank uncertainties would become zero
under a naive calculation; those blanks must remain missing. The remaining
blank or infinite positions likewise must not become positive recoveries.
Across all 123 EU summary rows D:J, 383 cells are blank; 328 are the
non-applicable G:J fields for rock/control rows.

SI S1 calls NA element absence; S5 uses NA for either absence from rock or zero
mean cultured concentration; S6 distinguishes NA from NL, but NL combines zero
biotic or abiotic extraction. These are heterogeneous source display categories,
not a quantitative detection-limit dictionary. For example, EU B and Ag have
negative raw bulk values, zero summarized inventory and `Inf` endpoint cells
`I21`/`I7`; S5 suppresses both as NA. No infinite yield is accepted.

### Unit and experiment boundaries

Main 2.3.2 describes bulk ICP-MS data per gram of rock (using the word
chondrite even for the mixed study); 2.8 says liquid micrograms/L were converted
to absolute chamber amounts with dilution factors. In contrast, both workbook
roles and SI p.12 use ppm. The workbook supplies no per-tube mass, volume,
dilution, unit-conversion or calculation columns. The known nominal experiment
used 0.15 g rock and 15 mL medium, but those alone cannot identify which
conversions are already embedded in the stored numbers. Consequently numerical
agreement does **not** independently validate a mass-balanced percentage of
the rock inventory. Do not multiply by an inferred correction factor or relabel
the stored values as absolute masses.

SI EU rows further report day-30 OD600 0.033 ± 0.005 versus control 0.010 (S2),
CFU/mL (1.48 ± 0.50) × 10^8 versus 0 (S3), CV OD570 0.124 ± 0.016 versus
0.002 (S4), and pH 6.61 ± 0.02 versus 6.97 (S8; fresh medium 7.01). These
corroborate the same culture-associated experiment, not a separate replication
or a measured ligand/molecular pathway. The pellet was not assayed. Single
control replication, endpoint-only liquid sampling and unmeasured isolated
product recovery remain limitations.
