## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Current species formula and neighboring minerals

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:laueite.

Current laueite species is distinct and appropriately grounded.

IMA G, historical year 1954 and ideal manganese-ferric formula agree. Stewartite and pseudolaueite have different structural arrangements; strunzite differs in water content. Historical Mn(III) interpretation is not substituted for current IMA identity.

### Natural composite crystal and orientation

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:laueite.

The 2025 natural-crystal epitaxial relationship is supported within its specimen limits.

The measured rhomb contains laueite and stewartite; separate diffraction datasets and indexed external faces support their orientation relationship. Minor stewartite reflections are weaker, overlapping intensities complicate refinement and the study is not a controlled mineral-conversion experiment. Proposed heterosite-assisted nucleation and solution chemistry changes are interpretative paragenesis.

### Chemical measurement versus refined and inferred composition

quantity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:laueite.

Distinct composition quantities are not conflated.

EDS metals scaled to three atoms give Mn0.66Mg0.17Fe2.17; simplified Mn/Mg scattering occupancy is 0.93(2)/0.07. Neither is the ideal species formula. Ferric assignment and hydroxyl/water adjustment use bond-valence/charge-balance reasoning, not direct oxidation-state or hydrogen assays. Baur reuses Moore coordinates and allows alternative small substituents.

### Deposited model-quality warnings

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:laueite.

Record preserves crystallographic limitations without treating alerts as automatic identity rejection.

Laueite report has three A alerts concerning Fe1/O2 non-positive/nearly 2D displacements and Fe2 anisotropy, missing H, formula/Z differences and no supplied structure factors for full checking. Rint 0.149 and GoF 3.36 are retained. Companion stewartite report also flags nonstandard B-centred notation; these observations limit detailed atomistic interpretation, not the existence of the natural species or measured orientation.
