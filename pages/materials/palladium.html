Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Resting-cell reduction of dissolved Pd(II) to elemental palladium

redox transformation Microbial

Associated Organisms: Desulfovibrio desulfuricans (NCBITaxon:876) [TaxonMech]

Desulfovibrio desulfuricans ATCC 29577 resting cells reduced soluble Pd(II) to cell-associated Pd(0) with an electron donor. Hydrogenase involvement was inferred, not established by a specific genetic test.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Laboratory anaerobic batch assay using washed, nongrowing ATCC 29577 cells (0.5 g/L; 2.5 mL in sealed 12 mL serum bottles) in 20 mM MOPS-NaOH, pH 7, at 30 degrees C. The substrate was 0.5 mM Pd(II) supplied as the palladium ammine chloride salt reported by Lloyd et al.; hydrogen replaced nitrogen in the headspace. The reported 100% dissolved-Pd(II) depletion after 24 h is an assay result, not a purified metal yield. Pd(0) identity was supported by X-ray diffraction and cell-associated deposits by electron microscopy and elemental analysis. This experiment does not establish performance in mixed waste leachates, ore extraction, or industrial deployment.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: lloyd_1998
Methods and results preceding Figure 1, and Figure 1A-C: the reported outcome was absent in cell-free, heat-killed, and electron-donor-free controls. These controls and solid-phase characterization support microbial Pd(II) reduction under the stated conditions in this strain.
Partial Source: lloyd_1998
Hydrogen-supported reduction and inhibition following copper pretreatment implicate hydrogenase activity, but do not identify a unique enzyme or establish its necessity genetically.
Context_only Source: mikheenko_2008
The introduction cautions that copper is not a specific hydrogenase inhibitor. The mutant experiments concern D. fructosivorans, a different species, and cannot establish enzyme necessity in ATCC 29577. This narrows the 1998 mechanistic inference without refuting its observed Pd reduction.
