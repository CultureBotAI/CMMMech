## Assay labels and newly inspected method context

Figure 3 labels its axis Time (h), while methods specify a 21-day experiment and Figures 1–2 use days. The day 14/day 21 significance groups remain the same for the plotted endpoints. Figure 2 and abstract indicate about 0.16 M titratable acidity; main results/discussion state 0.15 M. These are source discrepancies, not permission to choose revised values.

Section 4.3 names NMX-K-436-1977, whereas reference 36 links NMX-AA-074-1981. The original 1981 document p3 covers gravimetric and turbidimetric sulfate determination in waters; its turbidimetric scope is 10–60 mg/L as sulfate. The government-hosted 2008 programme p476/item 1405 identifies the 1977 code as a sulfate method for boiler-feed water. The original 2013 paper's methods p3 use the 1977 code and its reference 23 p9 names that same boiler-water method. This corroborates a method lineage but cannot choose which standard was used in 2020. The actual original 1977 procedure, experimental calibration, matrix blanks and dilution worksheet remain unavailable.

If the 1981 turbidimetric upper range of 60 mg/L applied to the reported 11028 mg/L sulfate result, a dilution of at least 183.8-fold would be needed to reach that upper range. This conditional calculation does not assert that the laboratory omitted dilution or measured out of range. It identifies a specific missing record. The official catalogue's later 2014 standard entry says it replaced the 1981 document, with 2015 publication/effective dates. That chronology is not retroactive evidence for the method actually performed. The 2008 programme's retained browser capture is dated 2022 and resides under a 2023 upload path; neither is the date of the cited 1977 method.
