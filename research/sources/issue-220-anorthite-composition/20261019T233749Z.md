## Primary evidence assessment

### Ma et al. 2024 — unchanged source contradiction

[DOI 10.12034/j.issn.1009-606X.223330](https://jproeng.ipe.ac.cn/EN/10.12034/j.issn.1009-606X.223330), *The Chinese Journal of Process Engineering* 24(8):982–992, is the existing primary fungal experiment. All original narrative sections were reread; the critical PDF pages 984–985 and 988–990 were rendered and visually inspected. The PDF SHA256 is `d992ae88313aa299c4a06165564f0bb1ca4ab769cf867566122494d7fb6a6234`, matching the 14 October acquisition. This pass does not repeat the earlier independent taxonomic or mineral-authority verification.

Section 2.1.1 names the supplier as 浩宇石行 and specifies crushed/sieved 38–74 micrometre powder. Table 1 reports bulk XRF oxide mass percentages: SiO2 30.93, CaO 24.21, MgO 19.17, Fe2O3 16.50, Al2O3 4.06 and others 4.59. Figure 1 labels the feed anorthite and references PDF 41-1486; Figure 11 labels anorthite and quartz. These remain source assignments, without quantitative phase fractions. Ideal CaAl2Si2O8 supplies no Mg, so the bulk chemistry does not support treating this feed as pure endmember anorthite. No particular Mg host can be selected from bulk oxides alone.

Table 2 instead reports selected surface EDS atomic percentages normalized over Ca, Mg, Al, Si and Fe. Its untreated row contains 0.63 at% Mg and 22.84 at% Al. These local atomic fractions have a different sampling and normalization basis from bulk oxide mass percentages; substituting them for Table 1 would not resolve specimen purity. The newly acquired CSV exports repeat both tables, including HTML markup in the first export's header, and contain no sample-level analytical metadata.

The medium question remains separate. Section 2.3.5 states a growth formulation per litre containing sucrose 30 g, NaNO3 3 g, K2HPO4 1 g, KCl 0.5 g, MgSO4 0.5 g and FeSO4·7H2O 0.01 g. Growth optimization subsequently selects glucose and NH4Cl. Section 2.3.7 calls the leach liquid Czapek medium; section 3.3.1 explicitly says Mg and Fe were removed relative to the growth experiment. The final glucose/NH4Cl amounts and a complete formulation for each leaching/control arm are not restated. It is reasonable to preserve the reported substitutions and omission as qualifications; it is not justified to fill the missing amounts by assuming equal mass or equal nitrogen/carbon.

The existing record already confines the fungal evidence to whole-feed culture-associated release and retains these limitations. This investigation supplies no basis for stronger phase-specific Mg release, pure-endmember yield, or isolated-product recovery claims.

### Chang et al. 2023 — recurring chemistry, unproved specimen linkage

[DOI 10.19964/j.issn.1006-4990.2022-0652](https://wjygy.magtechjournal.com/CN/10.19964/j.issn.1006-4990.2022-0652), *Inorganic Chemicals Industry* 55(9):57–65, is a separate abiotic carbonation study with overlapping authors. Sections 1.1–1.2 and 2.1, Tables 1–3, and Figure 1 were examined; the Table 2 PDF page and full-resolution Figure 1 were visually checked.

Table 2 repeats Ma's SiO2, MgO, Fe2O3 and Al2O3 values but gives CaO **24.41**, not 24.21 wt%, and individually lists SO3, K2O, Na2O, P2O5 and TiO2. Its methods describe crushed blocks without a supplier or specimen accession. Figure 1b and the accompanying text retain an anorthite assignment; the authors also discuss pre-existing carbonate and assign aragonite in the carbonation comparison. No quantitative Mg-bearing phase identification or explanation of the high Mg/Fe and low Al appears in these characterization sections.

Matching several bulk values identifies a useful provenance lead, not proof that the two papers used the same batch or that one table corrects the other. Neither the 24.41 value nor the carbonate interpretation can be transferred into Ma's record without a documented specimen link. No carbonation mechanism or yield is proposed for curation in this issue.

### Liu et al. 2025 — a distinct feed and a useful medium comparison

[Study on Screening Optimization and Leaching Effect of Anorthite Leaching Bacteria](https://castjournals.cast.org.cn/joweb/kyyjykf/CN/1226462300710813901), *Mining Research and Development* 45(10):267–278, cites Ma. The portal lists publication on 25 October 2025 and online posting on 6 February 2026; these are distinct dates. Its displayed metadata does not supply a DOI, so none is invented here.

Sections 1.2–1.3 and Tables 1–2 explicitly describe a different supplier, 石家庄雨馨建筑材料有限公司, and different chemistry, including MgO 5.913 and Al2O3 33.617 in the composition table. Table 2 marks MgSO4 and FeSO4·7H2O absent from the leach-medium arm. That is direct information about this bacterial study, not retrospective authentication of Ma's fungal medium. The remainder of its assay, controls and reported yields has not undergone an acceptance review in this pass. Defer it as a separate future mechanism lead, without importing it to resolve issue 220.
