## Source and boundary decisions

Curation dossiers and JSON acquisition/search receipts:
[B/F](round10-b-f-20261011.md), [Nb/Ta](round10-nb-ta-20261011.md),
[Ti/Zr](round10-ti-zr-20261011.md), [natural graphite](round10-graphite-20261011.md).
Each retains successful acquisition hashes, failed access routes, evidence locators,
source terms and bounded search coverage. Primary full text and relevant figures,
tables and supplements underpin the admitted scientific claims. Raw literature
stays outside Git. Hidden and ignored files were included in scoped deduplication;
this is not a machine-wide or exhaustive literature census.

Boron cites the original EU 2024 act, fluorine the dated Australian 2024 list, and
Nb/Ta/Ti/Zr the named United States final 2025 list. Natural graphite cites the
BGS/CMIC UK 2024 criticality assessment in replacement report **OR/24/059**.
The superseded landing-page label differs from the linked report's actual number;
the new cover and Tables 1/11 were checked. This assessment expressly does not
represent government policy. Its natural-graphite category is supported separately
from EU unqualified graphite and battery-grade graphite scopes.

Natural graphite has **no accepted microbial mechanism**. The checked 2007
bioflotation study targets sulfides in black shale and does not establish graphite
recovery. Newer battery-graphite leads remain deferred until full methods and feed
origin are verified. Tantalum non-detection is not measured zero or a universal
negative; a positive hybrid fungal/chemical lead has an explicit full-text access
gap. Zirconium characterization follows calcination and lacks reported matched
synthesis-control outcomes. These boundaries prevent overstatement while retaining
useful primary observations and research leads.
