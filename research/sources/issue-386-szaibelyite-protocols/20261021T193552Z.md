## Romanian experiment and thesis crosswalk

Marincea's 2001 analytical procedures, pp. 112–114/PDF 2–4, describe hand picking and magnetic/heavy-liquid separation, pulverization to −40/+80 mesh and a one-day cold 0.5 N HCl wash. The prose reports at least 90% purity and remaining serpentine detected by XRD. Table 4's normative result for specimen 1394 is 89.81% szaibelyite; that calculated result and the approximate preparation statement should not become a universal pure-feed guarantee.

The MOM 1500D air-TGA/DTG/DTA arm used 10 °C/min. The separate SETARAM TAG24/DSC111 arm used nitrogen flowing at 30 mL/min and evolved-gas IR. Methods p. 114 says 10 °C/min; Table 7 footnote 2 on p. 121 says nitrogen at 20 °C/min. Methods also describes 1 h at 20 °C under 10^-4 torr, whereas thermal Results p. 121 describes 12 h at 110 °C. Neither text assigns these to identified separate aliquots. IR powder preparation at 150 °C for 24 h is a distinct preparation statement and is not substituted for the thermal pretreatment.

The thesis general methods p. 41/PDF 65 describes MOM measurements mainly in air at 10 °C/min, with a standard 1 g amount, and a separate SETARAM/DSC111/FTS40 apparatus under argon, with 5–16 mg amounts and 10 or 20 °C/min. These are general method ranges, not the missing masses for individual DSC traces. Table 30, p. 241/PDF 265, specifically labels DSC as argon at 20 °C/min. Its first five Băița Bihor specimen rows repeat the article's Table 7 values, including the two negative enthalpies in **mJ**, not J/g. ## Earlier hydroxyl-ascharite comparison

Fleischer's original 1966 summary on p. 1818/PDF 4 identifies two papers: Grigoriev and Nekrasov's system study and Grigoriev, Brovkin and Nekrasov's natural variety. It describes an additional **exothermic** effect for high-water synthetic material, and separately reports the Siberian natural analysis with a proposed B→3H substitution, then calls that substitution improbable and the naming evidence insufficient. It does not verify Marincea's later description of a 150–200 °C endotherm from the inaccessible original experiment. The cited pagination also differs: Marincea lists Doklady 166, 125–128, whereas Fleischer lists 937–940. Translation/original pagination is a possible explanation, not verified here.

The newly acquired Kolesnikova et al. 1972 original contains a distinct 15 °C/min TVU-7 experiment, its own specimens 269/267/268/8812/8859, and explicit comparison with Siberian I-1. Figure 3 is the 8859 thermal trace; Figure 4 includes six distinct IR curves, with curve 5 attributed to I-1. The paper discusses excess water, disorder and possible fine Mg(OH)2 intergrowths, while stating that the evidence cannot confirm B-for-H substitution and cannot fully exclude it. Its p. 89 account of boiling 0.1 N HCl belongs to its description of the earlier 1966 work, not to a newly acquired 1966 protocol or the Romanian assays.

The 1972 table repeats the Siberian total of 100.70%; Fleischer's eight displayed components sum to that value. The separate 8859 table values H2O− 3.5 and H2O+ 11.3 total 14.8%, whereas its Figure 3 also labels a later cumulative 15.9%. These measurements/labels are not interchangeable endpoints. No hydration fraction is used to establish a new accepted mineral species, microbial mechanism or Romanian-feed explanation.
