## Identity and evidence assessment

IMA page 45 gives chrysotile `Mg3Si2O5(OH)4`, status Rd, `2007 s.p.`; page 185 gives riebeckite `☐Na2(Fe2+3Fe3+2)Si8O22(OH)2`, status Rd, `2012 s.p.`. Pages 1-2 define the columns: these special-procedure years are not mineral accessions. No invented IMA identifier is emitted. Handbook page 1 explicitly links asbestiform crocidolite to riebeckite. Its older formula is not substituted for the current IMA formula.

NCBI IDs 1573172 and 228745 resolve respectively to species *Deferrisoma palaeochoriense* and *Thermovibrio ammonificans*. Their type-strain synonym fields do not prove which experimental stock was used. Records retain species-level grounding without silently upgrading to MAG-PB1 or HB-1.

**Riebeckite / crocidolite:** Choi Methods and Figures 1A/C establish culture growth and an increased Fe(II) fraction after analytical HCl extraction versus mineral-medium controls. Figure 2A shows higher filtered dissolved iron in cultures, followed by decline. Conditions include 10 g/L UICC feed, 25 mL medium, 60°C, pH 6.1-6.4, 1.5 wt% NaCl, H2/CO2, triplicates and Fe(OH)3 growth comparators. The record separates the extraction from culture chemistry and does not reinterpret an extractable redox fraction as recovery. Figures 3A-C retain grain boundaries/crystallinity. Figure 4C does not establish biomass iron uptake. Coating-associated versus structural reduction and long-range transport remain unresolved proposals. The missing SI prevents recipe/extraction validation beyond main-text scope, and ICP-OES negative-control subtraction is ambiguous alongside displayed control series. No fibre destruction, safety improvement, purified product or deployment is asserted.

**Chrysotile:** Choi Figures 2B, 3D-F and 4A support a culture interaction and author-interpreted Si/biomass association but no demonstrated Mg/Si release advantage over mineral-medium controls. The dissolution assay uses 1 g/L, 25 mL, 75°C, pH 5.5, 2 wt% NaCl, Mg/Ca-free modified SME, H2/CO2 and nitrate, with triplicate inoculated/mineral-free/mineral-medium arms. Inoculum Mg required correction. The source's text concentrations, plotted Figure 2B bars and correction basis do not transparently agree; no single quantitative yield or rate is curated. Comparable changes occur without cells, and crystalline fibres persist.

Vigliaturo 2020 establishes the cited Balangero preparation context: wet grinding, filtration and autoclaving. Its nominal size fraction cannot be equated with the length of every chrysotile fibre: curvature and bundling prevented reliable length analysis. Pollastri 2016 Table 2 matches the nonideal Balangero formula quoted by Choi; Table 3 lists impurities in that earlier specimen. This warrants impurity caution, not copying phase proportions to a later experimental batch. The preparation and mineral structure papers are context, not independent microbial assays.
