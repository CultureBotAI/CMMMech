## Contradictions and limits

Independent review of a high-resolution Cai Figure 3 rendering corrected an
initial transcription: both intermediate panels are labelled 0.05. The initial
review and subsequent EDIT preserve that correction. Section 2.5 describes PbS as both less
and more soluble than PbMoO4. Figure S1's 30-minute point exceeds its 40-minute
point, without stated replicate uncertainty. Initial/final pH, agitation speed
and separation cutoff are not specified. Solids characterized at 25 degrees C
cannot be substituted for the 90-degree residues. Element-based purity estimates
of 96.83% and 97.32%, and absent impurity diffraction peaks, do not prove absolute
purity. No microbial contribution, industrial deployment, flotation campaign
or isolated-metal yield is inferred.
