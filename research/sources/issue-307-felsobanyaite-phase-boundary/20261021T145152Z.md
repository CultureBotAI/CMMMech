It attributes proton consumption to biological activity and reports an aluminium-removal headline near80%, with products called hydrobasaluminite and felsőbányaite. ## Carrero structural comparison and material boundary

**Samples and handling (printed2382; SI Tables S1–S3).** The crystalline felsőbányaite specimen came from a private collection with Felsőbánya, Romania provenance. The source's “natural basaluminite” was produced by titrating Perrunal acid-mine-drainage solution with0.01M calcium hydroxide and washing to remove gypsum. It was not an untouched mineral specimen collected in situ. Synthetic material was produced by adding214mL of0.015M Ca(OH)2 to30mL of0.05M Al2(SO4)3·18H2O. All samples were freeze-dried to complete dryness. These sample histories cannot establish the identity of Falagán's hydrated reactor precipitate. EXAFS preparation additionally used cyclohexane, filtration and room-temperature drying before low-temperature measurement.

**Structure (printed2383–2386; Figures1–4; SI Figures S1, S3–S7).** The diffraction/PDF comparison distinguishes broad basaluminite scattering from the crystalline felsőbányaite pattern, while finding similar short-range features. The fitted coherent-domain sizes are about1.2nm for the basaluminites and4–5nm for felsőbányaite; these are fitted correlation lengths, not a measured particle-size distribution or phase fraction. The27Al spectra report small tetrahedral/pentahedral contributions of about1%/5% in basaluminite and not in the felsőbányaite reference. That does not establish a universal formula or identity for all materials carrying the name.

The RMC models start from felsőbányaite-based structures: fels1 at Al/S4, fels2 with imposed aluminium vacancies and fels3 with additional sulfate, the latter two at Al/S2.7, with differently sized supercells. Similar fits do not uniquely prove a vacancy configuration or distinguish isolated nanoparticles from a network. SI Figure S6 is a modeled structure, not a direct image of missing atoms. The main paper itself discusses this nonuniqueness. Mineral nomenclature therefore cannot replace specimen-specific diffraction and hydration evidence.

**Chemistry and fitting (printed2383,2386–2387; Table1; SI pp3–6).** All183 selected PDF data-cell tokens were manually rekeyed and compared to native workbook addresses. The audit contains212 valid SI numeric components, one malformed numeric token, four missing hyphens and27 main Table1 numeric components. Forty-four conditional calculations cover displayed ratios, degrees of freedom/reduced χ², explicitly limited χ² comparisons, nominal reagent charges, reported TGA step sums and equation bookkeeping. These are diagnostics, not new measured observations, raw fit validation or recovered-product yields.

Main Table1 repeats selected SI S4/S5 models. Its natural first-shell fit lists N5.119±0.051, σ²0.0004±0.0002, R1.453±0.002Å, ΔE0.213±1.320eV, four variables andχ²16.3.
