### Thermal range and fitting

The abstract gives α0=6.0(2)×10⁻⁵K⁻¹, α1=5.7(7)×10⁻⁸K⁻² and V0=591.95(8)Å³. Results printed353 give6.4(1)×10⁻⁵K⁻¹,3.3(5)×10⁻⁸K⁻² and591.86(4)Å³. Both remain in the current original PDF/XML; the institutional copy is identical. The abstract/methods say298–746K and approximately30K steps, but Table5, TableS4, FigureS3 and all12 thermal CIF blocks end at628K. The118K gap is unresolved, not inferred missing data. Discussion printed355 gives1731–1273K while the abstract gives1173–1273K; neither is adopted as a verified natural stability boundary.

For a bounded check, Angel2014 printed410 defines the Berman form `V(T)=V0[1+α0(T−Tref)+½α1(T−Tref)²]`. Assuming Tref=298K, the printed abstract coefficients predict605.5078Å³ at628K; the Results coefficients predict605.4236Å³, versus Table5's605.41(2)Å³. Across the12 rounded table volumes, their unweighted RMS residuals are0.09126 and0.04577Å³, respectively. An unweighted quadratic fit of those same rounded central values gives V0≈591.86213Å³,α0≈6.40751×10⁻⁵K⁻¹,α1≈3.33546×10⁻⁸K⁻². This supports the numerical association of the Results set with the displayed table. It does **not** reproduce the authors' uncertainty-weighted fit, establish the missing high-temperature points, estimate covariance, or authorize replacing the abstract.

The298→628K table endpoints give Δa/a≈+0.6313%,Δc/c≈+1.0079%,ΔV/V≈+2.2894%, consistent with the qualitative anisotropy drawn in FigureS3. They are endpoint changes, not fitted coefficients. The corresponding AO8/BO10 endpoint changes are+2.3777%/+2.6742%. The latter series includes a local decline at391K; the AO8 series declines at598K. No pointwise-monotonic polyhedral behavior is asserted.

Pressure endpoints0.001→7.07GPa give approximately−2.4247%a,−3.4558%c and−8.0779%V. The printed BM2 bulk modulus69.8(4)GPa with fixed K′=4 is distinct from AO8/BO10 polyhedral values54(2)/72(3)GPa in Figure5. Figure6's caption literally prints units K for its14.5(7)×10⁻⁵ and14.5(6)×10⁻⁵ coefficient displays; this report does not silently supply an inverse unit or use those values for a bulk thermal model.
