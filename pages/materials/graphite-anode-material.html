Graphite anode material for lithium-ion batteries commodity

Graphite raw-material products manufactured and supplied as active material for lithium-ion battery negative electrodes. This end-use commodity family is a subset of the broader graphite commodity family, not a second disjoint graphite identity; no single origin or numerical qualification threshold is assigned to all members. SGL Carbon describes synthetic graphite anode materials with properties tailored to customer applications and distinguishes them from carbon-silicon composites and conductive additives. The record excludes complete electrodes, electric-arc-furnace electrodes and spent battery waste. SAMR names the product standard GB/T 24533-2019 for graphite negative-electrode materials, but its full technical tables were not inspected. Manufacturer use and testing descriptions establish product context, not a universal battery-grade purity rule or a microbial recovery result. No criticality designation is generalized from the broader graphite record.
