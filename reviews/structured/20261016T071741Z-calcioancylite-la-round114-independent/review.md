## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Species and nomenclature boundaries

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioancylite-la.

Current IMA September 2026 p. 34 verifies approved IMA2021-090 and (LaCa)(CO3)2(OH)(H2O). The 2024 nomenclature distinguishes Ca-dominant calcioancylites from Sr-dominant ancylites, with REE dominance defining the suffix. Type M16129 is a specimen accession, distinct from species approval and ore identity.

### Natural characterization and chemical provenance

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioancylite-la.

Wang et al. 2023 pp. 554–556 and Table 1 identify natural Gejiu nepheline-syenite material, individual crystals 5–20 micrometres and five electron-microprobe analyses. Mean La2O3 24.59 wt% and Ce2O3 23.32 wt% demonstrate mixed chemistry. CO2 is calculated on C=2 apfu and water from stoichiometry/charge balance. Raman OH bands support hydration without quantifying water. Al2O3 was excluded as possible inclusions or surface films, not an independently identified phase. Printed F mean 0.31 wt% exceeds range 0–0.16; retained as an unresolved source discrepancy.

### Structural model and observational limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:calcioancylite-la.

The FIB-extracted approximately 16×7×5 micrometre fragment was measured at 296.15 K. Pmcn R1=0.0652 for 347 reflections with I&gt;2sigma(I) agrees with abstract/text and official CIF; the Table 3 R-row uses a different printed Fo threshold. La/Ca site occupancies 0.66(4)/0.34(4) are scattering proxies, not separate assays of each measured REE. Full official CIF contains no H coordinates. Four-page official checkCIF states that structure factors were not supplied, has five C and ten G alerts and preserves formula/model inconsistencies. Paper formula weight 726.26 differs from CIF 363.13; no silent correction is adopted. Geological late-carbonation interpretation is not a controlled formation experiment.
