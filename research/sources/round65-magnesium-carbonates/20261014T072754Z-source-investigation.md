## Hydromagnesite and huntite claim assessment

The 2011 experiment uses separate halophilic strain cultures, rather than a consortium, on agar with organic substrates, acetate salts, 3.5% NaCl, starting pH7.2 and aerobic30°C incubation up to30days. Table3 supplies medium-level XRD phase assignments; Figure1C/F depicts hydromagnesite in C4/C12, and Figure1B huntite in C3. Huntite appears in Table3 only for C2/C3 (5.7mM Ca;28/65mM Mg). Hydromagnesite appears in nine media and is absent from the Table3 assemblages for C7/C10/C11. The raw diffraction traces and a strain-to-specific-phase matrix are not supplied in this original. Consequently no single exact strain is assigned as uniquely responsible, and printed phase percentages are not used as recovery or purity values.

Controls were included according to Section2.3; Discussion4.1 explicitly says uninoculated/dead-cell controls did not precipitate. Results3 nevertheless has the literal contradictory sentence that no precipitates were observed in any culture experiments. This is retained as a source inconsistency, without silently editing the source or treating reported controls as independently tabulated measurements. Other source limitations retained in records or here:

- Table3 includes hydromagnesite/struvite in C5, while Results prose also calls C5 high-Mg-calcite-only. Table2 gives struvite onset even for some media described as high-Mg-calcite-only elsewhere. Table5 lacks some isotope entries despite a corresponding Table3 phase assignment; an unreported isotope value does not establish absence of that mineral.
- Methods versus Table2 strain rosters disagree, including H.pantelleriensis DSM9661 versus3051, H.halmophila versus H.meridiana, and a shared printed ATCC49509 for H.eurihalina/H.salina. No authority ID is accepted for these unresolved exact experiment assignments.
- Bulk pH was measured with surface indicator paper. Table1 gives8.5 in some final arms; generalized prose says9. Microsite pH and individual metabolic steps were proposed, not directly measured.
- Table1's second Ca heading has a starting-condition superscript despite the table being before/after; no derived depletion dataset is imported. Section2.6 Na/Cl model inputs do not match the NaCl recipe stoichiometry. The modeled undersaturation values are not experimental proof of local saturation.
- Thirty-seven°C drying follows washing. Morphological preservation does not prove unchanged hydration state. The study supports author-assigned hydromagnesite/huntite phases in mixed cultures with limits, not pure recovery, industrial operation or atmospheric sequestration mass balance.

## Nesquehonite claim assessment

The 2019 solid LE1 culture supplies the accepted phase observation: Table3 and Figure5B have nesquehonite; the liquid arm does not. The culture result is after15days at30°C; the complete harvest-time series and replication are not specified. Calcium/magnesium acetates, LE-1 organic medium, 2%NaCl and20g/L agar are stated. The article prepares initial pH7.0 but models the culture row at8.2; these are kept distinct.

NCBI82801 currently resolves to **Desemzia incerta**, species rank. ENA **Y17300.1** is a1505-base16S sequence of **DSM20581**, not the LE1 experimental isolate. The paper's794–802-base partial sequences and99.8% closest-relative comparison justify only a reported species affiliation. No strain-ID identity is inferred. Primary authority artifacts and hashes are retained.

Uninoculated/autoclaved-cell controls are listed in Methods, but the inspected full article does not report their phase outcomes or matched XRD traces. Therefore the record states culture-associated formation rather than biological necessity or a quantified benefit. Table3's liquid row sums to116 and solid row133.5; the cell35 for nesquehonite is not a normalized mass fraction or yield. Hmg denotes combined hydromagnesite/dypingite, and most cell/EPS microscopy concerns that mixture, not a directly visualized nesquehonite nucleation mechanism. Mg-calcite liquid/solid prose and table also conflict. The stated H2O/Mg ratio2 for nesquehonite conflicts with its formula and IMA's total3waters and is not adopted.

The separate Atlin2007 laboratory study formed nesquehonite in an abiotic wetland-water arm while cyanobacterial arms formed dypingite with no detected nesquehonite. Its water/organisms/nutrient matching differ, so it bounds generalization without refuting LE1's narrower occurrence. Mavromatis2012 remains a relevant null-rate lead, but unavailable original figures preclude a detailed accepted comparison. McLean1997 (DOI10.1007/BF02770803) was discovered through2019 references and a bibliographic/abstract search only; full inspection remains future work.
