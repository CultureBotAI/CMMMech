## Decision and useful coverage

Create **petalite** and **polylithionite** as distinct mineral species with geological resource context. No microbial mechanism, criticality status, universal grade or present industrial deployment is assigned. **Lepidolite is a series name**, not an exact synonym of polylithionite; trilithionite is a distinct species and remains a future owner rather than a third addition.

The original September2026 IMA list gives petalite LiAlSi4O10, grandfathered/1800 (PDF170), and polylithionite KLi2AlSi4O10F2, approved/1998s.p. (PDF175). Original page1 defines these fields. Neither historicalyear nor special-procedure entry becomes an invented IMA approval CURIE. The reused originalPDF is hash-verified; the freshly read issuing homepage still links September2026. This does not pretend a freshPDF download.

The complete original 2001 Handbook entries support monoclinic character and bounded occurrence: petalite in granite pegmatites, locally lithium ore; polylithionite in late-stage/metasomatic syenite-pegmatite segregations and veinlets at Lovozero. Natural specimen chemistry and the Handbook F/OH generalization are not substituted for the current idealformula or a universal ore grade. The IMA-approved Rieder1998 report, original Table4 and SeriesNames section (printed589), places lepidolite on/near the trilithionite–polylithionite join. Crossref verifies the Clays and Clay Minerals46(5)586–595 DOI10.1346/CCMN.1998.0460513. Its later onlinehosting date is not the originalpublication date.

## Primary experimental evidence and explicit deferrals

- **Kirk2024**, DOI10.3389/fmicb.2024.1467408/dataset10.17632/djkf7tkm8d.1, is already represented under lithium/jadarite/spodumene. Prior worksheet duplication and control limitations remain; this is not a new experiment.
- **Sedlakova-Kadukova and colleagues2020**, DOI10.1038/s41598-020-71596-5, is a useful next phase-qualified lead. Full EuropePMC XML was acquired and its mineral-preparation/XRD text screened: the Beauvoir crushed feed contains source-assigned polylithionite and muscovite, with different residue patterns among microbial systems. The original main figures and supplementaryPDF (especially FigS2) were **not** visually acquired in this pass. No species-specific extraction or transformation claim is accepted. Next step: inspect complete graphics/SI, exact phase evidence, controls, organisms and assay denominators, then decide mineral-arm versus mixed-feed ownership.
- **Li etal2025**, DOI10.3390/microorganisms13020415: completeXML obtained, identity/method text and supplement inventory screened. It concerns mechanically activated lepidolite-labelled feed and a mixed acidophilic community. Original main graphics and ZIP supplement were not inspected. Series terminology alone cannot establish a polylithionite endmember.
- **Quezada-Aldaco2026**, DOI10.3390/ma19132855, was already deferred in [the lithium handoff](../li-followup/20261009T054636Z.md). Supplier-described polylithionite-type clay does not establish a pure species. Prior source contradictions and residual-solid concentration accounting are not resolved here.
- DOI10.1016/j.biortech.2024.131920 / PMID39631544 is a metadata-only A.niger/lepidolite transcriptomics lead; no main-source claim imported.

The bounded petalite search did not yield a fully verified species-specific microbial experiment. This is a search limitation, not evidence that no such literature exists. Queries for the2020/2025 DOI plus correction/retraction returned no affecting notice in the inspected sets; the search is not exhaustive.
