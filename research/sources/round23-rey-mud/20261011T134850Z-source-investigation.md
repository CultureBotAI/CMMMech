## Adversarial scientific limits retained

- **Background comparison matters.** Supplementary Figure 1m–o gives fitted biomagnetic IRM components of 78.2% at 45 cm background, 75.7% at the 225 cm labelled enriched sample, and 76.4% at 525 cm background. The enriched sample does not have a higher fitted fraction. This does not negate all absolute magnetic-profile or phase-inventory differences, but prevents reading the fraction alone as selective microbial enrichment. Magnetic signal, mineral mass, microbial biomass and REY inventory are distinct denominators.
- **The reported 17.6% is a model inference.** Page 8 combines an operational iron fraction with fitted IRM and modeled sediment retention. The curator reproduced only the displayed arithmetic (1.48/6.65; 1.48/(6.65−0.81); multiplied by 0.757 and 0.92), not the full geological model. The initial poorly crystalline iron inventory, magnetic-signal-to-mass calibration and a direct REY mass balance are not supplied by those products. The paper's claimed minimum microbial fraction is therefore not accepted as a measured or independently verified lower bound. Figure 5's separate isotope endmember mixing estimate has another denominator and depends on assumed endmembers and unresolved phase isotope values.
- **Organism, time and scale remain unresolved.** Magnetotactic and dissimilatory iron-reducing bacteria are inferred functional groups. Final reviewer comments (PDF pages 80–82) distinguish ancient signatures from activity and question pelagic versus porewater timing. They also caution that gene predictions do not prove activity. Earlier criticism is preserved as part of the revision history, not misrepresented as a final rejection of the published qualitative interpretation.
- **Sample cohorts differ.** Detailed magnetic curves use 225 cm as enriched, whereas the main central interval and iron-fraction group use 240–390 cm. Figure 2's >700 mg/kg enrichment threshold is a different classification and also includes some background-labelled samples. Do not silently join these as paired aliquots.
- **Source inconsistencies block exact imports.** Main-text Fe2O3 range and isotope minimum differ from supplemental/workbook values; the main and supplemental younger age bounds differ. Workbook κ-T says K whereas published axes say °C. The record acknowledges the substantive model/age/unit limitations; exact conflicting values and locators remain in JSON. No age, microbial yield, native concentration cell or climate causation is assigned.
