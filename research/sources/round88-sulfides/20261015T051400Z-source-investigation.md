## Selection and nomenclature boundary

The accepted owner is **gersdorffite, NiAsS, cubic P2₁3**. The September2026 IMA list (PDF1 definitions, PDF82 row) marks it Rn/2022s.p. ## Accepted original and supplement

The low-temperature/multistage origin in §§4–5 is geological interpretation, not experimentally demonstrated formation or extraction. No mechanism slot is used.
