No target-strain sequence accession was established in the acquired figure/SI. Phylogenetic bootstrap resampling is not biological replication of the adsorption experiments.

Table S1, native table 1 and local derivative page 4, contains 15 strains at five La concentrations, **75 cells each with a central value and an uncertainty term**. The header literally combines `Lanthanum concentration (mg/L)/mg`; its biomass caption does not resolve wet versus dry mass, assay volume, replicate count or whether the uncertainty is SD or SE. KLBMP 9669 has **553±48, 513±105, 560±21, 713±89 and 130±21** at 0, 100, 200, 300 and 400 mg/L. KLBMP 9576 and KLBMP 9673 occur as separate rows; their presence does not join the 2024 experiments to the 2026 study or patent.

At 400 mg/L, KLBMP 9669 has the highest displayed central biomass value, 130, while two other strains have 120 with overlapping reported uncertainty ranges. That descriptive comparison neither proves a significant resistance ranking nor establishes the highest tolerated concentration. Its 400/0 central-value ratio is approximately 0.2351 and its 300/0 ratio approximately 1.2893; these are conditional descriptive ratios, not normalized viable-cell counts or product yields. The indexed conclusion's 400 mg/L tolerance claim is retained as reported, with the missing assay definition explicit.

The abstract reports **90 mg/g** capacity and **94%** removal, but their exact shared conditions and denominators cannot be reconstructed from the available main text. The SI biomass values in mg, Figure 2 loading in g/L, abstract capacity and fitted parameters are not silently joined. Error bars are visible, but their meaning and replication are not established by the acquired materials. No curve points were digitized.
