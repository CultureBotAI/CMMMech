Helium element Critical Material

Helium (He), grounded by the neutral-atom ChEBI identifier. This element record is distinct from isotope-specific preparations, commercial purity grades and helium-bearing gas resources. The original EU 2024 act lists helium as a critical raw material; this does not designate every helium-bearing resource. No microbial extraction, transformation or recovery mechanism is admitted in this record. The bounded literature assessment in the round-11 helium dossier separates helium used as a tracer or analytical/plasma gas from helium as a microbial recovery target; this is not a universal absence claim about biological interactions with helium.
