## Brucite: primary evidence and adverse interpretation

The2011 HYDROPRONY field samples include young ST09 conduits around50 m depth and mature ST07 material. Main Methods p.5 describes laser microdissection and XRD of individual mineralized filaments, rather than relying only on bulk elemental chemistry. Figures2-3 identify brucite as the dominant crust phase with minor hydrotalcite. Figure5 DNA-stained sheaths and cells support the biological nature of the filament substrate. Figure3d labels one brucite spacing4.76 while its caption uses nm, unlike the neighboring0.236nm assignment; no numerical spacing is imported from that apparent decimal/unit inconsistency.

The p.11 discussion explicitly allows **abiotic brucite precipitation** from supersaturated high-pH fluids. It proposes microbial sheaths as nucleation surfaces and notes that biological alkalinization is not needed to provide the already alkaline environment. No matched sterile/killed-biomass/pH-control formation assay establishes microbial necessity or enhancement. SEM images are different spatial samples, not a measured time series. Minor hydrotalcite could be co-precipitated or transformed; a unique conversion pathway is not selected.

Table1 and p.11 describe33/41 targeted filament-rich clones belonging to one uncultivated Firmicutes-affiliated OTU, with only88% similarity to the nearest cultivated Dethiobacter relative. The record assigns no species CURIE, no isolate identity and no inferred pathway. Bulk community analysis differs from targeted filament-rich sampling and cannot be a phase-specific producer census. Storage/fixation temperatures are distinct from in situ precipitation conditions. The accepted scope is natural field-associated encrustation, not industrial deployment or Mg recovery.

## Dolomite: supported arm and preserved source inconsistencies

The accepted arm is **light plus1 mM added Mn(II),27°C, two weeks**, in washed mixed Fayetteville Green Lake enrichments. SI Section1 defines MFGL, Mg/Ca near2/1mM,44mM bicarbonate,50µM Na2S,12:12h light/dark and at least triplicates. Initial DCMU/ascorbate enrichments are distinct from the later studied medium; residual sulfate is reported below0.9µM. A mixed Chlorobium-affiliated community is retained without a pure-strain/species claim. No taxon identifier is asserted.

SI FiguresDR2-DR3 show diffraction with a mineral standard and labeled015/021 features; TableDR1 fits multiple ordered dolomite phases in the light/Mn arm. DR4A-C and DR5 show mineral association with cells/EPS. The accepted manuscript reports negative uninoculated Mn controls and explicitly says the product was **not perfectly ordered**. The record preserves author-assigned ordering and does not claim independent crystallographic refinement, pure endmember recovery, a biochemical pathway or universal microbial necessity.

The originals contain consequential discrepancies:

1. Main manuscript gives80%N2/15%CO2, whereas SI gives80%N2/20%CO2. The latter recipe is quoted with the conflict explicit, not silently normalized.
2. DR2 dark trace is Mn-amended; TableDR1 dark row says no Mn. No definitive quantitative dark-arm comparison is curated.
3. Main text says dark/no-Mn cultures have no minerals; DR7B identifies EPS-associated calcite, while DR8 shows absence only on cell surfaces. The broader null claim is not adopted.
4. Main text references DR4D/E as light/no-Mn; the original SI caption says dark. DR6 independently documents light/no-Mn EPS-associated minerals; controls are not silently relabeled.
5. DR3B graphic says021, caption012; DR2 caption lists112 elsewhere. The source's indexed features are retained as its qualified phase assignment without reproducing inconsistent crystallographic indices as an authoritative reanalysis.
6. DR5 describes nanocrystals but its graphic scale is labeled200µm; DR8 similarly says200µm for bacterial images. Main text/DR9 vary two-week versus two-month descriptions. No derived sizes or exact late-stage kinetics are curated.
7. TableDR1 phase percentages are fitted fractions without a complete mass denominator/uncertainty here. They are not recovery efficiencies, pure-product percentages or independent experiments.
