## Placement decision

Natural Baltic concretions supplied inocula. The quantified B-4 assays instead used defined MnSO4-containing PYGV medium; Fe deposition tests used a paper clip. They do not measure extraction from natural nodules. Their location of origin cannot make the salt assay a nodule-bioleaching mechanism. B-4/IFAM 1210 is not assigned a verified genus/species here. The primary article itself warns that superficial resemblance does not establish taxonomy.

Tables 2-3 support a potential existing manganese-record extension concerning isolate-associated oxidation/deposition, with high-Mn inhibition and bactericidal comparisons. They do not establish one enzyme or a crystalline mineral species. Methods list a maximum Mn dose of 12.6 mg/L while Results/Table 2 use 12.8 mg/L; the abstract's heating temperature differs from Table 3. These discrepancies require explicit treatment in any quantitative curation. Morphology alone does not prove the isolate formed natural concretions. No new resource/mineral record or nodule edit is made on this evidence.
