## Primary evidence and limitations

**Bian2024, DOI [10.3389/fmicb.2024.1416731](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2024.1416731).** Washed log-phase DW018 biomass formed a calcite-assigned 2 h product in the standard synthetic Tb/Ca/urea assay. Complete text and all ten figures/two tables were assessed. The source's genus assignment is preserved; related strains' Figure1 accessions cannot identify DW018. Figure7 supports calcite, while Figures8-9 provide morphology and elemental associations. They do not demonstrate Tb lattice substitution or a pure mineral/metal product. Early supernatant depletion in Figure2 and strong removal without added Ca in Figure5 preclude attributing rapid Tb removal to calcite. The final recommended no-added-Ca recipe remains separate from the calcium-supplemented calcite arm. Missing pH-matched abiotic controls, the two-factor adsorption comparator, processed specimens and different heat-treatment protocols constrain causality. Absent crystalline peaks do not exclude the amorphous material proposed elsewhere in the paper. No removal percentage is adopted as a mineral-specific yield.

**Rehmanji2025, DOI [10.1039/d5ra06824j](https://doi.org/10.1039/d5ra06824j).** Complete main and SI were read, including the rendered phase plot S7 p24 and separate control plot S5. The culture treatment used prepared DSM33 biomass, unsterilized natural Cornish brine and urea in laboratory flasks. The final dried precipitate is assigned to calcite, vaterite and magnesite. No quantitative phase refinement or in situ phase time series establishes pure-phase yields, metal allocation or a vaterite-to-calcite sequence. Sampling/drying details are preserved. The record retains the incorrect S6 citation, minutes-versus-hours conflict, inconsistent Mg/Mn percentages, declining comparator Mn, differing S5/main trajectories, saturation-index disagreements and unclear metal-aliquot separation. The Li wavelength is absent from S2; S9 calls retained Li recovery. No lithium product or downstream DLE improvement is inferred. Separate cow-urine and spent-yeast arms remain separate.
