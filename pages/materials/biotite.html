Biotite mica series mineral group

Biotite denotes a compositional series of trioctahedral micas in or near the field between the annite-phlogopite and siderophyllite-eastonite joins in the IMA-approved mica nomenclature of Rieder et al. (1998). It is represented here as a mineral group, not an individual mineral species. The IMA's 2019 compendium reproduces Whitney and Evans (2010), which labels biotite as GROUP. No constituent criticality designation is inherited. The experimental feed below is author-designated biotite; its bulk oxide composition does not establish a particular member species or quantitative phase purity.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

C4-10 culture-associated dissolution of a biotite feed

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Priestia aryabhattai (NCBITaxon:412384) [TaxonMech]

Priestia aryabhattai C4-10 cultures increased measured Fe and Si in biotite-containing medium relative to uninoculated feed controls. Acidification, organic-acid production and mineral-associated cells accompany the dissolution phenotype; their individual causal contributions and the necessity of particular genes are unresolved.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
C4-10 is a living soil isolate grown in LB at 37 degrees C and 180 rpm for 16 hours, harvested, washed and resuspended in sterile distilled water at 10^8 cells/mL. Triplicate laboratory dissolution flasks contained 60 mL sterilized BHm, 0.6 g biotite and 1.5 mL suspension, incubated at 37 degrees C and 180 rpm and sampled at 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 36 and 48 hours. BHm was prepared at pH 7.0 with 2 g/L glucose; the biotite particles were 75-150 micrometres and reported to contain 39.90% SiO2, 14.75% Fe2O3 and 13.69% MgO. Mineral cleaning refers to an earlier protocol without restating its steps. Fe and Si were measured by ICP-OES and described as dissolved, but separation of these elemental aliquots is not specified in the inspected main and supplemental methods. The separate transcriptome experiment used 0.5 mL inoculum and an 8-hour endpoint; it is not the same assay as the dissolution time course. Lizardite substitution and its MgSO4-free medium are covered by the separate lizardite record.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: sheng_2026
Results and Figure 1A show the Fe increase over the 4-48-hour biotite time course and elevated Si relative to uninoculated controls from 12 to 48 hours. Figure 1B shows culture acidification relative to controls, and Figure 1E shows stained mineral-associated cells after 24 hours. Figure 1 reports n=3 with one-standard-deviation error bars. These support the conditional culture-associated mobilization claim, without establishing a recovered product.
Partial Source: sheng_2026
No pH-matched abiotic assay separates acid dissolution from other cellular effects. Figure 1D and the main text call the early 1.7-7.6% CAS siderophore-unit values detectable, whereas supplementary p2 classifies values below 10% as negative; later biotite time points are reported as nondetections. A positive siderophore mechanism is therefore not assigned. Gene-expression correlations are not functional tests, as the Discussion acknowledges. Cell-associated Fe digestion and surface fluorescence do not demonstrate intracellular uptake. Feed member-species identity, phase purity, fraction-specific mass balance, isolated-metal recovery and industrial deployment remain unestablished.

Priestia aryabhattai C4-10 organic-acid metabolism, medium acidification, and biotite Fe/Si mobilization

REPRESENTATIVE EXAMPLE Mechanism: c4_10_biotite_dissolution

Causal pathway linking Priestia aryabhattai organic-acid metabolism enzymes upregulated in the 8-h biotite transcriptome (Table 1 of Sheng et al., 2026: Citrate synthase citA Gene03098 / UniProtKB:A0A7W3RFI1, 169-fold; Phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase pckA Gene04826 / UniProtKB:A0A1S2C101, 46.49-fold; Malate synthase G glcB Gene02911 / UniProtKB:A0A7W3RDZ1, 11.98-fold; Acetate-CoA ligase acsA_2 Gene04776 / UniProtKB:A0A7W3NBV8, 6.14-fold) and the co-localized TCA-cycle Malate dehydrogenase (mdh HNP21_003257 / UniProtKB:A0A0J6SZC4) to organic-acid secretion (malic acid 245-598 uM, acetic acid 123-250 uM, oxalic acid 327-649 uM in Fig. S3B-D), culture acidification (pH 6.21 to 4.52 in Figure 1B), and increased ICP-OES Fe (0.99-29.5 uM, 4-48 h) and Si (24.4-35.3 uM, 12-48 h) from 0.6 g biotite (75-150 um) in 60 mL BHm (Figure 1A), while preserving that the 0.5-mL transcriptome arm is distinct from the 1.5-mL dissolution time course and gene necessity is untested.
