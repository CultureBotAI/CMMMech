## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Natural feed and organism identities

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:carbonatite-associated-rare-earth-ore.

The mixed primary-resource scope and cautious organism grounding are appropriate.

Association with a deposit does not establish modal carbonatite classification for every tested rock. The original ore contains quartz, apatite, calcite and REE phase assignments; it is not pure monazite/parisite or waste. Tmix cites OR053813.1 and OR053804.1, resolving to deposited strains 2mn and 1mn. The separate NBRIP culture identifier 4TH0UUSC016 remains unresolved, and its Gram-negative description adds uncertainty. Neither strain purity nor member causality is assumed.

### Pretreatment, chemical leaching and controls

scope: supported. Targets: cmmmech:carbonatite-associated-rare-earth-ore.

The recorded result remains treatment-associated.

Pretreatment used 40 g ore and 80 mL culture in 250 mL flasks at 30 C and 200 rpm for seven days. Solids were filtered, rinsed twice and dried at 60 C, then leached in 1 M H2SO4 at a 1:6 solid/liquid ratio, 30 C and 200 rpm for 20 h. Duplicate experiments with standard deviations do not establish biological causality. No matched sterile 9K/NBRIP pretreatment or killed-cell control isolates microorganisms from acidic iron/sulfate medium. Final extraction is chemical leaching.

### Reported extraction percentages and mass-accounting limits

quantity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:carbonatite-associated-rare-earth-ore.
