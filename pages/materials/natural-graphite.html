Natural graphite commodity Critical Material

Graphite of natural mineral origin considered as a commercial raw-material commodity. USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 distinguishes flake and high-purity, amorphous, and lump and chip forms in its natural-graphite chapter, and identifies synthetic graphite among competing materials. This record covers the natural-origin commodity rather than the carbon element, a mineral specimen or species identifier, synthetic graphite, or a particular battery product specification. Natural origin alone does not establish battery-grade suitability. The criticality entry records the named BGS/CMIC assessment; the report explicitly states that it does not constitute government policy. No microbial extraction, purification or recovery mechanism is asserted here.
