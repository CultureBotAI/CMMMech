## Primary-source assessment

Segura et al., *Sulfidogenic Bioreactor-Mediated Formation of ZnS Nanoparticles with Antimicrobial and Photocatalytic Activity*, **Nanomaterials 13:935 (2023)**, DOI [10.3390/nano13050935](https://doi.org/10.3390/nano13050935), was published 4 March 2023. The Crossref indexing date, 1 September 2026, is a metadata date; it does not establish a revised experiment or paper edition. The article is CC BY 4.0. Complete text and original image files remain outside Git.

Sections 2.1–2.2 describe a laboratory process in which a sulfidogenic bioreactor supplies H₂S to a separate Zn precipitation step. Diffraction concerns the collected, dried product. Instrument description includes Cu Kα radiation and a LaB₆ calibration standard. This supports the distinction between biological sulfide production and downstream precipitation; it does not by itself establish biological selection of a particular polymorph.

The phase paragraph on p.6 identifies the dominant product as cubic zincblende, citing the (111), (220) and (311) peaks and card `04-012-0803`. It separately attributes minor peaks at 34°, 36.2° and 47.6° 2θ to hexagonal (002), (101) and (102), with `a = 0.3822 nm`, `c = 0.6375 nm`, and card `00-036-1450`. The same section specifies `λ = 0.1541 nm`.

The curator calculation was independently rerun from those printed inputs:

`1/d² = (4/3)(h² + hk + k²)/a² + l²/c²`

`2θ = 2 asin[λ/(2d)]`, with first-order diffraction and the angle converted to degrees.
