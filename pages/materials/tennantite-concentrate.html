Tennantite concentrate primary resource

A mixed mineral concentrate enriched in source-labelled tennantite, represented here by the feed studied by Kondo et al. (2024). This resource is distinct from a pure mineral species, the separately described tennantite-(In), and elemental copper or arsenic. The study reports mineral liberation analysis of about 60 wt% tennantite, 23% pyrite, 8.2% sphalerite, 6.3% gangue, 1.6% galena and 0.97% chalcopyrite (rounded values). Bulk elemental composition is reported as S 31%, Cu 23%, Fe 12%, Zn 10%, Sb 7.8% and As 6.2%, with median particle size 41 micrometres. Its geographic origin and beneficiation route are not given. Generic discussion of flotation wastes does not establish that this particular feed was discarded waste. The informal tennantite phase label does not resolve a modern mineral species or establish the ideal Cu12As4S13 composition of every grain. No criticality designation is transferred from a constituent element.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Acidophile-associated copper mobilization with variable carbon addition

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Sulfobacillus thermosulfidooxidans (NCBITaxon:28034) [TaxonMech]

In inoculated acidic laboratory suspensions, copper entered the filtered liquid while the effect of activated carbon depended on culture history and experimental conditions. Source-reported final copper dissolution in 50-day flasks was about 70% with 0.01% activated carbon versus 45% without it; larger carbon additions gave 52% and 38% at 0.025% and 0.05%, respectively. In later 100-day pH-controlled reactors both carbon-free and 0.01% carbon systems reached about 60% copper dissolution. These percentages describe reported dissolved copper relative to the mixed feed inventory, not isolated metal recovery or a pure-tennantite yield. Carbon-free systems were also inoculated; no uninoculated leaching control is described. Redox profiles, cell growth and community composition are consistent with microbial involvement but do not isolate its advantage over matched chemical leaching or prove a particular member's necessity.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Unwashed, unsterilized concentrate was used. The initial inoculum contained source-labelled Acidimicrobium ferrooxidans ICP (DSM 10331), Acidiplasma sp. Fv-Ap, Acidithiobacillus caldus KU (DSM 8584), Sulfobacillus sibiricus N1 (DSM 17363), S. thermotolerans Kr1 (DSM 17362) and S. thermosulfidooxidans AT-1 (DSM 9293), with equal initial cell densities before pre-growth on concentrate. The paper's acknowledgement instead calls Fv-Ap Acidisphaera; that inconsistency is not resolved here. The structured organism denotes one reported community member, not the entire consortium or a verified strain accession. Terminal 16S V3-V4 amplicon analysis reported 99.9% S. thermosulfidooxidans in flask residue and dominance in reactor residue; this is not a time-resolved census or proof of strain identity or causality. Duplicate flasks contained 200 mL acidophile basal salts medium in 500 mL vessels, 0.02% yeast extract, 5 mM Fe(II), 5% w/v concentrate and 10% v/v pre-grown inoculum, initially pH 1.5 with sulfuric acid, at 45 degrees C and 150 rpm for 50 days. Carbon doses were 0, 0.01, 0.025 and 0.05% w/v; pH subsequently diverged, so the carbon comparison is not pH matched. Two later 2 L reactor systems contained 1.2 L medium, 0.02% yeast extract, 5% concentrate and 10% inoculum descended from flask cultures, at 45 degrees C and 150 rpm. Each carbon condition is one described reactor, not duplicate reactors. Sulfuric acid maintained pH 2.0 through day 37 and 1.5 from day 38. Day-53 Fe(II) additions differed: 20 mM without carbon and 10 mM with carbon. Terminal outcomes therefore cannot be treated as a clean factorial carbon effect or independent replication of the earlier flasks. Filtered 0.45-micrometre aliquots were measured by ICP-OES; plotted total-element concentrations do not identify oxidation state. No isolated copper product, complete sampling-corrected mass balance or industrial deployment was demonstrated.
