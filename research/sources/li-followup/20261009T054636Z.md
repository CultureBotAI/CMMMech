## New quantitative issues, with exact locators

These are observed inconsistencies or unresolved mappings, not corrected data.

- **Units:** all three bioleaching Li block headers say `mg/kg` (Jadarite!A3
  and standalone Sheet1!A2); the paper presents solution results in mg/L.
  No solution density or documented unit-conversion rule was located in the
  inspected deposit. Do not silently equate these units.
- **Digest basis:** Digestions!B2 labels micrograms/g; C8:E8 gives Li values
  230, 120 and 80 for jadarite, spodumene and lepidolite. Paper section 3.1 gives
  2.2, 1.2 and 0.66 wt.% respectively. These are not equivalent under the stated
  units. Dilution factors and an original instrument/digestion worksheet are
  needed; no guessed factor was applied.
- **Average mismatch:** in the jadarite workbook, Jadarite!C5:E5 (day 3)
  contains 11.728226589, 30.584129367 and 32.001891527. Their arithmetic mean
  is 24.771415828, while F5 labelled Average Inoculated is 43.341460456.
  Jadarite!F7 also differs from the C7:E7 mean. Two of ten jadarite Li rows
  differ from the numeric-replicate arithmetic mean at absolute tolerance
  1e-9; ten of ten standalone spodumene Li rows differ, while lepidolite's
  corresponding means agree to that tolerance. These stored values are not
  spreadsheet formulas; undocumented transformations cannot be excluded.
- **Identical replicate blocks across materials:** C3:E12 in the standalone
  spodumene and lepidolite files contain exactly the same 30 numerical values.
  This requires provenance clarification before treating them as independent
  material-specific replicate series. It does not establish which block, if
  either, is erroneous.
- **Fe column interpretation:** in standalone Spodumene!Sheet1 row 15,
  C15:E15 average to 87.890919661, which appears under G15 labelled
  Non inoculated; F15 labelled Average Inoculated is zero. Similar patterns
  appear in the lepidolite Fe block. Labels and arrays must be reconciled by
  source provenance; this assessment does not swap columns.
- **Original loading/acid gaps remain:** paper section 2.3 calls 2 g in final
  200 mL 2% w/v although arithmetic gives 1%. Its acid-dose prose and figure
  captions differ, and treatments receive unequal acid additions. No dose or
  mass/volume ledger was found in the returned workbook content. An attempted
  fresh PMC acquisition of the 2024 article returned HTTP 200 challenge HTML,
  not its supplement; no supplementary acid-dose content is claimed acquired.

Jadarite day-30 stored values (F13 103.149606279; G13 106.574717219, in the
workbook's units) are consistent with the paper's qualitative control convergence,
and the separate acid-block day-30 value is higher. That limited consistency
does not resolve earlier discrepancies or support a normalized recovery yield.
The calculation audit, including exact unrounded cell values, is retained in
the manifest rather than replacing source cells with calculated means.

## Later 2026 study: primary text acquired, performance claim deferred

[Quezada-Aldaco et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma19132855), *One-Step
Lithium Bioleaching from a Mineral Concentrate: Comparison Between Consortium
and Isolated Native Strains*, Materials 19(13):2855, was published 3 July 2026.
[PubMed 42451934](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42451934/) resolves the DOI and
PMCID. The [PMC primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13362697/)
was successfully downloaded after earlier browser-reader challenges. It carries
the authors' CC-BY license. The direct publisher request returned 403; this
public PMC copy was used for actual methods/results, not search snippets.

The study used a Sonora mineral concentrate supplied by Bacanora, reported as
derived from polylithionite-type clay (section 2.1), an enriched native community,
and three morphology-selected isolates ITDB101, ITDR102 and ITDN103. Section
2.3 explicitly says molecular identification was not performed and omits
taxonomic classification. API sulfate medium, 10% v/v inoculum, 250 mL flasks,
30 degrees C, varied initial pH/pulp/agitation and triplicate runs are described
in section 2.4. The isolated-strain and abiotic-control mineral was autoclaved;
equivalent sterilization of the consortium/biotic-control feed is not stated,
limiting a simple matched causal comparison.

Hold reasons:

- Highlights place approximately 99% at initial pH 8, 10% pulp and no agitation;
  the abstract places 99% at pH 6, 30% pulp and no agitation. Table 2 actually
  reports run 13 as 100.00% at pH 8/10%/0 rpm, and run 11 as 99.23% at
  pH 6/20%/0 rpm. The abstract's condition is not that Table 2 row.
- Methods sections 2.4.1-2.4.2 say 18 days, while optimization and validation
  sections 3.6-3.7 say 12 days. The study's initial medium comparison was a
  separate 30-day experiment. These durations must not be interchanged.
- Section 3.7 cites 94.5% consortium validation, but Table 3 lists biotic
  control 96.35%, abiotic control 14.74%, and the three isolate values
  91.92/93.27/91.73%, without a separate 94.5% row. Do not collapse these
  labels or fabricate a missing measurement.
- Section 2.5 Equation 1 uses the reduction in **solid concentration**,
  `(Ci-Cr)/Ci`, measured by AAS. It is not an aqueous Li assay or a complete
  solid/liquid mass balance. Residual solid masses, concentration basis,
  digestion details and uncertainty are needed before interpreting these
  percentages as recovered Li mass or a purified-product yield.
- Sections 3.8-3.9 identify abundant non-Li phases and report initial Li
  0.0052%; polylithionite identity is attributed to the supplier/earlier work,
  not independently resolved by this sample's XRD. Do not instantiate this
  concentrate as a pure mineral record.
- Section 3.6.3 explicitly states intermediate metabolites/byproducts were not
  identified. Sulfate reduction, H2S, organic acids and specific molecular
  pathways remain hypotheses in this assay, despite stronger conclusion wording.
  Data availability points to the article and author inquiries, not an inspected
  raw-data deposit. No author was contacted.

Exact DOI correction/retraction and dataset/corrigendum queries returned the
original articles and related index results without an affecting notice in the
first returned sets. This bounded check is not an exhaustive integrity audit.
The newer study's summary of Kirk2024 is not substituted for Kirk's own controls.
