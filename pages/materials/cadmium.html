Cadmium element Critical Material

Cadmium (Cd), atomic number 48. The neutral-atom identifier grounds the element, not Cd(II), cadmium chloride, cadmium sulfide, or a mineral species. India's June 2023 committee list names the cadmium material; that dated designation is mapped to this element without extending it to its compounds. This is the 30-material committee assessment, not a claim about the separate statutory critical/strategic schedule or current law. Curated laboratory evidence concerns culture-associated cadmium-sulfide formation.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Culture-associated cadmium-sulfide formation

biomineralization Microbial

Associated Organisms: Klebsiella planticola (NCBITaxon:575) [TaxonMech]

Living Klebsiella planticola Cd-1 cultures formed CdS-associated solids. This supports laboratory immobilization, not elemental-cadmium reduction, purified-product recovery or industrial deployment.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Anaerobic synthetic CdCl2 medium used pyruvate/glucose and Trizma buffer, initially around pH 7.1. Washed, freeze-dried late-stationary biomass from 5 mM Cd(II) cultures was characterized spectroscopically. A separate thiosulfate-amended assay used 5 mM Cd(II) and 5 or 10 mM thiosulfate. Exact precipitation incubation temperature, elapsed harvest time and vessel volume are not explicit. NCBITaxon:575 identifies the published species, not a verified Cd-1 strain accession.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: sharma_2000
Figures 2-3 compare sulfur XANES and cadmium EXAFS against reference compounds and cadmium-free biomass, supporting Cd-S coordination; Cd-O ligands also remain unresolved. The separate washed-pellet methylene-blue assay estimates approximately 1 mM CdS from sulfide, not directly measured cadmium recovery.
Partial Source: sharma_2000
No matched cell-free precipitation comparison is reported. Thiosulfate interferes with the sulfide assay and was removed by pellet washing. These experiments do not identify an enzyme, establish a complete cadmium mass balance, or distinguish all abiotic contributions.

Causal Graphs & Molecular Pathways (1)

Klebsiella planticola Cd-1 anaerobic sulfide generation and biomass-associated cadmium sulfide formation

REPRESENTATIVE EXAMPLE Mechanism: cd1_cadmium_sulfide

Causal pathway linking Klebsiella planticola thiosulfate sulfurtransferase GlpE (UniProtKB:A0A443VK69), L-cysteine desulfhydrase Cds1 (UniProtKB:A0A2X2E5R3), cysteine synthase A CysK (UniProtKB:A0A0D8SNU2), and thiosulfate-utilizing cysteine synthase B CysM (UniProtKB:A0A2X2ECB8) in species reference proteome UP001293169 to anaerobic sulfide production in 5 mM Cd(II) synthetic pyruvate/glucose Trizma-buffered medium (with or without 5-10 mM thiosulfate) and formation of biomass-associated CdS solids characterized by S-XANES, Cd-EXAFS, and washed-pellet methylene-blue sulfide assay (~1 mM CdS estimate) in Klebsiella planticola Cd-1 (Sharma et al., 2000), while preserving unresolved Cd-O ligands, lack of cell-free controls, and absence of elemental Cd reduction or closed Cd mass balance.
