## Claim-level decisions and adversarial notes for the reviewer

- Preserve the reported organism FC1 and historical name. The species mapping
  is nomenclatural, not evidence that today's type strain was tested.
- Read the pretreatment and filtrate assay before assigning the process:
  this is dissolution of a sodium-sulfide-washed Gamsberg concentrate. No
  purified elemental zinc, isolated zinc salt or downstream metal recovery
  was demonstrated by the curated assays.
- The two-compartment electrolysis apparatus keeps bulk iron chemistry
  comparable; it is an experimental control method, not a commercial recovery
  cell. Uninoculated chemical runs were checked microscopically for bacteria.
- In the 1999 source, paired enhancement follows an early interval without
  enhancement. Figures 5-6 examine surfaces later than the Figure 2 time
  course; do not assign their 69 h/100 h endpoints to all plotted dissolution
  measurements. No curve was digitized and no yield was computed.
- Ten analytical replicates in the Methods quantify instrument/counting
  repeatability. They do not establish ten independent biological reactors.
  Biological replication and effect uncertainty remain unquantified.
- SEM/EDAX comparisons and shrinking-core fits support the authors' proposed
  sulfur-layer explanation. Neither identifies a causal enzyme, directly
  measures a sulfur mass balance, nor independently establishes sulfur
  oxidation state. Avoid a direct enzymatic ZnS oxidation claim.
- **Unresolved source inconsistency:** Figure 2's 1 g/L Fe(II) series and the
  explicit diamond series in Figure 3 show a later departure from the
  equal-dissolution line. The Discussion describes no enhancement at low
  ferrous concentration and a boundary above 1 g/L. The 1998 study reports
  its null at 1 g/L. The curated high-ferrous observation survives, but a
  universal 1 g/L threshold does not. Record this uncertainty rather than
  silently reconciling the prose and plots or digitizing a new result.
- The 1998 Figure 3 caption reports Fe(II) 8 g/L and Fe(III) 1 g/L despite
  the Methods stating Fe(II) 1 g/L for the series. Its current trace is not
  curated numerically. The low-ferrous null rests on Methods and Figures
  4-6 plus the paper's explicit comparison, not that anomalous caption.
