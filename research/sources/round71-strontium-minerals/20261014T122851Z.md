## Selection and concept boundary

Create strontianite and celestine as distinct mineral species. Strontianite is orthorhombic SrCO3, not Sr-substituted calcite or a mixed carbonate. Celestine (celestite in the 1994 study) is orthorhombic SrSO4, not a commodity or generic Sr-bearing sulfate. Neither inherits elemental strontium criticality. The IMA September2026 original was re-inspected, with definitions pp.1-2 and rows p.207 (strontianite, G,1791) and p.39 (celestine, A,1967s.p.). Neither year/special-procedure notation is a unique approval accession. Both complete Handbook entries were read and visually checked.

The two studies provide complementary laboratory settings: S. pasteurii coatings on printed PLA and cyanobacterial surface-associated sulfate/carbonate mineralization. No industrial use, ore extraction or pure-product recovery is established. Existing `strontium.yaml` uses Horiike2017 TK2d and does not supply a pure-strontianite phase result.
