## Conditions, controls and causality

The complete cached §2.1, pages 61–62, describes source-designated *P. tricornutum* CCMP632 ENS Pt186 in polycarbonate bottles, artificial seawater enriched as f/2, 16 °C, 14 h light/10 h dark, with or without air bubbling. The extracted recipe's `MgCl2·2H2O` at 7.5 g/L is retained literally in the original audit; no hydration repair or accepted magnesium inventory is made. Current NCMA catalogue axenity dated 2019 and present L1/20 °C maintenance do not authenticate historical stock purity or supply 2008 assay conditions. No organism CURIE or strain accession beyond the source designation is added.

Bubbled T1–T5 samples, the acidified T5 aliquot and static endpoint preparations are different specimens. The 50 mL in §2.3.1 is a microscopy aliquot, not a demonstrated working culture volume. The cell-free control uses enriched medium plus NaOH at pH 10.5. A different living culture uses CO2-enriched air to hold pH 7.5–8.5, followed by NaOH to pH 10 to examine EPS formation. These are useful but unequal interventions, not a fully matched sterile/killed-cell/pH control series. Their reported outcomes support a role for solution chemistry while limiting claims of microbial necessity or exclusive cellular causation.

Sample preparation includes fixation/embedding, staining distinctions, SDS treatment for SEM/AFM and AFM in air. Wallstat and HWstat involve isolation/extraction, including boiling for 1.5 h; their spectra are not intact living-cell spectra. HCl neutralization to pH 8 is reported to dissolve much of the shell and return silica to solution, with no closed Mg or Si balance. No isolated brucite mass, phase fraction, Mg assay time course, independent replicate count or quantitative uncertainty ledger is supplied in the available source scope.

The original figures strengthen association and phase assignment without resolving the source's explicit §4.3 limitation: the authors cannot determine whether nucleation occurs on the cell surface or magnesium precipitates before chelation. Carboxylate/sulfate participation and magnesium-mediated polymer stabilization remain interpretations constrained by overlapping FTIR bands. The broad schema process `biomineralization` is used for the qualified culture-associated mineral formation; it does not claim the paper demonstrated biomineralization in its narrower *sensu stricto* usage.
