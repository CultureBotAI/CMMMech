## Issue dispositions

The bounded acquisition objective in [#151](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/151) is addressed by complete alternative originals from 1979, 2001 and 2022 and reviewed stannite/kësterite records. The PR requests closure on that basis. Optional Hall 1978, Moore 1984 and full 1981 originals remain opportunities to strengthen evidence; abstract-only 1981 material is not treated as an independent replication of the overlapping 1979 experiment. This is an acquisition issue, not a corrective record-review finding.

A new acquisition issue, [#153](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/153), retains the zircon-sand and concentrate evidence gaps. Bansal 2007 requires the original graphics and feed-characterization supplement; relative surface/compositional changes cannot be converted into bulk zirconium recovery. Hedrich 2023 requires complete methods and results to assess concentrate identity, matched-pH chemical comparisons and the boundary between mechanical processing and microbial experiments. Neither feed is imported from an abstract or incomplete graphic set.

Existing acquisition issues [#103](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/103), [#104](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/104), [#120](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/120), [#129](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/129), [#130](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/130), [#133](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/133), [#134](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/134), [#137](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/137) and [#140](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/140) remain open.
