Manganese sulfate monohydrate commodity commodity

Manufactured manganese sulfate monohydrate products, nominally MnSO4.H2O. The monohydrate commodity is distinct from manganese metal, other sulfate hydration states, dissolved manganese in a leachate, and a mineral specimen. Pan Chem supplies monohydrate powder for feed and fertilizer applications, and Ihumate publishes the monohydrate formula and an agricultural product specification. These sources establish a manufactured product family rather than one battery qualification. Walsun separately describes high-purity manganese sulfate for lithium-ion cathode precursors, while SAMR registers HG/T 4823-2023 for manganese sulfate for batteries; the complete standard tables were not inspected. Neither that end use nor this one salt exhausts the broader battery-grade manganese family. No universal purity cutoff, criticality designation or microbial production of qualified crystals is asserted.
