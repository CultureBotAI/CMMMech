Fluorine element Critical Material

The element F, distinct from fluoride anion, molecular fluorine, individual organofluorine compounds, fluorite mineral and the fluorspar commodity. Australia's explicitly named Fluorine entry is mapped to the element; its comparative footnote about US and EU fluorspar does not establish elemental-fluorine criticality in those jurisdictions.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Enantioselective hydrolytic defluorination by recombinant Pseudomonas

other Microbial

Associated Organisms: Pseudomonas putida (NCBITaxon:303)

Pseudomonas putida ATCC 12633 engineered to constitutively express a Delftia acidovorans strain B defluorinase grew on two model fluorinated acids while releasing inorganic fluoride. Approximately half of the fluorine in each racemic substrate was released, consistent with preferential conversion of its S enantiomer. This is hydrolytic C-F bond cleavage, not fluorine reduction, universal PFAS degradation or isolation of a usable fluorine product.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Laboratory recombinant culture grown with 20 mM racemic alpha-fluorophenylacetic acid or 2-fluoropropionic acid as sole carbon source in mineral salts basal medium. The medium had 40 mM sodium/potassium phosphate buffer at pH 6.8, ammonium chloride 1 g/L, trace metals and vitamins, and 50 micrograms/mL kanamycin for the recombinant strain. General flask methods specify 20 mL in 125 mL baffled flasks, 200 rpm, initial OD600 0.05-0.1 and growth at 28 or 30 degrees C; the main methods do not assign one of those two temperatures unambiguously to each flask result. The separate 96-well lag assay specifies 30 degrees C. Inocula were washed once and pregrown on mandelic acid or D-lactic acid, respectively. Fluoride was measured with an ion-selective electrode calibrated with NaF; OD600 and colony counts assessed growth and viability. Species taxonomy is grounded separately from the ATCC strain and its laboratory expression construct. The introduction's isolated 12663 spelling conflicts with 12633 in Methods, Results and the culture authority; this record follows the latter. No fluorine-containing mineral, mixed environmental waste or industrial process was tested in the curated assays.
Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: dodge_2024
Figure 2 and Table 2 show growth-associated fluoride release. Table 2 reports 9.9 +/- 0.1 mM fluoride from 20 mM racemic alpha-fluorophenylacetic acid and 9.9 +/- 0.2 mM from 20 mM racemic 2-fluoropropionic acid, with times to maximal OD600 of 29 and 78 h, respectively. Values are means and standard deviations of three biological replicates; the authors report similar results in one independent repetition. The denominator is total racemic substrate: this is about half of its fluorine, not complete conversion or a recovered-product yield. Wild-type ATCC 12633 did not grow on these test acids in the stated tests.
Support Source: dodge_2024
Purified-enzyme assays include substrate solutions without enzyme as spontaneous-release controls. Figure 3 and stereochemistry methods distinguish two experiments: polarimetry of residual alpha-fluorophenylacetic acid from a growing culture supported retention of the R enantiomer, whereas stereospecific lactate dehydrogenases identified D-lactate after purified-enzyme treatment of racemic 2-fluoropropionic acid. These observations support selective hydrolysis. The modeled active-site attack is an interpretation, not a new crystal structure or mutational proof.
Partial Source: dodge_2024
Table 1 reports activities below 0.01 micromole/min/mg for tested difluorinated substrates and a fluoroolefin under its enzyme-assay conditions; these limits are not zero or proof about all PFAS. Table 2 shows lower final OD600 on fluorinated substrates than on the corresponding nonfluorinated products, and Figure 2F shows an initial colony-count decline before recovery. CrcB involvement is inferred from genomic comparison and other-strain literature, not a transporter knockout in this recombinant strain. Wild-type nongrowth is not itself a quantitative abiotic fluoride-release control for the flask cultures. Intracellular fluoride concentrations and an isolated fluoride product were not measured in these assays.
