# Round26 ingest frontier

Four distinct secondary-resource records are curated from primary evidence with documented access limitations and sent through independent review: [EAF dust](../../data/records/electric-arc-furnace-dust.yaml), [BOF steel slag](../../data/records/basic-oxygen-furnace-steel-slag.yaml), [pharmaceutical blister waste](../../data/records/pharmaceutical-blister-packaging-waste.yaml) and [beverage-carton hydropulping reject](../../data/records/beverage-carton-hydropulping-reject.yaml). Evidence availability, identity clarity and useful controls determined selection. No feed inherits its constituent element's criticality. Source limitations and null results remain explicit. Acceptance and exact-state links are recorded in the separate publication report and manifest.

## Next ingests in priority order

1. **Ni-based superalloy residue powders**, [10.3390/pr13072157](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr13072157). Acquire original figures, complete control design and supplement inventory. Partial primary text already identifies three stock-specific residues, unequal lixiviant acidity, incomplete aqua-regia digestion and a cobalt conversion discrepancy. Inspect those before accepting yields or stock/strain identities. [Assessment](round26-superalloy-sources-20261011.md).
2. **Beverage cans**, [10.1016/j.wasman.2020.11.012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2020.11.012). A distinct packaging resource; public abstract and closed institutional deposit are insufficient for claim-level curation. Obtain full methods, figures, controls and supplement. [Packaging assessment](round26-packaging-sources-20261011.md).
3. **EAF steel slag**, [10.1016/j.chemosphere.2014.09.089](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemosphere.2014.09.089). Follow the BOF paper's primary reference and earlier frontier. Distinguish slag from EAF dust, and establish culture-supernatant conditions and feed chemistry before creating a root. [BOF assessment](round26-bof-sources-20261011.md).
4. **Brake-pad manufacturing residues**, [2017 encapsulated-culture study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2016.11.010) and [2019 pretreatment study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2019.02.047). Acquire full primary evidence, distinguish production residues from spent pads, and audit heat treatment, stock identity, immobilized/planktonic comparisons and enhancement denominators. Current publisher previews and metadata-only API responses do not establish endpoints. [Assessment](round26-bof-sources-20261011.md).
5. **Stainless-steel pickling sludge**, [10.1016/j.chemosphere.2022.134213](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chemosphere.2022.134213). Acquire full MICP experiment and distinguish lysate/cell arms, immobilization, mineral identity and extraction. No recovery record follows from an abstract alone. [Assessment](round26-bof-sources-20261011.md).
6. **Same-resource extensions**, rather than duplicate roots: [2025 PolyAl comparison](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2025.114913) may extend carton reject; [2021 steel-slag carbonate modification](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jes.2020.08.023) requires exact slag-route identity. Full methods must establish whether the treatments are microbial, reagent-based, or mixed. The [2026 designed-adhesive study](https://doi.org/10.1039/D6GC00733C) concerns engineered packaging, not proof of that formulation in hospital waste.

## Scientific questions retained in current records

- EAF dust: reconcile Dust 1 tables versus extraction graph, Fe precipitation 48% versus about 42%, and the exact feed/dilution connecting leaching to electrolysis. Obtain complete deposit and material balances; current catholyte depletion is a stage-specific result.
- BOF slag: reconcile Table 1 with both batch and column inventories, document initial background and sampling corrections, identify replicated columns and use chemistry-matched controls. Preliminary community fingerprints do not establish pure strains or a specific causal pathway.
- Blister waste: verify the original equation layout and intended denominator, resolve silver-fraction composition discrepancies, quantify foam-associated material and confirm exact acid-lot provenance. Preserve the high-temperature null and scale-dependent decline.
- Carton reject: resolve the DSM 583 source/catalogue naming conflict, quantify residual composition, document non-detection limits and characterize the final precipitate. Chemical acid comparability and less effective direct culture remain visible.

Source PDFs, HTML, figures and access receipts remain outside Git; the paired dossiers retain inspected scope and checksums.
