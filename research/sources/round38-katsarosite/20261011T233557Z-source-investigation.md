## Exact assay and control contract

A.niger Ch4/07 was source-isolated from damaged Chersonesos marble. Fresh GenBankKF768341.1 agrees with strainCh4/07, marble and NCBITaxon5061; the deposit does not authenticate the actual inoculum independently. The surface cultures used30mL Czapek-Dox in cultivation mattresses,25°C,28days,initialpH6.0,triplicate. Recipe g/L:glucose30,NaNO3 2,KH2PO4 1,MgSO4·7H2O0.5,KCl0.5,FeSO4·7H2O0.01. Methods addZnSO4·7H2O at0,250,500,1000,2000micromol/L. Autoclaving121°C is reported; duration and spore inoculum density/volume are not given.

Uninoculated zinc syntheses and zinc-free fungal controls were examined. Chemical hopeite precipitated before inoculation; free zinc in Table2 refers to that chemical stage. It is not a fungal-removal result. Day28 PXRD concerns ground mycelium plus crystals; days7–21 were sampled selectively after microscopy. Table3 reports first katsarosite atday21 in the2000arm, Figure4a supports day28 monoclinic assignment, and Figure5c-d/EDX give Zn-dominant crystals withMg/Zn0–0.04. No specimen water assay or quantitative phase purity was obtained. Lower doses include Mg-dominant monoclinic material and minor orthorhombic glushinskite, so pattern similarity alone does not establish katsarosite.

Organic acids were measured at14and28days using native versus1NHCl-treated material with cation exchange; the operational difference estimates an insoluble acid fraction, not a katsarosite-specific zinc yield. No matched killed-cell, cell-free, pH-matched or oxalate-only series isolates metabolic necessity. Proposed oxalate-mediated extracellular precipitation is supported as a culture-associated explanation; enzyme causality and isolated metal recovery remain unproven.

## Contradictions and limits retained

- The 2025 paper identifies companion Penicillium chrysogenum Cs/21 with OP758843; the actual OP758843.1 GenBank record identifies Penicillium granulatum II47-8, taxon395885. Do not authenticate or silently reassign the companion culture. Only independently matching A.niger Ch4/07/KF768341.1 is an accepted mechanism organism.
- Methods define ZnSO4.7H2O additions in micromol/L; headings sometimes shorten to micromol and Discussion4.2 uses mmol. Retain Methods basis and flag the inconsistency.
- A.niger 2000 micromol/L day28 pH is2.8 in Table1,2.5 in Table3/approximately Figure2a, but Discussion4.1 says5–6 at days14and28. Other Table1/Table3/Figure2 differences include500 day28 and zinc-free endpoints. Do not reconcile to a unique precipitation pH.
- Abstract alpha-to-beta threshold at Mg/Zn>1 is incompatible with monoclinic Mg-rich compositions in body. Phase plus Zn-dominant composition is needed; lower-dose isostructural Mg oxalates are not automatically katsarosite.
- Data availability says no new data were created/analyzed despite experimental claims. No underlying assay dataset inspected; this is a provenance/access inconsistency, not a basis for declaring experiments fabricated.
- Natural mineral article chemistry includes Zn0.90Fe0.05Mg0.03Mn0.02, while natural CIF uses Zn0.90Fe0.07Mg0.03 with no Mn. Ideal Zn-dominant identity is unchanged; minor occupancy is not transferred into the culture product.
- The source suggests urea hydrolysis/urease without directly testing it; listed medium has nitrate and no urea. No enzyme mechanism adopted.
- Source crystal-size wording includes a micromol-for-size typo; not imported. Early high-Zn growth descriptions and graphical biomass observations are not adequate for an exact viable biomass estimate.

- Matched uninoculated zinc syntheses and zinc-free fungal controls exist, but no killed-cell, cell-free, matched-pH or oxalate-only control series isolates metabolic necessity or a particular enzyme.
- Abiotic hopeite precipitates before inoculation; Table2 free zinc is pre-growth chemical precipitation, not fungal removal. GC-MS soluble/insoluble acids are operational native-versus-acid-treated fractions, not zinc-specific product yields.
- No endpoint closed zinc balance, isolated mineral mass, product purity, specimen water assay, quantitative phase fraction, industrial deployment or demonstrated remediation efficacy.
- Full publisher PDF/JATS available; MDPI HTML/XML/notes website paths denied403 and web notes unavailable. No comprehensive version-history claim. Main JATS has7 figures,4tables,zero supplementary-material nodes; PDF has no SI section/link. This is bounded link inspection, not universal absence.
