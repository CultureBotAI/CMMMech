## Primary experiment and controls

Methods on pages 1565–1566 use acclimated living MR-4, washed/resuspended M1 medium with 30 mM lactate, initial pH 7.4, nominal 200 µM MnOOH and 5 × 10^6 cells/mL in open 250 mL flasks under 10% H2/90% N2 at stated 27°C. Casamino acids were restricted to pregrowth. The source-prepared solid used MnSO4/H2O2/NH3, 95°C for six hours, hot/cold washing and frozen storage. Needle morphology and a recipe support a source assignment; no batch-specific diffraction, refined composition or phase-purity result is given. Separate pH/temperature assays use 50 mL serum bottles and two sampling times.

Figure 3 contrasts active cultures with no-cell/killed-cell, oxygen, CCCP and dialysis-separated solid comparisons. Strongly lower release supports culture/contact/metabolic dependence within the protocol. The no-added-carbon arm retains release and is not donor-free because the atmosphere contains H2. Table 1 preserves strong CCCP/HgCl2 inhibition versus weak HQNO and reported zero cyanide inhibition. No molecular reductase or universal contact requirement is established.

At pH 4 and 5, Table 1 inoculated/control means are 101.3/103.1 and 39.4/44.0 µM: no positive microbial increment is shown. At pH 7.4 they are 12.2/1.2 µM. These are table means, not yields. Three samples per flask and multiple AAS readings are technical replication, not three independent cultures. The assay measures 0.2 µm-filtered acidified elemental Mn by AAS; Mn(II) is the source interpretation rather than direct speciation. Complete eventual reduction is stated with data not shown; no complete mass balance or isolated recovery yield is curated.

Figure 2's 100-fold inoculum range differs from 300-fold in the text. The repeated standard is 200 µM in methods but the duplicate Figure 1 series is labelled 100 µM.
