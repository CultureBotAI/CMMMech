## Claim decisions and adversarial checks

- The Methods transfer culture plus glucose into the leaching assay without a
  stated cell-removal step before ore addition. Do not describe this as cell-free
  supernatant leaching. Post-leaching filtration is an analytical preparation step.
- Main Figures 1-3 support a genotype-associated extraction difference; the
  reported 53.1% and 73.1% are relative improvements over wild-type. S2 cell S22
  and S3 cells T33/T42 retain the wild-type mean denominator. No 73% absolute
  extraction claim is accepted. Main Figure 3 labels medians of 12.1/16.7/18.2%
  at 10% and 12.6/18.0/20.5% at 1%; these remain source context, not newly
  validated structured recovery measurements.
- S3's Figure 3 sheet sums fourteen elements, excluding Er and Sc. Other source
  sheets use other element sets. Row21 supplies a mineral-sand denominator but
  detailed feed-assay provenance and the relationship of the other calculation
  sheets to the main plotted experiment are incompletely documented. No assay
  cells are imported into the mg/kg structured-observation contract.
- Main Methods specify 24 h biolixiviant production; figure labels specify pH at
  36 h. The printed 500 mL analytical aliquot follows a 1 mL withdrawal. Preserve
  both inconsistencies; do not silently substitute microlitres.
- Main assays are reported in triplicate but split an upstream culture. Do not
  claim three independent biological cultures. S6 has four rows per treatment.
- S1's no-bacteria rows 23-25 have substantially higher pH than microbial arms.
  They do not separate acidity from other culture effects. Figure S2's medium-only
  comparator is retained; its neat nitric acid extracts more REEs, under different
  mixing/volume conditions from the main assay. No universal advantage over
  chemical extraction is supported.
- S6 Panel B labels omit the deletion carried in Panel A and Figure S2. Record the
  discrepancy rather than claiming an independently identified second genotype.
- The Discussion acknowledges unknown metabolite composition/concentration changes
  and speculative phosphate-regulon and oxygen-transfer explanations. Relative
  metal proportions are not purified-product selectivity. Sausage photographs in
  S3-S4 are not safety certification or lifecycle evidence; S3's caption repeats
  nitric acid for both ends while the image labels distinguish the treatments.
- This is a laboratory experiment, with no isolated REE product or demonstrated
  commercial process. Author patent/company interests and other-feed economics
  do not change that assessment.
