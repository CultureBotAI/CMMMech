# Remaining CMMMech frontier after round 11

The [exact-root reconciliation](round11-frontier-20261011.json) compares the
current corpus with the unchanged, dated
[authority inventory](../sources/expansion-authorities/20261011T040103Z.md).
A proposal is represented only when both its exact record ID and proposed
material kind match. This is a local reconciliation, not a new census of all
criticality lists or a decision that every commodity proposal warrants a record.

The corpus now contains **71 records: 64 elements, four mineral species, two
secondary resources and one commodity**. This batch adds beryllium, hafnium,
helium, iridium, lutetium, osmium, promethium, rhenium, rubidium and thulium.
The [Be/Rb/Re](round11-be-rb-re-20261011.md),
[Ir/Os/Hf](round11-ir-os-hf-20261011.md),
[Lu/Tm/Pm](round11-lu-tm-pm-20261011.md) and
[helium](round11-helium-20261011.md) dossiers distinguish accepted evidence from
research leads. Beryllium, helium and promethium have identity and dated
criticality only; the other seven add narrowly scoped laboratory observations.

Of the inventory's 94 proposed roots, **65 are represented**. All its element
proposals have a record; **29 overlapping commodity proposals remain**. This
is not exhaustion of critical materials, microbial mechanisms, organisms,
mineral species, resource types or jurisdictional designations. Root coverage
does not measure experimental evidence coverage, and an identity-only record
does not establish microbial feasibility.

## Next priorities

1. **Adjudicate commodity scope before adding records.** Borates, fluorspar,
   phosphate/phosphate-rock, potash, bauxite/alumina and grade-specific materials
   require definitions distinct from their constituent elements and mineral
   species. The [previous graphite assessment](round10-graphite-20261011.md)
   retains natural graphite while deferring unqualified and battery-grade
   graphite proposals. They overlap and are not a disjoint partition. The JSON
   retains every remaining proposal and its original mapping notes.
2. **Acquire full evidence for identity-only records.** Beryllium has 1990 algal
   and 2011 granular-biomass leads requiring full methods, precipitation controls
   and mass-balance assessment. Promethium has a historical radiotracer study
   lineage requiring figures, neodymium-carrier and background-correction checks;
   the report and journal article are not independent replication. Helium searches
   must distinguish tracer or plasma use from helium as the microbial target.
   Its physical gas-separation literature can support separately scoped abiotic
   research but does not establish microbial extraction.
3. **Resolve the strongest deferred mechanism claims.** Rhenium's 2009
   condition-specific null and 2024 pyrite-associated positive redox leads need
   a matched assessment of full methods and analytical evidence. The accepted
   dried-yeast association result does not reconcile those experiments or prove
   perrhenate reduction. Hafnium protein binding is cell-free, not whole-cell
   recovery; osmium end products inferred from hydrogen use need phase/valence
   verification before stronger claims. Iridium's protocol and figure/text
   inconsistencies, condition-specific supporting information, and biomass taxonomy remain
   explicit. Lutetium and thulium observations apply to their tested organisms
   and biomass states, not all rare-earth elements or industrial feedstocks.
4. **Retain earlier scientific follow-ups.** The
   [round-10 frontier](round10-frontier-20261011.md) and prior dossiers preserve
   inaccessible hybrid tantalum processing, niobium binding/control questions,
   graphite-feed origin, and other phase/measurement gaps. Supported corrections
   require actual EDIT history and successor reviews with exact finding lineage.
5. **Refresh authorities explicitly when needed.** The dated inventory remains
   unchanged. The UK OR/24/059 replacement assessed in round 10 is not silently
   propagated to every historical mapping. Record-specific official editions
   and explicit group membership take precedence over generic criticality labels.

Enumeration includes hidden and ignored files and follows relevant symlinks in
`data/records`, `research`, `reviews` and `history`; the JSON records the actual
bounded inventory. Dossiers declare their cited raw-source roots and acquisition
limits. No unrelated machine-wide absence claim is made. Shared experiments
across elements, host materials or resources remain one study lineage.
