Anatase mineral

Anatase is the TiO2 mineral species listed by IMA-CNMNC in September 2026 with approved status A and the designation 1962 s.p. The latter denotes a special procedure, not a numbered approval identifier. Anatase is distinct from rutile and brookite despite their common ideal formula, and from elemental titanium, titanium-bearing ores and unspecified TiO2 nanoparticles. The curated experiment concerns a manufactured anatase counterpart produced using engineered bacterial biomass, a soluble titanium reagent and subsequent heat treatment. It does not establish microbial formation of natural anatase, dissolution of a natural titanium mineral or recovery of titanium from an ore or waste. No constituent-derived criticality is asserted.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Engineered bacterial template followed by thermal anatase synthesis

other Microbial

Associated Organisms: Escherichia coli (NCBITaxon:562) [TaxonMech]

Escherichia coli BL21(DE3) bearing an ice-nucleation-protein anchor fused to repeated silaffin-derived R5 segments associated with titanium-containing precursor deposition and shaped the subsequently calcined product. Microscopy and diffraction support rod-shaped aggregates of anatase nanocrystals after heat treatment. The microbial contribution is an engineered biomass template and, under argon, a carbon source. The 600 degrees C crystallization stage is abiotic. This coupled process is not demonstrated growth on titanium, metabolic titanium reduction or isolated-metal recovery.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
The original supplement identifies BL21(DE3) and pET(INP-nR5) constructs with one, three or five R5 repeats; NCBITaxon:562 maps the species only. Its synthesis section uses cell pellets in 10 mL Tris-buffered saline, specified as 50 mM Tris-HCl at pH 7.0 and 300 mM NaCl, with 2 mL titanium(IV) bis(ammonium lactato) dihydroxide solution. The precursor stock concentration and pellet biomass loading are not supplied there. This is a synthetic reagent feed, not a mineral leachate. The mixture was incubated at 37 degrees C for 24 h and then at 80 degrees C for 24 h, centrifuged, water-washed and freeze-dried. Products were heated at 600 degrees C for 4 h in air or argon, with respective heating rates of 10 and 4 degrees C/min. The supplement's separate membrane-fraction preparation is for protein analysis and is not substituted for the cell-pellet synthesis procedure. Viability during mineralization or after heating was not measured in the reported assays. The buffered starting pH is not a measured reaction pH trajectory. These are laboratory material-synthesis and coin-cell experiments, not industrial deployment.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: ping2016
Main Figures 1-4 and Supporting Figures S2, S4-S8 support cell-associated precursor deposition, retention of a rod-like template after heating, and an anatase-assigned crystalline product characterized by XRD, electron diffraction and lattice imaging. The five-repeat air-treated product is described as approximately 5 nm particles, while Figure 3 labels its size 5.8 plus or minus 0.8 nm. Those descriptions are not treated as identical measurements or a production yield. Argon-treated material additionally contains carbon observed by microscopy, elemental mapping and Raman analysis; it is an anatase-carbon composite, not an assertion of pure recovered titanium.
Partial Source: ping2016
At 37 degrees C, Figure 1b electron diffraction and Figure S2g support largely amorphous precursor rather than established crystalline anatase. The authors interpret a weak broad peak near 26 degrees in S2h after the 80 degrees C stage as anatase nuclei; that low-crystallinity assignment is weaker than the post-calcination diffraction evidence. The main text on page 6332 cites powder reference 21-1271, whereas Figure 2e and Supporting Figure S6 both label 21-1272. No reference-card identifier is independently adopted here. The phase assignment rests on the source's combined reported characterization, not on nominal TiO2 composition alone.
Partial Source: ping2016
INP-only cells provide a relevant protein-display comparator: Figure S2 shows smoother surfaces than the five-repeat arm, and S4b shows aggregated particles with loss of the rod shape after heating. Thus the control supports a morphology effect; it does not show that titanium-containing solids can form only with R5 or only through microbial metabolism. The one-, three- and five-repeat comparisons further associate repeat number with particle size. No matched cell-free precursor control, live-versus-killed comparison, titanium mass balance or quantitative isolated-product yield is reported for this synthesis. Biological replicate counts and the statistical basis of Figure 3's size uncertainties are not specified.
Context_only Source: ping2016
The electrostatic binding, hydrogen-bond catalysis and protein confinement steps in Figures 1d, 3d and 5 are proposed molecular explanations, not direct measurements of each catalytic intermediate or proof of a specific active enzyme pathway. Figure S1 documents membrane-protein bands without a viability assay. Subsequent lithium-battery capacities, fitted diffusion coefficients and modeled carbon coverage concern material performance; they are not titanium extraction or recovery endpoints and are not imported as such.

Causal Edges (3):
  • inaz_surface_anchor → outer-membrane chimeric fusion display (displays_on_surface) → sil1_mineralization_peptide : InaZ anchors and exposes the C-terminal Sil1 peptide on the outer membrane of E. coli BL21(DE3).[ping2016]
  • sil1_mineralization_peptide → interfacial hydrolysis and condensation of Ti precursor (nucleates) → amorphous_ti_precursor_shell : Surface-displayed Sil-1 peptide is proposed to hydrolyze/condense TALH into a cell-bound amorphous titanium-oxide shell in Tris-buffered saline (without a non-engineered cell control).[ping2016]
  • amorphous_ti_precursor_shell → 600 C thermal calcination and crystallization (transforms_into) → calcined_anatase_tio2 : Abiotic 600 degrees C calcination converts the cell-templated amorphous titanium-oxide precursor into hollow porous anatase TiO2 structures.[ping2016]
