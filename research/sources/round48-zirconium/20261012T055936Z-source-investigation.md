The Bansal handoff needs particular scrutiny: washed fungal biomass was exposed to natural sand; an acidic water control was used. Reported enrichment percentages derive from XPS/EDX relative Si/Zr values, not a closed bulk mass balance or isolated zirconium product. Enzyme selectivity is proposed, not directly established. Repeated fresh-biomass exposures change both biomass and retained feed masses. Its author-copy upload date is2014, while journal publication is2007. No microbial result from this feed is transferred to the mineral owner.

The Hedrich abstract explicitly reports no significant bio/chemical difference under matched conditions. Future curation must retain that comparison and keep mechanically processed pilot sand separate from laboratory bioleaching scale. Abstract extraction percentages are not imported.
