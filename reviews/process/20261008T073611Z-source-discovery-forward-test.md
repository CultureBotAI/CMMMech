## Observed fitness and hazards

1. **Observed coverage:** T1 contains 61 dictionary rows/3 columns; T3 contains **87 assay rows/12 columns**; T6 contains **22 digest rows/7 columns**. The catalogue HTML advertises 11 attached files, about 9.76 MB; this test inspected only the dictionary, metadata and two assay tables, not all assays or images.
2. **Censoring:** all ten T3 analyte columns are described as mg/kg (ppm). There are **380 negative-coded values**, representing below-detection observations with the absolute value as the detection limit—not negative concentrations or zero. Step 8 also declares `-9999` for nulls, but T3 has an actual blank `K_ppm` for `V.brr_RefFrit` (CSV line 74). This is a concrete metadata/value-convention discrepancy: a future parser must recognize the observed blank as well as the documented sentinel. Do not silently convert aqueous ppm to mg/L.
3. **Keys and joins:** `(RunDate, SampleID)` is unique in each inspected assay table. After normalizing US month/day/year formatting, **22/22 T6 rows join to T3** by that pair. The proposed grouping `(ExpName, ExpCondition, DigestMedia)` is not a unique observation key: six combinations repeat. `RunDate` is the analysis date, not experiment elapsed time. Preserve sample IDs, source row numbers and file checksums; add explicit run/component mappings before aggregating.
4. **Measured outcome:** T6 is element recovered by digestion, in **millimoles**, not a percentage yield or product purity. It mixes working-electrode, PTFE-holder/filter and reference-frit samples and sequential/different acid digests. For example, `BESU.8Va_AcidExtract` reports **0.228 mmol** under abiotic conditions; `BESU.8Vb_AcidExtract` reports **0.294 mmol** under biotic conditions, using different digest media. These individual measurements cannot establish a microbial effect without matched experiment identities, starting inventories, sampled volumes, fraction coverage and replication.
5. **Biological scope:** metadata describes *Shewanella oneidensis* MR-1, dissolved metal additions to defined electrolyte, two-chamber 100 mL laboratory cells, a working electrode poised at **−800 mV versus saturated Ag/AgCl**, and durations spanning 72–574 h with a stated 42.9 h abiotic-U repeat. Abiotic controls and pre-equilibration sorption are real parts of the design. The sampled files do not independently prove a specific electron-transfer pathway, mineral identity, pure product recovery, or deployment on actual industrial waste.
6. **Uneven element coverage:** T6 has U, V, Cr and one initial-abiotic As row; it has **no Sb recovery row**. Methods explicitly say the abiotic Cr working electrode was not recovered for digestion. Consequently a dataset-level abstract about As/Sb/U/V/Cr must not become five equivalent microbial mechanism records. T3 also includes digest-derived samples, despite its aqueous filename.
7. **Reuse/version:** XML declares CC0-1.0, complete status and no planned updates. Citation and metadata date are **2026-09-15**; the official portal posting is **2026-09-17**; the stated experimental coverage is 2024-01-01–2025-12-01. No numbered data edition was established. The newer page date is not a new data version.
