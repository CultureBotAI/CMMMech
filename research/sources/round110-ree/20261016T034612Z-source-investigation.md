## Conflicting and limiting evidence

Milani's material is explicitly a synthetic La/Ca analogue. Its nominal formula is not its analytical composition. Prose and CIF give Na2.41(14), while Table 1 gives 2.41(13). The prose/table state a five-oxygen normalization without identifying a metal-oxide-only basis. Such a basis could explain the wording and is not itself proof of a wrong carbonate formula. Abstract thermal coefficients (6.0(2)×10⁻⁵, 5.7(7)×10⁻⁸, V0=591.95(8)) differ from Results (6.4(1)×10⁻⁵, 3.3(5)×10⁻⁸, V0=591.86(4)). The abstract/methods maximum of 746 K exceeds Table 5, Table S4 and Figure S3 coverage to 628 K. The discussion's 1731–1273 K differs from the abstract's 1173–1273 K. These thermal/model claims and a single exact analytical formula are not imported. The 1953 P63/mmc assignment is historical; it is not silently combined with the later synthetic P63mc refinement.

Conconi's local composition averages remain tied to their specimen and spot counts. Fictitious scattering occupancies and calculated densities are not measured chemical composition. Low fluorine may reflect beam loss. Natural growth and Th-related damage explanations remain interpretations, with disorder, inclusions and beam/FIB limitations. The paper itself notes tension between screw growth and radial zoning. No microbial, ore-leaching or recovery experiment is inferred from the characterization.
