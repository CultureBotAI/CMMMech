Potash commodity Critical Material

The commodity category of mined and manufactured salts containing potassium in water-soluble form. USGS distinguishes potassium chloride (muriate of potash), potassium sulfate (sulfate of potash), and potassium magnesium sulfate products in agricultural use, while also describing industrial uses. This is not elemental potassium, potassium oxide despite K2O-equivalent statistical reporting, a single potash-bearing mineral species, or all potassium-bearing rocks. In particular, potassium feldspar is not equated with a potash product. The named US 2025 designation applies to potash as a commodity. No microbial production or recovery mechanism is asserted.
