## Primary-source reconciliation

### Feed and preparation

The2020 §2.1 calls the study material0.5–1.5mm sand after nepheline flotation and gives mixed mineral ranges in wt%; it then describes ore-block crushing and1.5kW planetary milling below40µm. Section2.2 gives a separate1h ball milling with media:concentrate5:1, citing reference26. Neither a sample key nor a preparation-arm crosswalk joins these statements. The10 mean±spread oxide values are also printed in2019 for Koashva ore/titanite, whose mineral ranges are explicitly modal%. Equal numbers do not establish that the mined-rock and flotation-tail feeds are the same sample, or that modal% can be replaced by wt%.

Reference26 is now directly available. Samburov/Kiselev printedp53 specifies jaw crushing, **6h milling with media:concentrate3:1**, dry sieving below40µm and initial TiO2/Al2O3/P2O5 of20.5/4.36/5.6wt%. Its cleaning charge is400g,80g/L H2SO4 or50g/L HCl,1:4,50°C,2h. Its subsequent sulfate decomposition uses90g in a500cm³ flask,850–950g/L H2SO4,1:4,4–12h, stated boiling ranges132–135°C and143–145°C. These are attributable to this2018 study, not missing2020 charges. The2020 reference gives the volume-level DOI; the original supplies the article suffix `.51-56`. This source neither reproduces the2020 milling recipe nor connects its feed to the2020 experiments.

### Quantities that remain unaccepted

Figure9 on original2020 p8 labels diameter in **nm**, while nearby prose gives0.4–1µm for over90% of TSK. The factor1000 discrepancy remains unresolved; plausibility is not a correction. Tables3/4 give surface and technical properties, not stream recovery. Reported whiteness60→87→92.5% is optical performance, not Ti extraction. The pilot statement onp7 concerns pigment production and does not demonstrate operation of the acid-decomposition route.

The2020 Table2 TiO2 grade enrichments relative to16.0% feed are1.71875,1.875 and1.96875. They do not demonstrate the almost-fivefold yield statement or approximately90wt% Ti/Si extraction into precipitate. Under a conditional conserved-Ti/common-basis model, their maximum product/feed dry-mass ratios would be about0.582,0.533 and0.508; no observed product masses are provided to use those limits. Lower Al/P grades likewise cannot become removal fractions without residual masses. Repetition of a90% statement in2019 is not independent mass-balance validation. The older volume's1.3–1.5 grade increase pertains to its20.5% feed, not the2020 feed.
