## Stevensite

- **identity:** Main §3.1/§4.1, Figure1, SI S1-S2/TableS1: broad diffraction and treated spacing support likely synthetic stevensite, not phase-pure certainty. IMA Q retained prominently.
- **biotic:** Main §2.4/§3.3/§4.2-4.3, Figure5, Table3, SI S5-S6: low-abundance dypingite only in Last Chance BIO3 with mixed mat and clay, six months. No exact carbonate mass fraction or clay-exclusive Mg source. BG11/lake water supply Mg.
- **controls:** Clay-only controls began five months later in dark, reached seven rather than 12-13 months; no fully matched biological-necessity test. No-clay controls have different phases. No causal organism or strain identified.
- **contradictions:** BIO3/BIO3null final Mg swapped between main Tables3/4. ABIOswing207min pH7.27 versus8.21; starting alkalinity609 versus465mEq/L. SI S6 coating Mg12.4wt% versus15.4 in starting clay despite main enrichment wording. No quantitative removal/capture adopted.
- **abiotic:** Main §2.3/§3.2, Figures2-4, Tables1-2, SI S2-S4: ~28-hour acidification/pH-increase assays release Mg/Si but no detected carbonate. Saturation/calculated theoretical capacity is not observed product/capture.
- **limits:** Evaporation/sampling affects solution chronology; borosilicate potential Si input; community pathways proposed, not measured causal taxa. Shared main/SI is one study, not independent replication.

## Sauconite

- **identity:** IMA193 G1875; Handbook; complete original Fig1-6, Tables2-3 support Zn-rich trioctahedral expandable smectite. Authority formula used; printed doubled source ideal formula has inconsistent hydroxyl normalization.
- **formation:** Table1/Figs1-2: washed/freeze-dried hydrothermal gel products at100°C1/3/7/14d have no XRD-detected impurities;150°C series also hemimorphite;200°C7d also willemite. Table1 has no200°C14d observation.
- **limits:** No XRD detection limit, refined phase fraction, complete conversion or metal-recovery yield. Excess Zn may be adsorbed/interlayer; not all Zn demonstrated structural. Prior differently formulated gel had limited100°C formation, so recipe-specific. Abiotic, no taxon or microbial claim.
- **bibliography:** Printed issue2006 but accepted6April2007 and J-STAGE online20September2011; preserved rather than silently repaired.
