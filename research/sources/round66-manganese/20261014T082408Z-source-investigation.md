## Cryptomelane: accepted primary evidence and conflicts

[Yin et al.2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2022.07.014) was assessed from the complete44-page repository artifact (including cover), all ten original figures and both tables, and the verified [supplement](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0016703722003635-mmc1.docx), all five figures/four tables. The original uses chemically synthesized potassium-bearing HB/Ni10 birnessites, dried40°C and aged in sealed dark room-temperature storage for five/eight years. Eight-year powders were heated in air, 10°C/min and2h per selected temperature. These laboratory powders and furnace conditions are not microbial extraction or industrial deployment.

Diffraction and microscopy support cryptomelane-assigned products at400°C for HB and500°C for Ni10. The Ni500 material also contains minor NiMnO3. A representative imaged crystal has a2×2 framework; this does not establish purity or allocate every Ni atom. Main-text “fully” transformed HB400 is qualified by SI TableS2:0.91cryptomelane/0.09birnessite, stated fraction error0.01. The coefficients fit Mn K-edge spectra against experimental HB500/Ni300 references; they are not gravimetric yields. Ni400 is only0.13cryptomelane by that fit. Table2 Ni K-edge fitting stops at400°C. SI FigureS5 is Ni200, not Ni500.

The separate acid assay used0.0400g in250mL2MHCl,37°C and stirring, with0.22µm-filtered5mL aliquots measured by AAS. Ni500 dissolved much more slowly, ultimately in about50days. The headline396-fold comparison is a ratio of fitted Kabai coefficients, not recovery fraction, universal rate ratio or permanent immobilization. Phase, size, crystallinity and nickel environment co-vary. The record keeps framework incorporation as the authors' interpretation. Dissolution/batch replication and acid-only blank are unspecified in inspected evidence; Figure2 does separately report2–3TGA measurements and standard errors. SI TableS1 HB300 printed Mn(IV) sum0.653 differs from0.198+0.161+0.460=0.819; no unresolved AOS estimate is imported.

[Grangeon et al.2015](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12932-015-0028-y), complete original12figures/Table1 plus both supplements, supplies conditional counter-context. Its precursor is **Na-rich**, while the crystallographic calculation uses a K-cryptomelane structural model. Ten-year low-pH dry aging produced cryptomelane-like domains with heterogeneous tunnel sizes; this is not compositional verification of K-dominant cryptomelane. Type2/3 intermediate roles are inferred; selected-area diffraction could not isolate them. Type4 observations, beam-induced amorphization without resin, limited model sensitivity in SI, and contrasting low-pH/small-crystal versus Yin orderedK-precursor conditions prevent a universal transformation sequence claim. The pH4 change between eight and ten years is time-dependent evidence, not a contradiction.
