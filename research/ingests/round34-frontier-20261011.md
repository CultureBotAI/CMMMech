# Remaining evidence priorities after round 34

This is a bounded handoff, not evidence that the CMM source space is exhausted.
Round 34 admits four separate mineral identities: galena, greigite, mackinawite
and uraninite. The organism, medium, phase, control and measurement boundaries
remain specific to each experiment. No mineral inherits elemental criticality.

| Priority | Candidate or existing owner | Evidence lead and next action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anglesite candidate; existing galena | Pacholewska 2004 identifies anglesite in both inoculated and control residues. Verify the distinct IMA identity and independently review this product-phase scope. Acquire full DOI [10.1021/bp025523k](https://doi.org/10.1021/bp025523k) before accepting the reverse lead-sulfate/PbS process or battery-feed scope. |
| 2 | Ningyoite candidate | Bernier-Latmani 2010 DOI [10.1021/es101675a](https://doi.org/10.1021/es101675a), especially the D. reducens spore experiment and original SI, supplies a concrete minor-phase lead. Verify authority, exact organism and phase assignment; do not equate microscopic minority detection with bulk yield. |
| 3 | Existing galena | Inspect full primary experiments in DOI [10.1186/1472-6750-4-22](https://doi.org/10.1186/1472-6750-4-22), [10.3389/fmicb.2020.557548](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2020.557548) and [10.1111/gbi.12199](https://doi.org/10.1111/gbi.12199) before adding a source-specific extension. Resolve chloride, phosphate and mixed-feed controls rather than pooling extraction claims. |
| 4 | Existing greigite and mackinawite | Compare the Picard and Gorlas experiments at the level of phase detection, active versus inactive biomass, sulfide chemistry and aging. Additional magnetotactic-bacterium studies need their own primary phase and taxon checks. The current records do not establish a universal biological origin or magnetic-recovery process. |
| 5 | Existing uraninite | Review the 2008 MR-1 structural study cited by the 2010 supplement, then the field-speciation study DOI [10.1021/es502701u](https://doi.org/10.1021/es502701u). Laboratory particles do not establish durable field immobilization. |
| Access queue | Tungsten mining waste and ilmenite | [Issue #103](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/103) and [issue #104](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/104) remain open. Acquire the original methods, figures and supplements and reconcile experimental identities before admission. |

These leads are research priorities, not automatically accepted records or claims
of novelty. Search current records, research, reviews, history and cited downloads
including hidden/ignored files before the next addition. Preserve each study's
controls, measurement basis, contradictory results and access limitations. Use
the local add-record and review skills for any accepted change.

The four source dossiers under `research/sources/round34-*` retain acquisition
and inspection details. Source articles and raw figures remain outside Git;
their presence in a download directory does not mean every candidate claim has
been reviewed.
