# Round 15 material frontier

This batch adds two mineral identities, **dittmarite** and **glushinskite**, plus
**lithium-bearing geothermal brine** as a primary geological resource. The corpus
now has 122 records: 64 elements, 30 commodities, 16 minerals, 11 secondary
resources and one primary resource. These are material identities, not 122
independent experiments or mutually exclusive resource inventories.

The two minerals reuse the Suyamud2020 experiment already curated under magnesium.
The brine record preserves a laboratory pretreatment result: impurity depletion
while lithium remains in the measured liquid. It does not claim isolated lithium
product recovery, a criticality designation, or industrial deployment. The new
`primary_resource` kind addresses this real feed identity without relabeling it
as a commodity or secondary waste.

## Next ingests and evidence work

The first four priorities carry forward the [previous frontier](round14-frontier-20261011.md),
with the completed mineral/brine leads removed. Later entries come from the new
source dossiers and independent reviews. This index does not itself acquire or
verify those prospective studies.

1. **Process-specific catalyst feeds**: acquire complete Gholami2011
   DOI10.1016/j.hydromet.2010.11.011 and establish whether its feed is distinct
   from existing spent hydrocracking catalyst. Fully assess retained Das2019
   FCC fungal-aluminium XML as an extension of the existing FCC record.
2. **Mineral-species scope of rare-earth feeds**: acquire vanAlin2023 AppendixS1
   to resolve monazite/xenotime species and mixed-sample identities. Do not
   turn a generic group name into a phase-specific extraction result.
3. **Additional ash and pyromorphite mechanisms**: review full methods for
   DOI10.1016/j.hydromet.2023.106038, DOI10.1016/j.chemosphere.2004.12.060,
   and Topolska2013 ASGP83:343–351. These likely extend existing records.
4. **Finished-product evidence and grade definitions**: acquire complete
   DOI10.1021/acssuschemeng.8b02810 and authoritative specifications for the
   five deferred grade umbrellas. Product identity alone cannot establish
   microbial production or universal battery qualification.
5. **Produced-water and brine extension**: the brine supplement cites Hu et al.,
   Water Research 190 (2021) 116753. Acquire the primary methods and establish
   the hypersaline produced-water origin, organism resolution and lithium
   relevance before adding a resource root. This is a new lead from the brine
   dossier, not evidence already curated here.
6. **Other magnesium-silicate transformations**: the independent mineral
   review surfaced Cai et al. 2013 DOI10.1080/01490451.2012.688924 as a
   forsterite/talc comparison lead. The publisher abstract is insufficient:
   acquire full methods, mineral identities and abiotic oxalate controls before
   creating roots or extending glushinskite evidence.
7. **Newly documented study gaps**: quantitative mineral conversion/product
   purity and the brine paper's timing, Mn/Mg-control, analytical-fraction and
   model/phase inconsistencies require additional evidence. The source's
   cow-urine assay is a synthetic-calcium experiment, not a natural-brine
   validation. Preserve those boundaries when following references.
