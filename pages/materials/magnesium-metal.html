Magnesium in metallic commercial forms, considered as a raw-material commodity. The USGS separates the magnesium-metal market from magnesium compounds and describes primary and secondary metal production and its use in castings, alloy manufacture and metallurgical processing. This commodity is distinct from the magnesium element as a chemical identity, dissolved magnesium ions, magnesium-bearing minerals and magnesium compounds. Alloy and scrap statistics expressed as magnesium content do not make every alloy or waste stream synonymous with this commodity. The designation below preserves the original EU 2024 strategic-list wording; no universal purity threshold or microbial production, extraction or recovery mechanism is asserted.
