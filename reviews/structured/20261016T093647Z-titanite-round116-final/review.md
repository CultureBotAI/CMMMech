## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Mineral species versus actual mixed concentrate

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:titanite.

Titanite identity and experimental feed are properly distinguished.

IMA CaTiO(SiO4), equivalently CaTiSiO5, has approved A status and special-procedure token1967s.p., not a numbered proposal. Sphene is a historical synonym. The source sand after nepheline flotation contains10–50wt% titanite plus other minerals; the purified concentrate is reported approximately80% titanite. The species owner does not identify the entire mixed material as pure titanite or elemental Ti.

### Feed preparation and acid treatment

consistency: supported. Targets: cmmmech:titanite.

Accepted conditions agree with the full original while inconsistencies remain explicit.

The record retains0.5–1.5mm sand, the separate below40µm ore-block preparation and1h ball-milling description without inventing their relationship. DiluteHCl30/50g/L, ratios1:3/1:4 and20/40C concern cleaning. Text3h versus Table2 2h remains unresolved. Subsequent32%HCl preheated80C, concentrate mass:acid volume1:3, boiling5h with vapour return then4h stripping/condensation are supported. No boiling temperature, charge scale or independent replicate count is invented.

### Abiotic decomposition and product identity

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:titanite.

The described TiO2/silica solid is supported without a quantitative recovery claim.

Figure5 marks anatase and rutile peaks in DTK and the text describes amorphous silica and calcium transfer to solution. This is not pure TiO2, a fully selective separation, or proof of complete mass balance. Dilute-acid resistance and concentrated-boiling-acid decomposition refer to different conditions. Figure8 calcined filler and mechanically modified pigment are separate treatments. No microorganism participated.

### Numerical and analytical inconsistencies

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:titanite.

The owner correctly withholds unsupported extraction and product-performance values.

Table2 gives concentrate compositions rather than extraction fractions. Approximately90wt% Ti/Si extraction and fivefold-yield prose lack displayed stream accounting. FTIR chemical-composition and XRF phase-attribution language conflicts with separately described XRD/XRF instrumentation. Figure9 nm axis conflicts with micrometre text. The owner preserves these limitations without silently selecting a correction.
