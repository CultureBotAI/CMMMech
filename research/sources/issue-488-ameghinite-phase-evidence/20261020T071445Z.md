The official [1967 ameghinite paper](https://msaweb.org/AmMin/AM52/AM52_935.pdf) is complete (11 pages). All extracted text was read; original pages 939 and 941–944 were visually checked, including Table 5 and Figure 5. Its thermal results support the following qualified reading:

| 700°C event | p.943: the proposed phase was not found. **Unresolved.** |

The [1967 ezcurrite companion](https://msaweb.org/AmMin/AM52/AM52_1048.pdf) was inspected at printed pp.1048 and 1056–1059, including Figure 6, directly from its image-only scan. Its slow-cooled melt product shares the reported optical indices of the ameghinite 635°C product, but the listed strongest diffraction peaks differ. The feed and thermal program differ (its DTA uses 20°C/min). This supplies comparison context, not independent reproduction of the ameghinite experiment or a resolution of its formulas. The companion is already known from Round116.

The [2017 final article](https://www.rruff.net/odr/view/downloadfile/83014), DOI [10.3749/canmin.1700026](https://doi.org/10.3749/canmin.1700026), is an eight-page publication by Xiong, Kirkes, Knox and Marrs, pp.1001–1008. The complete text, all three figures and Tables 1–4 were inspected. Methods p.1002 describe borax in 0.01/0.1 mol/kg MgCl2 at 22.5±0.5°C; Figure 1 identifies the distinct hexaborate phase alongside borax in the 0.1 medium. Table 1 and model-derived constants are not conversion measurements. Page1007 proposes future investigation of transformation to ameghinite. **Demonstrated conversion remains unresolved.**

The [27-page OSTI accepted manuscript](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1421624) was checked at pp.5–6,11–13,25; p.11 and Figure 1 p.25 were visually compared. One p.11 formula string ends `(OH)9`; final p.1006 uses `(OH)8`. This version difference is not a discovered formal erratum, and the owner already has the correct species formula. Neither version is a separate replication. Independent thermodynamic-model recalculation was outside scope.

The reused [September 2026 IMA list](https://cnmnc.units.it/files/editor/IMA_Master_List_(2026-09).pdf), PDF p.9, verifies the ameghinite row: NaB3O3(OH)4, status A, IMA1966-034. Species identity does not identify heated products. The 1975 structure paper/deposit remain prior evidence; they were not re-audited in this pass.
