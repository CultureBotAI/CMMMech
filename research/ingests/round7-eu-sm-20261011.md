These two element records extend the existing rare-earth coverage using the
separate Eu and Sm experiments of Fischer et al. (2019), rather than transferring

in the [round-six frontier](round6-frontier-20261011.md). They were selected

Live authorities resolve [CHEBI:32999](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:32999)
to europium atom (Eu, neutral) and
[CHEBI:33374](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/CHEBI:33374) to samarium atom (Sm,
neutral). Those identifiers identify the elements, not the experimental
nitrate salts or dissolved ions. The
[USGS Final 2025 List](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-11-07/html/2025-19813.htm)
explicitly lists europium on Federal Register page 50496 and samarium on page
50497. Records preserve United States jurisdiction, issuing authority, named
list, edition and mineral-commodity scope. No timeless or latest-list claim
is made.

[NCBI Taxonomy 1840102](https://eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/entrez/eutils/efetch.fcgi?db=taxonomy&id=1840102&retmode=xml)
resolves the strain-named species-rank node `Anabaena sp. SAG 12.82` used for
Eu. The [SAG catalogue](https://sagdb.uni-goettingen.de/detailedList.php?str_number=12.82)
currently calls it non-axenic, with bacterial and other contamination. This
supports a purity caveat, without proving the composition of the historical
culture. The Sm experiment's `SAG 1403.2` notation corresponds to
[SAG 1403-2](https://sagdb.uni-goettingen.de/detailedList.php?str_number=1403-2).
[NCBI Taxonomy 272123](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=272123)
explicitly lists it as a co-identical strain of its preferred label
`Anabaena cylindrica PCC 7122`. This is an identifier mapping, not a claim
that a separately obtained PCC culture was tested.

## Primary evidence and numerical boundaries

The accepted primary source is
[Fischer et al., RSC Advances 9:32581–32593](https://doi.org/10.1039/C9RA06570A),
first published 11 October 2019; PMC release/indexing in 2022 is not its
publication year. Its full XML, experimental methods, Results, Discussion,
figure captions and five-page supplement were inspected. Supplement pages
S2, S4 and S5 were rendered and visually checked, including their tables and
plots. The available main Figure 1 thumbnail and full Figure 3 spectrum were
viewed. Main Figure 8 pixels were not acquired; the Eu localization statement
is explicitly attributed to the main Results and caption, not a remeasurement
or independent chemical identification from microscopy.

Cultures used modified Bold's Basal Medium with half nitrate, 22 degrees C,
reported culture pH 7.3, initial medium pH 6.8, shaking and a 12 h day/night
cycle. The Eu fed and starved arms differ in initial Eu dose as well as medium.
Initial biomass loading and biological replication were not specified in the
inspected main article/supplement. The zero-time point is an appropriately
diluted stock reference without biomass, not a matched incubated abiotic
control. Untreated biomass and salt references support qualitative LIBS
comparisons. Apparently viable cultures, color stability and visual absence
of precipitate do not establish exclusive active uptake or exclude abiotic
loss. ICP-MS determines elemental concentrations; no oxidation-state-specific
measurement is inferred.

| Assay and locator | Selected exact source values | Accepted interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Fed Eu, Table S1/Figure S1, page S2 | Initial `0.111` mmol/L; `2.47E-3` at 57 min; `6.16E-4` at 1692 min | About 97.8% and 99.4% calculated solution decreases; not a one-hour 99.4% result |
| Starved Eu, Table S4/Figure S4, page S5 | Initial `0.191` mmol/L; `5.79E-2` at 40 min; `2.21E-2` at 65 min; `2.85E-2` at 185 min; `4.03 E-2` at 1490 min | Incomplete removal and a later increase; the three-hour near-total uptake summary is unsupported by this table |
| Sm, Table S3/Figure S3, page S4 | Initial `0.139` mmol/L; `1.52E-3` at 18 min; `6.95E-4` at 53 min; `9.42E-5` at 708 min; `1.06E-4` at 1488 min | First decline is about 98.9%, leaving about 1.09%; later concentrations remain nonzero |

The main narrative's percentage/timing summaries conflict with these exact
cells. The records retain the discrepancies. The table error column is labelled
`RSD [mmol/L]`, but its statistical meaning and replicate count are not defined;
no confidence interval or biological replicate count was constructed. Reported
sampling uncertainty is ±10 min. These measurements stay qualified prose:
the native concentration-observation schema supports `mg/kg` cells, not these
`mmol/L` data. No schema change or unit conversion was warranted.

LIBS indicates Eu or Sm in washed biomass but is explicitly nonquantitative.
The Sm spectrum concerns the separate A. cylindrica assay after 200 h; Eu LIBS
concerns the 0.191 mM starved arm. The evidence does not provide an elemental
mass balance, desorption yield or selective isolation from mixed rare earths.

For Eu, the abstract places particles in vegetative cells while Results,
Figure 8 captions and Conclusion place them in heterocysts. The latter account
is preserved with the contradiction disclosed. Electron density and lack of
Bragg peaks do not identify Eu(0), establish a pure phase, or rule out all
noncrystalline compounds. No crystalline Sm phase was demonstrated in the
2019 assay; this is not an assertion that no later experiment can observe Sm
particles.

## Follow-up and counterevidence search

Searches on 2026-10-11 used the web search provider's default first returned
results, without domain/date filters or extra pagination, except where an
exact official-domain query was specified. Exact queries are retained in JSON.
They covered the primary DOI with correction/retraction terms, supplemental
access, ChEBI identity, SAG/NCBI strain mappings and the later Sm particle
paper. No correction or retraction was identified in the returned bounded
results; this does not establish absence from all indexes.

The later primary
[Fritz et al., Nanomaterials 13:130](https://doi.org/10.3390/nano13010130)
(full XML acquired; published online 2022, journal year 2023) was inspected for
the narrower question of whether it resolves Eu oxidation state. Its Sections
2–4 describe microscopy/image analysis and amorphous Eu deposits, while its
abstract uses bioreduction language. No new Eu oxidation-state assay appears
in the inspected main methods. It therefore does not justify upgrading the
record to Eu(0). Detailed particle-size distributions were not curated; the
full supplementary dataset and high-resolution main Figure 1 were not acquired.

[Fritz et al., NanoImpact 26:100398 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.impact.2022.100398)
reports later Sm nanoparticles in A. cylindrica. Publisher indexed abstract,
Crossref identity and EuropePMC metadata were checked. Full text was not
acquired: publisher access returned 403, the public Elsevier metadata-linked
article API returned 429, and EuropePMC reports no full text. Methods,
chemical-speciation evidence and particle images remain unchecked. This is a
deferred extension, not evidence that the 2019 null phase result was wrong and
not grounds for adding Sm(0) to the record. Obtain legitimate full text before
considering a second mechanism or revising product chemistry.

`b8c15455505ac49222ae60eb5297d3ac4564833d29d22ccceb7b0918491e459b`.

Recovered images have the same per-entry integrity receipts. The second,
later-Eu archive also timed out; only its complete early members were retained.
A successfully recovered member is never represented as a complete archive.
