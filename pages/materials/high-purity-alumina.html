High-purity alumina commodity Critical Material

Refined aluminium oxide product distinguished by its high purity. Geoscience Australia describes HPA as a white crystalline Al2O3 powder characterized by purity levels such as 99.99% Al2O3, with uses in lithium-ion batteries, electronics and optics. This grade-defined commodity is narrower than broad alumina and is distinct from aluminium metal, kaolin feedstock, bauxite and aluminium hydroxide. Its Australian designation specifically names high-purity alumina; it is not transferred to every aluminium-bearing feed or product. No microbial production or purification mechanism is asserted here.
