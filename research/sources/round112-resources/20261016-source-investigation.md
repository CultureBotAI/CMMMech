# Round112 meteorite-feed investigation

Two natural primary-resource identities are supported: [L-chondrite meteorite](../../../data/records/l-chondrite-meteorite.yaml) and [eucrite meteorite](../../../data/records/eucrite-meteorite.yaml). The paired JSON binds the working records, acquisition paths/hashes, exact queries and bounded hidden/ignored/followed dedup search. Raw evidence stays outside Git.

The L-chondrite study uses NWA 869 material transported from Earth to the ISS. Primary Figure 5 and supplementary Tables 2-3 support the reported means but do not establish significant within-ISS PGE enhancement over sterile controls. Note 1 identifies ground fungal contamination and RNAlater/pH limits. Liquid-fraction measurements are not isolated products or mineral-specific dissolution assays.

The EU experiment is terrestrial, using CP1D in glucose-supplemented M9 for 30 days, with three biotic replicates and only one abiotic control. Figure 4B and section 3.4 provide EU-specific platinum summaries; the arbitrary twofold ratio threshold is not a significance test. EU has no exact specimen accession and conflicting supplier wording. Supplementary downloads were not obtained through the tested routes, so their raw cells were not reanalyzed.

Neither resource inherits an element criticality designation. No source exhaustion, economic viability or industrial deployment is claimed. Further work should resolve EU sample/supplement provenance and independently replicate controlled meteorite assays. Zinc-purification residue and aluminium-slag leads remain deferred for full methods access.
