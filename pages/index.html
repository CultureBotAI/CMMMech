Evidence & Curation Contract

CMMMech records enforce rigorous scientific limits:

  • Chemical identity separation: An element (e.g. Lithium) is strictly distinct from its mineral hosts (e.g. Spodumene, Jadarite) and its commercial form.
  • Mechanism evidence boundaries: Laboratory solubilization or binding is explicitly distinguished from full industrial recovery, elemental yield, or isolated pure metal.
  • Negative & control evidence: Abiotic controls, matched acid baselines, and null results are recorded alongside positive extraction claims to prevent inflated efficacy.
  • Structured provenance: Records cite their evidence sources and include material or organism identifiers where verified.
