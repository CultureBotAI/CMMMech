## Yttrium evidence

[Iovinella et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants11101376), PMID 35631801,
PMC9144214, supports washed-biomass Y association with living ACUF 427. The
full published XML methods/results and repository author-manuscript Figures 1
and 3 were inspected. At initial pH 2.5, the article reports 22.43 ± 2.05 micromol
Y/g dry biomass in the single-Y arm versus 5.08 micromol/g in the quaternary
Y/Ce/Eu/Tb arm. These are biomass-fraction measurements, not purified recovery
percentages. For ACUF 427, the reported pH changes in Y association were not
significant; SAG 107.79 behaves differently.

The record preserves 178 micromol/L nominal individual-metal loading, OD550 = 5,
2 mL, 37 °C, 24 h, tilt agitation, triplicate experiments, pre-exposure and
post-exposure washing, metal-only and biomass-only controls, and ICP-MS.
The source does not specify whether replicate experiments used independent
culture preparations.
It uses `other`: neither washing nor elemental analysis separates intracellular
uptake, surface binding and retained precipitate. No transporter gene is
claimed causal. The published main article is authoritative for text; the
repository PDF bears an author-manuscript header and different pagination.

Taxon grounding uses NCBITaxon:83373 **Galdieria**, genus, plus exact ACUF 427.
The 2022 source calls the strain sulphuraria, whereas
[Ciniglia et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.3390/plants14132043), Section 2, calls
it daedala. A genus ID avoids silently assigning the older species identifier;
the newer study contributes nomenclature context only.

Supplementary Tables S1/S2 and Figure S1 were not acquired: the EuropePMC
supplement transfer timed out and a publisher-hosted fallback returned 403.
No source-cell claim relies on them. No exact mg/kg/date observations were
invented from the micromol/g results. Quantitative Y blank recoveries and a
complete fraction balance remain unavailable in the inspected main article.

## Ytterbium evidence

[Horiike and Yamashita 2015](https://doi.org/10.1128/AEM.00300-15),
PMID 25710372, PMC4393457, contains an individual Yb assay despite its Dy title.
The full PMC HTML specificity methods and REE-specific results report 45%
Yb depletion after 3 days in a single-element 100 mg/L chloride treatment with
Penidiella sp. T9. The denominator is initial dissolved Yb. The text says almost
no accumulation occurred without T9; numerical Yb blanks are not supplied.

The cultivation protocol uses glucose/yeast-extract and phosphate-containing
basal salt medium, pH 2.5 buffered with 20 mM potassium hydrogen phthalate-HCl,
30 °C and 120 rpm. The analytical subsection is explicitly labelled Dy by ICP-AES.
Yb-specific calibration, biological replicate numbers and error estimates are
not presented; technical replicate readings described there are not promoted
to biological replication for Yb.

Figures 4 and 5 were visually inspected: their time-course, biological replicate
caption, EDX spectra and Dy/P localization are **Dy only**. They are not Yb
mineralogy or Yb loading evidence. Likewise, quoted yeast phosphate mechanisms
are literature discussion, not T9 Yb experiments. The record uses `other` and
states incomplete depletion, unresolved sorption/uptake/precipitation, absent
Yb mass balance and laboratory scope. NCBI 1391704 resolves to **Penidiella
sp. T9**, a species-ranked unclassified isolate node, not a formally identified
species or complete genome. No supplement link was found in the inspected
article HTML; that bounded observation is not a machine-wide absence claim.

## Deferred evidence and genuine source inconsistencies

[Palmieri et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.3390/microorganisms10112138),
PMC9694017, was fully inspected as XML and publisher PDF. Its printed
178 micromol/L Y and 2.5 mg/mL dry biomass imply a maximum of
**178 / 2.5 = 71.2 micromol Y/g** if every input Y atom is retained. Table 1 instead
reports 122.02 and 158.07 micromol/g. This is an internal mass-balance check under
its stated dosing, not an invented correction or measured replacement.
Figures 1/2 extend to 1440 min while methods/captions describe 360 min. Table 1
places the pH 4.5 Y maximum at 10 min while broad prose says 30 min, and Table 2
has nonsignificant Y pH/time terms. The source stays a deferred quantitative
lead; these defects are not transferred to the different living-cell design.

[Zinicovscaia et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jre.2020.07.019) has a
[two-page repository extract](https://repository.utm.md/handle/5014/26018)
containing abstract, keywords and references only. Its growth inhibition and
interelement ranking are leads, not verified full assays here.
[Jiang et al. 2012](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2012.06.016) has legitimate
publisher, JAEA, Kyushu and NTU metadata/abstract routes, but full methods were
not acquired. Its yeast phosphate mechanism is deferred pending full evidence.

Exact-DOI correction/retraction searches and organism/abiotic/precipitation
queries found related work but no matching correction notice in the returned
sets. Searches were bounded first-page web results, without exhaustive
pagination. This is not proof that corrections or contrary evidence do not exist.
