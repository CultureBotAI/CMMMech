## Selection and identity

Montroseite and paramontroseite are distinct current IMA mineral species. The September 2026 list gives montroseite as V3+O(OH), G, 1953 on PDF page147; paramontroseite as VO2, G, 1955 on page165. G means grandfathered, and these years are original-description years rather than numbered approvals. The rows and definitions were checked in a fresh official download, byte-identical to the prior round100 copy.

The pair supplies a carefully bounded natural alteration relationship and a later independent heating study. Species identity is distinct from elemental vanadium, mixed uranium-vanadium ore and generic VO2 polymorphs. ## Scientific boundaries

The 1953 separates contain altered material and other minerals. Their bulk chemistry and inferred phase-mixture calculations are not pure-endmember composition or recovery yields. The 1955 natural intergrowths support an alteration interpretation but do not directly track hydrogen migration or measure conversion kinetics. The proposed below50°C setting is not a controlled temperature threshold. Diffuse A may be an intermediate or an Fe-rich phase; it remains unidentified.

The 2023 Prachovice sample is Fe/Al-bearing and zoned. Its measured chemistry, refined occupancy and approximate Al-free reaction formula differ. Powder selected from U-free mapped areas still contained about8wt% calcite. Its20°C/min ramp,50-degree steps,2-hour holds and approximately150-minute scans are distinct from the separate6-hour/400°C crystal treatment. Gas composition and oxygen fugacity are unreported. The powder data retain parent phase at400/450°C; Figure7 ends500°C although methods state550°C, and Table3 ends450°C. No pure-VO2 comparison establishes a universal transition.

Section3.2 assigns about65wt% Fe2V4O13 and35wt% V2O5 to the final products, while Figure7 labels show V2O5 as the larger product and retain calcite. No final fraction or recovery yield is selected from this disagreement. The qualitative phase change is supported. An amorphous intermediate is excluded by the authors through an internal-reference inference, not an independently quantified glass assay. Raman irradiation alters crystals; overlapping peaks and the absent Fe2V4O13 reference prevent unique assignments. No observed rutile-type product applies only to the stated heating protocol.
