Kato et al. (2022), DOI **10.3390/ijms23052483**, PMID **35269625**, reports lead-mineral formation with *Pseudomonas* sp. KKY-29. The complete original article XML, all 14 original figures, and the publisher's eight-page supplement were inspected for the proposed hydrocerussite record. They support a narrower mixed-phase observation but leave experimental mappings unresolved.

Evidence gaps:

- Results §2.6/Fig. 10 presents A–D in ascending phosphate order; the Fig. 11 caption gives the reverse order. The diffraction labels associate A with pyromorphite and D with hydrocerussite, while the discussion describes hydrocerussite at low phosphate and pyromorphite at high phosphate. A precise phosphate-to-phase assignment cannot be recovered consistently from these statements.
- Biomass dosing appears as mg/L, mg/mL and, in supplementary Fig. S1, g/mL. Supplementary Figs. S2/S3 support mg/L for their respective assays; this does not justify silently correcting all experimental descriptions.
- Diffraction was measured on processed, freeze-dried/crushed cell products. No matched sterile/killed-cell diffraction series or drying-phase control establishes biological necessity or the phase present before processing.

Follow-up:

1. Obtain a primary correction, author-deposited experiment map or raw metadata identifying A–D phosphate concentrations and the actual biomass concentration for each experiment.
2. Seek the complete synthetic-medium phosphate recipe, pH/shaking metadata and original diffraction files with preparation/control provenance.
3. Reassess the record only against verifiable primary material. Preserve the present mixed-phase, unit and condition limits if these gaps remain unresolved; do not infer a condition-specific yield, phase-specific intracellular location or industrial recovery.
