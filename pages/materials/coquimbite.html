Coquimbite is a trigonal aluminium-ferric-iron sulfate mineral, ideally AlFe(III)3(SO4)6(H2O)12·6H2O. IMA-CNMNC proposal 19-F, approved in October 2019, redefined the species to recognize essential aluminium; the September 2026 mineral list retains that formula and redefined status. The older Al-free formula Fe2(SO4)3·9H2O is not the current species definition. Coquimbite and paracoquimbite have related structures and form the coquimbite group; aluminocoquimbite has a different topology and is not a member of that group. In Mauro et al. 2020, a natural Monte Arsiccio specimen was characterized by electron microprobe, spectroscopy and single-crystal diffraction. Twenty microprobe spots gave the empirical formula Al0.96Fe3.11S5.97O24·18H2O, with water calculated from the structure rather than directly measured. Table 3 and the associated CIF model an Al-dominant site with occupancy Al0.946(7)Fe0.054(7). These sample-specific chemical and structural results support the revised identity without implying an exactly ideal bulk composition. The study establishes mineral identity and structure; it does not test microbial formation, extraction or recovery.
