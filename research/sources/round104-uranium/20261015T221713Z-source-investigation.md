### Metatorbernite

The 2003 analogue was grown in copper-bearing gel at room temperature over three weeks. The fitted copper occupancy of 88.3(6)% belongs to that twinned P4/n crystal; oxonium compensation is proposed, not proven. The 2023 natural Cornwall specimen instead supports the authors' preferred P4/nmm refinement. Its powder air-heating figure reports changes at about 353, 373 and 483 K. Mixtures are assigned 313/463 K in Results but 303/473 K in Discussion. Figure4's start labels also differ from the method range. The record retains these differences and does not assign water stoichiometry from diffraction alone.

The 2019 synthetic phosphate end member was dried at 40 degrees C and studied in a sealed capillary. Figure S5 shows a distinct 423 K trace, largely diffuse 473 K trace, and changed diffraction after cooling to293 K. The article variously says at473 K, greater than473 K, greater than423 K and above373 K. This supports a qualitative sequence rather than one corrected onset. The a/c trend is fitted geometry, not a recovered-metal yield.

Only2a is explicitly assigned to single-crystal refinement. Figure5 vacuum-heating data are labelled merely2; no exact2a/2b assignment is assumed. The 2019 synthetic arsenate end member is orthorhombically indexed at110 K, mixed at293 K on reheating, and tetragonal by323 K; its unknown high-temperature phase is variously located at473 K, above373 K or150 degrees C. ### Biological follow-up, not an accepted mechanism

Purvis et al. describe field-collected *Trapelia involuta* on heterogeneous South Terras mineral substrates and a Scottish control, with hyphae touching metatorbernite/metazeunerite plates. Page161 reports no observed etch pits or rounding at those contacts. Metal localization, histochemistry and FTIR support a proposed melanin-like biosorption interpretation; the probe traverses are adjusted counts rather than calibrated concentrations. These are neither controlled mineral-dissolution trials nor recovery balances. The 1998 McLean correspondence is related preliminary work, not independent proof of the expanded2004 results.
