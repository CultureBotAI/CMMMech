### Measured oxide chemistry, censoring and estimated water

quantity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kernite.

The record preserves units, methods and specimen-specific denominators without turning composition into recovery.

Tables 1-3 report B2O3 50.90 +/- 0.20 wt% by titration and H2O 26.40 +/- 0.20 wt% estimated from mass loss during 20-800 C heating and 60 min at 800 C; these are not isolated water or boron recovery. Li2O below 0.01 wt% and REE oxide signals below element-specific detection limits are censored, not zero. ICP B/Na measurements rejected as nonreproducible in the original are not substituted for accepted methods. Prose 22.6(2) versus Table 3 22.55 +/- 0.20 wt% Na2O is compatible rounding, not a supported contradiction.

### Hydrogen disorder and endpoint temperature comparison

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kernite.

Modelled hydrogen disorder remains a preferred interpretation, not a new species or measured stability interval.

X-ray 293 K and neutron 20 K data retain the general structure. Two endpoints do not measure continuous thermal stability. Neutron refinement and maximum-entropy maps support positional disorder for two water-hydrogen populations; static disorder is favored, but proton tunneling was not completely excluded. Deposited Tables S1-S3 were not obtained, so this review does not re-refine raw data or verify exact supplemental occupancies. Main-text methods, maps and Table 4 support the appropriately qualitative record.
