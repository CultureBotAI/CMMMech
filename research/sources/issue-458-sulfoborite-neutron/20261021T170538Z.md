## What the complete 1983 original supports

The specimen is a transparent natural Westeregeln crystal from Royal Ontario Museum catalogue M6918; a roughly 0.2 mm fragment was measured with Mo Kα radiation (p256). The orthorhombic Pnma model has four formula units per cell. Both original pp255–256 print a/b/c = 10.132/12.537/7.775 Å, each ±0.001 Å. Their product is approximately 987.618 Å³, a conditional arithmetic check. The currently indexed publisher abstract's c = 1.715 Å is inconsistent with the original images and is not used. The original reports 1,502 unique reflections with 14 unobserved, empirical absorption correction up to 8%, and final R = 0.044. These are the article's historical refinement summaries, not newly reproduced results.

The model distinguishes two Mg octahedra and B/S tetrahedra, with sheets and connecting tetrahedra shown in Figures 1–2. Its F assignment depends on residual electron density, local charge requirements and the missing nearby hydrogen peak (pp257–258). The authors expressly state that neither their crystal nor the compared earlier crystal had a chemical analysis (p260). Earlier analyses of other material cannot be attached to M6918 as its assay. No temperature is assigned to this experiment from a secondary database's 293 K field.

All seven original pages and three authority pages were actually viewed. A manual audit records 289 selected numeric components: all Table 1 positional tokens and standard deviations, five Table 2 hydrogen isotropic parameters, every listed Table 4 distance/mean, Table 5 geometry and Table 6 nonblank contributions/sums. The Table 2 anisotropic matrix was visually inspected but not fully rekeyed. `numeric-audit.json` retains literal values, nulls and conditional calculations outside Git; no coordinate refinement or uncertainty propagation is claimed.

The model has five independent hydrogen sites and assigns hydroxyls rather than molecular water. Table 5's listed O–H distances range from 0.62(4) to 0.78(4) Å; three Table 2 H isotropic values are negative and remain uncensored. These are historical X-ray model limitations, not physical negative motion or measured water absence across every sulfoborite sample. The article treats two contacts as hydrogen bonds and explicitly judges the O6···O2 distance 3.164 Å too long for that classification. The other listed donor–acceptor distances are 2.678 and 2.837 Å. Reproducing its interpretation does not establish that the 2026 neutron study must use the same network definition.

Literal table cautions remain separate from owner defects. Table 2's Mg2 B12 cell shows `(10)` without a central value; it is not converted to zero. The final Table 5 angle row prints H(7), although Table 1 lists H1–H5. Conditional multiplicity-weighted Mg and B bond means reproduce the printed rounding, while the displayed S distances yield about 1.47325 Å versus the printed mean 1.476 Å. Table 6's printed row sums total 16.74, whereas its printed total is 16.84; the displayed O1 terms sum to 1.83 versus row sum 2.01, and O8 terms to 2.09 versus row sum 1.99. These diagnostics identify reconciliation needs without selecting authoritative replacement numbers or reconstructing missing contributions. None is imported into an accepted measurement field.

## Newer evidence and curation prerequisites

The Edinburgh abstract describes specimen chemistry and low-temperature neutron diffraction, with a more extensive hydrogen-bond interpretation. That is sufficient to justify obtaining the later study; it is insufficient to accept its numerical composition, occupancies, detection limits, displacement model, hydrogen-bond geometry or specimen-to-specimen comparison. The current record already retains this boundary. The same locality does not demonstrate that the 1983 and 2026 specimens are identical or chemically interchangeable.

Priority 1 is the complete accepted/final 2026 article and all relevant original supplements or native models. Its benefit is direct resolution of #458; access and exact-version identification are dependencies, followed by reading all chemistry/methods/tables/figures. Record specimen catalogue and provenance, EPMA standards/detection limits/normalization and sample correspondence, neutron instrument/wavelength/temperature, absorption/extinction/refinement constraints, F/OH occupancies, hydrogen-site treatment and the stated hydrogen-bond criteria. Compare those with the historical model and current IMA notation without transferring one crystal's composition to the whole species.

Priority 2 is the separate 1983 AM82-212 reflection deposit and an authentic model file, if numerical refinement comparison is required. The printed tables are a usable audit target, but cannot substitute for raw reflections, constraints or a missing uncertainty covariance. A later AMCSD transcription would need an exact source/table join and would remain same-study derived data. No bulk import is proposed.

A supported future change would target the existing owner's `description` and `sources[]`, retaining specimen and method qualifiers. Structural geometry and fractional coordinates do not fit the mg/kg concentration-observation contract and do not warrant inventing a mechanism or measurement class. Any material owner change requires canonical EDIT history, generated pages and an independent native record review. This source-only pass creates no such acceptance or history event.
