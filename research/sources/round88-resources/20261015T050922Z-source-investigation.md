## Accepted primary assessment

Wieczorek's waste is from wet-blue processing at a Polish tannery, described as 1–5 mm, 15% moisture and aqueous-extract pH 3.09. Its listed 4.4 ± 0.2% Cr(III) oxide, ash and nitrogen are attributed to earlier literature; this is not treated as independently measured current-lot composition. IPS21 was reported isolated from soil near a former dye factory. Methods use 40 mL medium in 100 mL flasks, 0.1–4% wt/wt shavings, 0.4/0.8 mL inoculum, 30°C, 190 rpm and 48 h, initial pH 7.07 and 121°C for 20 min autoclaving. Medium yeast extract is a separate nutrient source. Abstract NaCl 5 g/L versus stock-method 2.5 g/L and the printed OD600 `1,000` remain unresolved.

All five original graphics were inspected at legible resolution, alongside both original tables. Figures 1–3 support conditional enzyme-activity and pH responses. Figure 4 shows higher operational liquid Cr and lower combined solid-residue contribution in the 1% yeast arm versus its uninoculated comparator. Figure 4 stacks concentrations labelled g/kg across liquid and solid fractions, with no explained common mass basis, fraction masses, density or closed inventory. Its Results' 10.31 g/kg assignment to 0.1% yeast disagrees with the plotted value near 10 for 1% uninoculated supernatant. No exact extraction yield or reconstructed corrected measurement is adopted.

Table 1's 1% degradation-rate values exceed 4%, contrary to the text's optimum narrative. The unspecified biomass-derived calculation cannot be treated as a collagen or chromium recovery balance. The combined biomass/remaining-feed solids do not independently establish intracellular Cr exclusion; no pH-matched or enzyme-inactivated causal series separates proteolysis, alkalinization, sorption and possible precipitation. Protease and amino-acid findings do not establish all solutes originated from the shavings.

All five supplemental files were parsed and visually inspected: file 1 two-page amino-acid table; file 2 one-page Cr(VI) results; file 3a four-page sequence printout (fourth page blank); file 3b one-page identification report; file 4 one-page plate photographs (stored names `s0001`…`s0005`). The sequence/report samples 1304–1306 include two bacteria and Y. lipolytica C/1306. No explicit IPS21 crosswalk or public accession is supplied.
