Potassium feldspar family mineral group

Potassium feldspar (K-feldspar) is an informal family of potassium-rich alkali feldspars, including the microcline, orthoclase and sanidine structural forms; it is represented by the mineral_group kind without claiming an IMA-approved group name or a single mineral species. Whitney and Evans (2010), reproduced in the IMA-hosted nomenclature compendium, explicitly labels K-feldspar informal. Freeman et al. (2008) distinguishes these structural forms and the compositional end-member notation from an individual orthoclase phase. This family is distinct from the traded feldspar commodity and potash. The experimental feed below is only author-designated potassium feldspar; bulk oxide composition does not resolve its member species or phase proportions. No criticality designation is inherited.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

PMA2-2 culture-associated aluminium mobilization from a potassium-feldspar feed

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Priestia megaterium (NCBITaxon:1404) [TaxonMech]

PMA2-2 cultures increased filtered Al concentrations relative to uninoculated potassium-feldspar-feed controls, with accompanying acidification. This is an Al-release assay; neither potassium release nor production of a feldspar or potash commodity was established by these measurements.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Living Priestia megaterium PMA2-2, isolated from Dracaena cochinchinensis rhizosphere soil in Purple Mountain, Nanjing, China, was precultured in LB for 12 hours at 37 degrees C and 160 rpm, harvested, washed and resuspended in 0.85% NaCl. The other-mineral assays followed the dissolution protocol: triplicate 220 mL flasks with 60 mL sterilized glucose minimal medium, 0.60 g of 75-150 micrometre mineral feed and 0.2 mL of 10^8 cells/mL suspension, incubated at 37 degrees C and 160 rpm, with separate flasks for 12- and 24-hour endpoints. Medium preparation pH was 7.0; its stated composition was 0.015% MgSO4.7H2O, 0.05% Na2HPO4.2H2O, 0.025% (NH4)2SO4, 0.01% KNO3 and 0.5% glucose. Element measurements used ICP-OES after centrifugation and 0.45 micrometre polyethersulfone filtration of a 10 mL aliquot; these are operationally filtered total-element concentrations, without oxidation-state resolution. Feed origin, cleaning procedure and quantitative phase analysis were not specified in the inspected methods and supplement. Table S7 reports 19.24% Al2O3, 60.57% SiO2, 9.87% K2O and 4.51% Na2O, plus minor oxides, for this feed. These bulk data do not justify substituting a microcline, orthoclase or sanidine species identifier.
