## Source assessment

Only Ni4225 and SCCIII-14-20s are called voltaite in this five-sample study. Ni4225 is the 1988 Nikitovka coal-fire collection; SCCIII-14-20s comes from the Bolshoi Semiachik geothermal field. Original pp3–4/Figures2–3 show field occurrences and intimate associations with other sulfates. The estimated formation temperature at or below100°C is geological interpretation, not a controlled synthesis protocol. Single selected crystals and averages of chemical analyses are different evidence populations; a grain-by-grain chemistry/refinement join and full raw analytical ledger were not acquired.

Methods pp4–5 distinguish carbon-tape/carbon-coated grains from polished epoxy preparations. EDS was used to limit beam damage in hydrated minerals. Three laboratories used20kV with different currents, beam dimensions and standards; no unsupported assignment of a particular instrument to each of these two specimens is made. Table4 footnote3 explicitly says the listed Ni4225 and SCCIII analyses were unpolished and normalized to100wt%. Its `n` values are nine and twenty averaged analyses, not independently replicated formation/recovery experiments. Neither a distribution nor uncertainty for each chemical mean is printed there. `n.d.` means not detected; dashes in the formula table are retained as such, not zero-valued assays.

The central qualification is stronger than simply calling all Table4 entries measured EDS values. Footnote2 says N was detected but determined with wide deviations, so ammonium oxide was recalculated using a monovalent-cation sum of2.00. Section3.2/p8 calculates Fe oxidation-state proportions by charge balance, assigns water at18 molecules and normalizes tetrahedral(S,P,V) to12. Table4 p9 nevertheless prints Ni NH4/Na/K=0.11/0.07/0.82 (sum1.00) and SCC0.14/0.04/0.84 (sum1.02). Those disagree with its own footnote2 target. The printed numeric oxide entries each sum100.00, but a normalized total cannot select the intended correction. No cations are doubled and no vacancy, transcription or normalization cause is accepted by inference.

Table2 p6 gives Ni N/K occupancies0.365(14)/0.635(14), and SCC0.088(16)/0.912(16), each summing1.000 at the model site. These are site fractions, not a claim that the mineral formula has only one A cation. The article's ideal A2 formula and full site model remain distinct from Table4 chemistry. For Ni, the ratio of printed chemical NH4 and K excluding Na is11.828/88.172%, consistent with the printed12/88; it differs from the refined36.5/63.5%. For SCC the same arithmetic gives14.286/85.714%, whereas the literal chemical ratio is14/84. Its sum98 is preserved, not silently normalized.

The Fe/Mg boundary also remains qualified: SCC chemical Fe(II)/Mg2.54/2.51 gives Fe/(Fe+Mg)=50.297%, a difference of0.03pfu; selected-crystal fractions0.508(8)/0.492(8) are separate estimates. Ni chemical2.69/2.29 gives54.016%, and its refined fractions are0.531(7)/0.469(7). Table4's structural synopsis prints50/50 for both; that synopsis is not treated as exactly equal to every Table2 value. No statistical species reassignment follows from absent chemical dispersion or from mixing these modalities.

Additional printed-source inconsistencies are context, not new repository findings.
