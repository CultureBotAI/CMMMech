## Titanium: passive-film transformation

Accepted primary source: [Li et al., National Science Review 13(16), nwag426 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/nsr/nwag426).
The complete final published PDF was obtained from the authors' TU Delft
repository, with an extra repository cover page. The OUP landing route returned
403 locally; its public minimal-article route supplied the full text and actual
supplement link. The 14-page supplement was acquired and inspected. Both are
CC-BY-4.0; raw PDFs remain outside Git under `/private/tmp/cmm-round10-ti-zr-raw`.
The journal issue is August 2026; registered online publication is 11 July 2026.

Precise locators:

- Main printed pages 2-4 / PDF pages 3-5, Figure 1 and Figure 2: oxygen
  gradients, FIB/TEM passive-film thickness, TOF-SIMS and XPS comparisons,
  and Tafel-derived corrosion current. Figures 1f's ten sampled image locations
  are distinct from Figure 2's three independent experimental replicates.
- Printed page 6 / PDF page 7 and Figure 5 on printed page 7 / PDF page 8:
  riboflavin-overproducing RF, riboflavin amendments, abiotic controls and the
  separate anaerobic/lactate experiment. The DFT panels are predictions;
  fluorescence in Figure S7 is a different abiotic assay.
- Supplement pages 2-3 (S1/S2), 9 (S7), 11 (S9), 12-14 (methods): initial
  medium pH, evolving pH, spectral fits, genetic constructs, seawater recipe,
  deoxygenation and analytical limitations. Main Figures 1-6 and relevant
  supplementary S1/S2/S7/S9 pixels were inspected, not just their captions.

The actual primary identifies *Shewanella algae* as MCC 1A11468. The species
[NCBI:38313](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=38313&mode=Info)
was resolved independently; it does not verify strain identity. The 2024
related study spells the same number MCCC 1A11468, whereas the 2026 record
preserves its source spelling. The RF strain contains genes from MR-1;
MR-1 is not independently tested as a second organism in this mechanism.

The record preserves four limitations that would otherwise overstate the study.
First, the main article and supplement omit incubation temperature, starting
cell density and the concentrations of lactate and added riboflavin. Second,
Figure 5g reuses the earlier Figure S2 abiotic data for its anaerobic comparison;
a separately matched anaerobic XPS control is not documented. Its 80.6% TiO2
label also differs from the 84.2% label at 3 nm in Figure 1h. Third, the source
itself acknowledges charging, deconvolution and ion-sputtering reduction
uncertainties. Fourth, CRISPRi construction is described in supplementary
methods, but the inspected results provide no repression outcome. Accordingly,
the record does not claim a demonstrated MtrCAB requirement, exact oxide
conversion yield, recovered Ti product or an industrial process.

The related [Li et al. 2024 oxygen study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.corsci.2024.112400)
was acquired as a complete 16-page TU Delft PDF. Its abstract, methods and
conclusion were inspected as a conditional-effect lead: different oxygen
conditions alter corrosion. Its figures and supplements were not audited, so
its numerical outcomes and 25 degrees C/inoculum/oxygen settings were not
transferred into the 2026 record. This is a different experiment, not direct
refutation of the narrower 2026 observations. Its Taverne availability and
Elsevier copyright do not authorize redistribution; raw content stays outside
Git.

## Zirconium: culture preparation and calcination

Accepted primary source: [Koşarsoy Ağçeli et al. (2023), 16(1):224-238](https://doi.org/10.18185/erzifbed.1174027).
The DOI resolves to the DergiPark journal landing page and its complete 15-page
PDF was acquired. The PDF prints 30 March 2023 while Crossref says 31 March;
the citation deliberately uses the shared year. An explicit reuse license was
not located in the inspected article/landing page, and the PDF is not committed.
The landing page exposes one Full Text file; no additional supplement was
linked in the inspected article-file section or PDF. This is bounded access
coverage, not a claim that no other version exists.

Sections 2.1-2.3 (printed pages 226-227 / PDF pages 3-4) describe HC1,
K2ZrF6, culture growth and recovery of a solid followed by drying and 450 degrees C
calcination. Figures 1-6 (printed pages 229-232 / PDF pages 6-9) were inspected
visually. Microscopy and labelled Zr/O EDX support a Zr-bearing particulate
preparation; they do not establish a quantitative recovery yield. No verified
strain accession was provided by the paper, and none is invented.

The accepted claim is deliberately about the reported process and its final
material. Uninoculated medium controls are described, but their synthesis
outcomes are not reported in the full text or figures inspected. There is no
separate pre-calcination phase analysis. Figure 4 contains both synthetic
Tazheranite ZrO2 and Sylvite KCl reference patterns, while the prose refers to
tetragonal and monoclinic phases. Pure single-phase zirconia and microbial
necessity therefore remain unproven. Zr is formally +4 in both hexafluorozirconate
and ZrO2, so the paper's NADH/reductase discussion does not establish Zr reduction.

Other preserved discrepancies are LB in Methods versus Nutrient Broth in
Results; pH 5-7 in the Results protocol versus additional pH 8/9 curves in
Figure 1; number versus volume weighting in Figures 1 and 2; and a poorly
specified relationship between the claimed nanometre sizes and the
micrometre-scale aggregate image. No optimum particle size, enzyme mechanism,
metal yield or industrial deployment is adopted. The antimicrobial assays
test prepared particles and do not validate the synthesis mechanism.

  a more specific protein/hydrolysis lead; both official RSC full-text routes

  replace a fully inspected original article with figures. Resume acquisition.
- [Órdenes-Aenishanslins et al., B. mycoides TiO2 (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12934-014-0090-7):
  full PMC XML was read, but the synthesis lacks a matched abiotic blank and
  the anatase claim relies on an optical band-gap interpretation rather than

  PDF filename. Keep this separate from 2026 corrosion; it may support a
  carefully limited additional mechanism after figure acquisition and review.
