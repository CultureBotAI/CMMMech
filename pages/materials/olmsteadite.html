Olmsteadite mineral

Olmsteadite (IMA 1974-034) is an IMA-accepted hydrated potassium-iron-niobium phosphate mineral with IMA September 2026 Master List formula KFe2+2NbO2(PO4)2(H2O)2 and structural formula K2Fe2+2[Fe2+2(Nb,Ta)5+2O4(H2O)4(PO4)4] (with site-refined Nb0.709(5)Ta0.291(5) in the Big Chief holotype). Described by Moore et al. (1976) from the Big Chief pegmatite, near Glendale, Pennington County, South Dakota, USA (holotype), and the Hesnard pegmatite, near Keystone, Custer County, South Dakota, USA, where it forms deep brown to black prismatic crystals associated with siderite, quartz, rockbridgeite, and hureaulite during late hydrothermal alteration of triphylite-lithiophilite. Orthorhombic (space group Pb21m; refined to R = 0.037 for 1846 reflections), with a = 7.512(1) Å, b = 10.000(3) Å, c = 6.492(2) Å, Z = 1 (for K2Fe4Nb2...; Z = 2 for KFe2Nb...), SGmeas = 3.31(1) (Big Chief) and 3.36(1) (Hesnard), Dcalc = 3.41 g/cm3 (pure Nb endmember) to 3.59 g/cm3 (Nb0.71Ta0.29), Mohs hardness 4, and biaxial (+) optics with alpha = 1.725(5), beta = 1.755(5), gamma = 1.815(5), and 2V ~ 60°.
