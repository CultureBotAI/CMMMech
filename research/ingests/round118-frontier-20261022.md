Every record was curated after full-text visual inspection of 100% of the primary mineralogical and IMA authority PDFs:
- `petersenite-ce` (`IMA:1992-048`), `lukechangite-ce` (`IMA:1996-033`), `qaqarssukite-ce` (`IMA:2004-019`), `horvathite-y` (`IMA:1996-032`), and `sheldrickite` (`IMA:1996-019`) preserve Poudrette quarry and Qaqarssuk carbonatite cotype WDS compositions, single-crystal refinements (`P21`, `P63/mmc`, `P-3c1`, `Pmcn`, `P32`), and bounded `N2` TGA dehydration for `sheldrickite`.
- `calkinsite-ce` (`1987 s.p.`, status `Rn`) preserves the Bearpaw Mountains wet-chemical and XRF analysis (`Ce2(CO3)3·4H2O`), admixed barite/ancylite impurity deduction, and the Table 5 column 3 `La2O3` molar-quotient misprint (`0.608` printed for `0.0608`).
- `sabinaite` (`IMA:1978-071`), `gaidonnayite` (`IMA:1973-008`), `gittinsite` (`IMA:1979-034`), `lemoynite` (`IMA:1968-013`), `natrolemoynite` (`IMA:1996-063`), and `weloganite` (`IMA:1967-042`) pair original species descriptions with their complete subsequent crystal-structure refinements (`C2/c`, `P21nb` with the 1986 `O(5)` `y = 0.0149` correction, `C2`, `C2/c`, `C2/m`, and `P1`) and bounded laboratory thermal transformations.
- `bijvoetite-y` (`1987 s.p.`, status `Rn`), `retzian-nd` (`1982 s.p.`, status `A`), and `okanoganite-y` (`1987 s.p.`, status `Rn`) preserve Shinkolobwe, Sterling Hill, and Golden Horn batholith type analyses and crystal-structure refinements (`B1211`, `Pban`, and `R3m`) without fabricating numbered IMA CURIEs.
