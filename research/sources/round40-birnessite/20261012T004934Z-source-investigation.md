## Contradictions and limits

- No initial batch composition/polytype or quantitative phase purity reported.
- Visual clearing and growth measures do not quantify metal extraction; reprecipitation prevents equating clearing to aqueous recovery.
- Separate fungal arms; Serpula-specific diffraction not transferred to Aspergillus.
- Hot-water recovery and vacuum drying may affect hydration; no matched wet-versus-dried phase series.
- No direct metabolite or Mn oxidation-state measurements; organic-acid cause remains interpreted.
- No killed-biomass or pH-matched fungal controls; acid-well tests separate.
- 23-day growth/microscopy and25-day Serpula pH/clearing are separate sampling descriptions.
- TaxIDs resolve species names only; no ATCC or Serpula isolate authentication.
- No criticality list assessment; omission does not mean not listed.
