# Round 14 material frontier

The corpus contains **119 records**: 64 elements, 30 commodities, 14 minerals and 11 secondary resources. Eighteen additions follow the [previous source priorities](round13-frontier-20261011.md). Six are identity-only commodities; pyrolusite includes an explicitly abiotic alternative; eleven include scoped microbial-origin evidence. Related records can describe one experiment from different material perspectives, not independent replication.

The unchanged 94-candidate authority inventory remains **88 exact roots represented, one designation-specific alias and five deferred grade umbrellas**. These eighteen additions sit outside that inventory. Narrow product specifications do not settle every jurisdiction's grade definition or transfer constituent criticality to a salt, alloy or waste.
