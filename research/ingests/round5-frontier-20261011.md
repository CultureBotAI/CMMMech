# Fifth expansion frontier

This is an exact-root reconciliation of the historical authority inventory
against the five new round5 records and unchanged prior corpus. Base:
`e305dcc67e526e585349c60f849567f4c2bfc835`. The [JSON snapshot](round5-frontier-20261011.json)
records the actual UTC creation time, every record hash, prior-snapshot hash,
authority references and all outstanding candidate keys.

There are **32 records: 26 elements, 4 mineral species and 2 secondary
resources**. Of 94 historical proposals, 26 exact element roots are represented;
**68 remain unrepresented: 38 elements and 30 commodity proposals**.
Commodity proposals overlap and require concept adjudication before curation.
The six existing mineral/resource roots are outside this authority inventory.
These are coverage counts, not counts of independent studies or verified
mechanisms for every jurisdiction designation.

Enumeration uses `rg --files --hidden --no-ignore data/records` and parses every
YAML. Deduplication also includes hidden/ignored research, history, reviews and
cited task downloads; unrelated home/temp trees and all possible literature
are outside the boundary. Root IDs and kinds are compared explicitly. No
element substitutes for an ore grade, mineral species or waste stream.
