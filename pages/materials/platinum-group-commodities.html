This collective commodity family comprises ruthenium, rhodium, palladium, osmium, iridium and platinum. The USGS platinum-group-elements category identifies these six related metals; Australia's 20 February 2024 Critical Minerals List explicitly includes the same six in its platinum-group-elements entry. This is a grouped commodity scope, overlapping the individual element records, rather than a single element, mineral species, fixed-composition alloy or waste feedstock. The dated designation below applies to Australia's explicitly enumerated six-member group. Identity and designation are curated here; no shared microbial recovery performance is asserted for all six metals.
