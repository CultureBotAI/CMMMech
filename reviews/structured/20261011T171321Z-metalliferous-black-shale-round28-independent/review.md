evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:metalliferous-black-shale.

Maintained Heap-1 observations match original methods and Table 3. Culture-associated Fe/Mn changes are conditional; equal Cu and below-control Mn are preserved. Without added iron, pH1.5 Fe is 103.00 versus 74.25 mg/dm3. Jarosite/gypsum are reported in both inoculated and abiotic residues.

### Bacillus mixture after acid pretreatment

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:metalliferous-black-shale.

The reported reactor Cu/Ni values are correctly scoped to metals remaining in the washed, previously acid-bioleached Lubin feed. Broth mismatch and thymol confound biological attribution; temperature-legend/prose conflict and printed flask loading inconsistency remain explicit. Phenotypic names and leaching curves do not prove strain identity or metal-organic bond cleavage.

### Mixed-community energy-substrate comparisons

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:metalliferous-black-shale.

All arms are inoculated; WZ-1 is an unamended culture rather than an abiotic control. Ferrous addition raises the source Mo estimate but lowers V/Ni; pyrite lowers all three. Exact annotated values match original Figures 5–7. The record correctly treats these as residue-derived estimates and preserves uncertain dose basis, undefined error bars and absent mass balance.

WZ-1/2/3/4 annotated values:V 66.0/59.5/66.5/65.3%; Ni 82.4/80.6/82.5/80.8%; Mo 9.7/29.7/12.9/7.7%. The0.5 percentage-point V and0.1 Ni sulfur-arm differences are not represented as statistically proven improvements. Operational fractions do not uniquely identify host mineral phases.
