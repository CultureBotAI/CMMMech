## Original evidence and claim boundaries

All 63 rendered original pages were viewed: AAM 1–33, Erd 1–9, Konnert 1–16, supplement 1–4 and final first page. The full native CIF was read. The numerical audit is a specified selection, not a claim to have transcribed every historical powder-table cell or all unrelated comparative-mineral numbers.

**Powder evidence.** Supplement Figure S1, p. 2, shows the black product trace above red JCPDS 73-0283 reference sticks, with 2θ on the horizontal axis, arbitrary-unit intensity and wavelength 1.2358 Å. Its caption identifies the 5:1:1 H3BO3:CaCl2·2H2O:NaOH mixture at 120 °C. The caption itself does not state a duration; the 12-hour synthesis comes from AAM pp. 6–7. The original supports the manuscript’s powder comparison. It supplies neither a quantitative multiphase fit nor a phase fraction, raw intensity series, recovered mass or independent replicate. No purity or recovery percentage is estimated from the image.

**Selected-crystal model.** Supplement Table S1, p. 3, gives a = 12.872(4), b = 16.326(4), c = 6.5634(18) Å, β = 121.319(3)°, V = 1178.3(6) Å³, Z = 4, Mo Kα wavelength 0.71073 Å, 296(2) K, 5319 measured and 1970 unique reflections, 1730 in the printed observed subset, 240 parameters, R1 = 0.0413, wR2 = 0.1161 and goodness of fit 1.478. The same cell/model is in the CIF; agreement is a source-version join, not an independent crystallographic replication. Table S2, p. 4, has 32 atom sites, including ten H sites and 22 anisotropic non-H sites. **Hydrogen treatment.** AAM p. 9 describes O–H distances fixed at 0.95 Å and H–H distances constrained to 1.50 Å. The deposited CIF instead explicitly declares `_refine_ls_hydrogen_treatment refall` and `_refine_ls_number_restraints 13`; the H position flags are `D`. The ten O–H distances in the model reproduce AAM Table 2, p. 32: 0.916(19), 0.95(2), 0.950(19), 0.931(19), 0.940(18), 0.949(19), 0.958(19), 0.94(2), 0.941(18) and 0.943(18) Å. The model supports refinement of H parameters with geometric restraints, not an unconstrained determination. A count of 13 does not establish how the restraints were assigned: no `.ins`/`.res`, embedded refinement-instruction block or individual `_refln_` observation loop is present in these inspected archives/CIF. Summary `_reflns_` fields do occur. Exact target assignments and weights remain unverified. Rounded-coordinate distances are diagnostics of the deposited coordinates, not a re-refinement or uncertainty propagation.

**Other source inconsistencies remain literal.** Table S1 prints Fo > 4σ(Fo); the AAM and published first page use Fo ≥ 4σ(Fo), whereas the CIF threshold expression is I > 2σ(I). These labels are not silently equated or repaired. The CIF’s absorption-correction type is `none`, while its process text and AAM discuss SADABS; the conflict is retained. A CIF value in the measured-density field is accompanied by method `calculated` and agrees with the calculated-density field, so it is not counted as an independent density measurement.

**Synthesis and failed replication.** Erd pp. 917–918 describes a stirred 35 °C, 24-hour arm from 20 g calcium acetate monohydrate plus 40 g boric acid in 100 mL water, and a distinct unstirred room-temperature, 18-day single-crystal arm.
