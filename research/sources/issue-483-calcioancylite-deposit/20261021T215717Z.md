## Scientific assessment

### Natural material and measurement boundaries

The original p.554 supports approval IMA2021-090 and type-material catalogue M16129 at the Geological Museum of China. These are species and specimen identifiers, respectively. Figure 1's SEM specimen label `18CL18-5` is retained separately; neither label makes every measurement an independent biological or recovery experiment. The material occurs as 5–20 µm crystals in aggregates/veinlets among other minerals at Gejiu. The inference of late carbonation follows texture, not controlled formation or extraction.

Five EPMA spots underpin the reported mean formula (pp.555–556, Table 1). The JXA-8100 used WDS, 15 kV, 10 nA and a 1 µm beam, with named standards. Individual five-spot values are not in the acquired supplements. Mixed La/Ce/Pr/Nd/Ca/Sr/K chemistry is distinct from the La/Ca scattering proxy. CO2 and water are calculated using C = 2 apfu, stoichiometry and charge balance. Raman bands at 3529 and 3604 cm−1 support hydrous groups but do not independently quantify water; Raman was collected on the XRD crystal in the polished section with a 532 nm laser, 20 mW, 1 µm spot and 50× objective. Possible Al-bearing inclusions/films explain exclusion of Al from the formula as an interpretation; they were not separately identified phases.

The approximately 16 × 7 × 5 µm FIB-extracted colourless plate was measured with Cu Kα radiation, wavelength 1.54184 Å, at 296.15 K. Powder Gandolfi data and single-crystal data used the same named instrument but remain distinct measurements. Powder cell volume 312.32(36) Å³ is not substituted for single-crystal 311.17(4) Å³. The latter has 1,054 collected, 360 unique and 347 thresholded reflections, Rint 0.0526 and R1 0.0652 for the stated I > 2σ(I) subset. Table 3's R-row instead prints Fₒ > 4σ(Fₒ); the conventions must not be silently equated. Cell-refinement metadata's 663 reflections and theta interval are a distinct subset, not a contradictory total.

### Chemistry, formula weight and density

Table 1 means sum exactly to the printed 98.36 wt%, including −0.13 fluorine/oxygen correction. Calculated CO2 plus H2O contribute 29.02 wt%; that is not measured volatile loss. The fluorine mean is 0.31 wt% while its printed range is 0–0.16 wt% and SD 0.07. The mean exceeds its range by 0.15 wt%; no selection or decimal repair is justified. `bdl` and blank fields are retained as nonnumeric missingness, not zero observations.

The reported Nd2O3 mean 2.49 wt% versus Nd 0.10 apfu remains a normalization question. Conditional conversion using the printed CO2 22.86 wt% and C = 2 gives approximately 0.05699 Nd apfu. This calculation identifies a reconciliation need; it is not a replacement empirical formula and does not recover the missing individual analyses or their normalization protocol.

Table 3 formula weight 726.26 is exactly twice CIF 363.13. Combining 726.26 with the printed Z = 2 and V = 311.17 gives about 7.7513 g/cm³, while CIF weight 363.13 gives 3.87564, matching the reported proxy density 3.876. This locates an internal scale problem but does not prove which field an author intended to correct. The deposited formula sum (`C2 Ca0.67 La1.32 O8`), moiety (`C2 Ca0.677 La1.323 O8`) and rounded sites (`C2 Ca0.68 La1.32 O8`) also differ. The paper's structural formula includes H2.68 that is absent from the native atom/formula model. None is an independent mixed-element assay.

The checkCIF report compares a doubled calculated atom formula at Z = 1 with the reported half formula at Z = 2; its 0.99 molecular-weight alert is not permission to halve/double a catalog value by guesswork. Conditional rounded-site mass is 362.6223 g/mol, giving density 3.87022 and electron sum F000 329.68. Different masses from the printed sum/moiety are recorded in the numerical ledger, with the explicitly stated atomic weights and their rounding limits.

The reported empirical density 4.324 g/cm³ is not reconciled merely by distinguishing chemistry from proxy scattering. Using the **printed** empirical formula (La0.58Ce0.55Pr0.14Nd0.10Ca0.39Sr0.20K0.04)(CO3)2[(OH)1.25F0.06·0.69H2O], its mass is approximately 381.3436 g/mol and its conditional density at the printed Z/V is 4.07003. A density of 4.324 would instead imply mass about 405.1392. This is an additional explicit calculation of an unresolved source limitation. It neither assigns a measured density nor selects a corrected formula; missing analyses and an attributable calculation/deposit explanation are needed. The accepted owner currently imports neither density value.
