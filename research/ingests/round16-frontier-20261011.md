# Round 16 material frontier

This batch separates five mineral species—**forsterite, talc, lizardite,
vivianite and siderite**—and the **oil and gas produced-water** secondary
resource. Forsterite/talc share one experiment; vivianite/siderite share another.
The produced-water record is identity-only. Resource and mineral identities do
not inherit constituent criticality, and none of these records claims industrial
microbial deployment. See the [publication report](round16-20261011-publication.md)
and [exact record/review manifest](round16-frontier-20261011.json).

## Prioritized next ingests

1. **Enstatite and olivine transformation:** Klug et al. (2026),
   [DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-63817-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-63817-0),
   [PMC13473492](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13473492/), is an
   openly accessible primary lead from the silicate investigation. Independently
   verify the complete methods, supplements, feed phase proportions, strain,
   direct/filtered-biolixiviant comparison and quantitative denominators before
   curating an enstatite root or extending existing forsterite/glushinskite.
   Resolve mineral species versus olivine group or mixed feed. Bibliographic
   identity is verified; this index is not an assay acceptance review.
2. **Produced-water microbial mechanism:** acquire full Hu et al. (2021),
   [DOI 10.1016/j.watres.2020.116753](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2020.116753).
   Its acquired supplement cannot replace the missing main methods/figures.
   Resolve dilution, inoculum, controls, analytical fractions, source caption
   discrepancy and detection-limit outcomes before extending the accepted
   produced-water root. Do not turn ND into zero or pretreatment into lithium
   product recovery. See the [produced-water dossier](round16-produced-water-sources-20261011.md).
3. **Biotite scope and other silicate mechanisms:** fully ground the group or
   species identity of the companion feed in Sheng et al. (2026),
   DOI 10.1128/aem.02554-25. Its biotite and lizardite assay arms have distinct
   details; transcriptomic correlations do not establish gene necessity. See
   the [silicate dossier](round16-silicate-sources-20261011.md) for source and
   acquisition boundaries and further references.
4. **Other saline process feeds:** investigate the primary RO-concentrate lead
   [PII S2214714426008408](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214714426008408)
   and heavy-oil produced-water lead
   [PII S0304389425032376](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304389425032376).
   These remain discovery leads. Establish full source identity, feed origin,
   organism/strain, controls and actual scale before accepting claims or a new
   resource class. RO concentrate is not automatically oilfield produced water.
5. **Independent iron-mineral follow-up:** acquire and assess the additional

   The selected 2015 experiment does not quantify mineral-specific yield or
   isolate surface nucleation from pH effects; later studies need their own
   phase, control and material-scope assessment.
