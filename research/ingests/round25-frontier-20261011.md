# Round25 source frontier and next ingests

This batch investigates five distinct secondary resources: blast-furnace cast-house dust, heavy-fuel-oil combustion ash, discarded LED lamps, biogas-desulfurization sulfur residue, and spent zinc-manganese button-cell batteries. The [manifest](round25-frontier-20261011.json) records final record hashes, CREATE history and exact-state scientific reviews when completed. Full-text limitations and source contradictions remain part of the records and dossiers.

The oil resource uses **combustion ash**, because the inspected experiment does not identify the collection fraction necessary to call its actual sample fly ash. Likewise, cast-house dust is distinct from blast-furnace flue-gas dust and electric-arc-furnace dust. The sulfur residue is an oxidation substrate; battery black mass treated with the resulting liquor is a different resource and process stage.

The next-source ranking is provisional. It favors distinct process-defined resources with identified primary experiments, followed by extensions of existing roots. It does not certify those sources as ready for import.
