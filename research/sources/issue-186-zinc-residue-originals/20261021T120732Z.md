This is separate biological production of a reagent, not proof that the leaching strain performs sulfate reduction.

NaOH is a chemical intermediate step. The acidic bioleachate is adjusted with10M NaOH to pH2,3,4,5,5.5,6 or6.5, then filtered at0.45µm. Figure S5.4 (PDF267) directly shows increasing Fe removal, about85 ±2% atpH5 in the text, and detectable Zn loss above5. The later alkaline arms retain that adverse outcome.
