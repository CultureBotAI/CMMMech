Beryllium element Critical Material

Beryllium (Be), represented as an element distinct from its ions, compounds, isotopes, host minerals, alloys and commodity streams. The US 2025 designation names the beryllium commodity and does not designate every compound or host material. No microbial extraction, transformation or recovery mechanism is accepted in this identity and designation record; unresolved experimental leads are retained in the accompanying research dossier.
