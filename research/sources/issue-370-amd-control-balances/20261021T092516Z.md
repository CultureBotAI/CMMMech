## Silva2019: separate gas production and precipitation

Sections2.1–2.3 distinguish an upstream 2.3 L mixed acidophilic sulfidogenic reactor from separate 1 L real-water bottles exposed to its gas for 48 or 96 h. The upstream reactor used porous glass beads occupying40% vessel volume, pH4.5,30°C,40rpm and150mL/min N2/CO2(70/30). Its sterile Cu-free synthetic feed was pH2.5,13.86mM sulfate,5mM glycerol and0.01% w/v yeast extract. Those conditions are not measurements of downstream bottle temperature or stirring.

The real Pará water was pH5.1, with Cu325mg/L, Mn10.50mg/L and Zn0.12mg/L. The recovered dark precipitate was vacuum-filtered and dried overnight at60°C. Figures2–3 and5 support the authors' covellite assignment and changing morphology; final pH2.1 and colour/settling are not quantitative Cu recovery or proof of every proposed intermediate. No final aqueous Cu, collected dry-product mass, quantitative H2S delivery, independent reactor count or matched chemical-H2S/sterile comparison is supplied in the inspected report. The isolated `CuS98` wording persists in v2 section3.1 despite the48/96h design.
