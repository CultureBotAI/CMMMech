This manufactured secondary resource is distinct from pure tungsten carbide, a mineral species, tungsten mining tailings and metal grinding sludge. Electroformed copper-silver manufacturing scrap

Defective electroformed copper components retaining an inner silver-bearing conductive coating from manufacture on expendable wax mandrels. The studied tube-shaped rejects had incomplete copper deposition or uneven wall thickness. This manufacturing-derived secondary resource is distinct from elemental copper or silver, printed circuit boards, electroplating sludge and multilayer beverage packaging. Its selected composition does not define all electroforming waste or confer a jurisdictional criticality designation.

End-of-life crystalline-silicon photovoltaic modules

Discarded crystalline-silicon photovoltaic modules and their separated cell fractions are a composite secondary resource generated by photovoltaic equipment use and dismantling. Their silicon wafers, metal contacts, glass and encapsulants remain distinct from elemental silicon, silicon-metal commodity, silver and mineral species. This record excludes CdTe and CIGS thin-film modules, manufacturing scrap of unspecified origin and generic electronic waste. The curated experiment used a chemical extract from end-of-life Hyundai m-Si units, not intact modules in direct contact with microorganisms. Criticality designations of constituent elements are not inherited by this waste feed.
