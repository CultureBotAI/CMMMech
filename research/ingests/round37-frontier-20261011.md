## Search and acceptance boundary

This prioritization compiles the round 37 source investigations and the prior
frontier. It is not a new comprehensive literature search. Each linked dossier
records actual searches, original-source inspection, access failures and
hidden/ignored-inclusive duplicate checks, with its own referenced-download
boundary. No machine-wide absence or exhausted source frontier is asserted.

Before curation, repeat identity and experiment deduplication, include ignored
and hidden files and relevant symlinks/downloads, verify issuing identifiers,
inspect full primary methods and original figures/tables/supplements, and use
the add-record and independent record-review workflows. Preserve controls,
source contradictions and distinctions among solution change, mineral phase
formation and isolated product recovery. No follow-up listed here is already
an accepted mechanism solely because it appears in this queue.
