## Primary assay and measurement boundaries

Sections 2.1–2.6 and Tables 1–3 identify partially dewatered Lehtoniemi municipal sewage sludge from Kuopio, Finland, initially 5.59% total solids. Table 1 gives total-P **2 g/L fresh weight** and PO4-P **130 mg/L fresh weight**. Those concentration bases are distinct from dry-mass mg/kg. Cultures were A. ferrooxidans DSM 29444 and A. thiooxidans DSM 14887, grown separately for 15 days at 30 °C in DSMZ 882 and DSMZ 71; FT received a 50:50 culture mixture.

The leaching experiment began with 10 mL sludge plus 90 mL medium/inoculum/water in loosely closed 250 mL bottles at 30 °C and 150 rpm. Phase 1 lasted days 0–21, phase 2 days 21–42, and phase 3 days 42–63. Section 2.6 explicitly reports removing 10 mL incubated mixture and adding 10 mL fresh sludge at days 21 and 42. Sulfur was added at day 42: nominal 10 g/L, product described as 80% pure; no purity-corrected loading is substituted. The 63-day sequence is not replaced with a stand-alone 21-day assay.

O is water plus sludge; FC and TC are uninoculated respective media plus sludge; FTC contains equal volumes of the media plus sludge. Section 3.1 and Table 4 explicitly document indigenous sulfur/iron oxidizers growing in these controls, without molecular identification. They are not sterile sludge controls, and they are not medium-only nutrient blanks. Added-strain identity does not resolve the indigenous causal contribution.

Section 2.2 describes TXRF after acid digestion for total metals, total-S and total-P, plus Hach spectrophotometry for total-N, PO4-P, soluble iron, sulfate and total-P. Section 2.6 specifies 0.45 µm filtration and analysis of the remaining liquid. This measures solubilization into a filtered fraction; no isolated phosphorus product mass/purity is reported. Total-P, PO4-P and total elemental assays cannot be silently interchanged.

Tables 2 and 4 specify N=3 for microbial enumerations. Chemistry tables do not expose replicate values, sample keys, blanks or uncertainty. Figures show error bars without a raw replicate key or an error-definition statement in their captions. Section 2.7 refers to an Excel file imported into SPSS 25, but the Data Availability Statement is “Not applicable.” The complete XML has zero supplementary-material elements, and PMC's sole “supplementary” occurrence is generic Associated Data boilerplate. No workbook accession or public raw-data link was found in the inspected article links.
