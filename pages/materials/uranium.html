Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Uranium separation in an MR-1-inoculated electrochemical system

separation Microbial

Associated Organisms: Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 (NCBITaxon:211586)

A laboratory electrochemical system inoculated with Shewanella oneidensis MR-1 showed aqueous uranium depletion and uranium associated with the carbon-felt working electrode. Abiotic systems also removed uranium. This supports separation in the combined biological and electrical system, with partial evidence for a microbial mechanism; microbial necessity, a specific electron-transfer pathway and a purified uranium product are not established by this record.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
Defined electrolyte, not ore or industrial waste: 100 mL working and counter chambers separated by Nafion 117, with carbon-felt electrodes. The working solution contained 30 mM bicarbonate, 10 mM PIPES, 30 mM ammonium acetate, 40 mM NaCl, and Wolfe's vitamins and trace metals. Dissolved U(VI) was prepared from U metal in HCl and hydrogen peroxide. Chambers were purged with nitrogen containing 400 ppm CO2. After equilibration and the second solution sample, the biotic system was inoculated and the working electrode poised at -800 mV versus saturated Ag/AgCl. The metadata specifies ambient-temperature equilibration and experiments lasting 72-574 h, with a shorter abiotic U repeat; it does not provide an exact controlled assay temperature or inoculum density/preparation. The original biotic U series has recorded pH values of 7.01-8.21 after inoculation, so this was not a constant-pH assay. Filtered samples were measured by ICP-OES. Electrode material underwent carbonate extraction followed by concentrated acid extraction. Independent replicate counts and a statistical microbial-effect estimate are not assigned here. Neither stable mineral identity nor industrial deployment is claimed.
Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: kane_2026_bes
FGDC process steps 1-4 and tables T1-T3 and T6 support the laboratory separation observation. In T3, the original biotic series has 975 mg/kg U at BESU.8Vb_W.EQ and less than 0.1 mg/kg at BESU.8Vb_W.END; the latter is encoded as -0.1, a detection limit, not zero or a negative concentration. T6 reports uranium in the subsequent electrode acid extract. These observations are not a mass-balanced recovery percentage or product purity measurement.
Partial Source: kane_2026_bes
Abiotic T3 samples also decrease, from 914 mg/kg at BESU.8Va_W.t0 to 0.819 mg/kg at BESU.8Va_W.END. T2 shows different pH histories, and the source reports pre-electrolysis sorption. T6 original biotic and abiotic acid extracts use concentrated HCl and HNO3 respectively, so their amounts are not a matched assay of microbial enhancement. These controls limit attribution of the observed separation specifically to MR-1 metabolism.
Support Source: kane_2026_bes
T9_SEM.pdf pages 2-6 show deposits and U-labelled EDS signals on both abiotic and biotic U electrodes, supporting electrode association. T8_XRD.csv supplies diffraction counts for these conditions and a carbon-felt blank, but this curation does not assign a crystalline phase or oxidation state from those data.
