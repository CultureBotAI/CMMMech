No empty-charge, no-REE-feed or matched replicate control is invented.

**Run8 continuous brine-melt path.** Above200°C the authors describe bastnaesite transforming to carbocernaite; above350°C calcite transforms to nyerereite. Dissolution proceeds above500°C; the charge is homogeneous at690°C/9.5kbar. Fluorite is present in the final characterization; the text's inferred below400°C appearance is not a precisely observed onset.

**What identifies the run8 product.** Figure4 joins a mixed post-run BSE assemblage with representative EDS spectra labelled calcioburbankite, nyerereite and fluorite, and corresponding Raman traces. The calcioburbankite-labelled EDS spectrum contains C/O/Na/Ca/La/Ce/Nd labels plus Au associated with sample preparation. Its ordinate is counts versus energy, not weight% or atomic%; peak heights cannot be converted into a compositional ratio. The displayed Na3(Ca,REE)3(CO3)5 formula is the authors' phase annotation. There is no deposited XRD/CIF, refined Ca-site occupancy, numerical spectral-fit table or quantitative phase fraction for this product in the acquired asset set. The record therefore says **author-assigned calcioburbankite phase**, retaining the natural-mineral distinction from burbankite and acknowledging the lack of quantitative Ca/REE composition or product purity.
