## Molybdenum: accepted narrow experimental scope

[Ahmad et al. 2013, DOI 10.1155/2013/871941](https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/871941)
was acquired as complete Europe PMC XML and an original institutional PDF.
The study is CC BY 3.0 according to the XML license link; raw files remain outside
Git. Full methods/results, figure captions and Tables 1-3 were inspected; the
PDF page 6 was also rendered and visually checked for Figure 7/8 and Tables 1/2.

The record captures DRY1 culture-associated molybdenum-blue production, with a
sodium molybdate culture assay kept distinct from cell-extract enzyme assays
using 12-molybdophosphate and NADH. The 865 nm peak and 710 nm shoulder identify
an optical signature consistent with reduced phosphomolybdate-like material,
not a directly measured oxidation-state mixture or crystallographically resolved
phase. Centrifuged-supernatant absorbance is not total-Mo removal or an isolated
product yield. Growth-stage cultures and extract assay stages are not conflated.

Live GenBank DQ226202.1 cites the study and has 1452 bases, isolate DR.Y1 and
NCBI taxon 351651. Live taxonomy calls that taxon `gamma proteobacterium DR.Y1`;
the paper's tentative Pseudomonas assignment and failure to resolve species are
explicit. NCBI's species-rank placeholder is not a resolved Pseudomonas species.

Material restrictions retained in the record:

- Fresh medium is the spectral baseline, not a matched incubated cell-free
  control. A killed-cell comparison and total-Mo mass balance are not reported.
- Section 2.2 says 48 h for optimization; Figures 2-7 say 72 h. The fixed
  Section 2.1 50 mL, 10 degrees C, 72 h culture and Figure 8 20 degrees C
  time course are separately specified.
- Section 3.7's 95%/5% distribution conflicts with 13.775/1.225 micromole values;
  those quantities sum to 15 and imply about 91.8%/8.2%, not the printed split.
  No corrected value is substituted as a source measurement.
- Section 2.8 and Table 2 disagree on inhibitor-dose mapping. Table 2's negative
  inhibitor responses do not resolve the electron-transfer pathway.
- Section 3.8 and Table 1 show partial purification, low retained yield and
  multiple PAGE bands. The catalytic interpretation is retained as partial.
- Section 3.4's 10-20 degrees C range differs from the abstract's 15-20 range;
  no merged numerical optimum is curated. Figure 7's 1.21% sodium molybdate
  recipe differs from the 45 mM statement in Section 3.5; no unified optimized
  recipe is asserted.

The new molybdenum record and canonical CREATE history are complete; independent
review remains separate. The accompanying JSON contains its hash and checks.

## Tungsten: accepted controlled release experiment

The initial [Han et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhazmat.2023.131490)
lead could not be acquired beyond abstract/metadata: ScienceDirect returned403,
the publisher XML endpoint returned429, and DOI resolver HTML was only a redirect
page. Metadata labels it CC BY4.0, but this is not a full-text inspection.
[Ogi et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.iecr.5b04843) was another bounded lead;
publisher403 and closed-access repository metadata prevented full methods
inspection. Neither experiment is accepted from abstracts. The indexed
November2026 issue of 10.1016/j.mineng.2026.110582 was not acquired or accepted.

A suitable open primary alternative was acquired in XML and original PDF:
[Blazevic et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2019.01492), PMC6614383.
Its complete methods/results describe M. sedula DSM5348 with sterilized,
impure scheelite concentrate and an uninoculated counterpart; ICP-OES of
clarified 0.44 micrometre filtrates shows enhanced W release over 21 days.
Original PDF page6 (Figure3) was visually checked. The figure uses day labels
while its caption says hours and describes a log10 ordinate with concentration
units. No exact concentrations are digitized or exponentiated.

The completed record retains the impure feed, unknown actual energy donor and
direct versus indirect dissolution uncertainty. W-bearing surface encrustation does not
justify claiming a chemically pure WC product, a resolved W redox pathway or
industrial recovery. The full-text XML explicitly links CC BY4.0. The linked publisher supplemental
anchor returned the same main article and the Europe PMC supplementary-files ZIP
request timed out without usable files at 2026-10-11T05:42:46Z. No supplemental
quantitative or video claim is curated; missing supplementary files do not limit
inspection of the narrower main-text/figure-supported release result.
