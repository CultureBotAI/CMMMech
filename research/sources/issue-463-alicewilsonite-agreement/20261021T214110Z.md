## Specimens, measurement bases and source limits

Part 2 original pp144–146, Figures1–3, and Table 1 separate specimen CMNMC53660, the holotype from Mont Saint-Hilaire, from six other analyzed specimens/localities. The visible holotype crystal has an outer alicewilsonite rim and an inner donnayite/gaidonnayite assemblage. The methods state that the selected single-crystal grain came from the rim and contained only alicewilsonite; this does not make the whole photographed crystal a pure product. The six-analysis holotype chemistry was measured on a crystal subsequently used for structure study. Beam instability motivated20–50µm beams at20kV/10nA and time-dependent intensity correction.

Table 1's CO2 and H2O are calculated, not measured. The article explicitly reports insufficient isolatable pure material for those analyses. Its full chemistry table must not be converted into a measured whole-composition balance or purification yield. Infrared Figure 4 and p148 support water qualitatively (O–H3350–3410cm−1 and bending1683cm−1), without establishing a new water mass assay or located H positions. The CIF validation reply says H atoms could not be reliably located and were manually added to the formula. Its atom loop contains no H site.

The final structure is a simplified model constrained by chemical evidence, scattering factors, bond valence, distances and charge balance. Ca was omitted; Ce and Dy are proxies for lighter/heavier lanthanides. Preliminary refined binary occupancies and final fixed mixed occupancies are different quantities.
