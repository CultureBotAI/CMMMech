## Disposition of each acceptance strand

### 1. Sequence-to-culture identity: unresolved, with a sharper reference-label conflict

Han2022 Section 2.1 assigns a cultured saltern isolate to *Halomonas utahensis*
using its 1,477-base sequence and SI Figure S1. Current NCBI ON000249.1 remains
annotated as an uncultured *Halomonas* environmental clone WMS2, with taxon
173971 and isolation source Salt field. Its source features have neither a
`strain` nor a `culture_collection` qualifier. Both the unversioned and explicit
version requests give the same record, last updated 2022-03-21. The GenBank
reference is an unpublished study attributed to Han Z.; this contextual connection
does not itself establish that the deposited sequence came from the assayed pure
culture. ENA retains clone/environmental-sample qualifiers; its different March
2022 record dates are exchange metadata, not evidence of a later culture correction.

**Newly traced limitation:** SI Figure S1, p. S8, pairs WMS2 with a branch labeled
*Halomonas utahensis* DSM3050, NR024701.1. The exact accession
**NR_024701.1** currently identifies *Halovibrio variabilis* DSM3050, with
`culture_collection=DSM:3050`, type-material annotation and taxon31910.
Its current update date is 2022-08-05; no historical version reconstruction was
performed. DSMZ's current DSM3050 and DSM3051 pages and the abstract of Opinion93
explain the historical exchange of the two type-strain designations. DSM3051 is
the *H. utahensis* type culture; DSM3050 is *H. variabilis*.
This is a demonstrable mismatch between the published comparator label and its
current authoritative accession. It does **not** justify transferring either
species or DSM culture accession to WMS2, nor prove which laboratory culture was
used. A sequence-similarity calculation cannot supply the missing culture custody.

Retain the owner's explicitly qualified genus context, without promoting it to
authenticated species/strain identity. Establish the sequence-to-culture link
through an authoritative corrected accession, verified WMS2 culture deposit, or
published clarification, and reconcile the Figure S1 reference accession/label.
The collection-domain queries found no
verified WMS2 deposit in the returned results; no global catalogue-absence claim
is made. The comparator problem belongs with this existing identity follow-up.

### 2. Lowest magnesium recipe: unresolved

Section 5.3 still lists Mg2+ 0.01 M at Ca2+ 0.01 M, whereas Table 1, Figure 2
and SI Figures S4/S6 designate the lowest arm Mg/Ca0. These inputs cannot be
silently made equivalent. The currently deposited SI supplies phase plots and
photographs, not an arm-specific preparation ledger or corrected recipe.
No correction/run map was acquired. Keep the accepted 0.07/0.09 M arms and
retain the conflict. Needed: a primary correction or original preparation/run
mapping that identifies which magnesium concentration produced the nominal zero
arm, including any final-volume convention relevant to reported concentrations.

### 3. Ratio5/day140 phase: unresolved

Section 2.3's discussion of Figure 4c1/c2 attributes selected morphologies to
MHC at day140. Table 1's ratio5/day140 entry instead gives aragonite and
hydromagnesite. Figure 2 and SI Figure S6c3 agree with the latter phase set;
S6c3 labels 61.51% aragonite and 38.49% hydromagnesite among the fitted phases.
These plotted fractions do not establish a recovered product yield, an absolute
absence of MHC, or that the SEM and XRD specimens were the same preparation.

The official Figshare item and BioStudies mirror expose no individual diffraction
arrays, Materials Studio refinement project or specimen-to-image/run mapping.
They repeat the existing PDF. The disputed arm therefore remains excluded as
proof of MHC persistence. Needed: the original diffraction/refinement files and
sample ledger joining Table 1, Figure 2, Figure 4c and S6c3, or a published
correction explaining the discrepancy. Do not pick a typographical repair merely
because several panels agree.

### 4. Reproduction and late controls: unresolved

The incubation values 37°C/120rpm occur in Sections 5.2.3–5.2.4 for salinity/growth
assays. Section 5.3 does not explicitly carry them into the mineralization series;
its 1% suspension dose has no stated percentage basis. Section 5.4 specifies
characterization instruments, without mineral-harvest washing/drying conditions.
Drying of bacterial microscopy grids (5.2.1) and washing/heating/lyophilizing EPS
(5.6) concern different preparations and cannot fill that gap.

SI Figure S4, p. S11, shows control photographs at days0,1,7,14 only. It cannot
extend the illustrated negative observation to days27/140 or provide a numerical
detection limit. The main text's broader no-mineral control statement remains
qualified in the owner. Needed: mineralization-specific temperature/agitation,
inoculum volume or mass basis and concentration, harvest/preparation logs, and
matched late-time control observations with their analytical detection scope.
Neither control failure nor late precipitation is inferred from missing logs.
