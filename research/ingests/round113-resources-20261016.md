## Accepted evidence

- Haghshenas et al. 2007, *IJE Transactions B* 20(2):133–140. The full eight-page
  original was acquired from `https://docs.sadrn.com/papers/278.pdf`, redirecting
  to coauthor Sadrnezhaad's publication site. His
  `https://sk.sadrn.com/publications/journals/` catalogue independently lists the
  title, authors, year, volume and pages. No DOI is asserted. Sections 2–4,
  Table 1 and Figures 1–5 were read. Rendered pages 5–7 were visually checked,
  including graph axes and captions. XRF oxide labels are not mineral phases;
  XRD is described as amorphous. Co/Mn selectivity conflicts between results
  and conclusion; peroxide units and redox terminology also conflict. Aqueous
  concentration curves are not isolated-product yields. The brief solvent
  extraction assertion lacks experimental documentation in this paper.
- Teixeira et al. 2022, DOI `10.3390/min12091141`, complete 15-page publisher
  PDF and Crossref identity/date/license metadata. Methods, results, conclusion,
  Figures 1–12 and Tables 1–5 were read; rendered pages 5, 8, 9, 11 and 12 were
  visually checked; page 13/Figure 12 was additionally checked during correction.
  Figure 5b and page 9 support 91.2% chloride removal at the
  selected 90-minute condition, contradicting the abstract/conclusion's >92%.
  Evaporation is 85 C in text but 50 C in Figure 3. Table 5 and XRF disagree on
  residual and recovered-product composition; neither implies pure salts or
  metallic-Al recovery. XRD non-detection is not a gas-emission measurement.
  Product crystals were characterized, but flux reuse is proposed. Independent
  review found that the initial record's blanket mass-balance limitation omitted
  Section 4's preliminary scaled solids/element balance. The corrected owner
  acknowledges 481 kg feed to 116.4 kg salts and 364.6 kg residue, with reported
  assay-based purity/recovery and closure estimates; it distinguishes those from
  a measured industrial throughput or complete gas/water balance. The initial
  observation remains immutable and the final successor records its resolution.
