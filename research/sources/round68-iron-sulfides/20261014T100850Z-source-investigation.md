## Experiment, limits and claim mapping

Methodspp3–4 specify anoxic modified metal-toxicity medium with lactate, sulfate, HEPES and organic supplements, initially pH7.2; nominal total Ni+Fe500micromolar and NoFe/5:1/1:1/1:5 arms. NoFe means no added Fe stock, not trace-metal-free medium. The inoculum was pre-grown72h at30°C with shaking then transferred1% into100mL assay medium; the assay temperature is not separately restated. Biotic solids were collected6–25days. Sodium-sulfide abiotic arms used50mL15mM titrated at2mL/h into50mL double-strength medium and were collected5–36days. Biotic finalpH is approximately7.1; abiotic approximately8.0–8.2. The arms also differ in sulfide delivery and harvest/processing. Neither pH matching nor an equivalent measured sulfide flux is established.

XRD samples were washed and dried on glass. Biotic TEM samples underwent detergent/proteinase/lysozyme treatment45°C1h, washing, shipment and sonication; abiotic harvest used filtration. The paper notes later oxidation of some Fe-rich mounted samples, analyzes grids within24h, and compares brief air/anoxic drying. This does not prove every phase formed before harvesting. EDS is semi-quantitative, Table2n denotes analytical spots, and particle-size histograms count particles; independent bottle replication is unreported. No phase-pure yield, isolated recovery, long-term stability challenge or industrial deployment is established. Local acidic microenvironments and secreted-organic mechanisms are hypotheses, not directly measured pH/metabolites or established enzymes. Figures9–11 are explanatory models/schematics, not additional experiments.

- **Millerite:** Resultspp4–5, Figure2d–f and supplemental diffraction spots support a millerite-assigned crystalline component in NoFe biogenic or aged abiogenic particles. Early diffuse rings cannot exclude other phases, and amorphous shells prevent treating entire spheroids as millerite. TableS1 shows overlaps. A provisional curator reading of the2.91Å index was corrected before freeze after root's independent pixel check: the actual label is101 and agrees with main text. This is not a source discrepancy and does not appear as one in the record.
- **Polydymite:** Table2, p5 and Figure4d support a minor assigned component. Figure4c's supplement labels3.38Å100p whereas main/S1 use220; Figure5b's supplementary thiospinel labels also conflict with the reference indices. Fe-added patterns overlap violarite. The source itself discusses preparation oxidation as an explanation for abiotic polydymite. The record retains these alternatives and does not call the material pure or exclusively biogenic.
- **Vaesite:** Figure1 small XRD features, Figure4a–b and supplement4b reflections near3.25,2.01,1.62Å support vaesite-assigned particles specifically in the biotic NoFe arm. Nondetection in the supplied abiotic preparations is retained without claiming biology is necessary in all conditions. The source discusses known abiotic formation routes and does not measure the proposed cell-local acidic pH.
