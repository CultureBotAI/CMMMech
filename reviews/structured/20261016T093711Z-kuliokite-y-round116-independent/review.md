### Specimen and analytical evidence

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kuliokite-y.

Three natural Ploskaya crystals were analysed by WDS, each averaging4–7 spots; water was calculated from structure data. The293(2)K monoclinic Im model uses total-occupancy-one mixed Y/Yb scattering curves. These proxies support a site-partitioning interpretation rather than elemental assays at every site. Chemical mean site scattering47.31 differs from structural46.29 electrons, which is closer to row3 at46.14; the record does not invent a matched diffraction-crystal row.

### Model and process limits

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:kuliokite-y.

One H position was located; F3 OH/F was fixed. The studied idealized(OH)2.5F4.5 composition and speculative species are separated from current IMA formula. The1986/1993 references and unacquired CSD2482967 CIF are not independently accepted model or alteration evidence. The internally inconsistent topology-uniqueness assertion is not adopted.
