## Primary evidence and limitations

[Sun et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3390/min10030289), publisher PDF, provides
pentlandite methods in sections 2.1–2.3, solution comparisons in 3.2–3.3 and
Figures 9/12, and surface analyses in Figures 13–17. Both comparison figures were
rendered and visually checked. The pH-stat experiment uses 8 g enriched feed in
400 mL, 33 C, 250 rpm, maintained pH 2 or 3 and living mixed acidophiles. Its
0.22-micrometre-filtered ICP-OES assay measures solution metal. Inoculated nickel
release exceeds the abiotic proton-leaching comparison; pH 2/3 inoculated nickel
curves are similar. No points were digitized. The ferric-ion series is a distinct
3 g/100 mL, initial-pH-1, kanamycin-containing experiment.

The two described bioleaching runs end at 8 and 16 days. Endpoint replication and
error-bar meaning are consequently unresolved. Controlled pH does not match
redox or ferric concentrations; community profiles do not establish causal
roles of each named taxon. Reaction sequences in section 4 are conceptual, with
undetected intermediates. Residue layers, continuing nickel mobilization and
references to commercial heaps do not demonstrate complete conversion, purified
product, universal absence of passivation, industrial deployment of this assay
or an engineering mass balance.

[Kirk et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmicb.2024.1467408) was reread from the
retained publisher full text, whose checksum still matches its original receipt.
Methods 2.1–2.5 and Results 3.1–3.4 support mixed Jadar Valley material and Bikita
spodumene/quartz feed, reported ore-derived acidophile culture, static 23 C,
30-day flasks and uninoculated/acid comparisons. Jadarite-associated peaks
changed and lithium mobilization was transiently higher with inoculation,
but controls converged by day 30 and acid-only leaching was stronger. Unknown
additional Li phases preclude assigning all release to jadarite. The new
27.9-degree peak occurs in both arms and is not an unambiguous recovered
boric-acid phase. Spodumene showed no material lithium enhancement in this
protocol; that is not a refutation of all microbial spodumene treatment.

The 2 g/200 mL versus 2% w/v loading conflict, unequal jadarite acid dosing,
acid-volume discrepancies and missing strain accession remain explicit.
The [Mendeley version 1 deposit](https://doi.org/10.17632/djkf7tkm8d.1) is the
same previously acquired dataset. Three relevant workbook checksums were
reverified. Sheet XML confirms identical 30-cell Li replicate blocks in the
standalone spodumene/lepidolite workbooks and arithmetic discrepancies at
Jadarite F5/F7 relative to C:E. Headers versus paper concentration units and the
absence of a dose/loading ledger prevent independent quantitative confirmation.
No numerical cells, corrected values or normalized yields were imported.

[Bayat et al. 2011](https://doi.org/10.2225/vol14-issue3-fulltext-7), retained
SciELO PDF, was rehashed and its methods, Tables 1/3 and figure/results text
reread. Growing reported A. niger ATCC 11414 cultures released soluble boron
from mixed colemanite-bearing ore with organic-acid production. All round-12
review limits remain: Table 1 totals 126.8 wt%, reported B2O3 is not a valid
recovery denominator, temperature and pH reports disagree, no corresponding
abiotic boron series establishes the microbial dissolution increment, and
kinetic/endpoint details conflict. Chemical comparison conditions differ and
no isolated product or mass balance was shown.

## Other leads and contradictory evidence

[Giese 2021](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13205-021-02711-0) full PMC text was inspected
as a pentlandite comparator. Its sample is stated as 0.29 wt% Ni, which permits
2.9 mg Ni/g ore if that composition applies, while a reported 45.6% is paired
with 26.5 mg/g ore. This unresolved denominator tension and acid-identity wording
require reconciliation before adopting quantitative claims. Nothing was silently
corrected, and this inconsistency is not a universal refutation of bioleaching.

[Van Alin et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1111/1751-7915.14260) full XML was read.
Its main text resolves the experimental feeds only to monazite/xenotime groups;
monazite ore and monazite/muscovite crystal are mixed. Biofilm microscopy is not
a new xenotime recovery yield, and the erosion comparison concerns high-grade
monazite ore. Appendix S1 (advertised 59.4 MB DOCX) and video ZIP were not
acquired. Species-level mechanisms are deferred pending that characterization.

[Rezza et al. 1997](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1472-765X.1997.00199.x) is an
abstract-only fungal spodumene lead. Full methods/controls remain unverified;
its existence helps keep the Kirk null narrow. A pyrolusite primary
[1990 mineral-composition study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/000925419090108J)
was located at abstract level, with a mineral-specific null lead. Full text and
phase attribution remain follow-up work. Bastnasite and broader mineral groups
are still research candidates, not accepted records in this pass.
