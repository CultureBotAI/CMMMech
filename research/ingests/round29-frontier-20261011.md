## Prioritized follow-up

1. **Bastnaesite evidence extension.** Review the 2018 actinobacteria study (DOI 10.1016/j.chemgeo.2018.03.023) and Liang et al., Journal of Rare Earths 44(1), January 2026, 290–298 (DOI 10.1016/j.jre.2025.03.025) when full main texts are available. Keep their experimental feeds and species boundaries separate from the accepted 2025 GCA study. The DOI's 2025 component is not the journal issue year.
2. **Tungsten mining wastes ([#103](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/103)).** Original supplementary community charts and six publisher figures are now available for DOI 10.1016/j.jhazmat.2023.131490. Complete main methods, captions, feed provenance and control definitions remain missing. The `+M` arms cannot be assumed sterile, and cumulative extracted mass cannot be converted to recovery without an established denominator. Related Han 2022 and thesis texts may help only after experiment overlap is resolved.
3. **Ilmenite and tin-tailing concentrate ([#104](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/104)).** The previously unresolved study is DOI 10.1002/apj.1663. A second verified study, DOI 10.1017/S1473550412000493, reports a null ilmenite enhancement result in its abstract; a related 2010 preliminary conference abstract differs. Acquire both full journal articles before reconciling controls, feed identity and outcomes. Do not transfer basalt or chemical-acid outcomes to microbial ilmenite extraction.
4. **Oxidized-ore evidence extension.** The distinct Ilyas 2012 mixed-ore column study, DOI 10.1007/s00449-011-0582-3, may extend this resource family after full methods, pretreatment and identity review. It cannot establish a cross-study rate advantage for the Horzum assay. Stronger claims for Demirci 2025 require raw replicates, explicit solids basis, numerical abiotic metal results and complete mass accounting.
5. **Basaltic geological feed.** DOI 10.3389/fmicb.2018.01252 surfaced as an open primary lead, but its experimental details and record novelty have not been assessed here.
