Enstatite mineral

Enstatite is the Mg2Si2O6 mineral species listed as approved through a 1988 special procedure in the September 2026 IMA list. The special-procedure year is not a unique approval-number identifier. This species is distinct from elemental magnesium and from the mixed San Carlos experimental feed, which contained 31 wt% enstatite and 69 wt% olivine by the authors' XRD characterization. No constituent criticality is inherited. The companion olivine-group record describes other material scope within the same Klug experiment, not an independent replication.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (1)

Enstatite-bearing mixed-feed leaching by engineered bacterial biolixiviant

bioleaching Microbial

Associated Organisms: Gluconobacter oxydans (NCBITaxon:442) [TaxonMech]

An enstatite-bearing olivine mixture released Mg and Ni into the analyzed liquid fraction in both cell-containing and cell-free bacterial-biolixiviant arms. XRD and the authors' mass-balance model are consistent with partial enstatite dissolution, but the study did not test a pure-enstatite feed or measure enstatite-specific aqueous flux.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
The selected feed was the 69 wt% olivine/31 wt% enstatite bulk powder, not the separate hand-selected olivine-only arm. The strain was Gluconobacter oxydans B58 delta-pstS, P112:mgdh, propagated from one colony; triplicate flasks per arm and sampling day were technical replicates from that shared culture. YPM growth and glucose-based biolixiviant production each lasted two days at 30 degrees C and 200 rpm. Prepared cell-containing cultures and centrifuged, 0.2-micrometre-filtered biolixiviant were tested separately. Each 100-mL flask contained 10 mL liquid and 0.5 g powder (5% pulp), incubated statically at 30 degrees C for 0, 5, 10 or 15 days. The Methods temperature takes precedence over the Introduction's room-temperature shorthand. Powders had typical particle size 40 micrometres after 20 minutes of tungsten-carbide milling; visibly carbonate-coated/altered grains were removed and a 60-minute deionized-water ultrasonic wash preceded milling. Powder sterilization was 135 degrees C for three minutes. Controls contained 20% glucose and mineral without cells or biolixiviant, with no acid or pH matching. Species taxon 442 does not identify the engineered genotype.

Curated Evidence Points:
Support Source: klug_2026_data
ICP-MS data worksheet, row 6, gives day-15 Mg means of about 8487 ppm with cells (T6), 7051 ppm in cell-free biolixiviant (AD6), and 265 ppm in the glucose-only mixed-feed control (J6). The workbook uses ppm as mg/L in its mass calculations, not native mg/kg observations. XRD minerals rows 7, 13 and 15 report enstatite in all three corresponding residues; Mg-oxalate is assigned in the direct residue but not in the mixed-feed cell-free or control residues.
Partial Source: klug_2026
Figure 6c-d attributes about 26% of total initial feed Mg to enstatite at day 0, with about 13% remaining in that phase with cells versus about 10% in the cell-free arm at day 15. Thus, the source's higher total mixed-feed leaching with cells does not establish superior enstatite-specific dissolution. These are model allocations based on assumed MgSiO3 and olivine Mg1.81Fe0.19SiO4 compositions, ICP-MS and XRD phase proportions, not direct phase-specific recovered masses.
Partial Source: klug_2026_si
Table S1 imports an average commercial San Carlos olivine composition from Lambart et al. (2022), rather than measuring this exact experimental batch. Figure S2 distinguishes the 69/31 mixture from the olivine-only material; Figure S4 preserves the cell-free mixed-feed Mg-oxalate null. Phase percentages describe residual solids rather than yields from the original mineral feed.
Partial Source: klug_2026
Leachate was centrifuged at 3214 x g for 20 minutes, frozen, thawed, passed through 0.45-micrometre filter plates and diluted 10000-fold in nitric acid for ICP-MS. Reported Mg and Ni were blank-corrected by about 1043 and 3.2 ppm respectively using a 100-fold-dilution comparison. These operationally defined filtrate concentrations are not isolated-product yields or resolved chemical species. Initial treated pH was about 2.4-2.5 but controls were about 8.7; later direct and cell-free pH trajectories also differed. The experiment does not isolate physical contact from evolving acid/metabolite chemistry, demonstrate a particular Fe-oxidation enzyme, or compare this mutant with wild type for mineral leaching. Laboratory results establish no industrial deployment or net atmospheric carbon removal.

Causal Edges (5):
  • psts_phosphate_binding → pstS deletion combined with P112:mgdh promoter replacement in B58 strain (modulates) → gdh_dehydrogenase : Engineered B58 delta-pstS, P112:mgdh strain generates the acidic biolixiviant; Klug et al. (2026) did not include a wild-type B58 comparator to isolate pstS.[klug_2026]
  • pqqc_synthase → supplies PQQ cofactor for holoenzyme assembly (positively_regulates) → gdh_dehydrogenase : PQQ cofactor biosynthesis enables active periplasmic quinoprotein glucose dehydrogenase.[klug_2026]
  • gdh_dehydrogenase → periplasmic oxidation of glucose to gluconic acid (produces) → gluconic_acid_biolixiviant : Membrane-bound Gdh oxidizes D-glucose to gluconic acid, lowering supernatant pH and supplying organic-acid ligands.[klug_2026]
  • gluconic_acid_biolixiviant → Engineered G. oxydans B58 biolixiviant leaches the 69 wt% olivine / 31 wt% enstatite mixed feed (solubilizes) → enstatite_mixed_feed : Cell-containing and 0.2-um-filtered cell-free G. oxydans B58 (delta-pstS, P112:mgdh) biolixiviants (initial pH ~2.4–2.5) react with 5% w/v (0.5 g per 10 mL) milled 69 wt% olivine / 31 wt% enstatite powder statically at 30 degrees C over 15 days.[klug_2026]
  • enstatite_mixed_feed → 15-day bioleaching of the 69/31 olivine-enstatite mixed feed releases dissolved Mg and Ni (produces) → dissolved_mg_fe_ni_ions : 15-day leaching of the 69 wt% olivine / 31 wt% enstatite mixed feed at 5% w/v yields ~8487 ppm Mg with cells and ~7051 ppm Mg cell-free (vs 265 ppm control) alongside dissolved Ni; Figure 6c–d mass balance allocates ~26% of initial feed Mg to enstatite at day 0 and ~13% (with cells) or ~10% (cell-free) at day 15.[klug_2026]
