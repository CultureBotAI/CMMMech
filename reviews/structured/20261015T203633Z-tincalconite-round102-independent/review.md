## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Natural accepted species versus synthetic structural models

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:tincalconite.

Current species identity and historical natural occurrence are distinct from later specimens and experiments.

IMA Na2B4O5(OH)4(H2O)3, G and description year 1878 agree; 1878 is not a sequential approval. Searles Lake crystals at about 116 feet are naturally occurring, but proposed direct crystallization from brine remains an interpretation. The 1973 aqueous-borax crystals grown near 80 C and 2002 layered NaBH4/water plus tetraheptylammonium iodide/acetone crystal are separately prepared synthetic specimens. Both refinements are R32; no biological formation claim follows.

### Water-site occupancy, formula discrepancy and nomenclature

quantity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:tincalconite.

Model occupancy and current IMA formula are preserved without silently choosing an unverified mass.

The 2002 freely refined O12 occupancy 0.337(5) supports a one-third model at 291(2) K and 8 water molecules per three tetraborate units, or 2.667 per unit; current IMA uses three. The Table 1 printed H28B12Na6O27 lacks eight oxygens compared with Na6[B4O5(OH)4]3·8H2O, which expands to O35. Its formula mass is not adopted. Occupancy is a crystallographic model, not an extracted-water yield or a nomenclatural redefinition. Without the deposited CIF or authoritative disposition, neither formula conflict is forced to a universal resolution.

### Mixed starting material, sequence and controls

scope: supported. Targets: cmmmech:tincalconite.

Natural source, laboratory alteration and measured RH experiment remain separate.

Powdered Lewis Cliff Ice Tongue qilianshanite aged 2.5 months in laboratory air produced tincalconite plus nahcolite. The RH-XRD sequence used 23 +/- 0.5 C, 10 to 90 to 10% RH in 10-point steps and a 2.5-hour delay before each 2-70 degree 2theta scan, 0.02-degree steps and 1 s/step. Seventeen continuous runs are one sequence, not independent replicates. Sulfate gravimetric arms and mixed-qilianshanite TGA do not quantify water uptake or product yield for this borate sequence.

### Hysteresis, coexisting phase and conflicting endpoint

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:tincalconite.

Diffraction supports bounded interconversion in a mixture, not pure-product recovery or an exact equilibrium boundary.

Figure 6 shows partial hydration to borax at upward 80% RH and return toward tincalconite through downward 50-40%. Nahcolite remains throughout, so one borate phase at end-member RH does not imply a one-phase total sample. Caption p. 31 says borax has disappeared by downward 40%, whereas p. 32 continuation includes borax through 40%; retaining a broader bracket avoids inventing resolution. The authors decline detailed tincalconite analysis because of sodium bicarbonate/carbonate mixing. No phase fraction, boron balance or isolated yield is demonstrated.

### Longer pure-material humidity experiment

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:tincalconite.

Independent primary context reinforces protocol dependence without contradicting the qualified owner.

Ruhl 2008 used greater-than-99% reagent borax and XRPD-checked prepared tincalconite in buffered-salt atmospheres at 298.15 K for four weeks; Table 3-1 and Figure 3-2 bracket a 59 +/- 6% RH inferred equilibrium. Figure 3-3 shows partial-conversion mixtures under another temperature/duration. This differs in feed purity, temperature and exposure by orders of magnitude from the 2020 2.5-hour staged mixed residue. Its equilibrium inference does not falsify the retained kinetic/hysteretic observations, and no thesis-derived number is inserted into the owner as a universal threshold.

### Material kind, criticality and deployment

Criticality is unrecorded, not declared absent: no authority/jurisdiction/list/edition is inherited from boron or borates.
