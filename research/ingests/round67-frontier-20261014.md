The source reports no demonstrated Mg/Si dissolution advantage over mineral-medium controls, and crystalline fibres persist. Preparation, inoculum correction and concentration discrepancies prevent a verified yield, fibre destruction or safety-improvement claim.
- **Riebeckite:** the UICC crocidolite specimen was tested with Deferrisoma palaeochoriense. Culture growth and an increased analytically extracted Fe(II) fraction support a bounded iron-redox observation. Structural versus coating-associated reduction, subsequent decline in dissolved iron and missing supplementary methods remain unresolved; no detoxification or isolated recovery is asserted.
