Cobaltite mineral

Cobaltite is the CoAsS mineral species listed by IMA-CNMNC in its September 2026 master list (PDF page 48). Its status is grandfathered; the accompanying 1832 value denotes the original-description year, not an IMA approval number. This mineral identity is distinct from the cobalt element and battery black mass. No microbial mechanism or criticality designation is assigned here.
