## Niu2021 experiment and limits

**Material and preparation — Methods2.1, PDF8–9 / printed7–8.** The supplier was Anhui SCR Environmental Science and Technology Company, Chuzhou. Its white regenerated powder had D90 about5µm and specific area about60m²/g. Upstream processing was water washing, ultrasonic washing, coarse crushing, NaOH/H2O2 arsenic removal, H2SO4/HF silicon removal, drying and grinding. Concentrations, durations and complete material balance for that upstream recipe are not supplied. This is distinct from the Xinjiang spent honeycomb feed in the maintained Niu2025 mechanism.

Following HNO3–HF–HCl digestion/ICP-OES, the main literally reports Ti75.46, W7.23, V0.27 and As0.037wt%. Retain those element labels; do not silently substitute oxide equivalents or normalize their 82.997% sum. The As entry converts to370mg/kg. **Culture and lixiviant — Methods2.2, PDF9 / printed8.** Source-reported A. thiooxidans ATCC19377, 5%v/v logarithmic inoculum, was grown at32±1°C and120rpm for8–10days, from initial pH2.0–2.5 to0.8. Per1000mL distilled water the recipe lists1g each (NH4)2SO4, KNO3, KH2PO4 and Na2HPO4,0.5g MgSO4·7H2O,0.25g CaCl2 and20g elemental sulfur.
