## What the original graphics and supplement establish

FigureS3 (PDFpage4) explicitly compares original and abiotically treated antigorite patterns with JCPDS44-1447. This provides the authors' phase assignment and a visible abiotic comparator. It does not quantify phase purity or trace impurities, prove identical preparation histories, or establish an exact-zero secondary phase below an unknown detection limit. Publisher mineral-sample excerpts locate material in the Luliangshan ophiolite mélange of North Qaidam; complete selection, pretreatment and loading remain unverified. The associated regional climate is not an incubation temperature.

Original Figure1 shows pellet/particle/hyphal associations, channels and pits, plus two lower comparator panels. Without the full caption, panel-specific treatment/time and symbol mappings are not generalized beyond matching excerpts and the self-labelled Figure2/TableS1 groups. Original Figure3 contains crystal images, elemental maps and a diffraction pattern marked antigorite plus glushinskite against JCPDS44-1447 and28-0625. These are mixed-sample phase and spatial observations. They do not identify an isolated recovered product, quantify antigorite conversion, resolve the source of every Mg atom, or directly prove that a particular ligand or mechanical force caused dissolution.

FigureS1 (page2) depicts FXJ8.102 in GYM medium after two weeks; this is culture preparation context and does not establish the composition or duration of the dissolution assay. FigureS2 (page3) compares inoculated media with and without antigorite at the experimental endpoint. Its mineral-free arm is distinct from a sterile mineral control. The publisher Results snippet mentions ten days and two starting mycelial pellets; the full protocol and independent experimental replication remain unavailable.

FigureS4 (page5) shows CAS-plate responses for DFAM standards labelled25,50,100 and200 micromol/L and filtrates labelled micro/bulk, with/without antigorite. Visible colour/halo comparisons are assay context, not chemically identified endogenous siderophore concentrations, mineral-specific causality or proof of a gene/protein mechanism. The original complete protocol, matrix controls and definitions of micro versus bulk remain needed. No concentration is interpolated from halo appearance.
