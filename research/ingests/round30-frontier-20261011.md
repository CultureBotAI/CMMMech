## Prioritized follow-up

1. **Tungsten microbial methods, [#103](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/103).**
   The new historical skarn-tailings resource is supported by separate 2019/2020
   studies. It does not supply the missing Han 2023 experimental methods or close
   the existing issue. Publisher figures, supplementary communities and thesis
   metadata are not substitutes for main methods. Resolve overlap among the
   journal article, preprint and thesis before treating them as independent evidence.
2. **Ilmenite, [#104](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/CMMMech/issues/104).**
   Acquire full DOI 10.1002/apj.1663 and 10.1017/S1473550412000493, then reconcile
   positive and null claims, exact feeds, controls and the preliminary 2010
   conference account. The basalt record does not resolve ilmenite bioleaching.
3. **Chromite-processing tailings.** DOI 10.3390/min8060261 concerns a mixed
   processing residue, distinct from the admitted chromite mineral. Verify full
   extraction and mineral-fraction evidence before a separate resource admission;
   release from nonchromite phases cannot establish chromite destruction. The
   2025 Cr-bearing Fe-spinel study, DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-57300-z, has a different
   composition from ideal chromite and remains a distinct identity question.
4. **Basalt evidence extension.** DOI 10.1016/j.gca.2007.02.017 and other cited
   studies may add different natural feeds and nutrient conditions after complete
   primary review. The 2018 manufactured BAS glasses must not become duplicate
   natural-resource records or be pooled with HSDP2 cores. Match pH and nutrient
   controls before attributing direct microbial dissolution.
5. **Pyrrhotite quantitative evidence.** DOI 10.1016/j.hydromet.2006.03.011 and
   PMID 27332502 are unassessed leads. The admitted 1993 solid-phase study lacks
   dissolved-metal accounting; the inspected 2021 study needs medium/inoculum
   iron corrections, clear community provenance and replication before stronger
   quantitative interpretation.
