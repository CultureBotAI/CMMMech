Zektzerite mineral

Zektzerite (IMA 1976-034) is an IMA-accepted sodium-lithium-zirconium silicate mineral with IMA September 2026 Master List formula NaLiZrSi6O15 and empirical formula Li1.00Na1.02(Zr0.94Ti0.05Hf0.01)Si6.06O15.15 (Table 1, p. 418 Zektzerite Analysis column: SiO2 68.24, ZrO2 21.84, TiO2 0.75, HfO2 0.5, Na2O 5.9, Li2O 2.8, total 100.0 wt%, vs theoretical NaLiZrSi6O15 Li2O = 2.82 wt%, with H2O reported as absent by the Penfield method on a 300 mg sample). Described by Dunn et al. (1977) from miarolitic cavities in riebeckite granite of the Golden Horn batholith near Washington Pass (48°32'N, 120°35'W), Okanogan County, Washington, USA (holotype NMNH 136030), where it occurs as colorless to pink pseudohexagonal tabular crystals up to 35 mm associated with microcline, quartz, riebeckite, astrophyllite, and elpidite. Orthorhombic (space group Cmca or C2ca), with a = 14.306(5) Å, b = 17.330(4) Å, c = 10.140(3) Å, V = 2514.0(9) Å3, Z = 8, Dmeas = 2.79 g/cm3, Dcalc = 2.80 g/cm3, Mohs hardness ~6, perfect {100} and {010} cleavages, light yellow short-wave UV fluorescence, and biaxial (–) optics with alpha = 1.582(3), beta = 1.584(3), gamma = 1.584(3), and 2V ~ 0°.
