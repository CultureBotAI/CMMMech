## Laboratory stages and missing controls

The 2021 specimens are mixed tailings from water-covered management areas at two decommissioned Elliot Lake sites, sampled in June 2012. The 22-week enrichment used 7 g fresh wet DT solids in 100 mL water, shaken at 200 rpm; no fresh RAT was available. Generic introductory genera are not identities of this unresolved native community.

Shake-flask methods specify 90 g tailings, 200 mL inoculum and 400 mL McCready medium, initial pH 2.0 ± 0.1 with sulfuric acid, and 20/30/40°C. The text says 35 days, while Tables 5–6 explicitly include week 8 and Section 3.3 describes an additional four weeks. Only the 20°C rows explicitly identify duplicate flask runs. These cannot be generalized into independent triplicates at every temperature.

Stirred-tank methods specify duplicate 4 L vessels with 290 g fresh charge and 250 mL culture, brought to 3 L, at 30°C and about 900 rpm. Approximately 2 mL is sampled four days per week, followed by pH readjustment; added acid is described as recorded but no usable complete addition/sampling inventory accompanies the published endpoints. RAT receives forced aeration after day-2 oxygen depletion; DT receives it initially. The stated approximately 12.5 L/min supply does not make the histories identical. Analytical triplicates are distinct from the duplicate reactors. Results say 30 days, Table 7 ends at week 4, and the abstract associates its results with 35 days. No exact calendar mapping is invented.

Sections 2.1–2.2 describe partial HCl or four-acid solid digestion, reference materials and ICP analyses; endpoint dry solids receive partial HCl digestion. These procedures do not supply per-sample original instrument outputs, moisture bases, digestion recoveries, inoculum inputs, cumulative sampled metal or residual phase inventory. Growth tubes and their uninoculated pH controls are a separate MPN assay. Counts of iron oxidizers and reduced pyrite diffraction peaks support biological compatibility, not a measured microbial increment, species causality, direct uranium valence assay or a closed mineral balance. No matched sterile acid/medium leaching arm is supplied for the disputed experiments.
