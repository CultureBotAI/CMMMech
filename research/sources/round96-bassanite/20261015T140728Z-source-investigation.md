Bassanite is a distinct calcium sulfate hemihydrate mineral, not an elemental calcium criticality claim or an industrial product-yield assertion. Current IMA2026-09 row and natural Allen/Kramer1953 chemistry/XRD support identity. The complete Al Disi2023 original supports extracted bacterial EPS-associated mixed sulfate solids after evaporation and workup; the complete Silva-Castro2015 original supports saline-culture-associated mixed sulfate precipitates. These are different experiments, not independent pure-bassanite production demonstrations.

## Boundaries and contradictions

- Al Disi2023 is five pages, not six; the independent initial review caught and the owner corrected this bibliographic defect with append-only EDIT provenance. Complete article is present.
- Al Disi: extracted EPS is not live-cell metabolism. 50mg EPS/50mL synthetic evaporated-seawater solution,35C5–6days; solids washed and40C dried before XRD. Terminal phase proportions are not in-situ35C phase proof or recovered yield. Z27/S27/S7D1 control labels conflict. Water activity/Ca-binding explanations remain proposed. Hydration7days30C retained mixed phases rather than complete conversion.
- Silva-Castro: saline isolates and medium-level XRD do not authenticate strain genomes or resolve isolate-specific phase yields. Solid SM media contain real brine, pH7.2,25C30days; controls are described but their phase-resolved patterns are not shown. Duplicate SM5 final Table5 row is preserved. Liquid depletion is not assigned to solid-bassanite recovery.
- Silva-Castro mineral workup cites Rivadeneyra2013 DOI10.1007/s13762-012-0084-0; full original was not acquired through attempted routes. Preparation-induced hydration changes cannot be excluded.
