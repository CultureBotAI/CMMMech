Title: Clarify secondary aluminium dross roasting recipe and mass-balance basis in Lv et al. (2022)

Lv et al., *Effective Extraction of the Al Element from Secondary Aluminum Dross Using a Combined Dry Pressing and Alkaline Roasting Process*, DOI [10.3390/ma15165686](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma15165686), supplies the complete primary evidence for the new secondary-aluminium-dross owner. The official 16-page PDF and full original XML have been acquired; this is a source-clarification follow-up, not a missing-paper or current record defect.

The record preserves two limitations that prevent a unique reproduction recipe and an overall product-recovery claim:

- Table 2 (PDF page 6) gives the carbonate-ratio series at 1100°C and 60 min leaching; the abstract and §3.5/Table 4 (pages 1 and 13) give the selected condition at 1150°C and 30 min. The source does not clearly connect every recovery datum to one of these conditions. Figure 5’s caption also swaps the b/c descriptions relative to the actual axes and alkali labels. The caustic liquor is specified as C(Na2O) = 60 g/L and alpha_k = 3.0 without a complete preparation recipe.
- Equations 1–2 (§2.3, page 5) use Mg-normalized clinker/residue compositions and contain incomplete brackets. The reported 90.79% Al and 92.03% Na values concern this solid-balance estimate, with added sodium reagents. Independent solution inventory, conserved-Mg verification, roasting/filtration/wash losses and isolated alumina yield are not supplied. Lower N/Cl concentrations are not a complete gas/emissions or detoxification balance.

Acceptance requires a legitimate original supplement, deposited raw data, published correction or comparably attributable clarification that:

1. maps each adopted recovery result to its exact roasting temperature, duration, reagent ratio and full leaching-liquor recipe, including the 30/60 min discrepancy;
2. defines the balance equations unambiguously and documents the relevant solid, solution and loss terms, including whether magnesium was conserved and whether washes were included;
3. provides direct evidence before adding any isolated alumina yield, condensate-purity, complete detoxification or industrial-deployment assertion.

Until then, retain the qualified laboratory chemical-extraction record and its original source discrepancies. No author contact, guessed corrected values or transfer of unrelated aluminium-smelting-slag bioleaching results is requested.
