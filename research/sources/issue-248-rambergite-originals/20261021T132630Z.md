## Scientific claims still requiring the original

The current official abstract reports MR-1 producing gamma-MnS during concurrent MnO2/thiosulfate reduction with hydrogen as electron donor. It names double and triple outer-membrane cytochrome deletion strains and interprets direct MnO2 reduction as dominant over reduction by biogenic sulfide. It also reports a lactate condition with both MnCO3 and MnS in roughly comparable amounts despite omitted bicarbonate, and names several phase-analysis methods and equilibrium modelling. These remain attributed abstract claims, not an independent verification of mechanism, phase fractions or genetic causality.

The claimed gamma-phase assignment requires the actual micro-XRD pattern, reference phases, backgrounds and treatment identifiers, plus microscopy/diffraction calibrations and raw or clearly legible phase-discriminating data. Local Mn/S detection or a generic nanoparticle image cannot distinguish MnS polymorphs. A natural reference line list alone cannot determine which phase formed in culture. A mixed carbonate/sulfide result cannot become pure rambergite or an inferred50%yield; 'nearly equivalent' supplies neither a numeric fraction nor a closed Mn balance.

Mutants require verified genotypes, growth/reduction phenotypes, culture preparation, viability and any complementation evidence. The double/triple comparison does not by itself isolate a single protein's causal contribution or rule out a pleiotropic effect. Do not add UniProt examples from gene names in an abstract. Direct cellular electron transfer must be distinguished from thiosulfate reduction followed by sulfide-mediated chemical MnO2 reduction and precipitation. No-cell, no-donor, missing-acceptor and added-sulfide controls need the actual methods and data, including which strain and donor were used in each arm.

The oxide preparation/loading, thiosulfate dose, medium/buffer, gas/headspace, inoculum, pH history, temperature, duration, sampling and replicate scope remain unverified. Hydrogen and lactate conditions cannot be pooled; exclusion of added bicarbonate does not establish absence of inorganic carbon produced by metabolism. The abstract's proposed environmental sink is not field deployment, metal recovery or proof that biological activity is necessary for all MnS precipitation. Equilibrium prediction agreement is not a kinetic or causal test; TableS1 inputs, database/version, mass balances and phase-selection assumptions must be examined before reproducing the modelling claim.
