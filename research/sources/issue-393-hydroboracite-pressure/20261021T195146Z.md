## Experiment and pressure-arm audit

The AAM methods describe two natural hydroboracite crystals from Bigadiç, Turkey, from the Sorbonne mineral collection, approximately 20×5×4 µm, studied at room temperature in helium-loaded diamond-anvil cells at ESRF ID15B. Ruby fluorescence supplies pressure with reported ±0.05 GPa. These are two crystal sequences, not a replicated yield assay. The destructive phase transition and reduced reflections prevented solving the high-pressure structure. The approximate monoclinic cell and unknown space group do not justify a new species assignment.

## Native CIF audit and remaining formula-unit inconsistency

MOESM1,2,3 cell metrics map to first-arm Table 1 rows **0.32,14.40,7.35 GPa**, respectively. This is a metric-based crosswalk, not a pressure field or complete specimen/run identifier embedded in each CIF. Precision differences are retained: for example file2 has b 6.3301(10)Å versus the table's6.330(1)Å; file1 V 628.60(6)Å³ versus the table's628.60(7)Å³. All three are below the transition and do not solve hydroboracite-II.

Each science block contains 14 non-H sites, four P2/c symmetry operations, full occupancies and positive isotropic displacement values. Expansion gives **Ca2Mg2B12O34** per cell. There are no H-coordinate rows, despite the generic hydrogen-treatment field saying constrained. Placeholder `?` rows in anisotropic/twin/restraint/torsion/H-bond loops are not populated measurements. Refinement scalar fields report 50 parameters, zero restraints and zero constraints. These literal fields do not supply missing instructions or validate the actual treatment.

All files declare formula B6CaH12MgO17, formula weight 413.3 and Z 1. Reported densities are 1.0918,1.3182 and1.2207 g/cm³. Using the stated formula weight and cell volumes gives 1.09179255,1.31816152 and1.22061110 g/cm³; the third is close but not an exact rounding match. Conditional densities for two such formula units are 2.18358510,2.63632304 and2.44122220 g/cm³. Neither alternative is adopted as a corrected observation.
