Calcite mineral

Calcium carbonate mineral with ideal formula CaCO3, distinct from its polymorphs vaterite and aragonite, calcium as an element, limestone or marble resources, and manufactured calcium carbonate products. The September 2026 IMA list gives grandfathered status and original-description year 1836, not a new-mineral approval accession. Curated laboratory experiments identify calcite in bacterial-treatment solids; neither metal-removal percentages nor criticality designations are inherited by this mineral from their target metals.

Microbial & Biochemical Mechanisms (2)

Calcite formation in a calcium-supplemented DW018 terbium assay

biomineralization Microbial

Associated Organisms: Lysinibacillus (NCBITaxon:400634) [TaxonMech]

Washed urease-producing Lysinibacillus sp. DW018 biomass produced calcite-assigned diffraction peaks after 2 h in a calcium- and urea-supplemented synthetic Tb(III) solution. SEM-EDS associated Ca and Tb with the cell-bearing product, but does not establish Tb substitution into the calcite lattice, a pure calcite product, or phase-specific Tb uptake. Early solution Tb depletion occurred before the resolved calcite peaks and also without added calcium. Calcite formation therefore does not establish the cause or necessity of the rapid Tb-removal response.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
DW018 was isolated from ionic rare-earth tailings in Gannan, China, and assigned by the authors to Lysinibacillus using 16S rRNA analysis; the genus identifier does not identify this strain or establish a species assignment. The experiment used synthetic solution rather than tailings leachate or actual wastewater. Log-phase cells were harvested at 4000 rpm, washed three times in sterile water and resuspended in sterile saline. The standard 30 mL assay had final OD600 1.0, reported 400 micromolar Tb(III), 25 mM CaCl2 and 2% urea, at 37 degrees C; the Tb salt counter-ion and percentage basis for urea are not specified. Each experiment was repeated three times. The adsorption comparator replaced both CaCl2 and urea with water; a separate calcium-dose arm included no added Ca. The final recommended no-added-Ca/0.5% urea recipe is distinct from the standard Ca-supplemented calcite experiment. No matched-pH cell-free or killed-biomass phase-control result is reported. XRD solids were centrifuged at 8000 rpm for 10 min, washed, pre-cooled at -20 and -80 degrees C, freeze-dried for 24 h and ground; SEM specimens additionally underwent glutaraldehyde fixation, phosphate buffer washing and ethanol dehydration. Prepared ex situ solids do not establish the unaltered in-solution amorphous precursor or Tb coordination. The supernatant fluorescence assay measured depletion after centrifugation, not a purified Tb product or an industrial recovery operation.

Calcite assigned in ureolytic geothermal-brine treatment solids

biomineralization Microbial

Associated Organisms: Sporosarcina pasteurii (NCBITaxon:1474) [TaxonMech]

Prepared S. pasteurii DSM 33 biomass with urea generated mixed carbonate-bearing solids during treatment of one natural geothermal brine. The authors assigned calcite together with vaterite and magnesite by powder XRD. This mineral-level observation shares the Rehmanji2025 experiment with cmmmech:vaterite and cmmmech:lithium-bearing-geothermal-brine; those records are not independent replications. It does not identify the proportion of Ca, Mg, Mn or Sr entering calcite, demonstrate a pure calcite yield, or recover lithium into a separate product.

Experimental Context & Analytical Limits:
DSM 33 cells were grown in TSB with 500 mM urea at 28 degrees C and 180 rpm, harvested in mid-log phase at 3200 g for 15 min and PBS-washed. Unsterilized natural brine from an unnamed Cornish geothermal project was treated under reported non-growth conditions: 100 mL in 250 mL flasks, initial OD600 0.05-0.4, 500 mM added urea, 28 degrees C, static for 24 h, in triplicate. The source-designated B. subtilis ATCC-2385 comparator and a separate brine-plus-urea/no-added-cell arm provide treatment comparisons, but no matched-pH chemical or killed-cell phase-control series is reported. The comparator strain designation remains unverified. Section 4.6 sampled 15 mL at 24 h, centrifuged at 3200 g for 15 min and dried the pellet at 60 degrees C for 24 h for XRD; the reported dry-pellet quantity is not a mass-balance mineral yield. Phase assignments concern processed mixed solids, without quantitative refinement or an in situ phase time course. Cow-urine/CaCl2 assays and spent-yeast biomass cultivation are separate arms. Laboratory flask treatment and slurry tests do not establish industrial deployment.
