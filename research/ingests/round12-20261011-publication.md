## Evidence and decisions

The linked curation assessments retain exact queries, acquisition successes/failures, raw-source hashes, original and corrected record hashes, inspected locators and scientific limitations: [aluminium and borates](round12-aluminium-boron-20261011.md), [industrial minerals](round12-industrial-minerals-20261011.md), [carbon and metal products](round12-carbon-metals-20261011.md), and [rare-earth/platinum groups](round12-groups-20261011.md). Each has a JSON receipt. Copyrighted full text and bulk data remain outside the repository.

Borates retains a colemanite culture-associated leaching observation with unresolved abiotic boron release, internally inconsistent conditions and nonclosing composition. Phosphate rock retains separate soluble-phosphate versus rock assay arms, fluoride inhibition, limited control sampling and unavailable supplementary material. Coking coal retains thymol controls, final acid washing, source table inconsistencies and the absence of demonstrated coking performance. The rare-earth family retains an engineered bacterial-protein separation of synthetic Nd/Dy ions: pair-based purity, eluate-based reported yield, limited column controls/replication and no demonstrated whole-feed recovery, living-cell extraction or industrial deployment.

Economic group identities and designated product forms are explicit. Bauxite, alumina and high-purity alumina are distinct; broad graphite includes natural/synthetic products and overlaps the narrower natural-graphite record. EU light/heavy groups use a named assessment convention. The Australian sixteen-member rare-earth family includes promethium and excludes separately listed scandium. The EU permanent-magnet entry is an end-use grouping of seven named elements, not finished magnets.
