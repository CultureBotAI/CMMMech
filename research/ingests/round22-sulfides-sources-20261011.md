## Accepted evidence and experimental interpretation

[USGS Fact Sheet 2025–3017](https://pubs.usgs.gov/fs/2025/3017/fs20253017.pdf), version 1.1 April 2025, Table 1/page 2, independently distinguishes hydrothermal polymetallic sulfides from other seabed resources. Its US 2022 asterisks concern constituent elements. The previously acquired PDF and rendered page 2 were rechecked; no fresh download is claimed.

[Saito et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.2473/journalofmmij.131.627), Journal of MMIJ 131(12):627–633, published 1 December 2015 and released online 1 January 2016, is the primary assay. The seven-page Japanese main text, English captions, Table 1 and all nine figures were inspected. The official publisher HTML lists supplementary material (0). Full-paper SHA256: `662d6ca9585a91556e345ee3b4a2a20bb5e79ad57c392be321fb93a53db964b9`. Reading rights are free access; redistribution was not established, so the PDF remains outside Git.

Methods 2.1–2.2 describe marine hydrothermal ores near Japan (exact sites unresolved), grinding/sieving 38–53 µm, two distilled-water washes and room-temperature drying. XRD resolves mixed sphalerite/chalcopyrite/galena/montmorillonite; Table 1 sample A includes 45.4 wt% SiO2, not a pure sulfide mineral. The adapted living culture is A. brierleyi DSM 1651, verified at [DSMZ](https://www.dsmz.de/collection/catalogue/details/culture/DSM-1651), which identifies a Yellowstone thermal-spring type strain. No marine-isolate claim or extra taxon CURIE is introduced.

Batch flasks contain 200 cm3 modified medium in 500 cm3 baffled vessels, shaken 100 rpm at 65°C. Elemental sulfur and ferrous sulfate are omitted from the specified medium, while yeast extract 0.5 kg/m3 is included. Serial ore transfers reached similar leaching after the eighth transfer. Figure 1 sample A uses 5 kg/m3 loading, 1.0 × 10^13 cells/m3, initial pH 1.8; section 3.1 reports 1.8 ± 0.3. The prose day 10 values are approximately 85% Cu and 80% Zn with culture versus 4% Cu and 20% Zn sterile control. The paper computes these fractions from measured liquid metal concentrations (ICP/AAS) and starting ore content. These are conditional solution-extraction percentages, not isolated-product yields. Graphs were inspected, not numerically digitized.

The controls begin without inoculation. Detailed sterilization protocol, replicate number and uncertainty are unreported; no dynamically pH/redox-matched control establishes a unique molecular pathway. Proposed contact action and ferric/sulfur chemistry remain interpretations rather than separately isolated contributions. Figure 2 XRD runs start at pH 1.2, different from Figure 1. Figure 9 reuses earlier terrestrial-concentrate experiments; it is not a second independent marine-ore experiment.

## Contradictions, null outcomes and boundaries

- Pb and Au liquid concentrations are reported below 0.1 g/m3 analytical detection, not zero. Inoculated-residue anglesite peaks support a changed Pb-bearing phase; dissolution followed by reprecipitation is inferred, not time-resolved product accounting.
- Figure 3/section 3.3 report lower Ga extraction with higher loading, along with residue Ga/Au enrichment. Section 3.3 prints a liquid Ga value of 116 ± 20 g/m3. Table 1 sample A Ga is 46.7 mg/kg; at 5–40 kg ore/m3, the entire starting inventory is only 0.2335–1.868 g Ga/m3 (curator unit calculation, not source measurement). Thus the printed aqueous value does not close against the stated feed. No unit correction or numerical Ga recovery is guessed.
- The conclusion broadly claims above 80% Cu/Zn in 10 days across the loading range; section 3.4.3 describes about 80% after 15 days and Figure 6 shows lower high-load early endpoints. The record accepts the exact Figure 1 scope, not that blanket conclusion.
- Figure 8 NaCl caption uses kg/m3 for values printed as kmol/m3 in Methods/section 3.4.5. Higher nominal salt alters abiotic leaching and suppresses copper bioleaching; no resolved salt threshold is asserted. Growing organisms and no-added-NaCl laboratory medium do not demonstrate seawater operation.
- The pH-variation subsection reports ±0.1 drift, narrower than section 3.1 ±0.3. Conditions remain per-experiment rather than harmonized silently.
- No native concentration observations were imported: the selected outcomes are extraction fractions, and Table 1 lacks the source analysis dates required for native mg/kg cells. No schema change is needed.

Exact DOI/title correction/retraction/erratum searches, the publisher page and Crossref relation/update fields did not reveal a relevant correction in inspected results. This bounded search is not exhaustive; discrepancies remain unresolved.
