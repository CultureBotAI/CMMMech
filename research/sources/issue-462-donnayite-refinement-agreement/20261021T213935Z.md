## Specimen and processing boundaries

The paper's Table 1 and pp. 133–135 reassess seven museum specimens formerly catalogued as donnayite cotypes. Six become alicewilsonite-(YCe); CMNMC 39396 is the retained donnayite holotype under proposal 22-F. Its single-crystal refinement gives R1 = 0.139 without a satisfactory model. The successful model in Table 5 belongs to separate CMNMC 90405 at 293 K; its cell and model composition agree with the official supplemental CIF at the precision audited here. The article reports CSD 2223388 for that model, while the newly authenticated dataset metadata joins the deposition to the article without independently naming CMNMC 90405. These are different strengths of provenance, not a direct deposit-to-specimen certificate.

Table 2, original PDF p. 4 / printed p. 136, separately reports 17 holotype analyses and 11 CMNMC 90405 analyses. WDS used 20 kV, 10 nA and a 30 micrometre beam with time-dependent-intensity corrections. CO2 and H2O were calculated from stoichiometry. Selected means sum to the printed totals 99.15 and 99.19 wt%; the historical comparison column's listed non-dash means sum to 98.47 wt%. Calculated volatile sums are 37.25, 35.59 and 37.32 wt%, respectively. The latter historical column was read in this 2023 paper, not independently in the original 1978 article. Missing entries remain dashes, and these compositions are not extraction yields or validated mass balances.

The holotype's Rigaku/CrysAlis and face-indexed absorption workflow cannot be assigned automatically to CMNMC 90405. The successful specimen is described as measured on a Bruker Kappa APEX II in the article; the CIF names a Bruker Kappa X8 APEX and SADABS 2016/2 multi-scan correction, with transmission factors 0.5822–0.7479. Its data-collection, cell-refinement and data-reduction software fields are `?`. The CIF cell-measurement subset (4,647 reflections, theta 2.603–34.666 degrees) is a different metadata category from the 13,700 collected reflections and final theta limits 3.308–28.279 degrees; the audit does not call those separate categories an error.

## Agreement discrepancy and quantitative audit

Original Table 5, PDF p. 6 / printed p. 138, and the official Table 5 workbook report Rint = 0.092. The official CIF reports `_diffrn_reflns_av_R_equivalents = 0.0342`; it separately reports `_diffrn_reflns_av_unetI/netI = 0.0421`. These three fields are preserved literally. Their source definitions and processing lineage are not interchangeable. The difference 0.0578 and ratio about 2.6901 are diagnostics, not evidence that either value is correct.

The CIF retains 13,700 collected, 4,270 unique and 3,366 above-threshold reflections, 272 parameters, three restraints, inversion-twin refinement and Flack parameter 0.10(3). Its R1 values 0.0548 and 0.0711 correspond approximately to the paper's 0.055 and 0.071; the all-data wR2 0.1263 corresponds approximately to 0.126. The observed-subset wR2 is 0.1195 in the CIF versus 0.119 in Table 5; no truncation or version explanation is chosen. Agreement of other summary fields does not resolve Rint.

The one-block CIF contains 112 scalars and six native loops, including 54 atom-site entries, 27 anisotropic rows, 133 bonds and 951 angles. It has one identity symmetry operation, P1. The site entries reduce to 45 distinct coordinate positions; mixed cations share sites. Summed occupancies give C6 Na1 Ca1 Sr2.1 Ba0.9 Y0.75 Dy0.25 O21. No H atom is located, although the formula declares H6. The Dy contribution is a scattering proxy for lanthanides, not an element-specific assay. The final fixed occupancies are distinguished from the initial refined scattering estimates in Table 6's footnote.

All 1,084 listed geometry rows were recalculated using the deposited cell, coordinates and explicit lattice translations. Every residual is within the listed standard uncertainty plus half the final printed digit. All 27 anisotropic matrices pass a positive-definiteness check. These are internal model checks, not refinement reruns, validation of experimental observations or uncertainty propagation. The metric volume is 434.8637 cubic angstroms versus printed 434.86(7); formula weight and printed cell give density about 3.4065 g/cm3 versus CIF 3.406. Occupancy electron count is 410.95 without H and 416.95 with formula H6, consistent with rounded F000 = 417. Neither check independently locates hydrogen or validates water content.
