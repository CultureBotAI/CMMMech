Petalite is the lithium aluminium silicate mineral species LiAlSi4O10, listed by IMA-CNMNC in September 2026 as grandfathered, with 1800 as the year of its original description (PDF page 170). This year is not an IMA approval-number identifier. The Handbook of Mineralogy describes monoclinic petalite occurring in granite pegmatites, where it can locally constitute lithium ore. This is geological resource context from the 2001 entry, not a claim about present production, universal ore grade or extractability. Its listed associations include spodumene and lepidolite; their occurrence together does not make those minerals or the lepidolite series synonyms for petalite. The species is distinct from elemental lithium, a bulk pegmatite feed, and a manufactured lithium product. No microbial mechanism or criticality designation is assigned here.
