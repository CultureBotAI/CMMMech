## Source assessments

### Saléeite

Current IMA identifies a decahydrate distinct from metasaléeite and arsenate analogues. Dal Bo et al. investigated natural Shinkolobwe saléeite associated with torbernite. Pages2–7, Table1 and Table3 support the recorded P21/n cell and R1 at293(2)K. Qualitative EDS found no Fe/As, while the crystallographic model refined0.97(1)Mg/0.03(1)Fe: the record retains these analytical distinctions. Hydrogen positions used O–H restraints and constrained displacement parameters. No percentage is inferred from the ambiguous percent-labelled twin-proportion table. The CSD-430599 deposit is cited but uninspected; no re-refinement was performed. Bassetite oxidation is a separate mineral discussion.

ORBi labels the file Publisher postprint, whereas its pages say PrePub Article. Crossref confirms final journal28(3):663–675 and2016 identity. The inspected version remains explicitly identified. All figures/references were inspected; the deposit was not. The PDF shows Schweizerbart copyright and remains outside Git.

The Poland study is deferred. Indexed text suggests metatorbernite traces, beam/vacuum water loss and a proposed weathering/sorption sequence. Its pH and causal claims require complete methods/figures to distinguish observation from inference. Earlier Bacillus precipitates remain saleeite-like; a newly curated mineral owner does not upgrade that assignment.

### Sabugalite

Frondel's doubled formula is equivalent to the current IMA half formula. Mina da Quarta Seira material was cleaned and optically homogeneous; the Kariz analysis retained an estimated5% meta-autunite admixture. Water-by-difference and a different direct-water measurement are not pooled. The original symmetry assignment was uncertain, so no current space group is inferred.

Pages677–679 and Tables5–6 directly support the heating record. Loss is percent of original water, not total mineral mass or uranium yield. Patterns persisted at44/68°C, were mixed at101/117°C and changed to the source-assigned historical meta-II pattern at135/165°C. Constant-weight wording coexists with explicit optical heterogeneity and inadequate time to equilibrate. Durations, rates, atmosphere, humidity, mass and replication are unreported. Moist-air observations do not establish universal irreversibility, and water immersion did not measure dissolution rates. Failed synthesis routes remain preparation-specific nulls.

Later literature requires version reconciliation. Springer labels DOI9994-0 an erratum; the purported original6916-0 redirects in Crossref to a journal record listing that DOI as an alias, and the publisher original-article link loops to the erratum. These are observed metadata behaviors, not an established scientific error. Without the complete correction, no later temperature/composition claim is admitted. The1983 synthesis source,1988 thermal/IR DOI10.1007/BF01913913,1951 saléeite/nováčekite paper and2013 natural Ca/Mg-sabugalite DOI10.1111/rge.12019 remain bibliographic leads only.
