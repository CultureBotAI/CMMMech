## Evidence inspected and limitations

The full 340-page thesis is retained outside Git, SHA256 `efe06523c80a2b0586ff6b54011fd0e486a7a499b65c3f756155ed743b065b3e`, 21,893,318 bytes; PDF signature/page count were verified. The curated subset is Chapter 2 specimen characterization and disc experiment, especially printed pp. 89–133 (PDF 97–141), with original sample origin pp. 83–86 checked. Tables 2.4/2.7 identify mixed Bancroft material: chemical allocation gives 78.81±0.6 wt% nepheline, microprobe 84.0±8.5; albite, sodalite, biotite and magnetite also occur. The source calls the collected material near-pure, but its own analyses preclude a pure-phase interpretation. Chemical allocation assumptions and sparse microprobe sampling have different uncertainties.

Tables 2.8,2.10–2.11 and section 2.3.4 define CMI cultures, sucrose LM1, disc preparation and one destructive disc per timepoint. All Figures 2.21–2.26 were visually examined, including microscopic gel/hypha association, Al/pitting relationship, blank trace and the separate syenite illustration. Figure 2.25 shows greater Bancroft pitting for historical CMI 31821 than the near-zero generic blank. No points were digitized. Text names CMI 243400 while the figure legend prints CMI 234300; the second-strain identity/performance remains unresolved. One stated blank per material and one disc per timepoint limit statistical interpretation, and the plotted blank is not separately labelled by material. Disc temperature, agitation and liquid volume are not restated, so conditions from other experiments are not silently transferred. Whole-disc Al release is not phase-specific yield; proton, ligand and physical effects are not individually isolated. Reactor performance, economic claims and kinetic models elsewhere in the thesis were not fully reviewed or curated. The 1987 King–Dudeney journal citation is a follow-up lead, not an independent supporting experiment here.

IMA September 2026 PDF 154 was visually inspected: nepheline formula Na3K(Al4Si4O16), Rd 2018s.p., original 1801 reference. NCBI XML resolves 5061 to *Aspergillus niger*, species rank; it does not authenticate CMI 31821 or update historical stocks by inference.
