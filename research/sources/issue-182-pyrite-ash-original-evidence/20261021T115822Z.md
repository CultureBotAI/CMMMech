## Claim-level assessment

### 2017 abstract: promising result, unresolved denominator

The current [official SDU abstract](https://avesis.sdu.edu.tr/yayin/29f87aeb-c9f3-4920-94be-38bca36bd6fc/synergistic-effect-of-biogenic-fe3-coupled-to-s-degrees-oxidation-on-simultaneous-bioleaching-of-cu-co-zn-and-as-from-hazardous-pyrite-ash-waste) and [indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27915100/) describe a hematite-rich ash and meso-acidophilic iron/sulfur oxidizers. Reported eight-day percentages for Cu/Co/Zn/As are 95/97/78/60, at 10% pulp, pH 1.75, 10% v/v inoculum and 9 g/L Fe(II). Pulp's mass/volume basis is unstated in the abstract. Corresponding reported rates are 1696/338/576/75 ppm/day and 12/12.2/9.8/7.5 percent dissolution/day. These are source-reported abstract values, not accepted measured solution concentrations or isolated-product yields.

Four endpoint/time checks yield 11.875, 12.125, 9.75 and 7.5 percentage points/day. Differences from rounded abstract rates do not identify a correction or a kinetic model. Neither abstract establishes complete medium/S dose, adaptation/strain provenance, working volume, sampling/volume replacement, biomass state, sterile/killed or acid/Fe-matched controls, replicates or uncertainty. The general iron/sulfur oxidation explanation is a proposed interpretation until those controls and measurements can be inspected.

This supports a stated research lineage, not identical batches, assays or independent replicated runs. The caption describes four individual reactors with jacketed temperature control; it does not establish four biological replicates or a numbered figure verified from pixels.

The [indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28881272/) reports Cu/Co/Zn/As dissolution of 94.01/98.54/75.95/60.80% over eight days at 150 rpm, 1 g/L sulfur and 30 °C, with reported daily percentages 11.75/12.3/9.49/7.6. Endpoint averages are 11.75125/12.3175/9.49375/7.60 percentage points/day.
