Virgilite mineral

Virgilite (IMA 1977-009) is an IMA-accepted lithium aluminosilicate framework mineral with a stuffed beta-quartz structure, with IMA September 2026 Master List formula LiAlSi2O6 and holotype solid-solution composition Li0.61(Si2.37Al0.60Fe0.02P0.01)O6 (approximately 61 mol% LiAlSi2O6 and 39 mol% SiO2; Table 1, p. 463 holotype NMNH 2143-17 electron microprobe oxides SiO2 77.29, Al2O3 16.57, FeO 0.64, P2O5 0.57 wt% with Li2O = 4.93 wt% calculated by difference, total 100.0 wt%, supported by ~5 wt% Li2O from laser spectrographic analysis of sample 2143-3). Described by French, Jezek, and Appleman (1978) from the Macusani peraluminous rhyolitic volcanic glass (14°S, 70°30'W), Carabaya Province, Puno Department, southeastern Peru (holotype NMNH 2143-17), where it occurs as colorless euhedral microphenocrysts (up to 50 um across, plus fibrous rosette-like overgrowths) with quartz, andalusite, sillimanite, and biotite. Hexagonal (space group P6222 or P6422), with a = 5.132(1) Å, c = 5.454(1) Å, V = 124.41(2) Å3, Z = 1, Dcalc = 2.46 g/cm3, Vickers microhardness VHN = 681–722 kg/mm2, and uniaxial (–) optics with n = 1.520 and birefringence 0.005–0.006.
