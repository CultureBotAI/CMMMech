## Exact claim boundaries

The Turin PDF and current PMC text agree on the unresolved calculation wording.
PDF p5 gives chrysotile-culture Mg 1.6 ± 0.11mM and mineral-medium Mg
0.87 ± 0.63mM, then describes a 0.69 ± 0.08mM inoculum correction and a
0.72 ± 0.02mM initial positive-control value linked to missing Table S2.
Figure 2B shows separate 0/48/168h Mg bars, with approximate culture heights
0.55/1.8/2.2mM and medium-only heights 0.55/1.7/2.0mM. Those visual readings
are diagnostics, not digitized measurements or replacement endpoints.
The figure defines error bars as standard deviations of triplicates.

Table 2, PDF p11, was also inspected as an original page image and in PMC.
Its own-study row explicitly uses **two days**, not the whole one-week assay:
Mg 0.55→1.83mM and Si 0.21→1.10mM with stated loading 1.0g/L, and reported
rate entries 0.64 and 0.45 under mM g^-1 day^-1 headings. The footnote states
that mineral-medium controls gave similar rates. The two-day values are
compatible with the approximate 48h bars, but do not resolve the p5 values
or identify every blank/correction. No table rate is imported or rescaled;
the volume, loading, time and subtraction basis need a complete calculation
trail before quantitative comparison. Cross-study table rows use different
conditions and some assumed initial zeros; they are not a uniform benchmark.

PDF p12 states that ICP-OES results subtract negative-control measurements,
while the paper separately plots mineral-medium series. It also specifies
triplicate mineral-free growth controls and mineral-medium controls. Without
the supplement or run table, the analytical blank, biological control and
inoculum corrections cannot be unambiguously assigned to each plotted value.
The main supports the absence of a demonstrated microbial Mg/Si release
advantage under the tested chrysotile conditions; it does not support a repaired
positive extraction effect.

For crocidolite, p12 separates filtered liquid from the unfiltered 8h analytical
HCl extraction and normalizes extracted Fe(II)/Fe(III) to their sum. Those
fractions are not feed recovery yields. The source references S9 for extraction
timing, and p13 references S10/S11 for electron-beam and medium controls.
Crystallinity, structural-versus-coating Fe and source-composition limits remain
as qualified in the accepted owner. Neither this access check nor measured
liquid release proves fibre destruction, reduced toxicity, purified product or
deployment. No new mineral or organism identity is inferred.
