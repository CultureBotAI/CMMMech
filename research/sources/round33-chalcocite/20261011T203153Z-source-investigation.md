No constituent criticality or unverified mineral CURIE is added. Its incomplete accounting is retained rather than repaired. The accepted claim is the reported leaching-index/potential association, not a validated extraction fraction or universal microbial mechanism.

## Accepted experiment and adversarial assessment

| Dimension | Original locator and bounded interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Natural specimen | Section 4.1, p18286: Zijinshan ore; hand sorting, gravity separation and flotation; separate particles below 0.074 mm. Table 2 p18280 gives 68.42 wt% Cu, 2.53 wt% Fe, 23.98 wt% S and 0.11 wt% SiO2 with a 92.40% purity label. Its elemental composition is not a quantified endmember phase fraction, and other copper-bearing phases are not excluded. |
| Community | Heap-sump inoculum, four to five days' laboratory growth/adaptation on 9K. Figure 6 gives genus-level sequencing composition of separate mineral assays; it is neither an authenticated strain profile nor a functional assay for the mixture. |
| Conditions | Section 2.2/Figures 4–5: chalcocite:pyrite mass ratio 1:2, 30 °C, 14 days, initial pH 1.8, inoculum liquid:pulp volume ratio 1:10. Section 4.2 reports initial density 2 × 10^7 cells/mL and acid adjustment using 20% v/v sulfuric acid. The density basis across inoculum ratios is unclear. |
| Missing protocol details | Flask working volume, solid mass, agitation, exact 9K recipe/external iron basis, inoculum washing, dynamic pH control, sterilization and sterility verification are not supplied. The raw-ore reactor's 5% loading/120 rpm cannot fill flask gaps. |
| Controls and reproducibility | Figure 4 has inoculated and uninoculated mixed-separate arms. They are not matched for dynamic potential or verified sterility. Section 4.5 states at least three repeats, mean values and SD bars. Sampling replacement/cumulative accounting are unknown. |
| Measured versus calculated | Section 4.4: supernatant Cu and total Fe by ICP-OES; Pt/AgAgCl potential converted to the hydrogen scale. Figure 4's ordinate is calculated leaching efficiency, not a raw concentration series. Figure 5 is the potential comparison. |
| Observed comparison | In Figure 4, source-reported Cu indices are similar at day 2, then higher with inoculation. Uninoculated release is substantial; inoculation also increases the Fe index. The record adopts no numerical extraction percentage. |
| Central accounting defect | Printed Equation 2 p18286 subtracts the C×V×M/(alpha×m) term from 1 while describing C as solution metal concentration. The expression decreases as C rises and does not transparently match the reported extraction direction. C/M units and independent source concentrations/masses/volumes are unavailable. Do not silently remove the subtraction or invent a corrected yield. |
| Sampling/identity limits | Sections 2.3.1–2.3.2 refer to absorbed/attached material, while Figure 6 and DNA methods describe solution samples. No attached-community-specific claim or genus causal assignment is accepted. |
| Mechanistic explanation | Ferric attack, sulfur oxidation and galvanic effects are interpretations without selective organism removal, contact separation or matched dynamic chemistry. Figures 9–12 and Tables 3–5 are density-functional calculations, not measured cellular catalysis or demonstrated defects in the actual specimen. |
| Scale/outcome | Laboratory observation and proposed separation scheme only. No isolated copper product, closed mass balance or industrial deployment. The industrial origin of the community is not process deployment. |

Other source tensions were checked without promoting unrelated arms: Table 1 labels Fe2O3 while text calls it Fe; Figure 3's Fe curve is below the prose's greater-than-40% wording; Section 2.3.2 uses greater-than-700 mV twice for different outcomes while Figure 7 shows the distinct potential trend. Model prose says some Cu–S lengths decrease while Table 4 increases them, and Figure 11's caption calls a chalcocite vacancy Fe rather than Cu. These prevent treating the paper's optimization and atomistic narrative as independently established. The record confines its accepted claim to Figures 4–5 and explicitly retains the shared Equation 2 defect.
