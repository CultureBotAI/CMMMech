## Evidence actually inspected

### Maureira et al., 2024

[Primary article](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbioe.2024.1393334), 13 June 2024,
Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology 12:1393334; PMID38938979,
PMC11208896. Actual publisher HTML, 17-page PDF and Europe PMC full XML acquired.
The full methods/results/discussion were read; Figures2-8 and Table5 were visually
inspected on PDF pages8-13, with Tables1-4 and complete text also inspected.
XML contains 0 supplementary-material nodes; Europe PMC reports hasSuppl=N.
The inspected primary publication did not expose an additional supplement; this
bounded observation does not establish that no other author data exists.

- Sections2.1-2.2: inactive T1 wall, seawater-processing site, sieving at2mm.
  Table2 is the analyzed feed; Table1 is a separately attributed theoretical
  composition, not interchangeable observations. Table2 uses ppm and percent.
- Sections2.3-2.6: isolate-level 16S assignments and urease assays. Table4's
  closest-BLAST-match accessions do not identify the experimental strains.
  The listed PP379235-PP379248 deposits were not independently mapped and are
  deliberately not asserted in records. No species-to-strain CURIE substitution.
- Methods heat tailings at60C for72h to reduce background microbes. Cores use
  150g with50mL suspension; Section4.4 selects the1Mcalcium:0.8Murea condition.
  Other dose combinations did not retain mold shape (data not shown). Methods
  print40%w/v preculture urea; its interpretation remains unresolved.
- Figure4 compares inoculated and no-added-strain controls; the negative control
  nevertheless yielded ureolytic CFUs (Figure8). This limits microbial necessity
  and strain-specific exclusivity. Inoculum density, replicate count and exact
  matching of medium additions were not sufficiently reported for reconstruction.
- Table5/Section3.3 versus Table3 give differing XRD/BMA calcite estimates;
  high amorphous content and phase normalization prevent interpreting percentage
  changes as an absolute pure-mineral yield. No claim of microbial production of
  each newly assigned silicate is accepted. Element maps alone do not identify
  calcite or metal immobilization.
- Preserved null: T130-14 compression difference not significant (Section4.4).
  Methods20days versus CFU caption30days remains unresolved. Several prose
  Figure/Table references are stale; actual caption/content governs the locator.
  Figure8's ureolytic CFU bars can exceed its total-CFU bars, so no quantitative
  CFU ratio or organism-fitness ranking is accepted.
- The study tests physical stabilization in the laboratory. It does not measure
  purified metal recovery, field performance or a metal-immobilization mass balance.

[Las Cenizas operator page](https://www.cenizas.cl/faenas_oficinas/faena-taltal/):
actual HTML inspected, especially Planta Concentradora Las Luces and separate
Planta de Oxidos sections. The live undated process description supports identity,
not a claim about current production capacity or proof of historic sample chemistry.

### Power, Dipple and Southam, 2010 (online 2009)

[Primary DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/es900986n), online1December2009,
issue1January2010, Environmental Science and Technology44:456-462.
[Author's public full copy](https://powergeolab.com/s/Power-et-al-2010-Environ-Sci-Technol-f4jx.pdf)
was linked directly by PowerGeolab's publications page. Web-tool redirect failed,
but direct authorized retrieval obtained a real7-page PDF with matching bibliographic
identity, not a challenge page. The paper's March2024 institutional download footer
is not its publication date. All main text and Figures1-3 were inspected.

[Supplement DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/es900986n.s001), ACS Figshare2802688,
file4497097: real19-page PDF with5tables and7figures, title/authors/main DOI
match. All SI text/tables and FiguresS1-S7 were inspected; TablesS3-S4 and figures
were visually checked. This is the same study, not an independent replication.

- Clinton Creek mixed tailings and fresh ground serpentinite are separate feeds.
  Acetic-acid-washed tailings are a pretreatment arm, not a separate resource root.
  The SI acknowledges washing may alter chrysotile reactivity.
- SI S2-S3: Kam Kotia-derived A.ferrooxidans enrichment and Wits Basin-derived
  sulfur-oxidizer reported as A.thiooxidans; no sequence/strain authentication.
  Culture conditions near21C do not prove column incubation temperature.
- Phase1 has abiotic corresponding-additive and no-additive controls. Sulfur and
  pyrite arms, loads and substrate variants are not pooled as one independent
  condition. Main-text aggregate Mg totals span multiple arms and are not used
  as single-column values. SI S3 shows the source cell denominator explicitly.
- Phase2's one-year8.5wt%sulfur arm releases317mgMg against13.7mg mean
  no-additive control;14.3% uses10gfeed and36.7wt%MgO. It is not a recovered
  product yield. There are no matched Phase2 abiotic-additive columns.
- Highest Phase2 sulfur load is12.5% in Methods but10.5% in Figure2/results/SI.
  Record preserves this conflict and uses only the consistent8.5% numerical arm.
- Existing carbonates contribute Mg and may lose stored carbon. Acid-dependent
  modeling does not prove a pH-independent bacterial effect. Sulfidic-tailings
  arms have metal pulses; the30% arm's decliningpH is a long-term limitation.
- The schematic facility and scaled carbon estimates are proposals. No net carbon
  balance, pure Mg product, industrial deployment or asbestos detoxification is
  established. The wider carbon-removal claim is narrower than title alone suggests.

[July2009 Yukon/AECOM report](https://emrlibrary.gov.yk.ca/aam/former-clinton-creek-asbestos-mine-overview-report-2009.pdf)
