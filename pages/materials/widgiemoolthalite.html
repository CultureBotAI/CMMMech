Widgiemoolthalite mineral

Widgiemoolthalite (IMA 1992-006) is an IMA-accepted hydrated nickel hydroxy-carbonate mineral, the nickel analogue of hydromagnesite, with IMA September 2026 Master List formula Ni5(CO3)4(OH)2·4-5H2O and empirical formula (Ni4.62Mg0.38)(CO3)4.15(OH)1.70·5.12H2O (Table 1, p. 820 electron microprobe and CHN elemental analysis: NiO 54.6, MgO 2.4, CO2 28.9, H2O 17.0, total 102.9 wt%). Described by Nickel, Robinson, and Mumme (1993) from the weathered zone of a nickel sulfide body at the 132 North nickel mine, 4 km southwest of Widgiemooltha, Western Australia, Australia (holotype Western Australian Museum M.1.1993), where it occurs as bluish-green fibrous crusts and spherulites lining cavities in weathered gaspeite along with kambaldaite, takovite, retgersite, and nickeloan paratacamite. Monoclinic unit-cell parameters indexed from X-ray powder diffraction by comparison with hydromagnesite give a = 10.06(17) Å, b = 8.75(5) Å, c = 8.32(4) Å, beta = 114.3(8)°; definitive crystallographic characterization was not obtained. SGmeas = 3.13(1), Dcalc = 3.24 g/cm3, and biaxial (+) optics with nmin = 1.630(5) and nmax = 1.640(5).
