Mixed-cut study feed is distinct from natural graphite, waste electrolyte, dross and constituent elements. No criticality assertion or pure-phase feed is inherited.

### Chemical treatment and sequential comparisons

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:spent-potlining.

Before/after and stage-specific measurements support mobilization without assigning isolated reagent causality, residue purity or a recovered carbon yield.

### Unresolved reproduction differences

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:spent-potlining.

Methods 0.5M acid versus Figure4 1M and TableS2 two-hour versus methods three-hour acid treatment are retained. Methods1.00BV/h versus Figures5–6/S33 0.50BV/h is also explicit; no single harmonized recipe is invented.

### Measured fractions versus model-defined recovery

quantity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:spent-potlining.

The59.0% value uses the main elution peak divided by dynamic-model theoretical column uptake; it is not original-feed-to-isolated-product yield. Column evidence is limited to two sample-A loading arms and the displayed fine-fraction elution.

### Proposed resin binding and modeled solution species

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:spent-potlining.

Aqion speciation and fitted breakthrough parameters are interpretations. Prior-work S1 binding and S2 conceptual circuit do not establish new structural measurements or operation of all downstream stages. Mixing losses include source-assigned cryolite with amorphous material; no saleable pure-product result is claimed.

### Replication, detoxification and scale

Electrode/duplicate measurement error bars are not established independent leach/column campaigns. No available labile cyanide meant destruction was not demonstrated; residual-solid leachability was future work. Authentic industrial waste is laboratory feed, not evidence of industrial deployment, repeat-cycle durability, full mass closure or economics.
