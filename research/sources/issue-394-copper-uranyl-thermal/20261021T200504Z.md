## Thesis lineage and water-content cautions

The Kulaszewska thesis is directly cited by the 2019 paper, but its experiments must still be joined individually. PDF 79–80 (printed 56–57) supplies synthesis and general TGA methods; PDF 92 (printed 69) describes 0.82603 Å synchrotron data, 0.5 mm Kapton capillaries, spinning and the low-temperature acquisition sequence. The synthesis text's stated mass/mole combinations are not silently normalized. The general TGA protocol is 25–35 mg in lidded alumina, 5 °C/min to 500 °C, a ten-minute hold, nitrogen or air; the selected TGA results section specifically says air. This is not a simultaneous gravimetric measurement of every sealed diffraction aliquot.

PDF 149–154 (printed 126–131, §§4.8 and associated Figures 4.17–4.20) reports about 15.8% phosphate loss, about 19% arsenate loss and about 15% mixed loss. The arsenate discussion interprets approximately 11.5 waters, a zeunerite-like starting state, despite a nearby figure caption using metazeunerite. Rounded stage percentages sum to 16%, 19.02% and 15.2%; their comparison with rounded total losses is a diagnostic, not a reconstructed mass balance. Poorly crystalline residues and pattern-shift interpretations do not establish isolated products, element recovery or phase-resolved water inventories. Tables 5.23–5.24 (PDF 185/printed 162) provide the mixed-composition cell comparison above. Selected low-temperature model pages 191–196 retain refinement and Cu-location limitations; the figure caption's “.125%” versus tabulated occupancy .125 and a small bond-valence prose/table difference remain source exceptions. No thesis model is promoted into the owners.

The MacIver-Jones thesis provides a **separate** programme: PDF 78 (printed 64) describes 0.82452(1) Å synchrotron work, heating at 0.5 °C/s with specified 30-minute stops up to 350 °C; PDF 79 (printed 65) describes deuterated neutron samples, partly wet loading, dry synthetic air at 24 sccm, about 2 °C/min and approximately two-hour datasets up to 450 °C. Its printed ISIS DOI is 10.5286/ISIS.E.RB1910215; raw frames from that route were not acquired in this pass. The arsenate neutron starting material is zeunerite. PDF 122–126 (printed 108–112, Figure 4.1 and Tables 4.1–4.2) compares several dehydration phases and different X-ray/neutron sequences. Table 4.2's B* entry is placed in a different method column from the surrounding narrative implication; it is not reassigned here. This context helps explain why experiments need a specimen/atmosphere key, but it is not a correction to the 2019 or 2023 series, and uninspected rehydration/model chapters are not used to assert universal behaviour.
