The mapping audit establishes these limits:

1. **Specimen labels are not unique keys.** All row-table and block labels occur in metadata, but `c1903-1-20a` occurs at Specimen Info rows342–343 under numeric keys104696 and104840. The first mass is0.1771 g and the second blank. The same label appears in two hysteresis blocks and two backfield blocks; neither measurement layout carries the numeric metadata key. A label-only join cannot select a mass safely.
2. **Mass and field provenance remain incomplete.** 119 of520 metadata masses are blank. Five hysteresis blocks in measurements33, beginning at columns37,43,49,55,61, use `T20-310-3`, `T20-411-5`, `T20-534-3` or `T411-1` with no metadata mass. Only one history entry mentions an uploaded mass revision, without old/new values. All mineral, Batch_ID and IGSN/parent fields are blank; 500 Z_coordinates and10 Site cells are blank. Numeric and text site conventions coexist. The workbook cannot alone resolve each label to the paper's packet, depth, collection time, phase and normalization history.
3. **Repeated content is not independent evidence.** There are33 exact duplicate history rows,25 duplicate Magnon rows and79 duplicate hysteresis-property rows. Among the wide blocks,28 pairs have identical labels, preambles, headers and complete parsed data. These are retained, not deduplicated into an invented set of independent experiments. Other repeated labels may denote genuine repeated loops, fits or distinct runs, but lack a demonstrated one-to-one run join.
4. **Unit conflicts remain explicit.** The mixed magnetization headers are unchanged. `MPMSdc measurements 4` cellR7C33 and `MPMSdc measurements 6` cellR7C108 contain25000 under `Bapp [T]` at header row3, for `411-1_dec_a` and `IHFO_mpms`. Standard MPMS blocks and the main method use2.5 T. The irregular blocks have no protocol label and require source clarification; no presumed Oe-to-T or magnetization conversion is applied.
5. **Reusable identifiers are archival locators only.** For this exact workbook, retain `(workbook SHA256, sheet, block start column, row, column)` or the exact row-table cell as a stable locator. That locator is not a biological replicate or an instrument run identifier. MPMS timestamps remain unzoned strings; other wide layouts lack timestamps. Signed magnetic values are not concentration censoring, and blanks are not zero.
