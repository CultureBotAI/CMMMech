# Remaining CMMMech frontier after round 9

The [exact-root reconciliation](round9-frontier-20261011.json) compares the
current corpus with the historical
[authority inventory](../sources/expansion-authorities/20261011T040103Z.md).
It is not a fresh census of every list or an assertion that all proposed
commodity scopes deserve separate records.

The corpus now contains **54 records: 48 elements, four mineral species and
two secondary resources**. Cesium, strontium, ruthenium, rhodium, silicon and
tin add six element roots. Their scientific boundaries and independent reviews
are linked in the [publication report](round9-20261011-publication.md).

Of the inventory's 94 proposed roots, 48 are represented by an exact record ID
and kind; **16 element and 30 overlapping commodity proposals remain**. This
does not measure coverage of every mechanism or every designation attached to
an existing material. Minerals and resources outside that inventory also remain
eligible. No exhaustion claim is justified.

The remaining element proposals are beryllium, boron, fluorine, hafnium, helium,
iridium, lutetium, niobium, osmium, promethium, rhenium, rubidium, tantalum,
thulium, titanium and zirconium. Each still needs independent identity,
designation-scope and primary-evidence assessment. Group membership is not
permission to infer an unqualified criticality entry.

## Next priorities

1. **Resolve commodity boundaries.** The
   [graphite handoff](round9-graphite-handoff-20261011.md) supplies freshly
   inspected authority documents and a primary flotation study. Prioritize a
   natural-graphite commodity assessment, then adjudicate its overlap with broad
   graphite and battery-grade graphite. An identity-only record is acceptable;
   a microbial mechanism must not be filled merely to match other records.
2. **Investigate the remaining elements in small evidence-led batches.**
   Separate metal, oxide, ion and organometallic identities before evaluating
   removal or particle-synthesis claims. In particular, the retained Horiike
   2015 T9 article studied Y, Pr, Nd, Eu, Tb, Dy and Yb; it supplies no Tm or Lu
   assay to copy into those proposed roots. This is a finding about that full
   article, not an absence claim for the broader literature.
3. **Expand existing mechanisms where evidence warrants it.** The
   [Cs/Sr dossier](round9-cs-sr-20261011.md) retains KMSZP6 and halophilic
   community alternatives that need assay and phase review. The
   [Si/Sn dossier](round9-si-sn-20261011.md) identifies separate purified-SIT
   and LCD-leaching experiments. The
   [Ru/Rh dossier](round9-ru-rh-20261011.md) keeps organism-specific Ru phase
   interpretations and catalyst preparation boundaries explicit. These are
   research leads, not additional accepted observations or replications.
4. **Close access and interpretation gaps.** Prioritize the unavailable TK2d
   and diatom supplements, donor-free Rh outcomes, and tin calibration/sample
   pooling ambiguities when legitimate new evidence becomes available. Record
   corrections in maintained inputs with new history and successor reviews;
   retain earlier observations.

The inventory was enumerated with hidden and ignored files included. Searches
were bounded to the task checkout, relevant symlinks and cited local evidence
paths, as documented in the dossiers. No unrelated worktree or machine-wide
absence claim is made, and unrepresented commodity proposals are not silently
counted as synonyms of their constituent elements.
