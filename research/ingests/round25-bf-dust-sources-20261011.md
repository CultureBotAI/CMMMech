## Mechanism, conditions and accounting

The living sulfur-fed culture was cultivated at 30 degrees C and 160 rpm in a 3-L working volume, with a reported pre-feed pH of 0.85 after seven days. Initial medium pH was adjusted with sulfuric acid; sulfate salts, yeast extract and elemental sulfur were present. Dust loading progressed from 50 through 75 and 100 to 125 g/L in 14-day cycles. Each exchange retained 1 L of culture, liquid and residual solid, then replenished fresh medium, dust and sulfur. Three consecutive 125-g/L cycles at 0.42 vvm preceded three at 0.50 vvm. These six cycles are not six independently inoculated reactors. Dust sterilization is unspecified; drying a characterization aliquot does not prove thermal pretreatment of all feed.

The source's pooled day-7 values are 68.1 +/- 2.1% Zn and 5.1 +/- 2.1% Fe, versus 75.2 +/- 5.5% Zn and 9.0 +/- 1.5% Fe at day 14.
