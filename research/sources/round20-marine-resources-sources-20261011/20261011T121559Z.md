## Scientific assessment

The accepted 2022 experiment used ground natural nodules, living mixed enrichment cultures, sealed flasks and sucrose/citrate medium. Starting-mud 16S composition does not resolve the active final culture or establish individual taxon causality; no organism CURIE is assigned. The medium contains added citric acid, and final runs lack a matched sterile or killed-cell chemical comparator. Leachate mobilization can be recorded, but a microbial increment, direct enzymatic reduction and specific organic-acid production cannot be isolated.

The 2120 mg/L Mn value is supported by Results 3.3 and Figures 2A/5 for the fourth sample-3 collection. It remains a reported leachate concentration, not a measured isolated yield or Mn(II) speciation. The methods name portable XRF for liquid/solid analysis and ICP-OES confirmation of solid residue; analytical replication is not independent culture replication. No filtering or analytical speciation protocol is invented.

Several source discrepancies limit stronger extraction claims:

- Methods 2.3 describes 10/20 g/L sucrose enrichment variants, Methods 2.4 gives 10 g in the tap-water bottle, while Results 3.3/Figure 3 describe 20 g/L. The final formulation and timing are not silently normalized.
- The nominal pH interval is exceeded by measured values; it is not maintained pH for all bottles.
- Figure 3/Discussion 4 give about 22% Ni at 10% pulp, conflicting with prose saying all extraction fell below 20%.
- Table 4 lists solid concentrations. Their depletion percentages, washed-residue handling and a reported 13.3% solid mass loss do not constitute a reconciled input/output metal mass balance.
- Figure 5 supplies numerical measurements for collections 1–5 only; the single 6–25 box has no measured series. Complete extraction after about 25 medium changes is therefore not an established observed endpoint.
- Precipitation boxes lack isolated-product measurement. The discussion's industrial BIOX limit concerns a different feed/process, and flask results do not establish nodule industrial deployment or environmental superiority.

No structured concentration cells were added: source analysis dates required by the native observation contract are unavailable. No schema change is needed.
