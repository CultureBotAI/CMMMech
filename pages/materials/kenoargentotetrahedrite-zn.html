Kenoargentotetrahedrite-(Zn) mineral IMA: Kattr-Zn

IMA-approved silver-copper-zinc antimony sulfosalt mineral (IMA 2020-075; symbol Kattr-Zn; status A, Approved 2020) with ideal end-member formula [Ag6]4+(Cu4Zn2)Sb4S12[] (written Ag6(Cu4Zn2)Sb4S12[] in the September 2026 IMA Master List; requiring Ag 33.99 wt%, Sb 25.58 wt%, S 20.21 wt%, Cu 13.35 wt%, Zn 6.87 wt%, total 100 wt%), representing the Zn-dominant keno-member of the freibergite series in the tetrahedrite group and the Zn analogue of kenoargentotetrahedrite-(Fe) (Qu et al., 2024, European Journal of Mineralogy 36:397-409). Discovered at the Yindongpo gold deposit in the Weishancheng Au-Ag-Pb-Zn ore belt, Tongbai County, Nanyang City, Henan Province, China (32 deg 33 min 02 sec N, 113 deg 25 min 25 sec E), where it occurs in hydrothermal veins hosted by silicified and carbonaceous quartz-sericite schist of the upper Neoproterozoic Waitoushan Formation, associated with kenoargentotetrahedrite-(Fe), tetrahedrite-(Zn), sphalerite, galena, pyrite, acanthite, chalcopyrite, and quartz; the holotype specimen is deposited at the Geological Museum of China, Beijing, under catalogue number M16112. Forms black, opaque anhedral grains and equant crystals up to 40 um in size with a black streak, metallic lustre, brittle tenacity, conchoidal fracture, indistinct cleavage, Vickers microhardness VHN50 = 288.7-324.1 kg/mm2 (mean 309.7 kg/mm2, corresponding to Mohs hardness ~3.5-4), and calculated density Dcalc = 5.209 g/cm3 from the empirical formula and single-crystal unit-cell parameters. In reflected light it is greenish-grey and optically isotropic with brown-red internal reflections. Micro-Raman spectroscopy (785 nm laser; Section 6, Fig. 6) shows a major band at 353 cm-1 (symmetric nu1 X(3)-S(1) stretching of SbS3 pyramids) with a shoulder at 346 cm-1 (antisymmetric nu3 Sb-S stretching), bending modes at 314 cm-1 (symmetric nu2 SbS3 bending) and 282 cm-1 (antisymmetric nu4 SbS3 bending), and lattice vibrations at 214, 201, and 160 cm-1. Electron microprobe analysis (9 point analyses, JEOL JXA-8100, 20 kV, 10 nA, 1 um beam; Table 1) yielded Sb 24.97 wt% (range 24.65-25.24, SD 0.19), Ag 22.51 wt% (20.32-24.97, SD 1.44), Cu 21.75 wt% (20.62-23.60, SD 1.02), S 21.29 wt% (20.48-21.80, SD 0.39), Zn 4.28 wt% (3.03-6.52, SD 1.16), Fe 2.16 wt% (0.45-2.87, SD 0.75), Cd 1.62 wt% (0.50-2.13, SD 0.48), and As 1.15 wt% (0.84-1.38, SD 0.18), total 99.73 wt% (98.74-100.60, SD 0.61), corresponding on the basis of 16 cations per formula unit (Sigma M + X = 16) to the empirical formula M(2)(Ag3.75Cu2.25)Sigma6.00M(1)[Cu3.90(Zn1.18Fe0.69Cd0.26)Sigma2.13]Sigma6.03X(3)(Sb3.69As0.27)Sigma3.96S(1)S11.94S(2)[] (with total Cu = 6.15 apfu partitioned into 2.25 apfu at M(2) and 3.90 apfu at M(1)) and simplified formula (Ag,Cu)6[Cu4(Zn,Fe,Cd)2](Sb,As)4S12[]. Crystallizes in the cubic system, space group I-43m (#217), with single-crystal X-ray diffraction unit-cell parameters a = 10.4624(4) A, V = 1145.23(13) A3, and Z = 2 (refined to R1 = 0.0247, wR2 = 0.0383, R1(all) = 0.0289, wR2(all) = 0.0389, GoF = 1.111, and Flack parameter -0.04(8) for 274 reflections with Fo > 4sigma(Fo) out of 294 unique reflections from 1089 measured on a 7 x 6 x 5 um crystal using MoKalpha radiation; general structural formula in Table 3 is Ag3.37Cu6.53Zn1.20Fe0.90Sb3.70As0.30S12.44). The crystal structure features a split M(2) site with triangular-planar M(2a) at 12e (refined occupancy Ag0.562(19), forming a subvalent [Ag6]4+ octahedral cluster with short Ag-Ag = 2.798(12) A, M(2a)-S(1) = 2.525(7) A x 2, and M(2a)...S(2) vacancy centroid = 1.979(9) A coupled to S(2) vacancy []0.562) and flat trigonal-pyramidal M(2b) at 24g (refined occupancy Cu0.219(9) x 2 = 0.438, M(2b)-S(1) = 2.340(9) A x 2, M(2b)-S(2) = 2.25(2) A coupled to S(2) occupancy S0.438(19) at 2a; note Section 5 prose on p. 401 writes 0.436(9) apfu for 2 x M(2b) whereas 2 x 0.219(9) = 0.438(9) as printed on p. 402), a tetrahedral M(1) site at 12d (fixed occupancy Cu0.65Zn0.20Fe0.15, M(1)-S(1) = 2.3427(16) A x 4), and a trigonal-pyramidal X(3) site at 8c (Sb0.928(19)As0.072(19), X(3)-S(1) = 2.436(3) A x 3). Gandolfi powder X-ray diffraction (MoKalpha; Section 5, Table 2) gives refined unit-cell parameters a = 10.4336(5) A and V = 1135.81(17) A3, with the seven strongest bolded reflections (dmeas in A (Imeas; hkl)) at 3.010 (100; 2 2 2), 1.844 (30; 0 4 4), 2.606 (22; 0 0 4), 1.572 (15; 2 2 6), 2.046 (9; 1 3 4), 7.35 (6; 0 1 1), and 1.909 (5; 1 2 5) (alongside 2.792 (5; 1 2 3) and 2.456 (5; 0 3 3)). No biological or microbial interaction data are reported in the type mineralogical description.
