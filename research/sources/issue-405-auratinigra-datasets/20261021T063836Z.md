### Units, transformed data and unresolved workbook labels

- `Data 1` is composition of a source ore layer (`µg/g` and percentages), not
  a La/Gd solution-recovery experiment. `Data 2`/`Data 3` contain reported uptake
  summaries with condition blocks and `±` columns; the meaning of the uncertainty
  and number of independent replicates are not established by the workbook.
- Several `Data 2` adsorption-rate blocks and the `Data 6` relative-decrease
  block label percentages while storing values on a fractional scale. For
  example, January `Data 2!F4` is 0.215 under a percent heading, and
  `Data 6!F3` is 0.75229 under `Relative decrease (%)`. These are preserved
  exactly; no factor-of-100 repair is applied without the methods and figures.
- `Data 7` contains transformed kinetic quantities (`t/qt`, logarithms) and
  `Data 8` contains isotherm transformations (`Ce/qe`, `lnce`, `lnqe`), not
  independent measured recovery. Here `Ce` is a model-column token; it is not
  silently mapped to elemental cerium. `Data 12` explicitly contains calculated
  speciation and formula cells; it is not measured solution composition.
- The XPS `Data 5` header `Frederik Southam Balfour` occurs at **20 column
  positions**, including M1, U1 and AC1. It is unchanged across the compared main
  workbooks. The inspection does not infer its intended meaning or rename it as
  a fitting component. Exact positions are preserved in the evidence JSON.
- In the January second workbook, `Data 10!A2:A7470` and `F2:F7470` are
  identical across all 7,469 numeric rows despite headers `Ph=2(Gd)` and
  `Ph=2(La)`. This is a reproducible array identity, not evidence of its cause.
  It must not be repaired or counted as independent traces. `Data 10` and
  `Data 11` provide no explicit wavenumber column; the axis from `Data 4`
  cannot be silently transferred merely because row counts match.
- Stable acquisition keys are dataset/version/folder/file ID/SHA256. Workbook
  interpretation additionally needs sheet, condition block, original row/column
  and units. No specimen/replicate ID joins the worksheets to all microscopy
  fields. A filename or species label alone is insufficient.

These workbooks support a reproducible inventory of reported measurements,
transformations and unresolved labels. They do not establish an isolated
La/Gd product yield, a mass balance or a causal live-cell effect.

## Microscopy, phase assignments and preparation limits

The official Figure 6 asset (SHA256 `e8f18dd304664aa6…`) presents separate La,
Gd and mixed-ion SEM/EDS fields. Its six SEM panels A/B/D/E/G/H visually correspond
to the six `Fig.6A/B/D/E/G/H.tif` objects in **`Figures (new)`**, not to the older
six `Fig. 6-A` through `Fig. 6-F` objects. The Figure 7 asset (SHA256
`760515e3b8d45145…`) visually corresponds to the nine deposited `Fig. 7-A`
through `Fig. 7-I` TEM/SAED/HRTEM objects shared by all releases. The paired JSON
binds each of these **15 visual correspondences** to full hashes and file IDs.
This is a curator's image-content inference; no author-issued treatment/specimen
crosswalk or pixel-identity claim is made.

The Figure 7 composite explicitly assigns La, Gd and mixed material to
monazite-type phases and adds SAED indices and HRTEM/FFT annotations. The La
panel shows indices (113), (300), (200) and an annotated 0.332nm spacing; Gd
shows (132), (221), (012), (020) and 0.342nm; the mixed panel shows (321),
(031), (120) and 0.312nm. Those are **source annotations**, not an independent
indexing or polymorph verification. The underlying deposited patterns and
lattice images were viewed, including the selected-area diffraction images
and all three HRTEM objects. No calibrated reciprocal-space reanalysis,
alternative-phase fitting or quantitative purity determination was performed.
Neither La/Gd/P EDS composition nor the generic formula REEPO4 by itself
distinguishes all possible structures, hydration states or mixed products.

There is also an unresolved **scale-label difference**: the recognizable fields
in deposited new Fig6A, Fig6D and Fig6G carry `5.00µm` in their acquisition strips,
whereas corresponding published panels show `2µm`; the high-magnification
B/E/H images use 500nm in both. The original strips also retain instrument
settings and `1/9/2026`, whereas the older SEM set retains `9/11/2024` in at
least the inspected Fig6-A object. The numeric date format, correct calibration,
field selection and preparation lineage are not reconstructed. No particle-size
measurement or source correction is derived from these labels.

Supplement Figure S8 (PDF p11) covers SEM/EDS for the three ion systems;
deposit filenames containing `S4` must not be joined by that number alone,
because the final supplement's Figure S4 (p7) is calculated speciation.
Supplement Figure S9 (p12) is enlarged TEM, while Figures S6/S7 (pp9–10)
contain pH-dependent and group-masking FTIR comparisons. Their presence does not
supply the missing biomass preparation, fixation/dehydration, medium phosphate,
sterile-solution controls, electron-beam dose or complete final-phase methods.
Source-assigned nanophase formation remains a promising but unaccepted mechanism
lead for the existing La/Gd owners.

## Strain and molecular evidence

The official supplement names **KLBMP 9576** throughout. Figure S1 on PDF p4
labels the sequence-tree node `Micromonospora sp. KLBMP 9576 (ON693947)` beside
the M. auratinigra type-strain comparator. The NCBI record returned
**ON693947.1**, partial 16S, 1,464bp, taxon 2950557, with strain KLBMP 9576 and
the genus-level label *Micromonospora sp.* Its source qualifiers include `soil`
and the unexpected `Homo sapiens` host. The sequence provides a strain/accession
join, not an independently validated species reassignment or experimental
biomass state. The host qualifier is retained as a metadata question; it is not
used to infer clinical origin or silently corrected.

Supplement Table S1 (p17) lists local `GM` gene IDs, including peptidoglycan,
transport/stress and phosphate-associated annotations. Table S2 (p18) is only
a pointer to a separate gene-expression workbook. The two Mendeley workbooks
contain Data1–12, not that separate expression table. Gene annotations and
pathway illustrations do not themselves demonstrate a causal phosphate-release
mechanism; no protein identifier, enzyme claim or causal graph is imported.

The indexed publisher reagent snippet names hydrated La/Gd chloride salts and
ultrapure-water stocks, but truncates the culture methods. The complete medium,
viability/biomass mass basis, washing and treatment details remain unverified.
The separate patent's KLBMP 9673/GDMCC 63268 cannot fill those gaps. Likewise,
the 2024 M. saelicesensis KLBMP 9669 question in #397 remains separate.
