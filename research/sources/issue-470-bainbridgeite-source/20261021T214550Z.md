## What the originals establish

Part 3 pp.184–185 identifies CMNMC 46324, with the suffix describing dominant cations at distinct sites. Table 1's holotype values average five microprobe analyses; other specimen columns are separate. The 19 non-dash holotype means sum to the displayed 97.98 wt%, including calculated CO2 and H2O. Dash means below detection in that table, not numerical zero. Methods on p.187 use a JEOL 8230 at 20 kV, 10 nA and 20–50 micrometre beams; beam-instability corrections are described. CO2 and H2O were not directly analysed. FTIR covers 4000–400 cm−1 with 150 scans at 4 cm−1 resolution; its water bands are qualitative support, not an empirical three-water assay.

The powder experiment used Cu Kα1, 1.54060 Å, and a roughly 200 micrometre powder ball, with continuous phi rotation and 10-degree rocking. The single-crystal experiment used Mo Kα, 0.71073 Å, and a different single-crystal model. The powder cell in the abstract/Table 2 has V = 447.85(2) Å³; the single-crystal cell in Table 3/CIF has V = 443.06(4) Å³. Their displayed volume difference is 4.79 Å³. These are distinct measurements, not an interchangeable version discrepancy.

Table 3 gives 18,515 collected, 4,373 unique and 4,196 above-threshold reflections, 299 parameters, and observed/all-data R1 = 0.040/0.041. The CIF values 0.0396/0.0411 and wR2 0.1118/0.1134 round compatibly. Its temperature is 293(2) K, inversion-twin parameter 0.215(14), and five restraints. The paper's 21:79 twin-domain description is consistent in scale, without identifying the minor domain by new measurements. The authors explicitly caution that poor crystals give split reflections and residual peaks; Ca was excluded, Ce/Dy stand for lighter/heavier lanthanides and final occupancies were constrained.

The native model contains 119 scalar tags and six loops: nine atom-type rows, one symmetry operation, 41 atom rows, 41 anisotropic rows, 81 bonds and 524 angles. The 41 atom labels occupy 33 coordinate groups; each group's exact listed occupancies sum to one. No H coordinates or per-reflection loop occur in this parsed file. Its counts of 4,373/4,196 remain metadata, not an acquired list of reflections. The chemical formula includes H6 and the atom-type loop includes hydrogen; absent coordinates do not mean the mineral lacks hydrogen. The native formula-weight/volume calculation reproduces about 3.62342 g/cm³, consistent with its 3.623 model density. The paper's 3.49 uses an empirical composition; these representations do not supply measured density or author-intended replacement.
