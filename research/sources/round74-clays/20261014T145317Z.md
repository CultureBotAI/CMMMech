## Kaolinite source assessment

Ams2002 Sections2.1–2.5 and3.1–3.3, Figures1–5, distinguish KGa-1b/KGa-2 growth assays from the separate KGa-2 siderophore assay. Full text and all claim-bearing graphics were inspected. KGa-1b has trace halloysite, KGa-2 trace anatase; weak cleaning does not assign iron solely to the kaolinite lattice. Optical-density growth above the no-added-Fe/no-mineral control supports indirect iron acquisition. The record does not claim direct Fe release, a structural Fe mass balance, or industrial beneficiation.

Growth assays were at22°C; siderophore assays at28°C. The latter pooled triplicate supernatants at each condition/time, and HPLC values used a surrogate DFAM calibration because an isolate-specific purified standard was unavailable. Per-cell and total signals differ. The control is no-added-Fe, not chemically Fe-free; pH diverged among treatments. The cited hematite series and Maurice dissolution results are separate lineages. Exact Al-release enhancement from unavailable earlier controls is not accepted. NCBI300 currently names Ectopseudomonas mendocina, while the paper used Pseudomonas; this species mapping does not authenticate the unaccessioned isolate or repair its weak historical identification wording.

The complete original is `/private/tmp/cmm-round74-clays-research/ams2002.pdf`, SHA256 `c471e24d70ac62cfee9642ad3430ee2cb06a4a8e8672ae2f12de50cd0306465b`. Printed Elsevier rights are reserved. No supplement is identified within that acquired original; broader absence is not asserted. The mineral authority is IMA PDF109 (A,1980s.p.) and the complete two-page Handbook entry, not a kaolin commodity identity.

## Nontronite source assessment

Liu2017 Methods7–8, main Figures1–6, complete TextS1/FiguresS1–S10/TablesS1–S5 support the NAu-2 scope. Main zero-humic-acid controls still contain MR-1; **SI FigureS5b separately includes sterile arms with and without humic acid**, with much lower iron(II). This distinction is preserved. MR-1 taxon211586 and source ATCC700550 naming are grounded; the separate SWy-2 montmorillonite arms are not transferred.

The reduction bottles used washed MR-1, deoxygenated PIPES, NAu-2, KCl and humic acids at30°C in darkness. Explicit donor and assay-buffer pH remain missing from the inspected main/SI; the pH7 stock/model is not a substitute. Total versus dissolved/solid-associated iron(II) is operational, not a recovery yield. Original Figure6 and SI microscopy support partial illite-like domains alongside remaining nontronite; potassium is externally supplied, the products are mixed, and no matched sterile phase series establishes a uniquely biological transformation. Siderite assignment rests on limited fringes and is not adopted as pure recovered product.

TableS4's undetectable aluminium is not zero; its threshold is unspecified. The potassium label/added-KCl basis is unsuitable for an invented elemental mass balance. Approximate main-text iron concentration, graph endpoints and cited initial inventory are not reconciled into a new percentage. TextS1 is a model with its own assumptions; TableS5 mixes prior studies and is not independent replication. Missing data are retained rather than silently repaired.

Main PDF `/private/tmp/cmm-round74-clays-research/liu2017.pdf`, SHA256 `e400e983c37063a6f85d9ee6e3ae3861c5d0731e3299564e3c7933a691225286`; actual complete SI `/private/tmp/cmm-round74-clays-research/liu2017-si-acquired.pdf`, SHA256 `c1fed5c32020eabd7ce7305229c76e05a29505fbbcfb13c0245d0c03ab19f74a`.
