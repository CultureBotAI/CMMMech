## Iridium: accepted claims and contradictions

[Minoda et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3RA01249B) is the sole accepted
mechanism experiment. Read the complete primary XML and main PDF, Methods
2.1–2.5, results, Table 1 and Figures 1–7. Main figures on PDF pages 3–5 were
also inspected visually. Recovered all eight supplementary pages from Europe
PMC; read the complete text and visually inspected methods, Figures S1–S4 and
Tables S1–S2. Main XML, mirror PDF and supplement are associated artifacts,
not independent replications.

The paper's Galdieria sulphuraria 074W is NIES-3638. The fresh NIES page
explicitly maps this strain to **Galdieria javensis**, with the former name
and 074W alias preserved. NCBI species 3569544 was independently resolved.
The initially investigated historical species NCBI 130081 is not substituted
for the current strain mapping. Collection maintenance temperature is not the
experiment's cultivation or adsorption temperature.

The accepted mechanism uses lyophilized biomass in synthetic HCl/hexachloroiridate
solutions, no-adsorbent controls and chemical desorption. Supernatant depletion
and desorption use different denominators; neither is an isolated Ir(0) yield.
The record keeps assay loading, volume, time, temperature and biomass state.
It does not generalize this result to living metabolism, mineral dissolution,
waste feeds or industrial operation.

Preserved contradictions and limitations:

- Abstract reuse elution says 6 M HCl; Results 3.1.5 and Figure 4 say 4 M.
  Declining repeated-cycle sorption is observed, but acid strength is unresolved.
- Table 1 thiourea elution is 5.3 ± 0.8%, not literally zero as the prose
  suggests. HCl 98 ± 7% describes desorption of the adsorbed pool.
- Mixed-feed prose omits Cd while Figure 3 includes it; Fe and Cd quoted
  percentages differ from the plotted bars. Table S1's Ir/Fe coefficient 1.3
  supports limited enrichment, not universal selectivity or purified product.
- Imaging used 1000 mg/L Ir, main-text XAFS 10 mg/L, supplemental Figure S3
  200 mg/L. They are distinct preparations. Ir-Cl-like spectra and a proposed
  outer-sphere/amine model do not establish a specific cellular ligand or Ir(0).
- Table S2's 98.04 mg/g is a fitted parameter, not observed saturation.
  The printed Langmuir equation and rounded `b = 0.00` prevent a reliable
  independent reconstruction. No fitted capacity is adopted.
- Figure S1 supports rapid uptake at its own conditions; Figure S2 concerns
  Rh and is not used as Ir evidence. Table 1 does not separately define its
  uncertainty/replicate count even though main figures give mean, SD and n=3.

## Hafnium: accepted claims and corrections

[Alexeev et al., 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nsb903), *A novel protein-mineral
interface*, was acquired from Edinburgh's institutional repository. Read the
main article, Methods, Tables 1–2, figure explanations and attached affiliation
erratum; visually inspected PDF pages 2, 4 and 5 for the structural/table and
method details. The publisher page independently records the affiliation
correction; it is not a changed Hf result or a retraction.

Purified recombinant Neisseria gonorrhoeae nFbp expressed in E. coli TOP10
retained Hf(IV) from defined NTA-complex loading. Table 1's preparation gives
4.03 ± 0.10 mol Hf and 1.39 ± 0.04 mol P per mol protein; these are three
analytical determinations, not three independent cultures. Separate crystal
preparation conditions and differently ordered three-/five-Hf clusters are
retained separately. Tyr195/196 coordination and anomalous diffraction support
the structural assignment, while disorder, twinning and oxide-based geometry
restraints bound its interpretation.

No Hf-specific Tyr mutant necessity or matched protein-free cluster result is
established. Iron-mutant results cannot establish Hf causality. The crystal
preparation's reported 4.8 Hf/protein and nominal four-equivalent loading are
left unresolved, not turned into a recovery fraction. No donor strain is
provided; NCBI 485 is a species mapping only. E. coli is the expression host.

Live PDB 1O7T API and mmCIF confirm the study/cluster linkage and current
version 2.0, dated 2026-09-16. Its revision history records metalloprotein
remediation affecting nonpolymer descriptions and derived coordination
information. The deposit is associated evidence, not a second experiment.
Coordinates were not re-refined and no all-atom validation is claimed. No
separate supplement link was identified in the inspected publisher HTML;
this is a bounded page observation.

Deferred discovery leads include an Edinburgh thesis (metadata lead, not a
second accepted experiment), probiotic delivery of pre-synthesized HfO2 in
10.1039/D1NH00626F (not verified microbial Hf synthesis), chemically prepared
MOFs in 10.1039/D5RA06894K (antibacterial testing is not microbial production),
and broad patent element lists without a verified Hf assay. Zirconium results
were not transferred to hafnium. Reading access to the Nature paper does not
establish redistribution permission; raw remains outside Git.

## Osmium: accepted claims and weak controls

[Woolfolk and Whiteley, 1962](https://doi.org/10.1128/jb.84.4.647-658.1962)
was acquired as a complete 12-page primary scan via Europe PMC (PMC277940).
Read all extracted text including methods, results, discussion and references;
visually inspected PDF pages 2, 5 and 8 (Methods and Tables 4–5).

Crude extract of historically named Micrococcus lactilyticus consumed H2 in
separate OsO4, OsO2 and Os(III)-chloride assays. Table 4 quantities are retained
as substrate and gas uptake, not product yield. The authors' proposed endpoints
are osmate from OsO4 and metal from OsO2/Os(III), based on stoichiometry and
appearance. OsO4 must not become a claim of complete reduction to metal.
The substoichiometric OsO4 uptake is retained as a limitation.

No Os-specific phase spectroscopy/diffraction, isolated product assay,
uncertainty, replicate count, heat-inactivated/no-extract/no-H2 comparison is
reported in the inspected article. These are bounded source-content findings,
not claims that all microbial osmium literature lacks controls. Other-metal
arms and nonspecific enzyme discussion cannot supply missing Os controls.
No modern strain identifier is inferred from the historical Veillonella
alcalescens synonym. No whole-cell respiration, gene necessity, natural
prevalence or deployment claim is admitted.

EAWAG pathway diagrams were discovery leads, not substitute primary evidence.
Osmium fixation/coating and electrochemical mediator contexts do not themselves
demonstrate extraction or recovery. Contemporary broad patent lists were not
accepted without element-specific methods. PMC reading access does not imply
permission to redistribute the copyrighted scan.
