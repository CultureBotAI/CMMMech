## Conditional arithmetic check, not a repair

Using the issue's printed 0.5 g/L FeSO4·7H2O and 0.250 g/L raw-liquor Fe(II), ordinary molecular weights give 0.10044 g/L Fe in that printed medium. If volumes are additive, that is the whole medium iron source, and no unreported adjustment occurs, `Cmix = f × 0.250 + (1 − f) × 0.10044` predicts 0.13035, 0.16026 and 0.19018 g/L Fe(II) for waste fractions 0.20, 0.40 and 0.60. The source instead reports 7.13, 5.35 and 3.57 g/L. Both magnitude and direction disagree.

Table 1's 0.159 mol/L corresponds to 8.87936 g/L elemental Fe, independently highlighting the recipe/assay-basis gap. These calculations do not identify which text, unit, dilution or stock is wrong. They do not substitute Xiao's separate B-stock or assume a hidden dilution. The exact constants, assumptions and outputs are retained in `iron-balance-diagnostic.json`.
