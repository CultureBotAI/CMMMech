## Scientific And Domain Assessments

### Species and specimen boundaries

identity: supported. Targets: cmmmech:phlogopite.

IMA phlogopite identity differs from generic mica/biotite groups, muscovite and elemental potassium. The natural specimen formula contains iron/titanium and differs from ideal KMg3(AlSi3O10)(OH)2; no trace-purity claim follows from no accessory XRD detection.

### Culture and controls

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:phlogopite.

ATCC 200175 is paper-reported; taxon 71150 identifies the species. S1 replaces KH2PO4 with ammonium phosphate to balance phosphorus, without demonstrating equal nitrogen. Residual potassium in agar/cellophane prevents a potassium-free assertion. Preparation, 21 days at 25 degrees C in darkness, five elemental replicates and five sequenced replicates are correctly separated.

### Fungal-associated alteration

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:phlogopite.

Figure 1b1–b3 shows pre/post exposure surface differences and clustered deposits without identifying a secondary crystalline phase. No aged fungus-free or pH-matched mineral control isolates biological enhancement from chemical attack.

### Potassium denominator and statistical null

evidence: supported. Targets: cmmmech:phlogopite.

Table 3 explicitly uses micrograms/g dry mycelium. Phlogopite 8.64±2.91 and depleted control 5.52±1.69 both carry group c; the elevated numerical mean does not establish enhanced potassium acquisition. These are concentration means±SD, n=5, not a fraction of mineral potassium or an isolated yield. Table 2 biomass differences cannot replace the concentration null.

### Proposed molecular pathways

No significant potassium-transporter upregulation and the vermiculite aluminium prose/table conflict are retained.
