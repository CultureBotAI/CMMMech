## Scientific boundaries and contradictions

Gorceixite chemistry and room-temperature diffraction support a natural Ba-Al phosphate with a=7.0538(3), c=17.2746(6) angstrom and R-3m symmetry using253unique reflections. This is not a universal space-group assignment. Earlier Cm findings, chemical versus refined Ba occupancy0.882(6), and inferred hydrogen/charge balance remain unresolved. Deposited structure-factor tables were not acquired; no independent refinement rerun is claimed. IMA's abbreviated second-reference page155 differs from the actual951–958 original.

The florencite supplementary image explicitly specifies Ce nitrate, Ce(OH)3 and CeAl3(PO4)2(OH)6. It reports200°C for three months; the unusual duration is preserved. Reagent0.01M/0.03M wording leaves amount/volume ambiguities. Main p6 generalized formula and synthesis-reference32 differ from the supplementary equation and1990reference. Main Pt/Pd versus supplementary Au/Pd coating is also unresolved.

Figure6A is the synthesized material's XRD, not post-treatment phase proof. Figure6B and text report weight-based solid TREE/P ratios about2.1→3.5→3.0 after5MNaOH then1.2MHCl, both90°C. Preferential dissolution is an interpretation of residue composition. The study supplies no closed synthetic-arm mass balance, isolated Ce product, acid-only synthetic comparator, explicit synthetic-arm loading/duration, or defined synthetic replicate/error-bar basis.
