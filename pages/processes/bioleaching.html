Ion-adsorption rare-earth leach residue

A processed, mixed mineral residue remaining after ammonium-sulfate extraction of ion-exchangeable rare earths from weathered ion-adsorption ore. Zhang and colleagues (2024) prepared the studied material in the laboratory from Guangdong, China ore; this is not evidence of sampling an industrial tailings deposit. It is distinct from the natural ion-adsorption rare-earth clay feed, individual mineral species and isolated rare-earth products.
