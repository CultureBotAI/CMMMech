## Accepted evidence and exact limits

- **Losey2004**, DOI10.2113/gscanmin.42.4.1105: complete eleven-page original obtained from the ORNL neutron-science teaching collection. All five tables and seven figures inspected, including natural samples Trip06/27/50/79/89. Tables1-2 establish specimens and composition; Methods page1108 uses assumed Li2O for matrix correction and fixed Li-site occupancy. The samples are natural solid solutions with Mg and other substitutions; Trip50 is near the Fe/Mn boundary. Synthetic Trip100 is borrowed context, not an additional natural crystal measured in this experiment. Structural trends do not measure lithium diffusivity or electrochemical performance. The referenced CISTI anisotropic-parameter/structure-factor deposit was not acquired, so raw refinement reproduction is not claimed. A printed crystal-size unit differs from the micrometre Methods description; no crystal size is curated.
- **Schmid-Beurmann2013**, DOI10.1007/s00710-012-0250-6: complete fifteen-page institutional postprint from ORBi, with all nine tables and five figures. Natural Palermo triphylite9706.41 contained microscopically observed sarcopside lamellae despite no significant XRPD secondary phase. Powder Tri-77 and cuboid Tri-87 are separate arms; their spectroscopy and spatial profiles are not collapsed into one measurement. Table1/page503 gives exact doses and times; Table8/page509, Table9/page510 and Figures3-5/pages511-512 support a 50-100µm depleted reaction rim with unreacted core. Approximately0.30(7) lithium atoms per formula unit in the rim is a local composition, not a recovery fraction. Powder Tri-77 has an amorphous diffraction contribution and roughly93% ferric iron, with no whole-feed phase yield. Potassium incorporation versus a pore-hosted phase and a unique microscopic pathway remain unresolved.
- **Synthetic lithiophilite Tri-74** in that same experiment used49.7mg powder,10.5mgKMnO4,1mL0.1NHCl,120°C and28days. The natural triphylite comparison used roughly20-21mg oxidant and8days, with different particle form and composition. Despite the original's “same conditions” wording, this is not a matched material-effect comparison. Tables1/4 and Results page511 report no detected phase/lattice change. There is no direct solution-lithium balance or Mn-valence measurement for this arm; zero dissolution/oxidation is not established. The paper's700°C/two-day recipe is for the Li3PO4 precursor, not a demonstrated final LiMnPO4 firing schedule. Table4 versus5 b-parameter and Methods versusTable1 reference-card discrepancies are retained; exact conflicting values are not curated. No acid-only/no-oxidant matched control or independent replicate count is reported. No biological component or industrial performance is asserted.
- **Lyalina2023**, DOI10.5194/ejm-35-427-2023: complete eleven-page publisher original plus actual two-member official supplement. All figures/tables/algorithm inspected; native workbook cells, formulas and cached values read without executing the workbook.
