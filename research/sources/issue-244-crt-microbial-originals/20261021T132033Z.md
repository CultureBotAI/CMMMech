## Beolchini2012 original graphics and feed boundaries

The current institutional abstract distinguishes acidophilic Fe/S-oxidizing bacterial treatments of three materials. It reports no biological improvement for the sediment arm; microbial improvement is reported for catalyst and CRT fluorescent powder. The CRT-powder abstract numbers are55%Zn and70%Y. These reported mobilization percentages are not isolated product yield, purity, complete recovery or a closed mass balance. Proposed toxicity/organic-component explanations for higher-solid inhibition remain interpretations. The abstract does not provide enough strain, preparation and comparator detail for an accepted mechanism.

`gr1` separates Ni/V/Mo percentage panels from Zn/Cr/Ni/As panels, with control/biological and sulfur/iron treatment labels. The abstract suggests distinct feed groups; absent captions, the plot order is not used as a verified run-to-feed join. No catalyst or sediment percentage is transferred to CRT material.

`gr2` contains a cell-count bar panel and a pH time plot with sulfur/iron condition labels. The missing caption prevents joining these curves to a specific feed, inoculum or control preparation. Cell abundance and pH trends do not establish that the bacteria caused metal dissolution independently of chemical acid/iron effects.

`gr3` explicitly labels sediment and Zn fractionation, with exchangeable/carbonate, Fe/Mn oxide, sulfide/organic and residual fractions in ppm. It is not CRT-powder composition. Operational fractions are not interchangeable with mineral phases; their sums, initial inventory and extraction completeness cannot be verified without the omitted methods and exact cells.

`gr4` has Ni,V,Mo curves in part a and Zn,Y curves in part b. Its shared legend prints solid concentration2%or10% and Fe3+/Fe2+ratio0or2, with distinct diamond/square symbols, and days up to16. `gr5` has cell-count panels a/b over the same labeled time span but its legend prints Fe3+/Fe2+ratio0or1, while retaining solid2%or10%. That different iron-ratio label is preserved as an unresolved source/context discrepancy. It is not silently corrected, pooled or converted into a common dose. Concentration basis, preparation sequence, ferric/ferrous measurements and panel-to-run mapping need complete captions/methods.

Higher-solid arms in `gr5` have low cell signals, but selected plotted values are not digitized and missing error-bar definitions do not establish independent replication. `gr4` has markedly different element responses and low Mo curves for some arms; the source's general improvement/inhibition language is not promoted into a universal response claim. The Zn/Y pattern is compatible with the abstract's CRT-powder context, but this is a provisional mapping, not verified experiment identity. Neither source establishes a laboratory-to-plant extrapolation.
