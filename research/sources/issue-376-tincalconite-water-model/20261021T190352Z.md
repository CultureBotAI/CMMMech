## Original experiment and water model

[Luck and Wang (2002)](https://doi.org/10.2138/am-2002-2-319), printed pp.350–354, concerns a **synthetic single crystal**, grown from layered aqueous sodium borohydride and tetraheptylammonium iodide in acetone. The crystal was trimmed, rolled in epoxy, fixed on a glass fiber and measured at **291(2) K**. It is not a natural-sample water-content assay. The existing record correctly keeps this apart from natural Pabst material and the earlier synthetic studies.

The original p.350 methods describe SIR97/SHELXL97 refinement, testing full, one-half and one-third O12 occupancies. The freely refined occupancy is **0.337(5)**; the preferred fixed one-third model was chosen using thermal parameters and refinement figures. The paper quotes **0.334(5)** for Powell's 108 K work. Hydrogen atoms attached to disordered O12 were found in difference maps but then fixed to ride on O12, with Uiso constrained to 1.5 times its isotropic equivalent; other H atoms were included in refinement. All non-H atoms were described anisotropically in the original refinement. The new **minimal** CIF contains only scalar Uiso/Ueq values, so it does not permit inspection of those original anisotropic tensors, constraints, restraints or full refinement instructions.

Original Figures 1–4 are ORTEP representations of Na environments and the borate unit; Figure 5 is a calculated packing representation. They are not microscopic images of product yield or independent water measurements. Figure 3 displays overlapping disorder alternatives. The **0.765(17) Å O12–O12 separation** in Table 3 is an alternative-site contact, not evidence for a normal simultaneously occupied O–O bond. The source itself explains that only two of the six displayed O12 positions participate in the relevant Na3 environment at a time.

### Formula, mass and denominators

The abstract/discussion formula **Na6[B4O5(OH)4]3·8H2O** expands to **H28B12Na6O35** and gives eight-thirds, or approximately 2.667, lattice waters per Na2B4O5(OH)4 unit. **Table 1, printed p.351 / PDF 2**, instead prints **H28B12Na6O27**, **Mr 727.87776**, **Z=3**, density **1.871 g/cm³** and F(000) **1301.7**. The eight-oxygen difference is preserved; the table is not silently repaired.

Conditional arithmetic in `numeric-audit.json` states its atomic weights and Avogadro constant. With those declared conventional weights, the expanded preferred formula has mass **855.8476 g/mol**, predicts density **1.87053 g/cm³** at V=2279.3 Å³ and Z=3, and has ideal neutral-atom F(000)=**1302**. Using the table's printed Mr instead gives **1.59084 g/cm³**; the literal table composition gives F(000)=**1110**. Small atomic-weight-convention differences in formula masses do not remove the eight-oxygen discrepancy. These are consistency diagnostics, not a corrected refinement or a statement of the authors' intended error.

The paper's **29.5 wt% H2O** is consistent with **total water-equivalent mass**, including six water equivalents from the twelve hydroxyl groups plus eight lattice waters per tripled formula: the declared calculation gives **29.4690%**. Lattice water alone is **16.8394%**. Neither number is a newly measured dehydration yield. For the three-water-per-unit formula, the same arithmetic gives **30.9231%** total water-equivalent. At the same printed 2002 cell volume it predicts density **1.90991 g/cm³**, whereas p.353 states **1.94 g/cm³** for that formulation. The source's unstated density basis remains unresolved; no adjustment is selected.

Additional original discrepancies are retained: the abstract labels 512 data by `Fo > 4σ(Fo)`, while Table 1 uses `|I| > 2σ(I)`; no equality of these selection rules is assumed. Table 3 gives Na3–O11 **2.3433(19) Å**, while the following p.352 prose prints **2.433(19) Å**. The table also contains selected uncertainty differences from Table 4. The coordinate-derived selected distance agrees approximately with the Table 3 central value; this does not authorize rewriting the source or expanding the owner.

The AMC file additionally preserves exact special-position fractions, allowing multiplicity checks without rounding them into extra sites. The minimal CIF drops uncertainties and anisotropic Uij; its metadata do not document the author's complete occupancy-constraint implementation.

Using exact AMC fractions, the unit cell contains **Na18 B36 O105 H78**. That is Na2B4O11⅔H8⅔ per Na2 unit, consistent with the minimal CIF's `Na2 B4 O11.667 H8.667` and density **1.866 g/cm³** (calculated with stated weights: **1.86615**). O12 has multiplicity 18 and occupancy 1/3, giving six O atoms per cell; H121 and H122 each have occupancy 1/6, together giving six H atoms. The paper's preferred eight-water tripled formula with Z=3 would contain **H84**, six more H per cell. Both the exact AMC and rounded CIF occupancies are retained. This is an unresolved representation discrepancy, not a license to double H occupancies or adopt the minimal formula as a new species formula.

Twelve selected Table 3 distances were independently recomputed from the rounded deposited coordinates and the printed symmetry transformations. The full residuals are saved. These calculations do not rerun a least-squares refinement, determine disorder correlation or confirm a water count independently.

The DIF file has **91 calculated powder lines**, using wavelength **1.541838 Å**. All 91 hkl d-spacings and Bragg 2θ values were recomputed within the printed rounding intervals.
