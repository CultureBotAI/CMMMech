## Mechanism and adversarial boundaries

Jew2014 pp22–24 describe gamma-treated commercial cinnabar, nominal purity greater than99.99%, 2g per250mL flask in filtered New Idria drainage water atpH3.25. Live or irradiated biofilm was added; incubation was21°C, unshaken. Oxic reactors used150mL for30days, anoxic and abiotic controls100mL; added protein differed slightly. Figures2–3 support greater operationally filtered total Hg in live oxic cinnabar than killed controls and a small initial anoxic pulse followed by decline. Figure4 provides iron-redox context. No causal species identifier is assigned to the unresolved community.

The record retains these limitations:

- Commercial phase assignment and >99.99% purity are not a displayed cinnabar diffractogram, quantitative polymorph analysis or post-irradiation verification.
- Unknown particle size; 150 mL oxic versus 100 mL anoxic and abiotic controls, slightly different protein inocula, and chemically altered killed biomass.
- Figures report n=3 and error bars, but Methods enumerates 25 total microcosms without clearly establishing independent reactor replication.
- Operational 0.02-micrometre-filtered total Hg, not independent Hg(II) speciation, mass closure or recovered product yield.
- Residual oxygen in unpurged anoxic medium and sampling outside the glove box qualify oxygen necessity. The isolated Thiomonas null used different medium and does not identify the causal community member.
- Page 25 says abiotic Hg declined below detection; pages 28–30 discuss abiotic release and similarity to killed controls. No plotted abiotic Hg series resolves that wording conflict.
- The day-three activity quotient is calculated for metacinnabar; the idealized solubility comparison is not measured cinnabar recovery.
- No supplement citation in the complete original and no linked supplement surfaced in bounded searches; the inaccessible publisher inventory prevents a universal absence claim.

The abstract's roughly500micrograms/L result concerns metacinnabar, and the maximum516.5micrograms/L across arms is not adopted as a cinnabar concentration. No graph-digitized cinnabar yield, industrial deployment, detoxification or isolated product is asserted. No schema change is needed.
